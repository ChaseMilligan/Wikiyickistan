    Arcannabis System Rework

    Arcannabis System Rework

    Jul 30, 202610 min read

    The future of Arcannabis is going to borrow heavily from the cooking section of Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting, and a little from Drakkenheim.

    Landrace Strains

    There are 4 landraces of Arcannabis to represent the 4 different rarity levels. Each landrace is the equivalent of a magical essence from a creature. These are the only strains of Arcannabis that occur naturally in the wild though it is incredibly rare. They are for the most part grown in greenhouses, or they are made from enchanting mundane cannabis with a magical essence of the appropriate potency.

    RarityNameDurationCost per Unit
    UncommonFizzlebud1 hr165 gp
    RareChimera Cookie Cake2 hrs615 gp
    Very RareMindflayer Haze4 hrs3,380 gp
    LegendaryTarrasque Breath OG8 hrs17,700 gp

    1x unit of Arcannabis is typically enough to Roll up for up to five Medium or smaller creatures.

    Smoking a Landrace Strain: Smoking a Landrace Strain with no other monster components mixed in will apply the 1Valor(X) condition to the character for the duration. The duration of the effect, and the number of d4 rolled for 1Valor increases by 1 for each rarity level above uncommon.

    Smoking a landrace strain can also remove 1 level of Contamination (Conditions of Drakkenheim) once per day due to trace amounts of Arcanite residue.

    Weed ‘em And Reap

    Seeds

    There are only seeds of Landrace Strains. When a player sows a seed they decide whether they want the plant to produce smokable Arcannabis, or seeds. Each plant can produce 1d8+2 seeds. Each plant can produce a minimum of 1d4+2 units of smokable Arcannabis. If seeds are purchased they cost half the price of their smokable product, and take up to 3 months in game to fully mature.

    Soil

    The growth speed can be increased based on the soil quality. Each soil quality level can handle a certain amount of seeds before the nutrients are depleted.

    Soil QualityNutrient CapacityGrowth RateCost
    Uncommon2 plants0x (3 months)15 gp
    Rare4 plants1.5x (2 months)25 gp
    Very Rare8 plants3x (1 month)40 gp
    Legendary16 plants6x (2 weeks)65 gp

    Fertilizer

    The yield can be increased by adding fertilizer to the soil. Adding fertilizer to the soil allows you to add an extra 1d4 units + 1 for every rarity level above uncommon. Each unit costs 30 gp, and is enough to fertilize one plant.

    Fertilizer Example: Fizzlebud yield without fertilizer is 1d4+2 units. With fertilizer it becomes 2d4+2 units. Mindflayer Haze without fertilizer is also 1d4+2 units, but with fertilizer it becomes 2d4+4.

    Water

    Each plant must be watered twice per day to continue growing. Plants will not wither away, but enter a dormant state until watered again. Most adventurers, and craft growers use Drizzlemote Pots as a watering solution.

    Drizzlemote Pot

    Wondrous item, common

    Component: Volatile mote of water

    This pot has been infused with a volatile mote of water harvested from an Elemental that saturates the soil within the pot with water evenly twice per day.

    Mix, Mix, Swirl, Mix

    Like crafting magic items and cooking magic food, brewing Arcannabis strains uses harvested monster components to impart magical effects on enchanted cannabis, known as Arcannabis. When smoked, Arcannabis strains have long-lasting magical effects. These effects vary based on two factors: the type of component (for example, eye vs blood), and the creature type that the component came from (an aberration’s eye has different properties to a dragon’s eye). Different strains call for different mixes of ingredients; Goodberry Cough requires just spice, while Medusa’s Gaze Haze needs an eye, blood, and scales. The more ingredients a strain calls for, the more effects it imparts simultaneously, and the harder it is to brew.

    Any strain that includes monster components from a contaminated Drakkenheim monster will apply 1 level of 2contamination when smoked

    Dispelling Arcannabis?

    For the purposes of the dispel magic spell, all of a strain’s effects count as one single spell of a level based on the strain’s rarity: uncommon, 2nd-level; rare, 4th-level; very rare, 6th-level; legendary, 8th level.

    Brewing Checks

    Brewing an Arcannabis strain requires these 3 things:

    • Monster Components
    • 1 unit of a Landrace Strain, or 1 unit of mundane cannabis and an Essence
    • Herbalism Kit, Alchemist Supplies, Brewer’s Supplies

    A creature with all these in their possession can spend 1 hour brewing. At the conclusion of the hour, the creature makes a Wisdom (Alchemist’s Supplies, Brewer’s Supplies, Herbalism Kit) check against the DC of the strain (see Ingredients and Strain DC table below). Depending on the difference between the DC and the result of the check, a strain can have quirks.

    Strains

    There are two types of strains: staple ones which can be brewed with monster components of any type, and ‘boss strains’ which require components specific to boss monsters. Players can discover new strains through experimentation. Players are encouraged to come up with their own names for their strains.

    Potency and Essence. Essence is required for all Arcannabis strains; it is the factor that decides how powerful the strain’s effects will be. As with crafting, essence dictates the rarity of the strain, from uncommon to legendary. Rarity’s effect on Arcannabis strains is described in the Smokable Component Effects section.

    Smokable Components. Edible components from Heliana’s Guide will double as Smokable Components for the purposes of Arcannabis brewing. The components in the harvesting chapter marked with a superscript ‘E’ (E) are edible—they all have magical effects if properly prepared. There are 10 types of edible components: blood, bone, brain, egg, eye, fat, flesh, heart, liver, and spice. Not all creature types supply every type of edible component. For a breakdown of each component type’s effects, see Edible Component Effects section. Any unlisted nonmagical ingredients are supplied from a player character’s normal rations, and don’t require detailing.

    Difficulty. The more ingredients a strain has, the more magical effects it imparts to those who consume it, and the higher the DC is to mix it. There are four tiers of difficulty:

    Edible Monster Component Summary

    Pass The Dutchie to the Left

    Roll up

    Breaking down 1 unit of Arcannabis takes 5 minutes, and makes up to 5 pre-rolls for medium or smaller creatures. Rolling a whole unit in one joint makes 1 large pre-roll for large or smaller creatures, but medium or smaller creatures that blaze an entire large pre-roll to themselves must make a VDC(strain difficulty) Constitution check, or roll 1d6 on the tweaker table below to determine a condition that will be applied to them for the duration of the strains effects.

    Tweaker Table (see Conditions of Drakkenheim)
    1d6Condition
    1Distracted
    2Staggered
    3Addled
    4Dazed
    5Defenseless
    6Shocked

    Blaze

    All that is required to smoke an Arcannabis pre-roll is a flame. Any flame will work. It takes 1 minute to smoke a pre-roll, and 4 minutes after to feel the effects and applies 1 Impairment Levels.

    Puff, Puff, Pass

    Puff, Puff, Pass is similar to casting a ritual spell as a group. Anytime two or more creatures spend 10 minutes rolling up and blazing pre-rolls from the same unit of Arcannabis together, they will gain the 1Valor(1d4) condition for an hour. Add +1 to the 1Valor roll for each strain rarity level above uncommon.

    Pack Flaws

    d8Flaw
    1Rotten Pack. Smoking a unit with bud rot leaves you poisoned and unable to benefit from short or long rests. Spells and magical effects that remove poison instead suppress the effect for 1 hour.
    2Pookin’ Pack. Visual and audible hallucinations cause you to become distracted. You have disadvantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma checks, and on initiative rolls.
    3Tongue Toked. Your tongue becomes enchanted; you can speak only in a language associated with one of the creature types whose magical component you smoked (GM’s choice). For example, if you ate a fey’s component, you might speak only Sylvan.
    4Fart Pack. The incredibly pungent smell of donkey fart clings to you, and your belongings. You have disadvantage on Charisma checks against creatures within 30 feet of you that can smell, and you have disadvantage on Stealth checks against creatures that can smell. After 1d8 hours (known only by the GM), you release a pungent miasma with the effects of the cloudkill spell, centred on yourself. This cloud lasts for 1 minute.
    5Hack Attacks. This strain was cured poorly, and has a very harsh smoke causing coughing fits for the duration. You have disadvantage on saving throws made to maintain your concentration.
    6CBD Pack. After 1d4 hours (known only by the GM), you become very sleepy and extraordinarily relaxed. You have disadvantage on Dexterity checks and Dexterity saving throws.
    7Anauroch Cottonmouth. Your tongue swells and your mouth dries to cracked parchment. At the start of each of your turns, you must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or use your action or bonus action to gulp down a drink, chew something, or otherwise try to moisten your mouth as you cough and smack your lips. While suffering this flaw, you also have disadvantage on Charisma checks that involve speaking clearly.
    8Couch-Lock Pack. This is that “one more hit and I’m not moving” kind of bud. Your body feels bloated, heavy, and rooted in place. Your speed is reduced by 5 feet as long as the effect persists.

    Pack Boons

    d8Boon
    1Iron Lungs. You have resistance to poison damage and advantage on saving throws made against the poisoned condition.
    2Sweet Terp Breath. The strain’s enchanted terpenes perfume your exhale with an oddly pleasant aroma. You have advantage on Charisma checks against creatures within 30 feet of you that can smell.
    3Pack of the Linguist. You gain the ability to speak one language associated with the creature type of each magical component you consumed (GM’s choice). For example, if you ate an elemental’s component, you might be able to speak Primordial.
    4Slow-burn Focus. The high comes on in a steady, manageable wave. The gentle, sustained buzz sharpens your grip on your spells. You have advantage on saving throws made to maintain your concentration.
    5Fearless High. A steady calm washes away intrusive dread. You become extraordinarily brave. You are immune to the frightened condition.
    6Zyzz Pack. This strain hits your muscles with an empowering hum. You feel as if you could move mountains. You have advantage on Strength checks and you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
    7Blissful Recovery. The buzz settles into a deep, restorative chill. The next time you finish a short rest, you regain 1 additional hit point per Hit Die you roll to regain hit points. The next time you finish a long rest, you recover an additional number of expended Hit Dice equal to your proficiency bonus.
    8Quickstep Sativa. This strain leaves you energized. Your speed increases by 5 feet.

    Footnotes

    1. Valor. Add a die to your attack rolls and saving throws. The die rolled is specified by the condition, such as Valor (1d4) (see Conditions of Drakkenheim). ↩ ↩2 ↩3 ↩4

    2. Contamination. Characters in the world of Drakkenheim may be exposed to eldritch pollutants or arcane radiation emitted by 3delerium. These hazards cause a new condition called Contamination, which causes otherworldly Mutations that have both detrimental and beneficial effects (see Conditions of Drakkenheim). ↩

    3. Delerium. Delerium is a magical mineral left behind by the meteor that landed in Drakkenheim. It appears in geode clusters of translucent, sharp-edged crystals which reflect octarine light. The eldritch stones softly hum in dissonant tones, and glow brightly at night or when exposed to magic. Deposits are found throughout Drakkenheim, often fused into stone streets and buildings like crystalline moss. ↩
