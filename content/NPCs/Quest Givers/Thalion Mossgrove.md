# Thalion Mossgrove

Thalion Mossgrove is a native [[Eladrin]] Arcannabis sommelier, master grower, and spiritual caretaker living in a [[Hidden Grove]] near [[Doobius House]]. Villagers call his home the “witch’s hut,” but the name comes from suspicion rather than truth. Thalion follows [[The Old Faiths]] and uses Arcannabis in ceremonies that connect cultivation, memory, ancestry, and the changing seasons.

He is a quiet counterpoint to the noisy commerce and strict religious authority of [[Stickyickistan]]. Thalion sees Arcannabis as a gift of the land rather than merely a commodity, and he treats every plant as a living relationship requiring patience, respect, and careful tending.

## Overview

**Name:** Thalion Mossgrove  
**Species:** [[Eladrin]]  
**Occupation:** Arcannabis sommelier, master grower, herbalist, and spiritual guide  
**Residence:** [[Hidden Grove]] near [[Doobius House]], [[The Outback]]  
**Faith:** [[The Old Faiths]]  
**Known for:** Cultivating Arcannabis strains, evaluating monster components, and offering unconventional botanical wisdom

## Appearance

Thalion has moss-green hair, warm brown skin, and an earthy calm that makes him seem more like a gardener than a dangerous spellcaster. His clothing is practical and layered: soft greens, browns, faded yellows, and weathered fabrics suitable for working among plants.

He often wears small wooden beads, dried leaves, seed packets, and carved charms tied to his belt. His hands are usually stained with soil, sap, pollen, or Arcannabis resin. He smells faintly of earth, herbs, smoke, and rain.

His Eladrin nature sometimes shows through his appearance. His eyes may brighten like sunlight through leaves, his hair may shift with the season, or small flowers may appear in his clothing when he is especially pleased, angry, or spiritually focused.

## Personality

- Laid-back, warm, and approachable.
- Speaks slowly and thoughtfully, as though tasting each word.
- Distrustful of coin, official churches, and people who treat living things as disposable materials.
- Patient with plants and animals, less patient with careless people.
- Generous with knowledge when visitors show respect.
- Dreamy and prone to wandering into tangents, but observant when discussing cultivation or spiritual matters.
- Prefers barter, gifts, favors, and shared meals over ordinary payment.

## Voice and Mannerisms

- Pauses before answering, sometimes for several seconds.
- Runs his fingers through his moss-green hair while thinking.
- Uses gardening metaphors in ordinary conversation.
- Smells a plant, component, or drink before commenting on it.
- Touches the soil when discussing an important decision.
- Refers to rushed people as “seeds trying to become trees overnight.”
- Occasionally forgets that other people cannot see the spiritual significance he sees in a root, leaf, or cloud of smoke.

> “Roots do not hurry because the stone is impatient.”

> “A plant will tell you what it needs. The trick is not talking over it.”

> “Coin is useful, but it has never once made a seedling feel welcome.”

## The [[Hidden Grove]]

Thalion lives in a small, secluded grove outside the main paths of [[Doobius House]]. The hut is built from wood, woven branches, clay, and living roots. Its windows are covered with translucent leaves that glow softly at night.

The grove contains raised beds, drying racks, shaded seed shelves, stone basins, compost mounds, and several small ritual circles. Different sections are cultivated for different purposes: flavor, medicine, ceremonial use, sleep, memory, emotional balance, or controlled magical effects.

The grove is difficult to find without Thalion’s permission. A person who approaches carelessly may walk past the entrance several times, become distracted by a pleasant smell, or find a path blocked by harmless but stubborn vegetation.

Thalion does not build lethal defenses. He prefers misdirection, embarrassment, sleep-inducing pollen, tangled paths, and plants that loudly announce trespassers to the entire grove.

## Arcannabis Expertise

Thalion is a master grower who can cultivate and craft all known Arcannabis strains, provided he has the necessary seeds, time, tools, and components. He understands the differences between recreational, medicinal, spiritual, and dangerous uses of the plant.

He does not view every strain as inherently good or bad. He judges a strain by its purpose, preparation, effects, and the responsibility of the person using it. He strongly disapproves of growers who hide harmful ingredients, exploit desperate users, or produce strains without understanding their consequences.

Thalion may help characters:

- Identify an Arcannabis strain.
- Determine whether a product has been contaminated or adulterated.
- Cultivate a rare strain.
- Prepare a ceremonial or medicinal blend.
- Evaluate a monster component as an Arcannabis ingredient.
- Replace an illegal or dangerous ingredient with a safer alternative.
- Understand how [[Arcanite]]-rich soil affects Arcannabis cultivation.

## Monster Components

Thalion purchases or accepts unusual monster components for Arcannabis cultivation. He is particularly interested in components that carry qualities related to growth, transformation, memory, dreams, blood, poison, shadow, or regeneration.

He does not always pay in gold. Depending on the component, he may offer:

- Arcannabis seeds or finished products.
- Cultivation advice.
- A favor from the grove.
- A ritual blessing.
- Information about local creatures or plants.
- Help identifying a component’s properties.
- A future share of a successful strain.

Thalion considers monster components sacred ingredients rather than simple commodities. He refuses components harvested carelessly, taken from protected creatures, or gathered in a way that needlessly damages the surrounding ecosystem.

## Relationship with Barnabas Huggins

[[Barnabas Huggins]] of the [[Hotbox Inn & Tavern]] is one of Thalion’s most reliable connections in [[Doobius House]]. Barnabas sells some of Thalion’s novice-level Arcannabis to travelers, while Thalion handles cultivation, strain development, and specialized requests.

Barnabas respects Thalion’s expertise but does not always understand his spiritual explanations. Thalion appreciates Barnabas’s ability to keep customers away from the grove when Thalion needs quiet.

Barnabas may direct adventurers to Thalion when they have unusual monster components, while Thalion may send people back to Barnabas when they need lodging, food, directions, or a less mystical conversation.

## Relationship with the Local Church

The local church considers Thalion a heretic and witch because he follows [[The Old Faiths]], performs ceremonies involving Arcannabis, and refuses to recognize the church’s authority over his spiritual practices.

Thalion avoids direct confrontation. He does not want the people of Doobius House punished for sheltering him, and he knows that open conflict would give his enemies an excuse to destroy the grove.

Despite this, he refuses to abandon his beliefs or stop helping villagers who rely on his medicines, plants, and knowledge. His relationship with the church is tense, quiet, and likely to become dangerous if someone discovers that the grove contains rare or politically valuable strains.

## Roleplaying Thalion

Thalion should be calm without being vague, humorous without becoming foolish, and spiritual without becoming preachy. He often answers practical questions with a metaphor first, then gives a useful answer once the listener demonstrates patience.

He is not helpless or naïve. Thalion understands that people exploit what they do not respect, and he has spent years protecting his grove from thieves, officials, zealots, and careless adventurers. His relaxed demeanor is a choice, not a lack of awareness.

He becomes sharply serious when:

- Someone harms a plant or animal for amusement.
- A visitor lies about where a component came from.
- The church threatens his grove or his neighbors.
- A dangerous Arcannabis strain is being distributed irresponsibly.
- Someone treats Doobius House’s indigenous traditions as obstacles to be removed.

## Adventure Hooks

- Thalion asks the party to recover a rare plant growing near the edge of the [[Grim Forest]].
- A shipment of monster components intended for the grove disappears on the road to [[Doobius House]].
- Someone steals a dangerous Arcannabis strain and begins selling it under Thalion’s name.
- Thalion needs protection while investigating a patch of plants that has begun moving toward the village.
- A church official threatens to expose the grove unless Thalion surrenders a rare strain.
- Thalion believes a monster component contains a memory that could reveal what happened to a missing traveler.
- A failed cultivation experiment produces a plant that imitates the voices of people who have died.

## Secrets

- Thalion knows more about the hidden paths around [[Doobius House]] than he admits.
- He maintains private records of every monster component he accepts, including where it was harvested and who delivered it.
- Some of his most powerful Arcannabis strains are not sold for coin and are reserved for ceremonies, healing, or emergencies.
- He suspects that certain factions in Stickyickistan are attempting to industrialize Arcannabis cultivation in ways that could damage the land permanently.
- He may recognize spiritual or ecological changes in the [[Grim Forest]] before official investigators do.

## Rewards and Favors

Thalion rarely gives a conventional quest reward unless a contract specifically requires it. His favors may include:

- A safe place to recover.
- A rare Arcannabis seed.
- A custom strain prepared for a character’s needs.
- Advice about harvesting or crafting.
- A warning about a local faction or creature.
- A hidden route through the wilderness.
- A promise to cultivate something difficult to obtain.

Characters who repeatedly assist Thalion respectfully may gain **Trusted Grove Guest** as a story award. This grants access to the grove, consultation on Arcannabis and monster components, and limited assistance with cultivation, subject to Thalion’s judgment.
