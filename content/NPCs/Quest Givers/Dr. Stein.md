# Dr. Francis Norman Stein

Dr. Francis Norman Stein is a brilliant, distracted, and deeply irresponsible biomancy wizard whose experiments have reshaped parts of [[The Outback]]. His work combines arcane engineering, biological augmentation, and experimental creature design. The results are often impressive, frequently dangerous, and rarely approved by the [[Nevercough Tribunal]].

Stein is best known for creating strange chimeras, unstable constructs, and self-sustaining magical systems. He insists that most accidents are simply “unexpected data.” His body is now more arcane life-support system than flesh, sustained by a network of Magitech components, alchemical reservoirs, and magical prosthetics.

## Role in Stickyickistan

Stein operates from concealed laboratories and temporary workshops rather than a permanent public institution. His experiments have contributed to the magical contamination and unusual wildlife found in the [[Grim Forest]]. Although he has attempted to control the damage with wards, containment fields, and redirected growth patterns, his safeguards are unreliable and often create new problems.

For several years, Stein maintained an unofficial relationship with [[N.E.R.D]], where he lectured on biomancy, magical engineering, and experimental creature design. The institution valued his original research but repeatedly censured him for bypassing ethical review, mislabeling hazardous specimens, and testing theories outside approved laboratories. Stein eventually lost his visiting appointment after a student project escaped into a restricted archive.

N.E.R.D still consults Stein when its researchers need expertise in biomancy, monster biology, or unstable Magitech. The relationship is now transactional and cautious: professors request his help through sealed correspondence, while Stein borrows equipment, cites restricted papers, and recruits students who are willing to accompany him into the field. Some faculty members want his work preserved, while others believe he should be permanently barred from every laboratory in Stickyickistan.

The [[Ashen Shadow Society]], the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]], independent researchers, and wealthy industrialists have all sought Stein’s expertise at one time or another. Many of them regret the arrangement, but few deny that his inventions work.

## Appearance

Stein is a thin, elderly Human wizard whose remaining organic features are surrounded by brass fittings, glass tubes, insulated cables, and articulated Magitech braces. His voice occasionally cuts out, emits a burst of static, or is replaced by the hiss of a pressure valve.

He wears a stained laboratory coat beneath a heavy traveling cloak. His pockets contain notebooks, surgical tools, incomplete diagrams, unstable samples, and objects he has forgotten are dangerous.

## Personality

- Brilliant but easily distracted.
- Treats ethical concerns as logistical obstacles.
- Speaks in technical language even when simple words would suffice.
- Views monsters as research opportunities before recognizing them as threats.
- Genuinely cares about his creations, though he rarely understands what they need.
- Becomes defensive when anyone describes his work as irresponsible.

## Motivations

Stein wants to understand life, death, transformation, and the creation of artificial organisms. He is especially interested in preserving consciousness through biological and mechanical means.

He also wants to recover lost research, reclaim abandoned laboratories, and prove that his work can be controlled. 

## Mannerisms and Sayings

- Takes notes during conversations and occasionally forgets the person is still speaking.
- Adjusts a valve on his neck whenever he is excited.
- Refers to explosions as “rapid unscheduled releases of energy.”
- Calls monsters “uncooperative research partners.”
- Pauses mid-sentence to record a new hypothesis.

> “A dangerous experiment is merely an experiment that has begun collecting its own evidence.”

> “I have not lost my laboratory. It has simply become mobile, hostile, and difficult to locate.”
