
Barnabas Huggins is the owner and proprietor of the [[Hotbox Inn & Tavern]] in [[Doobius House]], an aging inland village in [[The Outback]] of [[Stickyickistan]]. He is a stout, rosy-cheeked man in his mid-50s with thinning auburn hair, a permanently dusty apron, and a laugh that can be heard from the village square.

Barnabas is a warm and practical innkeeper, a natural storyteller, and one of Doobius House’s best sources of local information. He knows which travelers arrived, which hunters failed to return, which caravans are late, and which rumors are worth following. His stories blend truth with exaggeration so freely that even he sometimes forgets where one ends and the other begins.

## Overview

**Name:** Barnabas Huggins  
**Species:** Human  
**Occupation:** Innkeeper, tavern keeper, rumor broker, and informal community contact  
**Residence and workplace:** [[Hotbox Inn & Tavern]], [[Doobius House]]  
**Region:** [[The Outback]], [[Stickyickistan]]  
**Affiliations:** Local Doobius House community; informal contact for [[Free League of Stickyickistan]] adventurers  
**Alignment:** Neutral good or lawful good, depending on the needs of the campaign

## Appearance

Barnabas is broad through the chest and shoulders, with strong forearms shaped by years of hauling sacks, moving barrels, chopping food, and repairing the inn. His cheeks are usually flushed from the heat of the kitchen, the local weather, or an especially good story.

He wears plain but well-kept clothes beneath a stained apron. His hair is auburn and thinning, though he combs it carefully every morning as if discipline alone might reverse the process. He usually carries a rag, a ring of keys, and a small notebook containing tabs, supply lists, names, and rumors.

## Personality

- Warm, welcoming, and larger-than-life.
- Sharp-eyed despite his cheerful manner.
- Protective of the Hotbox, its staff, and the people of Doobius House.
- Pragmatic about food, drink, smoke, gossip, and coin.
- Suspicious of people who ask too many questions while refusing to answer any themselves.
- Willing to hear out adventurers, but dislikes reckless people who endanger locals.
- Believes a hot meal and a good night’s sleep solve more problems than most people expect.

## Goals

- Keep the [[Hotbox Inn & Tavern]] profitable and respected.
- Protect Doobius House from threats emerging from [[The Outback]] and the [[Grim Forest]].
- Make sure travelers have a safe place to rest before continuing their journeys.
- Maintain enough connections to learn about danger before it reaches the village.
- Help reliable adventurers find legitimate work without turning the inn into an official guild office.
- Preserve the Hotbox as a community gathering place rather than letting it become another abandoned frontier building.

## Fears and Weaknesses

Barnabas fears that repeated disappearances, monster attacks, and failing trade routes will cause travelers to avoid Doobius House. If the roads become more unsafe, the village’s economy may collapse and the Hotbox may become one more empty building on the edge of the wilderness.

He also worries that his willingness to pass along rumors and opportunities may eventually bring dangerous people to the village. Barnabas knows that [[The Roach Clip]] sometimes circulates through travelers and tavern contacts, but he does not officially endorse illegal work or knowingly put villagers in unnecessary danger.

## Voice and Mannerisms

- Speaks loudly, especially when the common room is busy.
- Laughs at the end of his own stories, whether or not they are funny.
- Wipes his apron with a rag that never seems to become clean.
- Winks when sharing a rumor, secret, or questionable deal.
- Slaps the bar when making an important point.
- Calls regular customers “family” and suspicious strangers “future paperwork.”
- Refers to dangerous trips as “walks with consequences.”

> “I can sell you a hot meal, a decent smoke, or a rumor worth more than either. But if you’re heading into those woods, you’d better have a reason, and a way back out.”

## Knowledge

Barnabas can provide information about:

- Conditions along the roads through [[The Outback]].
- Activity near the edge of the [[Grim Forest]].
- Events in [[Doobius House]] and nearby settlements.
- Merchants carrying food, supplies, [[Arcannabis]], or unusual goods.
- Local reactions to the [[Free League of Stickyickistan]].
- Rumors circulating through [[The Rolling Papers]] that arrive weekly in Doobius House.

He does not guarantee that every rumor is true. He usually knows who told him the story, how much they had to drink, and whether they had a reason to lie.

## The Hotbox Inn & Tavern

The Hotbox is Barnabas’s home, business, and information network. He serves common meals, [[Zooka Malt]], spiced Arcannabis drinks, novice-level Arcannabis, shared bunks, private rooms, and local rumors.

Barnabas is not an Arcannabis grower or strain-maker. He buys stock from familiar suppliers and directs customers seeking specialized cultivation or unusual monster components toward [[Thalion Mossgrove]].

The inn’s regular customers include villagers, hunters, travelers, caravan workers, adventurers, and passing merchants. Barnabas remembers faces and habits, making it difficult for strangers to operate unnoticed in the common room for long.

## Relationship with Adventurers

Barnabas welcomes Free League adventurers because they bring news, money, and occasionally solutions to problems the village cannot handle alone. He may:

- Provide directions toward dangerous wilderness areas.
- Introduce adventurers to hunters, guides, and caravan workers.
- Offer shelter to people returning from an expedition.
- Pass along public jobs posted in [[The Rolling Papers]].
- Hold messages, payment, or supplies for trusted local contacts.
- Ask adventurers to investigate threats before they reach Doobius House.

He is not a formal Free League officer and does not want the Hotbox treated as an official guildhall. He sees himself as a local contact who helps capable people find one another.

## Roleplaying Barnabas

Barnabas should feel like a trusted frontier innkeeper rather than a quest dispenser. He feeds the characters, asks where they came from, notices their equipment, and only gradually explains what has been happening.

He is generous with information when the party treats people respectfully. He becomes guarded if they threaten villagers, mock Doobius House, or treat the missing hunters as nothing more than payment opportunities.

If the party succeeds in protecting the village, Barnabas offers free room and board at the Hotbox for a week and considers them trusted guests. If they cause unnecessary trouble, refuse to pay their tabs, or endanger the inn, he records their names in his private ledger and makes sure every local knows what happened.
