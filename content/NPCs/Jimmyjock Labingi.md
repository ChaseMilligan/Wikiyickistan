Jimmyjock Labingi was a legendary bard, and the bastard son of a native concubine and [[Duke Doobius Highblade]]. There are little to no records of who his mother was, as she is only ever marked in official documents as Mary Halfling-woman. Doobius never truly accepted Jimmyjock as his actual son since he wasn't fully human. Doobius eventually went on to marry a human woman, have fully human children with her, and left all of his estate to them when he passed away leaving Jimmyjock with nothing. In light of who his father was, Jimmyjock staunchly opposed the incursion of settlers upon his maternal ancestral lands. He was highly educated for the time, a talented poet, and man of the people, so when the [[Twothirdlings]] made their own settlement, [[Spliffholm]], they elected him the leader.
