#Northbourne #Alive 
### The Serpent in Silk

**Race**: [[Human]]  
**Role**: Matriarch of [[House Northbourne]] / Social Manipulator  
**Personality**: Elegant, poised, and always in control—until she doesn’t need to be.  
**Public Persona**: A philanthropist and patron of the arts, known for high-society events, grand auctions, and charity galas.  
**Secret Role**: Ensures Stickyickistan’s power players remain indebted to House Northbourne, subtly controlling city officials, guildmasters, and rival nobles.  
**Notable Traits**:
- Maintains a network of spies among servants, stable hands, and courtiers.
- Runs a private ledger of every scandal and vice among the nobility, which she uses as leverage.

Secretly funds and influences mercenary groups, ensuring that no one faction can outmaneuver House Northbourne.
