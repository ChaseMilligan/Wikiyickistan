#Norfie #Northbourne #Alive 
### The Queen of the Pit

**Race**: Human  
**Role**: Daughter of the [[House Northbourne]]'s Head / Overseer of [[Belly of the Beast]]  
**Personality**: Intense, passionate, and obsessed with gladiatorial blood sport.  
**Public Persona**: Officially manages exotic game hunting expeditions for [[House Northbourne]].  
**Secret Role**: Runs the underground fighting pits at Belly of the Beast, organizing gladiator fights, beast battles, and high-stakes duels.  
**Notable Traits**:
- Has an eye for talent, recruiting pit fighters and underground champions to keep the blood flowing.
- Oversees the "special guests" room, where House Northbourne’s most elite clients make backroom deals over the blood sport.

Knows how to “fix” fights, ensuring the right people win—and the house always profits.
