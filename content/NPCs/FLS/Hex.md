# Hex

**Hex** is the Guild Artificer of the [[FLS Guildhall]] and the Free League’s specialist in magical equipment, enchantment, and arcanomechanical repairs. Their workshop is crowded with half-finished devices, labeled components, spare gears, cracked wands, and at least one object that has been humming for several weeks.

## Role in the Free League

Hex helps adventurers understand, repair, upgrade, and commission magical equipment. They maintain the Guild’s catalogue of known magic-item recipes and update it whenever adventurers return with new discoveries, monster components, or documented crafting techniques.

A recipe in Hex’s catalogue means they know how to make the item. It does not mean a finished item is currently in stock. Hex may accept a commission, place an item on order, or offer an existing item from the workshop’s limited inventory.

Hex works closely with [[Pearl Firehand]], who handles physical construction and forging, and [[Zaza]], who supplies potions, biological components, essences, herbs, and Arcannabis-related materials. Depending on the item, Hex may handle the enchantment personally or coordinate a larger crafting team.
