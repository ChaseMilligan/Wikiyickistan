# Hex

**Hex** is the Guild Artificer of the [[FLS Guildhall]] and the Free League’s specialist in magical equipment, enchantment, and arcanomechanical repairs. Their workshop is crowded with half-finished devices, labeled components, spare gears, cracked wands, and at least one object that has been humming for several weeks.

## Role in the Free League

Hex helps adventurers understand, repair, upgrade, and commission magical equipment. They maintain the Guild’s catalogue of known magic-item recipes and update it whenever adventurers return with new discoveries, monster components, or documented crafting techniques.

A recipe in Hex’s catalogue means they know how to make the item. It does not mean a finished item is currently in stock. Hex may accept a commission, place an item on order, or offer an existing item from the workshop’s limited inventory.

Hex works closely with [[Pearl Firehand]], who handles physical construction and forging, and [[Zaza]], who supplies potions, biological components, essences, herbs, and Arcannabis-related materials. Depending on the item, Hex may handle the enchantment personally or coordinate a larger crafting team.

## Crafting Services

Hex can:

- Maintain and expand the Guild’s permanent magic-item recipe catalogue.
- Enchant eligible mundane items using suitable monster components and essence.
- Study a magic item to determine whether it can be dismantled for research.
- Repair damaged magical equipment when its recipe and components are understood.
- Upgrade eligible magic items when the required materials, time, and checks are available.
- Maintain a limited stock of completed magical equipment for immediate purchase.
- Coordinate with Pearl Firehand on magical forging commissions.
- Help adventurers determine which harvested components are suitable for a proposed enchantment.

All commissions follow the time, material, tool, and check requirements in [[Sources/Crafting]]. Hex is happy to explain the process, but charges for time spent on research, preparation, and failed experiments.

## Appearance

Hex is a lanky tiefling with charcoal-grey skin, pale blue eyes, and short horns wrapped in copper wire. They wear a long coat covered in pockets, tool loops, chalk marks, and tiny brass plates engraved with reminders. Their gloves are mismatched, and their left sleeve occasionally sparks when they gesture too enthusiastically.

A pair of enchanted lenses usually rests on Hex’s forehead. They insist the lenses improve precision. Everyone else suspects they are mostly used to make Hex look more mysterious while reading labels.

## Personality

Hex is curious, excitable, and easily distracted by unusual mechanisms. They are more comfortable discussing an item’s magical properties than its price, which is why Sterling handles most final invoices.

Hex dislikes destroying magical equipment, even when dismantling it would provide useful research. They ask permission before taking anything apart and clearly explain what might be lost. They are especially protective of items with unusual quirks, flaws, or evidence of a previous owner’s craftsmanship.

## Mannerisms

- Names every major tool and becomes offended when someone calls it “the wrench.”
- Writes notes directly onto scraps of metal, then forgets where the notes came from.
- Tests a magic item by holding it at arm’s length and saying, “Probably safe.”
- Keeps emergency goggles in every pocket except the one they check first.
- Refers to catastrophic failures as “unexpected recipe development.”

## Recipe Catalogue

Hex uses two important labels:

> **Pearl of Power, Recipe Known**

This means Hex knows how to craft a Pearl of Power, but no finished item is necessarily available.

> **Pearl of Power ×1, In Stock**

This means one finished Pearl of Power is currently available for immediate purchase.

The catalogue grows through Guild advancement, successful adventures, monster harvesting, research, and approved commissions. Finding a magic item during an adventure does not automatically add its recipe to the catalogue or allow the characters to keep it.
