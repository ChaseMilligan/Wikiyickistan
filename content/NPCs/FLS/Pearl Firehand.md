# Pearl Firehand

**Pearl Firehand** is the master blacksmith of the [[FLS Guildhall]] and one of the Free League’s most trusted craftspeople. She manufactures weapons, armor, shields, ammunition, and other metal equipment for adventurers, and incorporates suitable monster materials into mundane gear.

Pearl is also working toward forging magical equipment. Her smithing hammer must first be blessed upon the Wyrmbone Anvil in the [[Hall of the Wyrmbone Anvil]]. She hires adventurers to enter the closed [[GreenQuartz™ Mine]], clear the threat from the Hall, and carry her hammer to the anvil. Once blessed, the hammer allows Pearl to take on magical forging commissions through the Free League.

## Role in the Free League

Pearl maintains the Guildhall forge and handles the physical construction of equipment. She works closely with [[Vex]], who specializes in magical enchantment, and with [[Zaza]], who supplies alchemical materials, biological components, and unusual reagents.

For a magical commission, Pearl may require harvested monster components, suitable mundane materials, access to a forge and anvil, and time to complete the work. Depending on the item and the method used, she may manufacture the base item separately or use the forging process to combine construction and enchantment.

Pearl follows the Guild’s crafting standards, but she is not interested in making every commission identical. A weapon should have balance, a shield should feel trustworthy, and armor should fit the person who has to survive inside it. She considers technical perfection less important than making equipment that works when its owner is frightened, exhausted, and surrounded by monsters.

## Appearance

Pearl is a muscular dwarf with copper-brown skin, tightly braided silver hair, and a long beard decorated with small iron rings. Her hands are heavily callused, her forearms are marked with old burn scars, and one of her eyebrows is permanently singed. She wears a soot-black leather apron over practical work clothes and keeps her hammer within reach at all times.

Despite her name, Pearl’s hands are not literally made of fire. The nickname comes from the pale, glowing scars across her palms, left by an early accident involving molten Arcanite and an improperly quenched hammerhead.

## Personality

Pearl is blunt, disciplined, and deeply protective of the Guildhall’s adventurers. She has no patience for boasting, careless measurements, or anyone who describes a weapon as “basically finished.” She respects people who take responsibility for their mistakes and will repair damaged equipment for a fair price if the owner admits how it was broken.

She is proud of her work, but not vain. Pearl believes a craftsperson’s reputation belongs to the people who survive using their equipment. She would rather hear that an adventurer returned alive with a battered sword than receive praise for a beautiful weapon that failed in the field.

## Mannerisms

- Tests every weapon’s balance by spinning it once, then immediately scolds anyone who tries to copy her.
- Taps her hammer against the anvil when thinking.
- Keeps a collection of bent weapons labeled with the names of the monsters that ruined them.
- Calls poor workmanship “a future corpse with decorative features.”
- Refuses to discuss magical forging while actively extinguishing a fire.

## Services

Pearl can:

- Manufacture mundane weapons, armor, shields, ammunition, and metal equipment.
- Repair and refit adventuring gear.
- Incorporate suitable harvested monster materials into mundane equipment.
- Evaluate whether a monster component is physically appropriate for a proposed item.
- Forge magical equipment once her hammer has been blessed upon the Wyrmbone Anvil.
- Work with other crafters as part of a manufacturing, enchanting, or forging team.
- Accept approved commissions through the Free League’s crafting system.

Pearl does not personally provide magical enchantments before the Wyrmbone Anvil is blessed. Those commissions are referred to [[Vex]] or another appropriate crafter.
