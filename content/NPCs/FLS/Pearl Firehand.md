# Pearl Firehand

**Pearl Firehand** is the master blacksmith of the [[FLS Guildhall]] and one of the Free League’s most trusted craftspeople. She manufactures weapons, armor, shields, ammunition, and other metal equipment for adventurers, and incorporates suitable monster materials into mundane gear.

Pearl is also working toward forging magical equipment. Her smithing hammer must first be blessed upon the Wyrmbone Anvil in the [[Hall of the Wyrmbone Anvil]]. She hires adventurers to enter the closed [[GreenQuartz™ Mine]], clear the threat from the Hall, and carry her hammer to the anvil. Once blessed, the hammer allows Pearl to take on magical forging commissions through the Free League.

## Role in the Free League

Pearl maintains the Guildhall forge and handles the physical construction of equipment. She works closely with [[Vex]], who specializes in magical enchantment, and with [[Zaza]], who supplies alchemical materials, biological components, and unusual reagents.

For a magical commission, Pearl may require harvested monster components, suitable mundane materials, access to a forge and anvil, and time to complete the work. Depending on the item and the method used, she may manufacture the base item separately or use the forging process to combine construction and enchantment.

Pearl follows the Guild’s crafting standards, but she is not interested in making every commission identical. A weapon should have balance, a shield should feel trustworthy, and armor should fit the person who has to survive inside it. She considers technical perfection less important than making equipment that works when its owner is frightened, exhausted, and surrounded by monsters.
