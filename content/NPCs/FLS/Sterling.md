# Sterling

**Sterling** is the quartermaster and financial clerk of the [[Free League of Stickyickistan]]. They manage the Guild’s contracts, payments, supplies, and records, making them one of the few people in the FLS Guildhall who knows where anything is supposed to be.

## Role in the Free League

Sterling registers parties, verifies contract terms, distributes approved payments, and keeps track of rewards earned during adventures. They also manage the Guildhall’s basic supply stores, including rope, rations, torches, chalk, oil, spare bedrolls, and replacement paperwork.

Sterling is often the first person an adventurer meets when accepting a job. They explain where the party needs to go, what the patron expects, and what evidence must be returned before payment can be authorized. They are particularly strict about recording gold, magic items, consumables, downtime, favors, and lasting consequences for shared Free League continuity.

## Appearance

Sterling is a tall, elegant half-elf with silver hair, dark brown skin, and immaculate clothing that somehow remains clean despite working in a building full of adventurers. They wear a charcoal waistcoat, polished boots, and a silver chain connected to a ring of labeled keys. Their desk is covered in ledgers, contract slips, ink bottles, and small signs reminding people not to place monster parts on official documents.

## Personality

Sterling is calm, direct, and almost impossible to fluster. They are not humorless, but their jokes are delivered with such a straight face that adventurers often realize several seconds too late that they have been insulted.

They genuinely want adventurers to be paid fairly and properly credited for their work. Their strictness comes from experience: too many parties have lost rewards because no one wrote down who accepted the contract, who found the treasure, or whether the cursed sword was supposed to be returned.

## Mannerisms

- Straightens papers whenever someone says “we can remember it later.”
- Keeps separate ledgers for legitimate work and jobs that “officially never happened.”
- Pauses before answering questions that begin with “hypothetically.”
- Uses increasingly specific labels for dangerous objects.
- Has a stamp reading **PAYMENT PENDING VERIFICATION** and uses it with enthusiasm.

## Services

Sterling can:

- Register a party for a Free League contract.
- Explain a patron’s expectations and payment terms.
- Issue travel papers, letters of introduction, and basic contract documentation.
- Provide standard adventuring supplies at cost.
- Record XP, gold, magic items, consumables, downtime, favors, and story awards.
- Verify completed objectives before releasing payment.
- Direct adventurers to the [[The Rolling Papers]] or [[The Roach Clip]].
- Arrange Guildhall storage, recovery support, and referrals to specialists.

Sterling does not guarantee payment for unofficial jobs from [[The Roach Clip]]. They can record that a party accepted such a contract, but any dispute must be settled with the patron.
