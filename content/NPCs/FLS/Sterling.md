# Sterling

**Sterling** is the quartermaster and financial clerk of the [[Free League of Stickyickistan]]. They manage the Guild’s contracts, payments, supplies, and records, making them one of the few people in the FLS Guildhall who knows where anything is supposed to be.

## Role in the Free League

Sterling registers parties, verifies contract terms, distributes approved payments, and keeps track of rewards earned during adventures. They also manage the Guildhall’s basic supply stores, including rope, rations, torches, chalk, oil, spare bedrolls, and replacement paperwork.

Sterling is often the first person an adventurer meets when accepting a job. They explain where the party needs to go, what the patron expects, and what evidence must be returned before payment can be authorized. They are particularly strict about recording gold, magic items, consumables, downtime, favors, and lasting consequences for shared Free League continuity.
