
**Zaza** is the proprietor of [[Zaza's Apothecary]], a trusted supplier serving the [[Free League of Stickyickistan]]. Their shop provides potions, poisons, medicines, herbs, monster components, harvesting supplies, and expedition gear to adventurers who are willing to follow instructions and sign the appropriate waivers. Zaza is also a member of the [[Blossom Union]].

## Role in the Free League

Zaza manages the Guild’s apothecary and helps adventurers turn dangerous discoveries into useful consumables. They can identify biological monster components, prepare medicines and poisons, and teach the cultivation and brewing methods described in [[Arcannabis System Rework]]. Zaza also maintains the Guild’s stock of Arcannabis seeds, soil amendments, fertilizer, brewing materials, and finished strains.

Zaza sells ordinary supplies directly, while more powerful potions, poisons, and Arcannabis recipes become available as the Free League unlocks them. Rare ingredients and unusual strains may require a completed adventure, an approved recipe, or a favor for Zaza.

## Appearance

Zaza is a compact, halfling with dark hair, bright yellow eyes. They wear a patched leather apron covered in pockets, loops, labels, and tiny glass vials. Their sleeves are usually rolled up, and their claws are stained with whatever they were brewing most recently.

## Personality

Zaza is practical, suspicious, and deeply enthusiastic about useful substances. They have little patience for adventurers who describe an ingredient as “some weird goo,” but will happily spend an hour explaining the difference between venom, poison, sap, resin, and “stuff that is technically all four.”

They are careful with customers but reckless with personal experiments. Zaza believes most problems can be solved with the correct preparation, a clean container, and enough time to observe what happens next.

## Mannerisms

- Labels everything, including objects that clearly cannot be confused with anything else.
- Taps glass containers three times before opening them.
- Sniffs every ingredient, even when warned not to.
- Keeps a running list of adventurers who owe them empty bottles.
- Refers to Arcannabis strains as “cultivars,” unless someone is being annoying.
