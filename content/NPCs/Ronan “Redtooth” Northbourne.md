#Northbourne #Norfie #Alive 
### The Enforcer

**Race**: [[Human]]  
**Role**: Smuggling Boss / Norfies Enforcer  
**Personality**: Brutal, direct, and without patience for subtlety.  
**Public Persona**: Officially a stablemaster overseeing [[Magebred Animals]] war beasts for [[House Northbourne]].  
**Secret Role**: Runs [[The Norfies]]' smuggling network, ensuring illicit [[Alcohol]] moves smoothly across Stickyickistan.  
**Notable Traits**:
- Fearsome in a fight, known for personally beating down rivals and informants.
- Drives a custom-built elemental smuggling cart, using [[Arcanite]]-powered illusions to conceal contraband.

Deeply loyal to the family, but resents his lack of legitimacy among the noble elite.
