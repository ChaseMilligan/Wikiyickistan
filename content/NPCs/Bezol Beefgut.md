Owner and Head Chef of the [[Belly of the Beast]]

# Call to Action

Bezol has contacted [[Kezrin Malvek]] about the possibility of returning to adventuring now that he is financially stable and living up to his oath. More than a decade ago, with Freyr as his witness, he swore on his life to nurture and protect the land as it had nurtured and protected him. After making that vow, he stepped away from the adventuring life, devoting himself instead to building a culinary empire in Stickyickistan. Every step of the way, he ensured that all his produce and meats were locally and ethically sourced. Through his dedication, he rose to the title of Reapmaster Harvester and became a wealthy restaurateur.

Nowadays, his business can run smoothly without his constant oversight, thanks to the loyal and capable staff at his establishments. With this freedom, Bezol has begun to consider a return to the adventuring life—especially after the grand time he had in the land of [[Greyhawk]], aiding in the defeat of the avaricious young blue dragon [[Zephyron]]. His motivation, however, is not wealth or treasure; his bills are paid, and he considers himself rich enough in Freyr’s favor. Instead, he is driven by a righteous purpose and a deep curiosity.

Bezol wishes to seek out and harvest exotic creatures that most would never consider as food, experimenting with new recipes and tasting flavors yet unknown. He dreams of traveling to distant, unfamiliar lands, savoring their cuisine, and creating dishes that honor both the place and its bounty. Through these journeys, he intends to uphold the tenets of his Harvest Oath—being brave in the face of death, respecting nature, and embracing his place within it—all in the name of his patron deity, Freyr. And perhaps, along the way, he will revisit the joys of the old days: crafting special breakfast treats and hearty bush lunches for his companions while out on the hunt.

# Backstory

Bezol is a a Follower of one of [[The Old Faiths]]. His patron god is Freyr, a prominent god in the Norse pantheon, associated with kingship, fertility, peace, prosperity, and good weather. Freyr is also linked to sunshine, and abundant harvests. Bezol grew up on a farm in the plains of [[North Booftown]]. From the time he could walk he was helping his father chore in the fields, but where he really felt the most passion was with his mother in the kitchen after harvest. His mother, Aslaug, and father Snorri, made sure their boy Bezol grew up big and strong, and indeed he did. Snorri was a very knowledgeable farmer, with a formal education in botany, and Aslaug was the head chef of the realm renowned kitchen at the [[The North Booftown Downs]] called [[Simmer Down]].  Both of his parents were staunchly religious in the ways of [[The Old Faiths]], and had a profound impact on the way he would choose to lead his life, but like his cleaver, Bezol had a bit of an edge. As a youngster, Bezol tended to get himself into trouble. Starting fights on the school yard, and breaking into the school late at night, to do... well he didn't really think about what he would do once he got in. He just wanted to see if he could. If you ask him about it today, you will notice that every one of these tales of mischief involve a one [[NPCs/Kezrin Malvek|Kezrin Malvek]]. These two were a maniacal duo. Terrorizing neighbours, and pranking anyone dumb enough to fall into one of their schemes. They would also both eventually go on to attend Paladin college at [[Sensimilla University]] together. Again, as expected, these boys partied like there was no tomorrow. They may have gotten in trouble with the law a time or two, but luckily that didn't break their oath. Maybe bent it a little, but it stayed in tact is the point. Eventually they decided to embark upon a life of adventure with a couple friends they had made along the way, but Bezol always felt the call of home. The smell of the kitchen, the way the heat from the oven hit his face when opened, and most of all, the taste of a home cooked meal from his mom's kitchen. After a while of adventuring, Bezol parted ways with Kezrin and crew, who all went on to do some equally badass shit, but that's for another time. Bezol had a new quest; to live up to his parents expectations. Snorri and Aslaug were a little displeased with Bezol's antics as a young adult, and his time adventuring seemed like waste to them because of his talents in the kitchen being unused, so they put him to work. Bezol is no nepo-baby, in fact, Aslaug refused to ever hire him at an establishment she oversaw, of which there were many at the time. So, Bezol, a fully trained paladin, went to work as a line chef at a greasy dive spot in the gutter of [[Stickyickistan]], and worked his way up from there. After working as a line chef for a while, and doing some work on the side as a butcher for the [[Handler’s Guild]], Bezol had become very skilled at producing delicate cuts of meat from many common and exotic creatures. He eventually saved up enough money to buy the greasy dive from his boss, and quickly transformed it into a speakeasy steakhouse where he served slabs of divine quality, and maybe a little [[Alcohol]] under the counter. During his adventuring days, Bezol and crew were hunting a bandit that had shorted a very powerful client on a delivery. The client wanted the Bandit dead, and he was hiding in the Underdark using his connections on [[The Silt Road]]. While on the trail, they met a Drow, that offered them an Alcohol hook up which they passed on at the time. As an oath of the harvest paladin and a chef, it seemed like a shame to Bezol that the bounty of this particular harvest should go to waste, and can't be enjoyed in Stickyickistan, so when he opened up shop he took the Drow up on her offer. With this little scheme, Bezol's restaurant was being spoken about in hushed tones amongst the hip of the city, and they flocked to it in numbers. He knew the risk was high, so he made the front of the restaurant into a convenience store as a front, and only let a few people in the back at a time on a booking schedule. The prices were high, and the "steaks" were higher, but he made it work, and eventually cashed out on it all to buy a larger more legit establishment. This is where the story of the [[Belly of the Beast]] begins, and  where Bezol would perfect his craft as an Artisanal Chef and Reapmaster. Bezol coasted by for a while on his savings from selling the steakhouse, and eventually got an offer he couldn't resist. The Handler's Guild that he had been working with for a while, and [[The Norfies]] which he knew through the Alcohol business, are linked through [[House Northbourne]]. They offered him a deal to own the restaurant portion of a business idea they had that involved gladiator style blood sport with a meal. House Northbourne will hire adventurers to fight beasts which they then preserve and give to Bezol to prepare and serve to guests of the show, and patrons of the day. They will also often ask adventurers to head into [[The Outback]] and beyond to procure exotic beasts for their meat. Bezol has now been the owner of the Belly of the Beats for more than a decade, and has also been able to purchase and run multiple other restaurants including [[Simmer Down]], after Aslaug decided to retire and sell it. She didn't give it to him for a discount either. He had to win a bidding war against some city elites, and even a noble. Of course he had a lot of help along the way, but most would consider Bezol to be a self made man. Bezol is very comfortable these days, and plans to retire to Isles of Y'ummm one day. He still has that edge to him though, and yearns to relive his days of adventure. On a whim he decided to accompany [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] on a contract from [[Bramble Ironspark]] of [[Greyhawk]] to kill a young blue dragon - [[Zephyron]], that had been terrorizing their port city of [[Gradsul]]. He was nearly slain in the battle, but he had truly never felt more alive. He is excited at the prospect of accompanying them on more contracts. Especially if he can keep the flesh of a rare creature.
