### **Kezrin’s Introduction and Plea for Help**

_The smell of cheap tobacco and spilled ale clings to the air around Kezrin like an old cloak. He slouches in the dim neon glow of The Rusted Chalice, a half-drained tankard in one hand and a smoldering roll of something pungent in the other. His coat—once fine, now frayed—hangs loosely over his wiry frame, and his tired, yellow eyes flick up as you approach. He exhales a slow plume of smoke, watching you through the haze before offering a lopsided smirk._

**"Well now, what do we have here? A band of fine upstanding souls, come to share a drink with a man down on his luck? Or are you looking for something a bit more… lucrative?"**

_He gestures vaguely to the empty space on the table, an unspoken invitation to sit, though there's little hospitality in the offer—just the vague hope that conversation might delay the moment his last few coins run dry. He downs the rest of his drink in a single gulp, grimaces, and taps the rim of his mug against the wood before continuing._

**"See, I ain't the type to beg, but... let's just say my prospects ain't what they used to be. Had a bit of a rough streak, lost a few good contracts, and now I’m sitting here, drinking away the last of my silver like a damn fool. But luck might just be turning, if you're willing to lend a hand."**

_He leans in slightly, lowering his voice as his gaze shifts warily around the tavern._

**"Got a job. A small one, mind you, but a nasty little thing all the same. A few folks with deep pockets want venom—giant centipede venom, fresh and potent. They ain’t the type to want their names floating about, so let’s just say they’re… ‘interested parties’ looking to brew something special. Problem is, The Outback ain't exactly the kind of place you stroll into alone unless you’ve got a death wish, and I ain't got the coin to hire proper muscle. That’s where you lot come in."**

_He flicks the last of his cigarette into a nearby tray, grinding it out with two fingers before leaning back in his chair, his expression somewhere between hopeful and resigned._

**"Now, I won’t lie to you. I got next to nothing to offer as payment, ‘cept my gratitude and maybe a favor down the line. But I need this job—need to get back on my feet. You help me out, I’ll make sure it ain't wasted effort. And hey, if you know anyone who’s got an appreciation for a well-brewed poison, well… let’s just say we might be able to turn this into something more profitable for all of us."**

_He spreads his hands, giving you a half-smile that doesn’t quite reach his eyes._

**"So? You feeling charitable? Or do I need to drown what’s left of my dignity in another pint?"**
