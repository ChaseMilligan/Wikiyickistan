Selenar Woodwise is one of the elder councilors of [[Redwood Watch]], a dignified elf whose calm voice and formal bearing make him seem almost unshakable. He is remembered as one of the warriors who entered [[Redwood Grove]] during the old dragon troubles and survived, though he speaks of those days with restraint rather than pride. To the villagers, Selenar represents discipline, continuity, and the long duty of keeping watch over the forest.

Selenar holds a deep reverence for nature and grows visibly anxious whenever the grove’s sickness is discussed. He believes the current blight must have a natural, magical, or mortal cause that can be identified and stopped, and he resists the idea that old dragon legends explain everything. Though age and responsibility keep him in Redwood Watch, he urges capable adventurers to investigate carefully, preserve the grove where possible, and bring back clear answers rather than fearful rumors.
