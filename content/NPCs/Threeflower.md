# Threeflower

Threeflower is a timid gnome druid and a field representative of the [[Blossom Union]]. He helps maintain [[Springarden]], guides pilgrims through [[Springwood]], and investigates disturbances along the fragile boundary between the Material Plane and the Plane of Fey.

Despite his nervous manner, Threeflower is observant, compassionate, and deeply familiar with the garden. He is most comfortable among trees, flowers, warm springs, and small fey creatures. In crowded taverns or unfamiliar company, he fidgets, apologizes frequently, and lets his blink squirrel companion, Moo, do much of the social work.

Threeflower is a familiar guide and representative of the Blossom Union, often meeting travelers at [[The Loot Tavern]] before leading them through the forest to Springarden.

## Appearance

Threeflower is a stocky gnome with moss-green hair and a gentle, weathered face. He wears roughspun clothing decorated with feathers, fur, leaves, and small wooden charms collected from creatures that died naturally. He carries a walking staff covered in fresh shoots and is usually accompanied by Moo, a many-eyed blink squirrel.

## Personality

- Nervous around strangers and crowded places.
- Relaxed and confident in nature.
- Kind, grateful, and uncomfortable with confrontation.
- Easily distracted by unusual plants, insects, and fey phenomena.
- More courageous when protecting Springarden or Moo.

## Motivations

Threeflower wants to protect Springarden, preserve the Blossom Union’s relationship with pilgrims, and keep the garden’s fey crossings peaceful. He prefers solving problems without unnecessary destruction and looks for ways to drive dangerous creatures away before resorting to lethal force.

He also wants a quiet, sunlit place to spend his evenings smoking [[Fizzlebud]].

## Fears

Threeflower fears offending people, direct confrontation, forest fires, and failing the Blossom Union. He worries that disturbances in Springarden could damage the garden or cause its magical crossings to become unsafe.

## Moo

Moo is Threeflower’s blink squirrel companion. Unlike an ordinary squirrel, Moo is a fey creature and does not respond to magic that targets beasts. Moo can teleport between nearby branches, disappears whenever conversation becomes boring, and has an intense interest in candy, shiny objects, and unattended spell components.

## Mannerisms and Saying

> “Yawns* Oh, h-h-hello. Am I in your way? No? Sorry. Moo would like to know if you have any candy.”

Threeflower often rubs his hands together when nervous, checks whether Moo is nearby, and apologizes to plants after stepping on them. When he becomes comfortable in nature, his stammer fades and he speaks with quiet confidence.
