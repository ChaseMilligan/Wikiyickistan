*[[Dwarf]] Gunslinger 2*

# Factions
- Former Gunslinger of **[[The Seekers of the Vein]]**
- Respected among local miners and prospectors
- Friendly with the **[[Order of the Emerald Breath]]**

# Allies
- The miners of [[Blacklung Quarry 1]]
- Local merchants and [[Arcanite]] traders
- Honest adventurers willing to lend a hand

# Enemies
- Bandits targeting Arcanite shipments
- Anyone who exploits or endangers miners

# Ideals
- **Community.** "A quarry's only as strong as the folk swingin' the picks."
- **Honesty.** A dwarf's word is worth more than a chest of gold.
- **Stewardship.** Arcanite is a gift to be respected, not abused.

# Bonds
- Blacklung Quarry is his life's work.
- Treats every miner like family.

# Flaws
- Too trusting of good people.
- Carries guilt for closing the quarry too late.
- Uses humor and [[Arcannabis]] to cope with stress.
- Would rather solve problems with a conversation than a gun—even when he shouldn't.

# Personality Traits
- Easygoing and rarely in a hurry.
- Loves terrible mining puns and dad jokes.
- Almost always seen packing or smoking a J.
- Calls nearly everyone "brah," whether they're friend or stranger.

# Short Backstory
Roggan Stonebrah spent decades as a celebrated gunslinger for the **Seekers of the Vein**, protecting valuable Arcanite shipments from bandits, monsters, and rival prospectors. His uncanny aim earned him the nickname **"One Shot Stonebrah,"** though Roggan insists, *"You don't miss fewer shots when you're stoned... you just stop takin' the bad ones."*

After retiring from caravan life, Roggan invested his savings into **Blacklung Quarry**, fulfilling his dream of owning a mine. Under his leadership, the quarry became known not only for its rich Arcanite deposits but also for the camaraderie of its workers, whom Roggan treats like family.
