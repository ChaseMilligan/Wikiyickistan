#Northbourne #Alive 
### The Guild Liaison

**Race**: [[Human]]  
**Role**: Heir to [[House Northbourne]] / Handler’s Guild Overseer  
**Personality**: Charming, ambitious, and business-focused, but with a hidden cruel streak.  
**Public Persona**: The face of the Handler’s Guild, ensuring its [[Magebred Animals]] and specialized handlers remain unmatched.  
**Secret Role**: Silently manages [[The Norfies]]' operations, overseeing their bootlegging network, black-market horse trade, and underground fighting rings.  
**Notable Traits**:
- Brilliant with finances, using shell companies and dummy investments to launder dirty money.
- Prefers subtle power moves over brute force, but isn’t above hiring enforcers when necessary.

Has deep connections with The [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]], ensuring law enforcement looks the other way when needed.
