#Norfie #Alive 
### The Fixer

**Race**: [[Human]]  
**Role**: [[The Norfies]] Deal-Maker / Bribe master  
Personality: Cunning, pragmatic, and always two steps ahead.  
**Public Persona**: Officially a low-ranking Guild scribe handling logistics for [[House Northbourne]]'s [[The North Booftown Downs]].  
**Secret Role**: Manages bribes, race-fixing, and black-market bets, ensuring the house always profits. 
**Notable Traits**:
- Can talk his way out of almost anything, but carries a hidden dagger for when words fail.
- Runs an underground bookmaking operation, controlling bets on arena fights, races, and high-stakes duels.

Secretly launders money through a front business, making it nearly impossible to trace illegal transactions back to the family.
