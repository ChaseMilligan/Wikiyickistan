Founder of [[House Highblade]].

- **Title & Standing:** A self-styled Duke whose “Highblade” lineage is more rumor than reality. He claims his ancestors forged swords so sharp they could slice smoke itself—though his family crest is suspiciously just a bong crossed with a longsword.
- **Personality:** Grandiose and mellow in equal measure. He insists on delivering flowery speeches but forgets his point halfway through. Despite his hazy demeanor, he has flashes of surprising insight, often stumbling upon clever political maneuvers by accident.
- **Appearance:** A portly noble in velvet robes tinged green with suspicious herb-smelling incense. He wears a ceremonial longsword (the “Highblade”) but rarely draws it, preferring to lean on it like a walking stick while puffing from a long pipe.
- **Strengths:** Charismatic among common folk, oddly resilient to poisons and charms (“built up a tolerance, bro”), and able to rally his militia with a mix of humor and haze.
- **Weaknesses:** Easily distracted, prone to indulgence, and underestimates foes who don’t share his laid-back pace. His rulership is more vibe-based than law-based.
- **Quirks:**
    - Keeps court sessions in a literal smoke-filled room.
    - Names his warhorses after strains of herb (e.g., “Lady Indica,” “Sir Sativa”).
    - Calls his soldiers the _Cloudguard_ and insists they march to the sound of lutes and panpipes.
