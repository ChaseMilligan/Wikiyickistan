#Northbourne #Alive 
### The Unshakable Patriarch

**Race**: [[Human]]  
**Role**: Head of [[House Northbourne]] / Master of The Handler’s Guild  
**Personality**: Cold, calculating, and utterly ruthless in protecting the family legacy.  
**Public Persona**: A distinguished noble and businessman, overseeing the city’s finest [[Magebred Animals]] horses and livestock.  
**Secret Role**: The shadowy orchestrator of [[The Norfies]]' criminal empire, ensuring every major smuggling deal, fixed race, and backroom blood sport benefits the family.  
**Notable Traits**:
- Will never get his hands dirty personally, but those who cross him vanish without a trace.
- Fiercely protects the Northbourne name, ensuring its public reputation remains untainted.

Despises [[The Seekers of the Vein]], viewing their jewel and mineral trade as a corrupting outside influence.**
