### The Shadow Courier

**Race**: Native [[Halflings]]  
**Role**: Smuggler for [[The Norfies]] / Driver-for-Hire  
**Personality**: Sarcastic, thrill-seeking, and always looking for the next big score.  
**Public Persona**: Officially a courier running luxury deliveries for [[House Northbourne]].  
**Secret Role**: Smuggles contraband, high-value goods, and fugitives across Stickyickistan, using backroads, underground tunnels, and elemental powered vehicles.  
**Notable Traits**:
- Drives a heavily modified elemental-powered wagon, loaded with hidden compartments and magitech enhancements.
- Has a love-hate relationship with the Northbournes, working for them but always looking for an opportunity to one-up them.

Knows nearly every escape route and bribable checkpoint, making her one of the most valuable smugglers in the city.
