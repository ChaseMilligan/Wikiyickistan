
**Jimmyjock Labingi** was a legendary bard, poet, and early leader of the [[Twothirdlings]] of [[Doobius House|Doobius House]]. He is best remembered as a sharp-tongued champion of his mother’s people and one of the loudest voices against Elyrian settlement of his maternal ancestral lands.

## Background

Jimmyjock was the illegitimate son of [[Duke Doobius Highblade]] and a native concubine whose real name has been almost entirely erased from official records. Surviving Elyrian documents refer to her only as **“Mary Halfling-woman,”** a label that says more about the recordkeepers than it does about her.

Though Jimmyjock was raised with an unusual level of education for the time, Duke Doobius never truly accepted him as a legitimate son, largely because Jimmyjock was not fully human. Doobius later married a human woman, fathered human heirs, and left his estate to them when he died, leaving Jimmyjock with neither inheritance nor recognition.

## Rise to Leadership

Rather than seek acceptance from the family that rejected him, Jimmyjock turned toward his mother’s people. A gifted bard, poet, and speaker, he became known as a man of the people: educated enough to argue with nobles, angry enough to mock them in verse, and beloved enough that common folk repeated his songs long after officials tried to silence them.

When Duke Doobius finally died, the [[Twothirdlings]], who heavily outnumbered the Elyrian population around Doobius House, seized the moment. They claimed the estate and surrounding settlement as their own and elected Jimmyjock as their leader.

## Legacy

To the Twothirdlings, Jimmyjock Labingi is remembered as a folk hero: the disinherited son who chose his mother’s people over his father’s name. To Elyrian loyalists, he was a rebel, demagogue, and thief of noble property. Both versions agree on one thing: Jimmyjock knew exactly how to turn a song into a weapon.

## Traits

- **Role:** Legendary bard, poet, folk leader
- **Known For:** Anti-settler resistance, revolutionary songs, leadership of Doobius House
- **Personality:** Charismatic, defiant, witty, bitter toward noble hypocrisy
- **Ideal:** A people have the right to rule the land that remembers them.
- **Bond:** His erased mother and the Twothirdlings who claimed him as kin.
- **Flaw:** His hatred of Elyrian nobility could make compromise nearly impossible.
