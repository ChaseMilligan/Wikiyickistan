### The Bootleg Alchemist

**Race**: [[Human]]  
**Role**: Illicit Brewer for [[The Norfies]] / Alchemical Genius  
**Personality**: Eccentric, paranoid, but a genius with alchemy and fermentation.  
**Public Persona**: Officially a herbalist and minor alchemist for the Handler’s Guild of [[House Northbourne]].  
**Secret Role**: Brews illegal [[Alcohol]], disguising it as arcane potions to bypass inspections.  
**Notable Traits**:
- Uses alchemical enchantments to create hyper-potent liquors that are in high demand but banned in official markets.
- Has burned down at least two warehouses from unstable brewing experiments.

Constantly on edge, fearing that the [[The Seekers of the Vein]] or [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] will come for him.
