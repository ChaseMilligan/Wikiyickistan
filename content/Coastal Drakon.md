Ships passing the southern coast of the city have been having some issues with a large winged snake. It has been seen in and around a particular tidal river where it may be nesting, and the waters in this area appear to be polluted with a pungent acidic excretion. It doesn't appear to be going out of it's way to destroy or take things, but any ship that passes too close to this potential nesting spot has become the target of the flying serpentine creature. Victims have reported severe chemical burns, and mild paralysis in their encounters. This creature is almost certainly draconic in nature based on reports, but may not be a true dragon or wyvern. A sighting of such an exotic species has caught the attention of the society and they have notified their favorite aberrant doctor, Cessix Rothvell.

This creature is of unknown origins and may offer some prestige among shadowy intellectuals if one manages to document the anatomy of such a creature. As it is also almost certainly of draconic nature, it's components could be used to make a Slumbering Dragon-Touched Focus for spellcasting.
