Though their populations are sparse, many other peoples inhabit the world of [[Mythmos]], including but not exclusive to catfolk, dragonborn, gnomes, [[Warforged]], 
[[Mycoforged]], goblins, orcs, [[Wererats]], and tieflings. No matter their origin, there is no reason any type of character would be out of place arriving in Mythmos. 

*Players should work with the DM to invent how their ancestry choice could have a place in Mythmos.
