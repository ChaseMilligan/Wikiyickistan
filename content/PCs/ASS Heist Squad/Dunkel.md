# Dunkel's Backstory

Dunkel's story began long before he ever set foot in [[The Ashtray]]'s twisting alleys.

His father was a human gang member working for the[[Cough City Crew]] (CCC), steadily making a name for himself as a reliable runner. During one assignment, he was partnered with a female orc [[Arcannabis]] grower. After a series of exhilarating events, the two ended up spending the night together before returning to their separate lives.

Years passed.

His father continued working dangerous jobs for the CCC until one day he noticed a teenage orc boy who had been following him through the streets for quite some time. Confronting the boy, he learned an unexpected truth: the teenager was his son.

The boy's name was Dunkel.

His mother had decided that now that Dunkel had grown enough, it was time for his father to take responsibility and teach him how to survive.

Dunkel inherited far more from his mother's bloodline than his father's. He had dark green skin, large tusks, and a powerful build, though by orc standards his frame was considered somewhat small.

His father took him in and taught him everything he knew about surviving the rough-and-tumble streets of The Ashtray. More importantly, he taught Dunkel how to move through the district unnoticed—how to climb, vault, and free-run across rooftops and alleyways, slipping through the city faster than anyone expected while avoiding unwanted attention.

When Dunkel was seventeen years old, tragedy struck.

His father accepted a particularly risky job for the Cough City Crew. Although he completed the task, he was murdered before he could return home.

With his father gone, Dunkel inherited his place within the organization.

So far, his assignments have consisted mostly of low-risk deliveries and courier work. He relies on the skills his father taught him, navigating The Ashtray with speed and precision, free-running across rooftops while remaining unseen whenever possible.

Despite his work for the CCC, Dunkel still occasionally visits his mother. She has spent years developing an experimental Arcannabis strain intended to enhance the user's adrenaline and pain tolerance.

Dunkel has served as little more than a guinea pig for her research.

The results came slowly. After months of regular consumption, he finally began experiencing brief moments where the strain produced the desired effects.

To administer the liquefied Arcannabis, Dunkel uses a small cylindrical tank fitted with a mouthpiece. Pressing down on the top releases a vapor that he inhales. The device is known as **Stimulate Enduring Rhino Kick**, or **S.E.R.K.**

The effects of S.E.R.K. place considerable strain on his body. Because of this, Dunkel can only safely benefit from its effects a few times each day, reserving it for moments when he truly needs an adrenaline boost.

Recently, while spending an evening in a bar, Dunkel entertained himself by challenging anyone willing to a game of darts, consistently defeating nearly everyone who accepted.

Then he met someone who would change the course of his future.

The stranger defeated Dunkel in both the first and second games of their best-of-five series. Having never needed to before, Dunkel took a draw from his S.E.R.K. device before the third match.

With heightened focus and sharpened reflexes, he mounted an incredible comeback, winning the remaining three games to take the series.

The stranger was less impressed by the victory than by the remarkable athleticism Dunkel displayed after using the mysterious device. Believing those talents could be put to greater use, he asked whether Dunkel would be interested in seeing if he was suited for the organization to which he belonged.

Over the next few days, Dunkel met with the man and underwent a series of trials. Passing each challenge, he earned his place within the ranks of the **[[Ashen Shadow Society]]**.
