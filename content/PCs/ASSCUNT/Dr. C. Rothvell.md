Illithids are creatures of intense curiosity, ambition, and cunning. Often seen as sadistic predators by many other sentient species, Dr. C. Rothvell is no exception to the rule. With a cold and calculating intellect the Doctor has made a quick rise within the Citadel's biological and biochemical research departments. An expert in biological augmentation, the Doctor has created both bio-weapons as well as magically altered creatures for use by the Citadel's bounty hunters. Constantly pursuing his next twisted innovation, ethics and altruism are rarely considered while seeking scientific advancement. 

Before ceremorphosis Dr. Rothvell was a researcher for a small medical institute. A modest lifestyle, he often found himself longing for more. His desire for a greater purpose would eventually be fulfilled when a Mindflayer Nautiloid appeared over his coastal town. It snatched those below it for food or worse, reproduction. Dr. Rothvell was one of the "lucky" few that was chosen for implantation. A slimy tentacle covered face forced his eye open with some otherworldly power, and gently placed an alien tadpole directly into his eye socket. The next few days are fuzzy, with the sensation of an icepick being driven through his head and his memories being forcefully wrenched from his grasp. However, during the process of ceremorphosis something attacked his Nautiloid prison. As Dr. Rothvell was in a state of stupor he was unable to recognize who the attackers were. This boarding and ensuing battle caused the crash of the Nautiloid as it hopped plane to plane, crashing into the swamps of the outback. Miraculously, he managed to survive and crawl free from the wreckage. With his new alien appearance, the Doctor wandered into the swamp and towards a massive mountain in this distance. As his transformation was interrupted in the end stages, he held onto shreds of some of his human qualities and memories. His curiosity and ingenuity remained, but his compassion and empathy were gone. Most of his memories were faint whisps with little meaning remaining, but Dr. E. Rothvell had an iron grip on one; his name. 

Stumbling into [[Stickyickistan]] several years ago, The Doctor quickly set up shop as a back alley "healer". The shop was a rough clinic for those who had nowhere else to go, as the Doctors methods were known to be exceedingly stressful on the body. During the night, it also served as his research laboratory, but anyone who saw inside witnessed a butcher shop. Bloodied surgical instruments, infirm citizens kept half alive with tubes and needles, and body parts strung across dirty tables were all in plain view to see. Despite his brutal methods, Dr. E. Rothvell did have impressive research results. His ability to biologically alter man and animal could rival the top research institutes of Stickyickistan. After a few years of the black market butchering, the [[Ashen Shadow Society]] got wind of his practice and recruited him, promising access to state of the art technology and "higher quality subjects". 

As Wraith has made a recent departure for some extra education, Dr. Rothvell will be joining the party to do some research. His focus will be on biological and biochemical weapons that can be created from the monsters hunted by the party. Besides the research opportunity, the Doctor will also be evaluating the party and department on their allegiance and loyalty to the Citadel. This is due to Wraith's recent failed attack on a higher ranking bounty hunter. 

Character traits:

Motivations:
- The desire and to learn and understand more, craves knowledge above all else. 
- Ambitious to the core.
- Also enjoys his barbaric research process, the more ethical issues the better
- Is not power obsessed like other Illithids, he presumes this is due to the transformation process being incomplete and his mind not ever being influenced by an elder brain.
- His passion to create, useful or not he loves to make something new.
- Curiosity, every interaction is an opportunity to learn. Maybe he will understand people better, one day.
Flaws:
- Lacks human emotion and empathy.
- He does not have an innate hate or disgust for other sentient species, just views them as unimportant. 
- Eats peoples brains. Sometimes lab based meals, sometimes enjoys a hunt in the slums of the city.
Quirks:
- Likes cats, enjoys their characteristics and personalities. Sees them as graceful, cunning apex predators (self reflection?) Will not hunt cats. Fed the alley cats near his lab, don't ask what he fed them.
- Impressive lexicon, will try to use smart words as frequently as possible.
