# Overview

Illithids are creatures of intense curiosity, ambition, and cunning. Often seen as sadistic predators by many other sentient species, Dr. Cessix Rothvell is no exception to the rule. With a cold and calculating intellect, the Doctor has made a quick rise within the [[Ashen Citadel|Citadel's]] biomantic research department. An expert in biological augmentation, the Doctor has created both bio-weapons and magically altered creatures for use by the Citadel's bounty hunters. Constantly pursuing his next twisted innovation, ethics and altruism are rarely considered while seeking scientific advancement.

# History

Before ceremorphosis, Dr. Rothvell was a researcher for a small medical institute. Leading a modest lifestyle, he often found himself longing for more. His desire for a greater purpose would eventually be fulfilled when a Mind Flayer Nautiloid appeared over his coastal town. It snatched those below it for food or, worse, reproduction. Dr. Rothvell was one of the "lucky" few chosen for implantation. A slimy tentacle-covered face forced his eye open with some otherworldly power and gently placed an alien tadpole directly into his eye socket.

The next few days are fuzzy, with the sensation of an ice pick being driven through his head and his memories being forcefully wrenched from his grasp. However, during the process of ceremorphosis, something attacked his nautiloid prison. As Dr. Rothvell was in a state of stupor, he was unable to recognize who the attackers were. This boarding and ensuing battle caused the nautiloid to crash as it hopped from plane to plane, eventually crashing into the swamps of the Outback.

Miraculously, he managed to survive and crawl free from the wreckage. With his new alien appearance, the Doctor wandered into the swamp and toward a massive mountain in the distance. As his transformation was interrupted during its final stages, he held onto shreds of some of his human qualities and memories. His curiosity and ingenuity remained, but his compassion and empathy were gone. Most of his memories were faint wisps with little meaning remaining, but Dr. Cessix Rothvell retained an iron grip on one: his name.

# Stickyickistan

Stumbling into [[Stickyickistan]] several years ago, the Doctor quickly set up shop as a back-alley "healer." The shop was a rough clinic for those who had nowhere else to go, as the Doctor's methods were known to be exceedingly stressful on the body. During the night, it also served as his research laboratory, but anyone who saw inside witnessed a butcher shop: bloodied surgical instruments, infirm citizens kept half alive with tubes and needles, and body parts strung across dirty tables were all in plain view.

Despite his brutal methods, Dr. Rothvell did achieve impressive research results. His ability to biologically alter man and animal could rival the top research institutes of [[Stickyickistan]]. After a few years of black-market butchering, the [[Ashen Shadow Society]] got wind of his practice and recruited him, promising access to state-of-the-art technology and "higher-quality subjects."

# Character Traits

## Motivations

- The desire to learn and understand more; he craves knowledge above all else and is ambitious to the core.
- He enjoys his barbaric research process; the more ethical issues, the better.
- He is not power-obsessed like other illithids. He presumes this is due to the transformation process being incomplete and his mind never having been influenced by an Elder Brain.
- He is passionate about creating. Useful or not, he loves to make something new.
- Curiosity drives him; every interaction is an opportunity to learn. Perhaps he will understand people better one day.

## Flaws

- He lacks human emotion and empathy. He does not possess an innate hatred or disgust for other sentient species; he simply views them as unimportant.
- He eats people's brains. Sometimes these are lab-based meals; sometimes he enjoys a hunt in the slums of the city.

## Quirks

- He likes cats and enjoys their characteristics and personalities. He sees them as graceful, cunning apex predators—a form of self-reflection, perhaps. He will not hunt cats.
- He feeds the alley cats near his lab. Do not ask what he feeds them.
- He has an impressive lexicon and tries to use intelligent words as frequently as possible.
