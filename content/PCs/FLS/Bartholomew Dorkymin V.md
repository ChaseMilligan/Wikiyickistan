# Backstory

Bartholomew Dorkymin the fifth, aka Bart, displayed immense intellect from an early age, earning admission to [[N.E.R.D]]. Institutions at the age of 13. There he studied a vast array of subjects, including casting magic through glyphs, solving complex mathematical equations, studying history, and approaching problems with a critical mindset. While spending a long night before an exam deep in one of the libraries, Bartholomew came across an ornately bound dusty tome. Curiosity compelled him to explore the contents of the book, where he first learned about angels and the transformation they went through when one fell from grace, becoming a devil or daemon. After that night he signed up for an obscure class that explored this newfound interest in depth, exoterosology. The word derives from ancient [[Abyss]]al, meaning 'outer,' referring to how these powerful beings reside beyond the material plane. Now, nineteen years old, Bartholomew has just completed his first year of exoterosology with the understanding that, if he ever wants answers from these planar beings, he will need to get more powerful. With the whole summer ahead, he decided to look for tribulations to solve, as overcoming difficult situations strengthens oneself. When a professor of his told him about a request for help investigating a mine that started having supernatural occurrences, Bartholomew Dorkymin V knew it was time to embark on his first adventure.
