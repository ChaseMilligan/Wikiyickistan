# [[The Loot Tavern]]

The Free League of Stickyickistan makes frequent use of resources from **The Loot Tavern**, especially material from *[[Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting]]* and related Loot Tavern releases. Their work fits the tone of the setting well: strange monsters, colorful NPCs, unusual magic items, crafting systems, monster-hunting procedures, and just enough absurdity to feel right at home in Stickyickistan.

Loot Tavern content is often used as inspiration, reference material, or direct mechanical support for Free League adventures. DMs may pull from Loot Tavern monsters, harvesting rules, crafting options, magic items, subclasses, spells, and encounter ideas when building sessions, as long as they are comfortable running the material and it fits the table.

Because the Free League is intentionally eclectic, Loot Tavern material is treated as part of the campaign’s extended toolbox rather than a separate exception. It is especially useful for monster hunts, strange wilderness jobs, magical biology, alchemical rewards, and adventures where the line between “dangerous creature” and “valuable ingredient” gets blurry.

As always, individual DMs have final say over what appears at their table. If a player wants to use Loot Tavern player-facing options, or if a DM wants to introduce a major Loot Tavern mechanic, creature, item, or reward, they should make sure it fits the adventure, the party, and the expected power level of the Free League.

In short:

**The Loot Tavern is welcome in Stickyickistan. Ask your DM, don’t abuse it, and please stop trying to harvest every NPC.**
