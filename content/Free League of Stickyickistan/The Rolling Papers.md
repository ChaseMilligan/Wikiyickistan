**The Rolling Papers** is Stickyickistan’s most widely circulated newspaper, equal parts investigative journalism, rumor mill, scandal sheet, and rolling tray liner. Its reporters chase stories across taverns, trade roads, noble courts, dungeon entrances, and suspiciously smoking back alleys, publishing whatever they can verify, mostly verify, or at least make entertaining.

The paper is famous for bold headlines, anonymous sources, dramatic woodcut illustrations, and a surprisingly consistent record of being right about things no one wanted printed. Adventurers often find their exploits exaggerated in its pages, while villains, politicians, Guildmasters, and minor gods dread seeing their names above the fold.

Its motto is simple:

> **“If it burns, bleeds, or blazes, it belongs in The Rolling Papers.”**

# What Gets Posted Here

Use **The Rolling Papers** for adventure hooks involving:

- Public contracts.
- Legal bounty work.
- Festival security.
- Caravan guard jobs on official roads.
- Missing persons notices.
- Monster attacks near towns, farms, roads, or trade routes.
- Civic emergencies.
- Noble announcements and public scandals.
- Guild postings with proper paperwork.
- Temple requests, relief efforts, and charity drives.
- Investigations where witnesses are expected to talk openly.
- Jobs where the employer is comfortable being named in print.
- Problems the authorities know about, tolerate, or actively want solved.

If a job requires secrecy, fake names, bribes, contraband, sabotage, theft, or the phrase “do not involve the authorities,” it probably belongs in [[The Roach Clip]] instead.

# Curse of Slaad

**Submitted by:** [[Ezra Astallon]]  
**Tier:** Tier 1  
**Recommended Level:** 3  
**Expected Length:** Approximately 3–4 hours  
**Tone:** Agricultural horror, monster hunt, investigation, horror-comedy  
**Location:** [[Astallon Acres]], [[North Booftown]]  

### Headline

> **ASTALLON ACRES OFFERS REWARD AFTER LIVESTOCK VANISHES AND CROPS BEGIN CROAKING**

### Public Notice

[[Ezra Astallon]] seeks capable adventurers to investigate a series of disappearances at the edge of [[Astallon Acres]]. Livestock has vanished from secured pens, two farmhands have failed to return from the wetlands, and valuable [[Arcannabis]] crops have become twisted, foul-smelling, and strangely mobile.

The source is believed to be an abandoned agricultural greenhouse near [[Zooka Mire]]. Adventurers are asked to identify the cause, rescue anyone still alive, and prevent the infestation from spreading through [[North Booftown]].

Applicants should report to the damaged Astallon Acres farmstead at the end of the north irrigation road. Ask for Ezra Astallon. Do not enter the greenhouse alone.

### What Players Should Know

- **Content / vibe:** Swampy horror-comedy, missing people, corrupted crops, slime, extraplanar creatures, and body horror.
- **Combat / roleplay / exploration mix:** Investigation and exploration leading to encounters with Slaad and a dangerous final confrontation.
- **Character hooks:** Good for monster hunters, local heroes, farmers’ allies, bounty hunters, investigators, or adventurers seeking unusual monster components.
- **Special notes:** The adventure takes place in Stickyickistan and begins in North Booftown. Characters should be prepared for difficult terrain, contaminated farmland, and an infestation beneath an abandoned greenhouse.

### Rewards Teaser

Ezra offers coin, access to Astallon Acres, and additional payment for recovering the planar spore or useful Slaad materials. The farmer also promises to arrange introductions to growers and merchants who can provide future work.

# Example: Monsters at the Northbourne Orchard Festival

**Posted by:** [[Lady Isolde Northbourne]]  
**DM:** Initiate of Tribulation, Vorg Lordcaster  
**Tier:** Tier 1  
**Recommended Level:** 3–4  
**Expected Length:** Approximately 4 hours  
**Tone:** Public festival trouble, monster hunt, light investigation  
**Location:** [[House Northbourne]] holdings  

### Headline

> **Northbourne Harvest Committee Seeks Licensed Adventurers After Prize Pumpkins Bite Back**

### Public Notice

The annual Northbourne Orchard Festival is seeking capable adventurers to provide lawful security, investigate unusual disturbances, and protect guests after several prize vegetables, decorative scarecrows, and at least one cider barrel displayed signs of hostile animation.

Representatives of [[House Northbourne]] insist the festival remains open, safe, and family friendly, provided no one enters the eastern pumpkin rows without an escort.

Interested adventurers should report to [[Lady Isolde Northbourne]] for guard assignments, hazard pay, and a complimentary festival ribbon.

### What Players Should Know

- **Content / vibe:** Public festival chaos, animated produce, rural mystery, light horror-comedy.
- **Combat / roleplay / exploration mix:** Balanced, with social scenes, investigation, and likely combat in the orchard.
- **Character hooks:** Good for monster hunters, local heroes, guards for hire, nobles, food lovers, or anyone seeking legitimate public work.
- **Special notes:** This is an above-the-board contract. Characters are expected to protect civilians, avoid property damage when possible, and not steal from festival vendors.

### Rewards Teaser

The festival committee offers coin, public goodwill with [[House Northbourne]], and possible access to rare seasonal goods.

**Seats:** 4–5 players
