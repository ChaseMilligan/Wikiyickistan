# Setting & Sources

Stickyickistan is my evolving homebrewed D&D world, a melting pot of various settings and sources blended into one. Intentional ambiguity allows the world to function as an open sandbox, giving both my players and me, as the DM, the freedom to explore limitless possibilities without rigid constraints. I am also lazy.

This world draws inspiration from multiple established settings, including the [[Forgotten Realms]], [[Eberron]], [[Ravnica]], [[Dragonlance]], [[Spelljammer]], and [[Drakkenheim]].  I am also heavily using the book [[Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting]] which adds systems for tracking, harvesting, crafting items from, and cooking meals made of monsters. I am trying to weave together elements of medieval fantasy, cyberpunk, and steampunk, creating an often chaotic world where magic, technology, and intrigue collide, sometimes in ways that defy logic, but that's part of the fun.

# Welcome to Stickyickistan

At the heart of the setting lies the sprawling metropolis of [[Stickyickistan]]. Stickyickistan is an island on the planet of [[Mythmos]] that is enshrouded in magic, and has one of the largest economies of all the realms. Their economy is based on the cultivation, and export of a wondrous magical plant called [[Arcannabis]]. The size of the island and how far away it is from other land masses is mysterious at best. Even the most sophisticated magic and technology isn't capable of measuring distance within a certain distance of the island. This area is a Bermuda Triangle like place where pilots, and sailors have reported extreme magical weather events, and terrifying creatures from above and below. The presence of such large amounts of [[Arcanite]] beneath [[Mount Zooka]] has warped the fabric of time, space, and the weave itself around the island which is what causes these phenomenon. This makes it incredibly hard to produce a map of the whole island, as the coastlines are enigmatic at best. It can be seen from the [[Lumytholarispace]] however, so it does appear on maps of Mythos, but appears much smaller than going there would lead you to believe.

# Planet of Mythmos

Mythmos is an oblate sphere of rock and water with an [[Arcanite]] core that orbits a fiery sun called [[Lumytholaris]] (loo-mi-tho-law-ris). It has one moon, and a few other celestial neighbours in [[Lumytholarispace]], but they are all largely uninhabited. Most trips outside the mythic atmosphere of Mythmos are mining expeditions for rare cosmic elements. Trace amounts of Arcanite and [[Delerium]] can be found in the solar system's asteroid belt. Sages from across the realms have put forth differing theories to explain the order of the cosmos throughout history. These included bizarre concepts such as the “great wheel”, the “world axis”, the “conjunction of the spheres”, “planetary orbits”, and “general relativity”. There is no consensus; every theorist is quick to point out the glaring flaws and logical inconsistencies in competing hypotheses.
