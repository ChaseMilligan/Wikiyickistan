Doobius House is a village in [[Stickyickistan]] inhabited by indigenous Halflings, and a small population of mixed folk called Twothirdlings. This village was founded in the earliest years of [[Human Colonialism]] and is named after the head of [[House Highblade]], and a great military leader of old, [[Duke Doobius Highblade]]. A thriving village in its hay day, it boasted the highest population of any inland settlement. In the current day, it is a run-down village with a very similar population to what it had in its prime. The growth of the town stagnated once the frontier, and native populations had been conquered / assimilated and there was no longer a need for a major inland post.

# History

During it's hay day the town had a bustling economy relative to the time. Many adventurers would pass through the village streets, and although it is nothing like the city of today, the village had plenty of options for commerce.

# POIs

### Hotbox Inn & Tavern

Owner: [[Barnabas Huggins]]

Players can rest here, and buy food, drink, and novice level [[Arcannabis]] strains from Barnabas. Barnabas also may have some info about happenings around the town. He can not make Arcannabis strains he just sells it.

**Barnabas Huggins** is a stout, rosy-cheeked man in his mid-50s, with thinning auburn hair combed stubbornly across his head and a laugh that seems to roll out of his belly like a thunderclap. He dresses in plain but well-kept clothes, his apron always dusted with flour or ale stains. Barnabas is a natural storyteller—half of what he says is truth, the other half is so exaggerated that even he forgets where the line is. He has a warm, booming hospitality, but he also has a sharp eye; nothing that happens under his roof escapes his notice. Regulars know him as a man who can get you anything—whether it’s a good smoke, a hot meal, or a whispered rumor—provided you’ve got coin and keep out of trouble.

Voice & Mannerisms
- Loud, booming laugh that fills the room.
- Talks in long-winded stories, often blending truth with exaggeration.
- Always wiping his apron with a rag, though it never seems clean.
- Winks knowingly when sharing a rumor or deal.
Attitude
- Warm, welcoming, and larger-than-life.
- Sharp-eyed despite his jolly demeanor—nothing slips past him.
- Pragmatic: willing to deal in food, drink, smoke, and gossip if coin’s on the table.
- Protective of his inn’s peace—rowdiness is tolerated, but troublemakers get the boot.
Interactions
- Greets newcomers like old friends, drawing them into tales of “what really happened” years ago.
- Sells food, drink, and novice-level Arcannabis strains, but insists he’s “no grower, just a seller.”
- Slips rumors or local news into casual conversation, often for the price of a pint.
- Knows how to balance being everyone’s friend with being the quiet watcher of the room.

### Village Greengrocer

Owner: [[Matilda "Tilly" Cabbagewhistle]]

Tilly is a sprightly halfling woman in her late 60s, though she insists she’s “still young enough to outrun a carrot thief.” She’s got wiry gray hair tied back in a messy bun, sharp green eyes, and hands stained from years of pulling weeds and weighing apples. She’s endlessly chatty—every purchase comes with at least two local rumors and three unsolicited cooking tips. Beneath her cheerful demeanor, she’s fiercely protective of her stall and her produce, wielding her broom with alarming speed when would-be thieves try their luck.

Voice & Mannerisms
- Fast-talking, cheerful, always has a story or rumor to share.
- Sharp green eyes that miss very little.
- Waves her hands when speaking, often holding a vegetable for emphasis.
- Keeps a broom nearby and isn’t shy about brandishing it.
Attitude
- Energetic and sprightly despite her age.
- Fiercely protective of her produce and her stall.
- Warm and neighborly, though a little nosy.
- Prideful about freshness and quality—takes offense at haggling.
Interactions
- Every sale comes with gossip, cooking advice, or both.
- Treats regular customers like family, but expects respect.
- Chases thieves with broom in hand—swift and merciless in defense of her goods.
- Has a soft spot for children, often slipping them fruit for free.

### Hidden "Witch" Hut

NPC: [[Thalion Mossgrove]]

The "Witch's" hut, is actually the home of a native [[Eladrin]] Arcannabis sommelier named Thalion Mossgrove.  Thalion is a laid-back Eladrin with moss-green hair and an earthy calm about him. He speaks with a slow, thoughtful rhythm, often pausing as though he’s tasting each word before letting it out. He sees Arcannabis as a natural gift of the land, blending his Fey heritage with a gardener’s patience. 

He’s warm, approachable, and always ready to share wisdom about “letting the roots find their way,” though he tends to wander into dreamy tangents. Despite the haze, he’s careful with his work and prides himself on cultivating the highest quality. He tends to hide away in his grove because the church in the village has labeled him a heretic and a witch since he is a follower of [[The Old Faiths]], and often uses Arcannabis in his ceremonies / rituals. The church in Stickyickistan is still skeptical about the idea of using Arcannabis, and vehemently detests any spiritual practices other than their own.

Thalion is a master grower, and can craft all strains of Arcannabis. He will also purchase monster components for that purpose. He has a stock of Arcannabis available as well, but he doesn't like accepting money since he sees money as the vessel of the church's subjugation of his people.

Voice & Mannerisms
- Slow, thoughtful speech—pauses often, as though savoring words.
- Runs fingers through his moss-green hair when thinking.
- Smells faintly of earth, herbs, and smoke.
- Uses gardening and root/plant metaphors in casual conversation.
Attitude
- Warm, approachable, and mellow; exudes earthy calm.
- Spiritual and deeply tied to the Old Faiths.
- Distrustful of coin and church authority—prefers barter, trade, or gifts.
- Dreamy at times, wandering into tangents, but sharp when speaking of cultivation.
Interactions
- Generous with advice and Arcannabis, but expects respect in return.
- Sees monster components as sacred ingredients, not just commodities.
- Rarely leaves his grove; wary of outsiders due to persecution by the church.
- Treats cultivation as ceremony—proud of his strains and rituals.

### Village Church

This is the church the common folk of the village come to for mass.

NPC: [[Father Aldric Fenlow]]

Aldric veils his prejudice towards the native population behind a guise of compassion. Claiming to be saving their poor souls, he is really intent on seeking out and silencing all natives who cling to their old ways. He believes the natives are a problem to be dealt with. They stand in the way of the church establishing itself in the new land. He believes that all the natives can be "saved" through forced assimilation and conversion to the Faith of the Sacred Flame if only he can "educate" them as children to "kill the native in the child". Only then could a native be considered "civilized" in his eyes.

Voice & Mannerisms
- Warm, steady voice—always sounds calm, even when saying cruel things.
- Frequently folds his hands as if in prayer while speaking.
- Uses gardening or farming metaphors (_“We must pull the weeds so the crop may thrive.”_).
- Keeps eye contact just a little too long, making people feel both comforted and scrutinized.
Attitude
- Outwardly humble, approachable, and compassionate.
- Inwardly paternalistic and prejudiced—believes the natives are broken people who must be “fixed.”
- Stubbornly convinced he is righteous; views assimilation as salvation.
- Thinks of himself as a shepherd, with the village (and the children) as his flock.
Interactions
- Offers quiet words of comfort, but frames suffering as necessary for faith.
- Publicly gentle and selfless—helping with chores, listening to villagers’ troubles.
- Privately insistent that the church must root out native culture to secure the Sacred Flame’s future.
- Cloaks oppression in “love,” calling forced assimilation “saving their souls.”

### Village School

Seemingly very innocent, a school where the youths of the village are taught the ways of the world, and the Faith of the Sacred Flame. In reality this is a place of pure torture for the children within. Run by the church, the school is the driving force behind the xenocide of Stickyickistan's native population. While it is said that the goal of the school is to enrich the lives of the students through education, it is really an indoctrination factory built to churn out loyal followers of the church. Indigenous children are taken from their families in the outback, and brought to the village to get an "education". These children are made to stay in the basement of the village church in very poor living conditions, and do labor every waking hour they aren't in school.

NPC: [[Sister Calvara Ashveil]]

Voice & Mannerisms
- Speaks in a soft, sing-song voice that sounds gentle, almost maternal.
- Smiles often, though the smile never quite reaches her eyes.
- Keeps her hands clasped tightly in front of her or folded neatly into her sleeves.
- Taps a ruler or rosary rhythmically when displeased.
Attitude
- Outwardly kind, presenting herself as patient and nurturing.
- Inwardly cold, loyal to the church, and unyielding in her belief that suffering breeds obedience.
- Sees herself as “saving souls” through strictness, even when it veers into cruelty.
- Believes the children’s culture and heritage must be “burned away” in the Sacred Flame.
Interactions
- Offers words of comfort laced with veiled threats (_“Pain is just the Sacred Flame’s way of teaching discipline.”_).
- Publicly praises obedience and humility, punishes even the smallest defiance harshly.
- Treats indigenous children as “projects” to be fixed, not people.
- Maintains the illusion of innocence to outsiders, portraying the school as wholesome.

### Apiary

Owner: [[Ambrose “Buzz” Calloway]]

Voice & Mannerisms
- Speak slowly, with long pauses like he’s half daydreaming.
- Use “sweet” or “sticky” metaphors (e.g. _“Troubles swarm like angry bees, best stay calm or you’ll get stung.”_).
- Hum softly or make a buzzing noise absentmindedly when thinking.
- Wipe sticky fingers on his trousers without noticing.
 Attitude
- Always friendly, laid-back, and eager to share honey.
- Rarely upset—he treats danger or stress like just another storm the hive will endure.
- Protective streak: if someone threatens the bees, he stiffens up and suddenly gets sharp.
Interactions
- Offers jars of honey as gifts or trade, sometimes forgetting he already gave one out.
- Gives advice that’s folksy and slightly nonsensical but comforting.
- Treats everyone like part of his “hive,” calling people “worker bee” or “queen.”

### Blacksmith

Owner: [[Bromm Stonemug]]

Voice & Mannerisms
- Deep, steady voice; deliberate when he speaks.
- Constantly has a faint ring of anvil echoes in his rhythm of speech.
- Runs a hand through his beard or across his jaw like he’s polishing steel.
- Grumbles under his breath while working, half-song, half-curse.
Attitude
- Stoic, dependable, rarely flustered.
- Believes every piece of metal has a “right shape” waiting to be revealed.
- Values hard work and honesty above all else.
- Reserved with words, but when he gives praise, it means a lot.
Interactions
- Examines weapons and armor with a craftsman’s eye, tapping the metal to “listen” to it.
- Offers practical advice, rarely flowery—straight to the point.
- If he likes someone, he’ll quietly reinforce their gear without charging.
