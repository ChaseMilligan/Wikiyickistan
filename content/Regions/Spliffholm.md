Spliffholm was a city in Stickyickistan that existed before the arcane civil war, and was destroyed in the [[Emerald Blight]] becoming the [[Ruins of Spliffholm]]. Spliffholm was founded by [[Jimmyjock Labingi]], the [[Twothirdlings]] bastard son of [[Duke Doobius Highblade]]. Despite who his father was, Jimmyjock staunchly opposed the incursion of settlers upon his maternal ancestral lands. He was a talented poet, and man of the people, so when the [[Twothirdlings]] made their own settlement, they elected him the leader. Spliffholm grew into a bastion of hope for the indigenous population, and stood firmly in the way of complete [[Elyria]]n domination. Spliffholm eventually became a major economic competitor with [[Nevercough City]] since they valued both settler and indigenous knowledge. This lead to rising political tensions between the two burgeoning governments and there have been many conspiracy theories regarding the real motives behind the Blight. Some speculate that it was a covert operation conducted by the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] to frame [[Valmorra the Hollowed]] for the act of terrorism among other theories.
