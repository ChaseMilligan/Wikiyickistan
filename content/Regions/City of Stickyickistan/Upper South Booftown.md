# Upper South Booftown

## [[The Ashtray]]

**Upper South Booftown**, better known as **The Ashtray**, is where the streets are rough, the air is thick with soot, and the law only exists if someone nearby is willing to enforce it. Wedged between the slow-churning lava flows of [[Mount Zooka]] and the more “civilized” parts of the city, The Ashtray is a district of smoke-blackened rooftops, crooked tenements, ash-slick alleys, and hustlers who can smell weakness through a closed door.

The native [[Halflings|halfling]] population makes up a significant portion of the district’s residents, though The Ashtray is also home to outcasts, fugitives, dock toughs, failed apprentices, smugglers, off-duty mercenaries, and anyone else too useful, desperate, or dangerous to fit anywhere cleaner. It is not a safe neighborhood, but it is an honest one in its own crooked way: everyone knows what everything costs, even if the price is not always paid in coin.

## Character

The Ashtray feels like a city stacked on top of itself. Buildings climb over one another in a tangled mess of shoddy construction, patched bridges, rooftop walkways, hanging laundry lines, and half-legal expansions. The ground level belongs to carts, gangs, gamblers, and stray dogs. The upper levels belong to lookouts, rooftop gardens, hidden grow rooms, and people who do not want to be found.

The air is always warm and dirty. Ash from [[Mount Zooka]] settles on windowsills, food stalls, shrine steps, and sleeping children. Locals joke that if you leave a white shirt outside overnight, it wakes up as part of the neighborhood.

## Power on the Streets

The [[Mandem on Booftown]] (MOB) and the [[Cough City Crew]] (CCC) fight an endless turf war over illicit [[Arcannabis]] strains, underground fight rings, protection rackets, backroom gambling, bootlegged [[Alcohol]], and black-market [[Magitech]]. Their conflict is loud enough to terrify outsiders but familiar enough that residents know when to duck, when to run, and when to pretend they saw nothing.

Neither gang fully controls The Ashtray. Power shifts block by block, rooftop by rooftop, and business by business. A tea shop might pay MOB on Mondays, CCC on Fridays, and a local auntie every day because everyone knows she is the one actually keeping the street alive.

## Notable Features

- **The Sootsteps:** A maze of exterior stairs, ladders, rope bridges, and rooftop shortcuts used by locals to avoid gang checkpoints.
- **Cinder Courts:** Open lots where dice games, fistfights, public arguments, and informal trials are held.
- **Backroom Taverns:** Hidden drinking halls serving bootlegged [[Alcohol]], bad decisions, and worse information.
- **Ash Gardens:** Rooftop planters where locals grow hardy food, medicinal herbs, and discreet [[Arcannabis]] strains under layers of volcanic grit.
- **[[The Black Stalls]]:** A shifting night market for stolen goods, rare poisons, counterfeit papers, and illegal [[Magitech]] parts.
- **Smoke Chapels:** Tiny street shrines where residents pray for protection, revenge, luck, or a clean death.

## Reputation

Despite its lawless reputation, Upper South Booftown is not just a den of crime. It is a proving ground. Fortunes are made and lost in a single dice roll. Gangsters rule like kings until someone hungrier takes their corner. Bounty hunters stalk their marks through smog-choked streets, and even the smallest [[Halflings]] can rise to power if they are clever, ruthless, and loved by the right people.

To outsiders, The Ashtray is dangerous. To locals, it is home.

## Adventure Hooks

- A gang truce collapses after someone steals a rare Arcannabis strain from a rooftop grow.
- A bounty target is hiding in the Sootsteps, protected by locals who hate the person hunting them.
- A batch of black-market Magitech starts malfunctioning and setting buildings on fire.
- A respected halfling elder asks the party to remove a gang lieutenant without starting a district war.
- A child finds something valuable, cursed, or alive in a fresh fall of ash from [[Mount Zooka]].
- The MOB and CCC both want the same monster part, and neither side knows why the other wants it.
