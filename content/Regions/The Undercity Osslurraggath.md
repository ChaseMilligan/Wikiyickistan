An underground metropolis that is a part of the city of [[Stickyickistan]], but it is the underbelly of the city where [[The Mortuary]] is located, and where the [[Dungsweepers]] toil. Home to many undesirables, the elites of the city above mostly ignore the happenings of this under city. the under city has it's own provisional government, but holds no authority in the eyes of the Nevercough Tribunal.

The Nevercough Tribunal refuses to acknowledge Osslurraggath, but every noble knows their waste doesn’t vanish by magic — it _goes somewhere._

- When Stickyickistan grew into a great metropolis, the [[Nevercough Tribunal]] needed a place for the poor, the diseased, and the dishonored dead. They built vast **bone vaults** beneath the city to serve as its necropolis.
- These vaults eventually collapsed into deeper Underdark channels. What should have been a tomb became a **threshold**, spilling with refuse, plague-bodies, and exiles.
- Into this void stepped the **[[Heralds of Dust]]** — led by **[[Factol Skall]]**. They declared that all life in Stickyickistan was already death, and that Osslurraggath would be the sanctum where this truth was embraced.
The name Osslurraggath comes from the common word "Ossuary", and the Drow word "lurraggath" meaning love.
