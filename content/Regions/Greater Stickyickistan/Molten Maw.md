
**Molten Maw** is a colossal underground volcanic city in the [[Underdark]], inhabited mainly by [[Duergar]] and [[Svirfneblin]]. Built around a vast open caldera beneath the earth, the city serves as both a natural forge and a deadly hazard. Its streets are carved into black basalt terraces, iron gantries, cooled lava shelves, and tunnels reinforced against constant tremors.

Rivers of semi-intelligent, magically charged lava course through the city’s depths. Locals call these flows **the Veins**, and treat them less like ordinary magma and more like dangerous spirits of the earth. The lava sometimes changes course without warning, answers certain forms of elemental magic, or rises into half-formed elemental creatures that guard the flows from thieves, miners, and careless outsiders.

## Character

Molten Maw is brutal, smoky, rich, and paranoid. Every surface is warm. Every shadow glows faintly red. The air tastes of metal, ash, and old bargains. Duergar foundries hammer day and night, while Svirfneblin engineers maintain hidden vents, cooling chambers, pressure gates, and secret escape tunnels.

The city is famous for:

- Infernal heat and volcanic hazards
- Masterwork metal, obsidian, glass, and gemstone craft
- Hidden smuggler dens
- Elemental lava cults
- Political exiles and escaped criminals
- The underground black market known as [[The Silt Road]]

## The Silt Road

Criminals, rebels, fugitives, and desperate surface-folk sometimes flee to Molten Maw, surviving in fire-resistant cave networks and hidden market warrens. Over time, these routes became the heart of [[The Silt Road]], a literal underground criminal market that moves contraband, people, secrets, cursed goods, monster parts, and forbidden magic beneath the notice of surface authorities.

The Silt Road does not truly belong to any one faction. Duergar clans tax it, Svirfneblin guides navigate it, smugglers profit from it, and local gangs bleed for control of it.

## Notable Features

- **The Veins:** Semi-intelligent lava rivers that move through the city like living arteries.
- **The Chain Terraces:** Stacked residential districts connected by chains, lifts, bridges, and mine platforms.
- **The Cinder Warrens:** Smuggler caves, fugitive dens, and black-market stalls hidden behind cooling vents.
- **The Glass Forges:** Workshops where volcanic glass, enchanted slag, and fire-tempered gems are shaped.
- **The Ash Courts:** Harsh civic chambers where Duergar clan leaders, Svirfneblin engineers, and criminal brokers negotiate.
- **The Mouth Below:** A deeper volcanic pit believed to be either a planar wound, sleeping primordial, or the source of the Veins.

## Dangers

- Sudden lava surges
- Toxic gas pockets
- Collapsing tunnels
- Elemental guardians
- Duergar press gangs
- Svirfneblin sabotage traps
- Criminal ambushes
- Heat exhaustion
- Deals that are harder to escape than the city itself

## Adventure Hooks

- A fugitive the party needs has vanished into the Cinder Warrens.
- A shipment moving along [[The Silt Road]] contains something far worse than contraband.
- One of the Veins has begun spelling warnings in molten stone.
- A Duergar clan hires the party to recover a stolen forge-secret recipe.
- A Svirfneblin engineer believes the Mouth Below is waking up.
- Surface rebels want weapons from Molten Maw, but buying them may empower the wrong faction.
