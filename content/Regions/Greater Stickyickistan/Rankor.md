Rankor is the main respite on this side of the [[Broadwater Span]], a tiny mid-sea settlement perched atop a ring of tall volcanic sea stacks rising from the open [[Voidsea]]. From a distance, it looks less like a town than a collection of lanterns, rope bridges, and weather-beaten platforms clinging stubbornly to black stone above the waves.

The settlement is carved into and around the stacks themselves. Docks line the sheltered interior of the ring, while ladders, pulley lines, and hand-cranked winches connect boats below to the wooden platforms above. Dark openings pockmark each stack, leading into living quarters, storerooms, shops, workshops, and cramped common rooms. Rankor is small, salty, loud with gulls and winches, and deeply welcome to travelers who have spent too long crossing open water.

> [!Read Aloud]
> Moss-covered sea stacks rise out of the haze, slender stone pillars cut with circular openings that watch the water like quiet eyes. Ropes trail between them in loose arcs, their knots thudding softly as the wind shifts. Above the bay, platforms jut from the cliffs, busy with figures hauling crates on swaying nets. Lanterns flicker along the upper paths, their glow catching damp rock and the spray drifting from below. Fresh boards brace broken sections, and frayed ropes hang like shed skin above a small protected bay holding a long, charred dock.
