Redwood Watch began as a lonely warden outpost on the edge of [[Redwood Grove]], established after the surviving elves withdrew from the grove in the wake of the old dragon troubles. For generations, its caretakers watched the redwoods for signs of sickness, tended boundary stones, and warned travelers away from paths where the roots were said to remember too much. What began as a duty post slowly became a village, as wardens raised families, hunters built homes, and artisans found beauty in the quiet redwood shade.

Today, Redwood Watch is a peaceful village of about a hundred people who work primarily as hunters, trappers, arborist, and artisans. Most residents are elves descended from the grove’s six original families, but the village’s quiet ways and idyllic surroundings have also attracted humans, orcs, and a few goliaths and dragonborn. The settlement consists of freestanding huts made from wood and leaves, large tree hollows that serve as homes and shops, and a handful of tree houses built by hardy villagers among the branches overlooking the forest floor. Owing to this multicultural heritage, all villagers know Common, and the residents are close-knit enough that most consider themselves part of one extended family.
