Doobius House is a village in [[Stickyickistan]] inhabited by indigenous Halflings, and a small population of mixed folk called Twothirdlings. This village was founded in the earliest years of [[Human Colonialism]] and is named after the head of [[House Highblade]], and a great military leader of old, [[Duke Doobius Highblade]]. A thriving village in its hay day, it boasted the highest population of any inland settlement. In the current day, it is a run-down village with a very similar population to what it had in its prime. The growth of the town stagnated once the frontier, and native populations had been conquered / assimilated and there was no longer a need for a major inland post.

# History

During it's hay day the town had a bustling economy relative to the time. Many adventurers would pass through the village streets, and although it is nothing like the city of today, the village had plenty of options for commerce.

## POIs

### Hotbox Inn & Tavern

Owner: [[Barnabas Huggins]]

**Barnabas Huggins** is a stout, rosy-cheeked man in his mid-50s, with thinning auburn hair combed stubbornly across his head and a laugh that seems to roll out of his belly like a thunderclap. He dresses in plain but well-kept clothes, his apron always dusted with flour or ale stains. Barnabas is a natural storyteller—half of what he says is truth, the other half is so exaggerated that even he forgets where the line is. He has a warm, booming hospitality, but he also has a sharp eye; nothing that happens under his roof escapes his notice. Regulars know him as a man who can get you anything—whether it’s a good smoke, a hot meal, or a whispered rumor—provided you’ve got coin and keep out of trouble.

Players can rest here, and buy food, drink, and novice level [[Arcannabis]] strains from Barnabas. Barnabas also may have some info about happenings around the town. He can not make Arcannabis strains he just sells it.

### Burial Barrow

This is an ancient burial place that the pioneers of the village have appropriated as their own.

### Celestial Chapel

This is a special church built by the clergy specifically for training new missionaries, and "converting" natives. Not where the common folk go to pray.

### [[Grim Forest]] Edge

The origins of the grim forest are not known. The only knowledge of this place is held by indigenous elders that haven't lost their oral history traditions. Evil plants, and some undead roam this forest. The edge of the woods is guarded by an ancient awakened radiant tree.

### Village Greengrocer

Owner: [[Matilda "Tilly" Cabbagewhistle]]

**Personality:**  
Tilly is a sprightly halfling woman in her late 60s, though she insists she’s “still young enough to outrun a carrot thief.” She’s got wiry gray hair tied back in a messy bun, sharp green eyes, and hands stained from years of pulling weeds and weighing apples. She’s endlessly chatty—every purchase comes with at least two local rumors and three unsolicited cooking tips. Beneath her cheerful demeanor, she’s fiercely protective of her stall and her produce, wielding her broom with alarming speed when would-be thieves try their luck.

**Intro Hook (when the party approaches her stall):**  
The scent of fresh herbs and ripe fruit hits you as you near a cluttered wooden cart overflowing with cabbages, turnips, and bunches of parsley. Behind it, a small halfling woman pops up on her toes to wave at you, her smile crinkling her whole face.  
“Morning, loves! Matilda Cabbagewhistle at your service—though everyone calls me Tilly. Carrots are crisp, apples are sweeter than a bard’s lie, and I’ll throw in a sprig of rosemary if you promise to tell me where you’re headed. Now then—what’ll it be?”

### Hidden "Witch" Hut

The "Witch's" hut, is actually the home of a native [[Eladrin]] Arcannabis sommelier named [[Thalion Mossgrove]].  

**Personality & Intro:**  
Thalion is a laid-back Eladrin with moss-green hair and an earthy calm about him. He speaks with a slow, thoughtful rhythm, often pausing as though he’s tasting each word before letting it out. He sees Arcannabis as a natural gift of the land, blending his Fey heritage with a gardener’s patience. He’s warm, approachable, and always ready to share wisdom about “letting the roots find their way,” though he tends to wander into dreamy tangents. Despite the haze, he’s careful with his work and prides himself on cultivating the highest quality. He tends to hide away in his grove because the church in the village has labeled him a heretic and a witch since he is a follower of [[The Old Faiths]], and often uses Arcannabis in his ceremonies / rituals. The church in Stickyickistan is still skeptical about the idea of using Arcannabis, and vehemently detests any spiritual practices other than their own.

Thalion is a master grower, and can craft all strains of Arcannabis. He will also purchase monster components for that purpose. He has a stock of Arcannabis available as well, but he doesn't like accepting money since he sees money as the vessel of the church's subjugation of his people.

### Stables

Owner: [[Corvin Dappledraft]]

**Personality:** Corvin is a gruff but steady middle-aged man with a deep love for horses and a sharp dislike for laziness. He’s practical, punctual, and speaks plainly, though he softens when talking to his animals, often treating them with more warmth than he does people. Villagers trust him for his reliability, even if his humor is dry as old hay.

### Village Church

This is the church the common folk of the village come to for mass.

Father: Aldric Fenlow

**Personality:** Aldric is a kindly, silver-haired human in his late 50s with a gentle voice and patient smile. He’s humble and approachable, more at home tending gardens or mending fences than giving fiery sermons. He believes faith should be shown in deeds, not just words, and he often helps villagers with small tasks or offers quiet comfort. Though compassionate, he can be stubborn when it comes to matters of morality, and he has a knack for asking simple questions that make people reflect on themselves. 

Aldric veils his prejudice towards the native population behind a guise of compassion. Claiming to be saving their poor souls, he is really intent on seeking out and silencing all natives who cling to their old ways. He believes the natives are a problem to be dealt with. They stand in the way of the church establishing itself in the new land. He believes that all the natives can be "saved" through forced assimilation and conversion to the Faith of the Sacred Flame if only he can "educate" them as children to "kill the native in the child". Only then could a native be considered "civilized" in his eyes.

### Village School

Seemingly very innocent, a school where the youths of the village are taught the ways of the world. In reality this is a place of pure torture for the children within. Run by the church, the school is the driving force behind the xenocide of Stickyickistan. While it is said that the goal of the school is to enrich the lives of the students through education, it is really an indoctrination factory built to churn out loyal followers of the church. Indigenous children are taken from their families in the outback, and brought to the village to get an "education". These children are made to stay in the basement of the village church in very poor living conditions, and do labor every waking hour they aren't in school.
