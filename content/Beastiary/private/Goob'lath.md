Goob’lath is an adult black dragon that has made a pact with the Demon Prince of Slime, [[Juiblex]]. Juiblex resides in Shedaklah, the 222nd layer of the [[Abyss]]. Goob’lath believes that one day, Juiblex will consume all, but he will be spared if he can establish his own domain of filth, and army of shapeless creatures. He is also under oath to wage war on anyone, or anything that opposes Juiblex’s goal. Goob’lath has also amassed a cult following of black winged kobolds that revere him as a god, and do his bidding. They often cover themselves in the tar that Goob’lath is spreading in the [[Seekers' Scrapyard]] in an attempt to mimic his scales. The Tar Elementals not only use this tar to travel as they are adept swimmers, but it is also needed for the summoning rituals that conjure the Tar elementals in the material plane from in between the planes of earth and water.

Juiblex does not have an avatar, as he sees it as a burden. Instead, when he wants to materialize, he just forcibly takes over an ooze, elemental, or otherwise slimy creature. This is why followers of Juiblex raise armies of shapeless creatures, and keep them close by, so Juiblex can contact them easily. Juiblex’s main goal is forceful possession of all things. The pact made between Goob’lath, and Juiblex serves both of them. Juiblex now has a loyal, intelligent, and powerful follower on the material plane that will spread his filth, and Goob’lath was transformed by the abyss into an even more vile being. Goob’lath is no longer an ordinary black dragon after his transformation. He has been granted a taste of shapelessness, which he views as a supreme state of being, an altered breath sac, and a thick sticky primordial soup of bubbling bile and nauseating fluid bound by a thin epidermis bathed in tar and thick mucus that covers his body.

# [[Wes Scantlin]]

Wes was found by Goob’lath’s followers, and brought back to him. Goob’lath took his corpse to the 222nd layer of the abyss, as he could tell Wes was a powerful caster in life. Juiblex resurrected Wes under the condition that he be taken on as a patron, so Wes made a pact with Juiblex similar to Goob’lath. Juiblex wants to transform Wes' form into the perfect avatar, so the other demon lords will have more respect for him.

# History

Veyrris was not born Goob’lath. Somewhere in the [[Shadow Fens]], He came into the world approximately four centuries ago, as the runt of a black dragon clutch, weak and despised by his siblings and ignored by his dam. While the others grew into raiders who reveled in the agony of their acid breath, Veyrris learned only fear and submission. His every attempt to claim something of his own was punished with humiliation. When at last he gathered a few desperate mortals to serve him in a lonely fen, his broodmates descended, drowned his followers in acid, and left him broken in the muck. That betrayal convinced him that family and kinship were lies, that only strength mattered, and that form itself was too fragile to trust.

For years he wandered [[Mythmos]], too weak to claim a swamp or hold a ruin, always chased away by stronger rivals. His exile ended when he came to  [[The Seekers’ Scrapyard]], a poisoned wasteland outside Stickyickistan. The scrapyard was a mirror of his own life: abandoned, corrupted, yet stubbornly enduring. Here he saw his chance to finally seize a throne of his own.

But the scrapyard’s heart was already claimed. Beneath the twisted heaps of rust lay the warped remains of [[Terplorr the Blazenborn]]’s caldera, preserved by draconic magic even after the tyrant’s death. A biomancer named [[Dr. Francis Norman Stein]] had made it his laboratory, filling it with experiments in forced evolution and planar breaches. To Veyrris, this was an unforgivable trespass: a mortal defiling what should have been the inheritance of dragons.

The wyrm struck without warning. His acid breath corroded constructs and mercenaries alike, and he tore Stein’s adventuring companions limb from limb. Stein himself barely escaped, fleeing through a collapsing planar rift and leaving behind his research and equipment. To the kobolds who still lingered in Terplorr’s shadow, the message was clear: a new dragon had taken the throne, one who had driven out even magitech and sorcery. They bowed to Veyrris as their rightful lord.

It was in the wreckage of Stein’s work that everything changed. Veyrris uncovered the mutagen [[Mut-x]] and the pulsing wound of the [[Abyss]]. Though a dragon, he was no brute — he studied, experimented, and inhaled the foul breath of the breach. From the darkness, a voice spoke. [[Juiblex]], the Faceless Lord, promised that all things would one day dissolve into slime, but that those who served him would be spared. Veyrris listened, and for the first time in his life, he felt chosen rather than discarded.

He embraced the promise, surrendering his pride, his name, and even his form. From that moment, he was no longer Veyrris. He was Goob’lath, the Tar-Scourge, dragon made shapeless. His scales fused with tar, his lungs boiled with bile, and slime seeped from every crevice of his flesh. The kobolds who had once revered him as lord fell to their knees as worshippers, smearing themselves with tar and filth to imitate their god.

The scrapyard rots under his wings now. Mutated tar elementals claw their way from the planes, magitech rusts into sludge, and the kobolds chant his name in cracked voices. Goob’lath believes bonds betray, mortals profane, even flesh itself fails. Only shapelessness is eternal. To him, destruction is not malice but mercy. Dissolving the world so it cannot betray again.
