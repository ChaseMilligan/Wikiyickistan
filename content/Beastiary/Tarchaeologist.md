## Description

Excavating ancient remains is no easy task; brushing off all of that fine dirt with the softest pegasus-tail paintbrushes can lead to some serious carpal tunnel syndrome. Co-opting magitech technology—which has been so crudely used for firearms—into archaeological apparatus has saved many a tarchaeologist from debilitating wrist pain. Unfortunately, having such devices on one’s person leaves said person open to possession by creatures who thrive on these gadgets’ arcane power sources. For this reason, the ASSES (Archaeological Society’s Systematic Excavative Study) requires all members to undergo arcane de-possession training; destroying the arcane power source deprives the controlling creature of its powers and has reduced archaeologist mortality by over thirteen percent!
