# Player Overview

**Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting** is a third-party D&D supplement from **[[The Loot Tavern]]** that the Free League of Stickyickistan uses heavily for monster hunts, crafting, harvesting, unusual magic items, subclasses, spells, and weird creature-driven adventures.

The book is built around the idea that monsters are not just obstacles. They are mysteries to investigate, dangerous encounters to survive, and valuable sources of materials if you know how to harvest them. A typical Heliana-style adventure might involve tracking a creature, learning its habits, preparing for its abilities, fighting it, then using parts of the monster to craft potions, gear, magic items, or other rewards.

For players, this means:

- Monster hunts may involve investigation, preparation, and research before combat.
- Creatures may have special traits, phases, weaknesses, or unusual rewards.
- Harvesting monster parts can matter.
- Crafting and alchemy may be more important than in a standard D&D game.
- Some species options, spells, items, and subclasses may come from Loot Tavern material.
- ==Always ask your DM== before assuming a Heliana option is available for your character.

In the Free League, Heliana’s content is treated as part of the campaign’s extended toolbox. It fits Stickyickistan well because it supports strange monsters, magical biology, dangerous ingredients, and rewards that feel more interesting than just “you find gold.”

# An Extraplanar Bestseller

In-universe, **Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting** also exists as a wildly popular adventuring manual, field guide, and coffee-table hazard across the planes. Copies have been found in Oeridian monster hunters’ lodges, Faerûnian wizard towers, Ansaloni taverns, noble Thranish libraries, Sigilite marketplaces, and the backpacks of people who absolutely should have read more than the first chapter before accepting a job.

The book is advertised as an “extraplanar bestseller,” though no one is entirely sure which planes are included in the sales numbers. Different editions are known to contain contradictory marginal notes, suspicious stains, regional monster warnings, and occasional advice that seems to update itself after a reader survives something awful.

Among adventurers, owning a copy is considered both practical and ominous. It usually means you are either preparing to hunt a monster, pretending to know how to hunt a monster, or about to become a cautionary sidebar in the next printing.

#### ***Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting***
Wondrous Item, varies

Full of useful knowledge and heavy enough to crush a skull, _Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting_ is nothing if not handy. The personalised quotes from [[Heliana]], [[Peeper]]’s deft proofing and editing, and the numerous ink stains and spilled watercolours give this hefty tome a homemade feel.

**_Fantastic Phalanges and Where to Find Them._** As an action, you can open this book and stare intently at its pages, which turn of their own accord to settle on an anatomical diagram of the corpse nearest to the book. You immediately learn all the harvestable parts of the corpse, as well as their Component DCs.

**_Know Thy Enemy._** As an action, you can place an object that belonged to a creature—such as its scat, scales, or clothing— between the pages of this book. One minute later, the book tells you a piece of information about the creature (number determined by rarity). At the GM’s discretion, this may be: its saving throw proficiencies, damage resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities; condition immunities; creature’s type; one of its traits; or one of the clues acquired during a hunt. Placing more objects that belonged to that creature (or a creature with the same statblock) yields no further results.

**_Weapon of the Learned._** This book can be used as a simple melee weapon that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage on a hit. If you are proficient in [Arcana](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), [History](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), [Nature](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), or [Religion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), you are proficient with this weapon.

_**Anatomiser (Very-rare).**_ If you read this book for 1 hour, you gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice for the next 24 hours: [Arcana](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), [Investigation](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), [Nature](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), [Medicine](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), [Religion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills), or [Survival](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills). Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again for 24 hours.
