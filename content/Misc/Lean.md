Lean is a special [[Alcohol]]ic concoction created by the [[Shadow Wizard Money Gang]]. Lean is a mixed drink consisting mostly of soda, but the money spell shadow alchemists in the gang have created an incredibly high proof, thick, sweet smelling, alcoholic syrup they pour into the soda. This mix has become a trend with some sub-cultures of youths in [[Stickyickistan]] since the gang affiliated music group [[BASED NEGATIVE SQUAD]] popularized its consumption along with their fashion sense.
