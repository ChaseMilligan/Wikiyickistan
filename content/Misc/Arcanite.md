Arcanite is the solid material element form of weave magic. Arcanite is incredibly rare in the multiverse, but not so much on [[Mythmos]], and especially [[Stickyickistan]]. High traces of this raw magical element can be found throughout the soil on the island, and [[Mount Zooka]] is a specific kind of volcano known as an Arcanite Cone. This kind of volcano can occur naturally when certain conditions are met, and the island now known as Stickyickistan is right in the goldilocks zone for this to happen. An Arcanite Cone often belches up Arcanite from the depths of the planet into the crater at the peak of the mountain. [[Dwarf]] miners are often employed to venture into the rim of the crater, and extract large amounts of Arcanite which is then used to power the city's [[Magitech]] grid, enchant items, and exported across the realms. Arcanite is the driving force behind the high-magic of Stickyickistan. Some [[Halflings]] with ancestry native to the island have been slightly altered by the Arcanite rich environment giving them some innate arcane skills. [[The Seekers of the Vein]] have a monopoly on the Arcanite mining industry in Stickyickistan.

### Arcanite Properties

All Arcanite samples have the following traits regardless of size:

- [Immune](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Immunity) to Necrotic, Poison, and Psychic damage; as well as Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from non-magical weapons.
- [Resistant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Resistance) to Acid, Cold, Fire, and Lightning damage; as well as Slashing and Piercing damage from magical weapons.
- [Vulnerable](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Vulnerability) to Bludgeoning damage from magical weapons.

### Uses for Arcanite

Arcanite has vast arcane potential. Beyond trade, crystals are used for several purposes:

**_Spell Component._** Arcanite may be used as an arcane focus or material component for any spell on the sorcerer, warlock, or wizard spell list. When casting a spell which requires a costly material component, a spellcaster may instead use Arcanite of equivalent or greater value to the required cost.

**_Magic Items._** For crafting purposes, Arcanite shards are the same as magic essence. Arcanite is an exceptional material for creating magical items of all kinds. Any existing magic item described in the [Basic Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items-a-z) could be made with Arcanite. Fragments, shards, crystals, and geodes may be used to make uncommon, rare, very rare, and legendary items respectively.

**_Stable [[Delerium]]._** The process for crafting magic items with delerium renders the crystals stable, effectively turning it into Arcanite.

**_Arcanite Dust._** Arcanite dust is usually made by grinding the crystals against one another to create a fine powder. Arcanite dust can be used in alchemy, as a spell component, a reagent for brewing potions, or mixed into inks used to scribe spell scrolls.

**_Improvised Weapons & Ammunition._** Arcanite fragments may be used as ammunition for a sling or fashioned into a makeshift club. Such weapons and ammunition deal an extra 1d8 Radiant damage, and contaminated creatures struck must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or gain the condition Jinxed (1d4). Arcanite used as ammunition in this way is destroyed.

## Delicate Balance of the Elements

Arcanite and [[Delerium]] are like yin and yang. Arcanite represents order, and light while Delerium represents darkness, and chaos. Arcanite's magical properties are perfectly stable making it very reliable at powering most magitech, but slightly less powerful and much more expensive. Delerium on the other hand is a highly volatile element allowing it to output more magical energy for powering magitech, but not as reliable and much more dangerous.
