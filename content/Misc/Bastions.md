## 5th Level
### Arcane Study

Level 5 Bastion Facility 
**Prerequisite**: Ability to use an Arcane Focus or tool as a Spellcasting Focus
**Space**: Roomy 
**Hirelings**: 1
**Order**: Craft

An Arcane Study is a place of quiet research that contains one or more desks and bookshelves.  

***Arcane Study Charm.*** After spending a Long Rest in your Bastion, you gain a magical Charm (see “Supernatural Gifts” in chapter 3) that lasts for 7 days or until you use it. The Charm allows you to cast Identify without expending a spell slot or using Material components. You can’t gain this Charm again while you still have it.  

***Craft Options.*** When you issue the Craft order to this facility, choose one of the following options:  

***Craft**: Arcane Focus.* You commission the facility’s hireling to craft an Arcane Focus. The work takes 7 days and costs no money. The Arcane Focus remains in your Bastion until you claim it.  

***Craft:** Book.* You commission the facility’s hireling to craft a blank book. The work takes 7 days and costs you 10 GP. The book remains in your Bastion until you claim it.

***Craft:** Magic Item (Arcana).* If you are level 9+, you can commission the facility’s hireling to craft a Common or an Uncommon magic item chosen by you from the Arcana tables in chapter 7. The facility has the tool required to craft the item, and the hireling has proficiency with that tool as well as proficiency in the Arcana skill. See the “Crafting Magic Items” section in chapter 7 for the time and money that must be spent to craft the item. If the item allows its user to cast any spells from it, you must craft the item yourself (the facility’s hireling can assist), and you must have all those spells prepared every day you spend crafting the item.

### Armory

Level 5 Bastion Facility
**Prerequisite**: None
**Space**: Roomy
**Hirelings**: 1
**Order**: Trade

An Armory contains mannequins for displaying armor, hooks for holding Shields, racks for storing weapons, and chests for holding ammunition.  

***Trade:** Stock Armory*. When you issue the Trade order to this facility, you commission the facility’s hireling to stock the Armory with armor, Shields, weapons, and ammunition. This equipment costs you 100 GP plus an extra 100 GP for each Bastion Defender in your Bastion. If your Bastion has a Smithy, the total cost is halved.

While your Armory is stocked, your Bastion Defenders are harder to kill. When any event causes you to roll dice to determine if your Bastion loses one or more of its defenders (see “Bastion Events” at the end of this chapter), roll 1d8 in place of each d6 you would normally roll. When the event is over, the equipment in your Armory is expended regardless of how many Bastion Defenders you have or how many you lost, leaving your Armory depleted until you issue another Trade order to the facility and pay the cost to restock it.

**Prerequisite**: None
**Space**: Cramped
**Hirelings**: 1

At the end of the week, roll 1d100 to determine your earnings (1d100 x 10 GP). The vault’s prestige grants you Advantage on Charisma based D20 Tests for the next 7 days.

**Enlarging the Facility**: You can enlarge this facility to Vast by spending 2000 GP or donating 2 uncommon magic items. If you do so you can choose to make the effect of Display of Wealth change to the following. At the end of the week, roll 1d100 to determine your spendings (1d100 x 10 GP). You gain an uncommon magic randomly rolled on the Magic Item table by the DM per 100 GP spent.

### [[The Old Faiths]]

#### Archives of Mat’Selesnya

Level 5 Selesnya Conclave Facility
**Prerequisite**: None
**Space**: Roomy
**Hirelings**: 2
**Order**: Research

The Archives of Mat’Selesnya house a wealth of knowledge and lore passed down through the
generations. This facility contains teachings on nature magic, druidic practices, and Selesnya
philosophy, helping guild members learn from their predecessors.

**Research**: Spiritual Insight. When you issue the Research order to this facility, you consult the
archives to gain Advantage on your next Wisdom based D20 Test during a Bastion Event or encounter.

#### Conclave Greenhouses

Level 5 Selesnya Conclave Facility
**Prerequisite**: None
**Space**: Roomy
**Hirelings**: 2
**Order**: Craft

The Conclave Greenhouses are a carefully tended sanctuary where rare plants and herbs are grown with the aid of Selesnya Druids. This facility is essential for cultivating natural ingredients used in creating potent healing and alchemical mixtures.

**Craft**: Soothing Potions. When you issue the Craft order to this facility, you can create 2d4 Potions of Healing or 1 Potion of Growth (or a similar plant-based potion, per the DM’s discretion). Crafting takes 7 days and incurs no monetary cost.

#### Garden of Vitality

Level 5 Selesnya Conclave Facility
**Prerequisite**: Ability to use a Druidic Focus
**Space**: Roomy
**Hirelings**: 2
**Order**: Empower

The Garden of Vitality is a peaceful oasis where plants, animals, and Selesnya members gather to
share in nature’s bounty. Filled with vibrant flowers and lush greenery, this facility rejuvenates and
revitalizes those who walk its paths.

**Empower**: Natural Renewal. When you issue the Empower order to this facility, all allies who rest
in this garden gain the benefits of the Lesser Restoration spell, and additionally gain temporary
hit points equal to your level plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). This effect lasts for the
next 24 hours.

**Enlarging the Facility**: You can enlarge this facility to Vast by spending 5000 GP. If you do so you can increase the potency of the effects of Natural Renewal to the following: All allies who rest in this garden gain the benefits of the Greater Restoration spell, and temporarily increase their Maximum Hit Points equal to your level plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). This effect lasts until your next Bastion turn.

#### Glade of Kinship

Level 5 Selesnya Conclave Facility
**Prerequisite**: None
**Space**: Roomy
**Hirelings**: 2
**Order**: Recruit

In the heart of your Bastion lies the Glade of Kinship, a tranquil, lush space imbued with the welcoming energy of the Conclave. Here, Druids, Priests, and new guild initiates gather for bonding rituals and community celebrations, reinforcing the spirit of unity and shared purpose. In this grove, potential recruits find a sense of belonging and devotion to the Conclave’s mission of harmony.

**Recruit**: Kin of the Conclave. When you issue the Recruit order to this facility, you may call upon 4 new initiates or caretakers to join your Bastion. These new members serve as messengers, scouts, or apprentice Druids, ready to support Bastion activities and defend the sanctuary if needed. These Defenders use the Horncaller (Guildmasters Guide to Ravnica pg.253) statblock, and their training takes 7 days, incurring no monetary cost.

**Special Benefit**: While the Glade of Kinship is active, each recruit gains a bonus +1 to Wisdom-based D20 Tests made within the Bastion, representing the Conclave’s influence on their Insight and Perception.

#### Sacred Grove

Level 5 Selesnya Conclave Facility
**Prerequisite**: None
**Space**: Roomy
**Hirelings**: 1
**Order**: Harvest

The Sacred Grove is a secluded and tranquil area within the Bastion, where plants, animals, and fey
creatures are nurtured by druidic magic. The grove resonates with primal energy, cultivating plants with potent healing properties.

**Harvest**: Healing Herbs. When you issue the Harvest order to this facility, you gather enough herbs to create 1d4 Potions of Healing or 1 Potion of Greater Healing. Harvesting takes 7 days and incurs no monetary cost.

#### Voda’s Healing Pool

Level 5 Selesnya Conclave Facility
**Prerequisite**: Ability to use a Holy Symbol or Druidic Focus
**Space**: Roomy
**Hirelings**: 2
**Order**: Harvest

Voda’s Healing pool is a tranquil retreat where wounded and weary adventurers can receive the
blessings of Selesnya’s healing magic. The Pool is a place of meditation, where sacred waters cleanse and rejuvenate those who bathe within.

**Harvest**: Blessed Waters. When you issue the Harvest order to this facility, you can collect three vials of holy water or one vial of a specially blessed elixir that restores 1d4 hit points each hour for 4 hours. Harvesting takes 7 days and incurs no monetary cost.
