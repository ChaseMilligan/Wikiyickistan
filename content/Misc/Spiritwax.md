Spiritwax is an [[Alcohol]]ic alchemical creation of [[Xon]] that allows the illicit product to be packaged in easy to transport bricks of a wax-like substance. The recipe is mixed and brewed inside a brick mold, where it hardens to form the bricks. The bricks of product can be melted back into a liquid form to be consumed through a process determined by the magical ingredients, and the brewer of the batch. some melt by flame, some require a solvent, some may require something else.

## Recipe
##### Mundane Ingredients
All are required and cost a total of 5 gp.
- beeswax
- distilled alcohol
- pine resin
- a pinch of powdered amber
##### Magical Ingredients 
Only one of these is required. they are DC 5 components, and have a base cost of 10 gp.
- Phial of sap (plant)
- Phial of congealed blood (undead)
- Phial of acid (ooze, *explosive*)
- Phial of blood (any)
- Pouch of dust (fiend, celestial)
- Primordial dust (elemental, *potentially explosive*)
- Phial of oil (construct, *explosive*)

## Mechanics

- Each brick could hold **one gallon of alcohol** (25 gp market value).
- Bricks weigh **half as much as liquid casks** and are far less conspicuous.
- Mishandling (wrong flame, too much flame, breaking the brick) could cause **spoiling or explosions** depending on the magical ingredients.
