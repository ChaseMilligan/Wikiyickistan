**Port ale** is an indigo-hued extraplanar beverage best known for its ability to turn an ordinary tavern door into a route to [[The Loot Tavern]]. If a creature drinks port ale and steps through the door of a tavern within one minute, they are transported to the exterior of the drifting [[Astral Plane]] tavern.

No one agrees on whether port ale is a drink, a spell, a potion, or a logistical crime. It tastes different to everyone, though most describe it as “strong,” “purple,” and “probably not approved by local authorities.”

Bottles of port ale sometimes arrive by invitation from [[Heliana]], appear in the hands of suspicious merchants, or circulate among adventurers as emergency exits. In [[Stickyickistan]], possessing a bottle usually means someone is either about to visit [[The Loot Tavern]] or run away from something loudly.

Port Ale costs 5 gp.
