
*Weapon (sword), rarity varies*

Mind flayers can turn any nonmagical sword into a mind blade. Only one creature can attune to a mind blade: either the specific mind flayer that created it or one of that mind flayer’s thralls.

In the hands of any other creature, the mind blade functions as a normal sword of its kind. In the hands of its intended wielder, the mind blade is a magic weapon that deals an extra 2d6 psychic damage to any target it hits.

## Intended Wielder

The mind blade’s intended wielder is chosen when the mind flayer creates it. The intended wielder is either the creating mind flayer or one of its thralls. No other creature can attune to the weapon.

If the intended wielder dies or is no longer a thrall of the creating mind flayer, the mind blade remains a normal sword unless the DM determines that the creating mind flayer can designate a new intended wielder.

## Properties

While wielded by its intended wielder, the mind blade has the following properties:

- It is a magic weapon.
- It retains the weapon type and properties of the original sword.
- It deals an extra 2d6 psychic damage whenever it hits a target.

When wielded by any other creature, it functions as a normal sword of its kind and does not deal the extra psychic damage. It is not a magic weapon in that creature’s hands.

## Creation

A mind flayer can transform any nonmagical sword into a mind blade. The process requires the mind flayer to establish a psychic bond with the weapon and designate its intended wielder.

The creating mind flayer determines whether the intended wielder is itself or one of its thralls. The weapon’s magic is tied to that creature’s mind rather than to the sword’s shape or material.

## Narrative Use

A mind blade may be used as a ritual component or focus at the DM’s discretion. Its actual properties, however, remain those described above unless a specific ritual changes or destroys the weapon.

Example: For the Rite of Mind-Corpse Convergence in [[Wyvern and the Wyrmhole]], a mind blade is plunged into the heart of a freshly slain wyvern. If the rite succeeds, the mind blade is destroyed and the corpse transforms into a [[Bicephal Wyrmhole]].
