# 6.

The underground Tar cave has 6-8 winged kobolds doing a summoning ritual for a Tar Elemental. If attacked or otherwise disrupted before the ritual is complete, half of the kobolds will try to fight back and the other half will try to complete the ritual. The Tar elemental will be summoned on the 10th round of combat unless the extra kobolds arrive then it is summoned on the 8th round. The players can continue on through the cave and eventually find a stairway leading up to the surface. At the top of the staircase is Goob’lath’s lair. This lair is a large clearing in the impenetrable jungle with a giant skull engraved into the stone floor which is surrounded by esoteric inscriptions, and thick black tar flows from the skull into the cave below where they just came from. Goob’lath will not be at the lair, but succeeding on a DC 16 investigation will allow the players to find a scale that Goob’lath shed off. They can use the scale with Heliana’s guide to learn more about what resides in this place, though it will not be able to explain why the scale is covered in black ooze.  

The black ooze that covers Goob’lath is from a pact he made with the Slime Lord, Jubilex. His patronage to the Demon Prince of Slime is what set him on this path of infecting the scrapyard with Tar Elementals. Goob’lath believes that one day, Jubilex will consume all, but he will be spared if he can establish his own domain of filth, and army of shapeless creatures. He is also under oath to wage war on anyone, or anything that opposes Jubilex’s goal. Goob’lath has also amassed a cult following of black winged kobolds that revere him as a god, and do his bidding. They often cover themselves in the tar that Goob’lath is spreading in the scrapyard in an attempt to mimic his scales. The Tar Elementals not only use this tar to travel as they are adept swimmers, but it is also needed for the summoning rituals that conjure the Tar elementals in the material plane from in between the planes of earth and water.
