# Hunt: Blunt Force Trauma

**Level:** 6  
**Complexity:** 1  
**Duration:** 1 Session  
**Target:** Ogre Zombie (Monster Manual)  
**Suggested Biome:** Cursed Forest (Outback-flavored)

---

## Introduction

Locals say the ghost gumtrees are moving again, and something’s been pounding wild pigs into sausage patties. All signs point to the reanimated corpse of Big Barry, a former pit-fighter who drank himself to death during a chili cook-off and rose again fueled by cursed eucalyptus and sheer vibes.

---

## Background

A cursed glade deep in the Outback’s forgotten forests has become a dumping ground for failed necromancy experiments and mystical party fouls. One such blunder revived an **Ogre Zombie**, now known as **Blunt Barry**, who stumbles through the bush looking for “one last sesh.” Unfortunately, Barry's version of a sesh involves pulverizing anything that breathes.

### Quest Hooks

- **Curiosity:** Local druids are baffled—undead shouldn't be this... chill?
- **Morality:** A traveling herbalist caravan never made it out. Their kin want answers.
- **Compensation:** A local rancher offers **750 gp** and a rare stash of aged medicinal herbs if Barry is brought down for good.

### Rewards

- **XP:** 1,100 XP (Ogre Zombie) + 2 × 450 XP (Twig Blights) = 2,000 XP  
- **Gold:** 750 gp  
- **Magic Item:** *Potion of Gaseous Form* (SRD)

### Key NPCs

- **Tilly Treechewer:** A halfling herbalist who saw Barry flatten a kangaroo and politely asks the party to “yeet that bloke into the next life.”
- **Dingo Ron:** A cracked-out ranger who swears the forest whispered Barry’s name while he was hotboxing a termite mound.

---

## Location

**Biome:** Cursed Forest in the dry outback  
Twisted ghost gums groan in the wind. The air smells like stale beer and eucalyptus. Some trees bleed sap that smells suspiciously like mango kush.

## Know Thy Enemy

### Clue 1: “He won’t stay down!”

- **Religion (DC 13):** Barry is an Ogre Zombie. Expect high HP and undead resilience.
- **Prep:** Bring radiant damage or crowd control.

### Clue 2: “His club's a log full of nails.”

- **Investigation (DC 13):** It’s just a tree trunk—but it hurts. A lot.
- **Prep:** Boost AC or kite.

### Clue 3: “He groans about missing his lighter.”

- **Arcana or Insight (DC 13):** Barry is mildly animated by cursed smoke and necromantic herbs.
- **Prep:** Fire or smoke-based spells may distract or stall him.

---

## The Battle Ahead

**Boss:** Ogre Zombie (CR 2, 1,100 XP)  
**Minions:** 2 Twig Blights (CR 1/4 each, 50 XP each)  
**Tactics:** Barry charges and smashes. The blights entangle and block escape routes.

**Lair Features:**

- **Misty Grove:** Lightly obscured terrain. Smells like weed and rot.  
- **Slick Leaves:** 10 ft patches of difficult terrain (DC 10 Acrobatics or fall prone).  
- **Hollow Log Bong:** A rotten log echoes moans. If touched, casts *Minor Illusion* once per day.

---

## Aftermath

### Curiosity:

Barry’s body evaporates into resinous smoke. The druids collect it for study.

### Morality:

The caravan survivors are rescued from his camp, tied up but alive... and mildly high.

### Compensation:

750 gp and a Potion of Gaseous Form (ironically labeled "Barry’s Last Breath").

## Treasure

### Harvesting (DC 13 Medicine or Arcana)

- **Putrid Club (mundane):** Heavy, nails, soaked in pine tar  
- **Residue of the Dank:** Acts as incense for *Augury* or *Speak with Dead*

### Mundane

- Tree sap laced with faint necromantic magic (sells for 25 gp to wizards)

## Beyond Deadly

This hunt is intended to be deadly but goofy. If players get careless, Barry can still one-shot a caster. Encourage smart terrain use and creative spellcasting for full laughs and chaos.
