Now that Skarguther considers you a trusted associate, he has another request of you. He knows full well that most folk are offput by the ideology of the [[Heralds of Dust]], but he assures you they truly are one of the most altruistic factions in [[Stickyickistan]] (true). He also understands that this request might seem a little strange coming from someone who just boasted the altruism of his faction. Because the Dusters detest anything that can restore life to the dead, he is asking you to eliminate one of his political enemies, the War Priestess [[Kiefra Battlehymn]]. Skarguther and Kiefra have never seen eye to eye, but their rivalry was not particularly spicy, until Kiefra breached a contract she made with the [[Heralds of Dust]] by using revivify to restore life to a duster that was witness to a crime so he could testify in court. 

Skarguther will say:
"Now, we are all for the proper use and execution of the justice system, except when it comes to our members Death rights. The Tribunal doesn't agree with us, but we don't care, and we will take justice into our own hands if we must to make sure our members are protected. She knew full well that restoring life to a Duster is depriving them of their chance at achieving True Death, and is in direct violation of the deal we made decades ago. She must pay for what she took from him with her life. If she thinks she can deny one of ours their rightful eternity in True Death, then we will deny her a chance at eternal life. She knows what she has done, and two can play at this game."

He will hand you a scroll of the spell Animate Dead which he wants you to use on her corpse should you defeat her. And he makes it very clear that this must not be traced back to him or the Dusters.

I recommend you not try to do this alone since Kiefra always stays with a security outfit of her followers, and she is a very powerful spellcaster. Attempting to kill her could end in your own demise so you know.
