
_A D&D 5e (2024) adventure for 4–5 3rd-level characters_

A few miles beyond the village of **[[Doobius House]]**, in the wooded interior of [[The Outback]], something ancient has emerged from beneath the earth.

For months, hunters and woodsmen have whispered about a stone doorway that appeared at the foot of a grassy hill beyond the forest. No storm uncovered it. No landslide exposed it. One morning, it simply existed. The doorway stands beside an overgrown trail once used by hunters and woodsmen traveling through the forest.

A dozen adventurers have entered since.

Only one returned.

Half-starved and barely coherent, the survivor spoke of stone guardians, corpses crawling from their tombs, and terrible glass vessels filled with swirling darkness.

But one detail disturbed the people of [[Doobius House]] more than any other.

He swore that after his companions died, **he could still hear them screaming from inside the tomb.**

The tomb is the hidden laboratory of **[[Dopendis Gravebreath]]**, a necromancer and arcanist of [[Orcus]] who discovered a means of capturing mortal souls and storing them within enchanted vessels. His work was part of the same hidden Orcus cult operation exposed in [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]], though his laboratory lay far from the mines, buried in [[The Outback]] beyond [[Doobius House]]. There, Dopendis refined captive souls into **[[Soul Diesel]]**, a necromantic fuel intended to power a mass raise dead ritual.

When the adventurers defeated [[Sarbrindar]] and destroyed the undead in the quarry, the cult’s wider network was thrown into panic. Dopendis Gravebreath abandoned his forest laboratory soon after, either fleeing before the trail could lead to him or answering some darker summons from his Orcus cult masters.

His experiments have not gone anywhere, and his illusion obscuring the laboratory has fallen.

---

# Adventure Hook

The characters begin at the [[FLS Guildhall]] on [[Nevercough Island]], looking for work through the [[Free League of Stickyickistan]]. A public notice on [[The Rolling Papers]] offers **150 gp** to investigate a newly revealed tomb in the wooded interior of [[The Outback]] and return with evidence of its purpose.

The notice explains that several explorers, hunters, and woodsmen from [[Doobius House]] entered the tomb after its discovery and never returned. Their sole survivor, **[[Bongston Vale]]**, was found half-starved and raving about black ceramic jars filled with screaming souls. He is recovering in the guildhall’s [[FLS Guildhall#Recovery support|recovery quarters]] and can be questioned before the party leaves.

Their families have contacted the Guildhall, desperate to learn what happened to them. A scholar from [[N.E.R.D]] has also taken an interest in the tomb after comparing Bongston’s account to reports from [[content/POIs/Blacklung Quarry|Blacklung Quarry]], where [[Bartholomew Dorkymin V]] helped document strange necromantic evidence recovered from the quarry. The scholar suspects the jars may be connected to a forbidden substance called **[[Soul Diesel]]**, but needs proof from inside the tomb. After hearing Bongston’s account, the families of the missing urge the characters to **free any trapped souls they find**.

Bongston remembers little clearly, but repeatedly warns:

> "Don't touch the jars. Gods above, don't touch the jars."

He remembers seeing his companions die, and then hearing their voices begging him for help from somewhere deeper inside the tomb.

## The Roach Clip Hook: Unfinished Business

Characters who took part in [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]], or who have ties to the Dusters, may find this coded notice in [[The Roach Clip]]:

> **Job: Recover the black soul-jars from the mysterious new tomb beyond [[Doobius House]]. Bring back every jar you can, fully intact. Do not open, smash, or hand them to the authorities. Payment on delivery, will be assessed by the buyer. Collect payment at the Dungsweepers’ mortuary in [[The Undercity Osslurraggath|Osslurraggath]] using the phrase "The dead are still moving."**

The contact is [[Skarguther Dungleboof]], a well-connected member of the [[Heralds of Dust]], CFO of the Stickyickistanian [[Dungsweepers]], and collector of cursed relics. He believes the jars are linked to the Orcus cult discovered beneath [[Blacklung Quarry]] and wants them recovered intact, not to fuel another ritual, but to remove the tortured souls from the cult’s reach and study how they might be guided toward [[Heralds of Dust|True Death]]. His methods are unofficial and sometimes illegal, but he is not necessarily malicious.

> **“Touch the jars. Bring me every one you can carry.”**

Skarguther pays **300 gp per intact active jar** and **100 gp per intact inert jar**. The four active jars in the ritual chamber are worth **1,200 gp** total, and the two inert jars in Area 3 are worth **200 gp** total, for a possible maximum payment of **1,400 gp** if all six jars are recovered intact. To collect, deliver the jars to the Dungsweepers’ mortuary offices in [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]] and give the clerk the notice or the phrase **“The dead are still moving.”** Broken jars and released souls earn no payment. The public 100 gp reward remains available, but this contract asks the characters to collect the jars rather than smash them.

# Reaching the Tomb

The tomb lies roughly **two days from [[Doobius House]]** through dense forest.

The journey doesn't need to be a full wilderness adventure. Instead, use it to establish the increasingly unnatural atmosphere surrounding the tomb.

During the second day, animals become scarce.

Birdsong disappears.

That evening, characters occasionally hear whispers between the trees.

A character who succeeds on a **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check hears fragments:

_"Come closer..."_

_"It hurts..."_

_"Please..."_

_"There's room for you too..."_

These aren't ghosts wandering the forest. They are psychic echoes emanating from the Jars of Souls.

---

# 1. The Entrance

A grassy clearing surrounds a large hill. Built into its base is an ancient stone doorway leading downward.

Weathered stairs descend into darkness.

Characters approaching within 30 feet hear faint whispers from below.

A **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check reveals that these aren't ordinary haunting manifestations. Something beneath the hill is magically attracting or containing spirits.

The stairs descend approximately 40 feet before reaching Area 2.

---

# 2. The Landing

A circular chamber marks the true entrance to the complex.

An enormous necromantic rune has been carved into the floor.

Four stone pedestals surround it, each holding a grotesque idol depicting a forgotten entity devouring humanoid spirits.

A successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check identifies the circle's purpose:

**It channels spiritual energy toward the center of the tomb.**

The large obsidian double doors leading to Area 7 have no handles or visible locks.

Magic seals them.

Casting _Detect Magic_ reveals strong Necromancy and Abjuration magic.

The doors open only when someone speaks:

**"Syvethis."**

The characters can discover the command word in Area 6.

Attempting to force the doors open automatically fails.

---

# 3. Stone-d Guardians

Two separate chambers contain one of Dopendis Gravebreath's earliest successful Jars of Souls each. These two jars are **inert**: they contain tortured souls, but they do not power the tomb's guardians or emit necrotic energy.

Each room contains:

- Two stone guardian statues
- One inert Jar of Souls
- Ancient necromantic inscriptions

When a creature approaches within 10 feet of the jar, the statues animate.

### Encounter

Use **2 Animated Armors** in each room.

However, the guardians aren't interested in pursuing intruders through the dungeon.

They exist solely to protect the jar.

If characters retreat from the chamber, the constructs return to their original positions after 1 minute.

### The Inert Jar

A black ceramic vessel rests upon a stone pedestal. Ghostly faces occasionally push outward from its surface before disappearing.

Each inert jar contains a dark, viscous Arcannabis concentrate called **[[Soul Diesel]]**: trapped souls mixed with humanoid blood. The souls remain conscious within the mixture, which is why the liquid whispers with the voices Bongston heard after his companions died. A creature that touches the jar hears dozens of overlapping whispers, including fragments of the souls’ final memories. Unlike the active jars in Area 7, an inert jar is not connected to the ritual machinery and does not empower the undead.

A creature that ingests a dose of Soul Diesel gains **1d4 bonus damage of the same type as its attack against humanoids** for 1 hour. The effect applies once per turn. Using the strain is disturbing and illegal in most civilized settlements, since its manufacture requires tortured souls and humanoid blood.

Characters who accepted [[Skarguther Dungleboof]]’s [[The Roach Clip]] contract can identify this as the item he wants. He pays **100 gp for each intact inert jar** in Area 3 and **300 gp for each intact active jar** in the ritual chamber. The vessel must remain sealed and undamaged; a broken jar earns nothing. Characters who did not accept the contract can still recognize that the jar would interest collectors of forbidden magic, but selling it would be dangerous and morally fraught.

This creates a clear choice. The characters can carry the jar to Skarguther, placing its tortured souls in the hands of a Duster who claims he can guide them toward [[Heralds of Dust|True Death]]. Or they can smash it, freeing the souls and allowing them to pass into the afterlife, but destroying the vessel and forfeiting its reward. They may choose differently for each jar, but Skarguther pays only for intact vessels.

### Breaking an Inert Jar

AC 12  
HP 10  
Immune: Poison, Psychic

Destroying an inert jar releases the souls trapped inside, but because the vessel is disconnected from the ritual machinery, it does not produce the violent psychic backlash of an active jar. The spirits emerge as pale figures, briefly surround the characters, and then disappear. A character proficient in Arcana or Religion realizes that the souls have finally been allowed to pass into the afterlife.

### Taking the Jar

The characters may instead carry the intact vessel. It is heavy, fragile, and constantly whispers to anyone holding it. Wrapping it securely prevents accidental damage, but does not silence the voices.

If the characters accepted Skarguther’s contract, they must transport the jar to the Dungsweepers’ mortuary offices in [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]] and present it to Skarguther or one of his Duster attendants to collect the reward. Otherwise, carrying it could attract the attention of necromancers, undead creatures, religious authorities, or [[Dopendis Gravebreath]].

# 4. Forsaken Sarcophagi

Several sarcophagi line the corridors surrounding the central chamber.

They initially appear dormant.

They aren't.

The tomb's guardians awaken whenever someone interferes with Dopendis Gravebreath's experiments.

The **first time the characters move, damage, or destroy a Jar of Souls**, two nearby sarcophagi burst open.

### Encounter

**1d4+1 Ash Zombies** emerge from the closest sarcophagi. The are 8 Ash Zombies total.

Afterward, the remaining sarcophagi occasionally rattle or produce scratching sounds, but don't immediately open.

When the characters eventually open the doors to Area 7, **the remaining Ash Zombies** emerge from their sarcophagi.

This splits the original six-monster encounter into manageable battles while maintaining the feeling that the entire tomb is waking around them.

---

# 5. East Libraries

The northern library has largely collapsed.

Rotten books cover the floor beneath fallen shelves.

Most are unreadable.

The southern library is protected by a locked wooden door.

**DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand using Thieves' Tools)** opens the lock.

Alternatively:

AC 15  
HP 15

Inside are Dopendis Gravebreath's research notes.

Characters spending at least 10 minutes examining them learn:

### Dopendis Gravebreath

Dopendis Gravebreath was a powerful mage and secret adherent of [[Orcus]], obsessed with the boundary between life and death. His research was part of the same cult operation exposed at [[Blacklung Quarry]], but his role was separate: he developed soul jars and refined the trapped dead into **[[Soul Diesel]]**, a necromantic [[Arcannabis]] strain, as fuel the cult hoped to use in a mass raise dead ritual. The cult’s promised reward was not wealth or immortality, but an army of the dead.

He believed souls weren't merely spiritual entities.

They were **energy**.

Energy that could be collected.

Stored.

And eventually consumed.

He constructed this laboratory to develop vessels capable of trapping souls before they could reach the afterlife.

His writings repeatedly refer to something called:

**"The Great Working."**

Apparently, Dopendis Gravebreath intended to gather hundreds, perhaps thousands, of souls to power a ritual.

Exactly what that ritual would accomplish isn't explained.

Dopendis Gravebreath's current whereabouts are unknown.

# 6. West Libraries

The southern library has collapsed.

The northern chamber remains intact but locked.

The door requires a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand using Thieves' Tools)** check or can simply be destroyed.

> [!read-aloud] Read Aloud
> The chamber beyond is a preserved records room. Shelves hold stacks of brittle journals, rolled architectural plans, and thin metal plates etched with diagrams. Most of the notes concern the tomb itself: its hidden entrances, sealed doors, guardian chambers, and the illusion that concealed the structure from the outside world. Diagrams show magical lines running through the floors and walls, all converging on the central ritual chamber.
>
> A large map has been pinned above a writing desk. It shows the tomb’s rooms and protective enchantments, with several sections crossed out and rewritten in a different hand. One note explains that the concealment enchantment was designed to last for decades, but the ink beneath it has faded over a long period of time. Near the doors to the central chamber, one word has been written in large letters: **SYVETHIS**.

Inside are records describing the tomb's magical protections.

Characters learn that Dopendis Gravebreath concealed the entire structure using an enormous illusion and transmutation ritual.

The enchantment was designed to last for decades.

The tomb's recent appearance means the spell has finally failed.

More importantly, they discover the command used to enter Dopendis Gravebreath's ritual chamber:

## SYVETHIS

Speaking the word near the doors in Area 2 causes ancient mechanisms to grind beneath the floor.

The central doors slowly open.

---

# 7. Ritual Chamber

The doors open onto an enormous circular chamber.

Broken ritual equipment covers the floor.

Black candles have melted into strange shapes.

Silver wire forms incomplete magical circles.

And surrounding the central ritual platform are **four active Jars of Souls**.

Unlike the two inert vessels in Area 3, these jars are connected to the ritual machinery and actively distill Soul Diesel: a mixture of trapped souls and humanoid blood. The ritual machinery feeds the concentrate into the undead guardians, empowering them while keeping the souls conscious and trapped.

Ghostly figures visibly swirl within the dark liquid.

Some pound desperately against the inside of the glass.

Among them are the souls of the adventurers who disappeared from [[Doobius House]]. As the characters enter, spectral guardians rise from the floor.


> [!read-aloud] Read Aloud
> The doors open onto an enormous circular chamber where the air feels heavy and strangely warm. Broken ritual equipment covers the floor, mixed with melted black candles and lengths of silver wire that form incomplete circles across the stone. At the center stands a raised ritual platform, its surface stained dark and marked with the remains of old magical symbols.
>
> Four glass Jars of Souls stand around the platform, each connected to the ritual circle by silver wires. Ghostly figures swirl within them, pressing their hands and faces against the glass. Some of the spirits look like the missing adventurers from [[Doobius House]]. Their silent mouths form desperate pleas, but no sound escapes the vessels.
>
> As you step into the chamber, the wires begin to glow. The jars pulse with cold light, and the shadows beneath the platform move independently of the objects that cast them. A skeletal hand punches through the stone beside the platform. Then another. Bones claw their way upward as a figure in a stained alchemist’s coat pulls itself from the floor. Glass vials clatter against its ribcage, and a cracked skull turns toward you, empty eye sockets burning with pale blue light. The skeleton raises one hand, and the silver wires snap taut as though answering its command, and two more spectral figures rise from the floor beside the Skeleton.

### Encounter

Use:

**2 Specters**

and

**1 Skeletal Alchemist**

The Skeletal Alchemist wears a stained alchemist’s coat and carries glass vials filled with corrosive fluid. It acts as the ritual chamber’s physical caretaker, using acid to drive intruders away from the active jars.

The Skeleton and Specters are bound spirits forced to defend the laboratory.

### Soul Diesel-Fueled Guardians

The active jars pump Soul Diesel through the silver wires and into the undead guardians. The Specters and Skeletal Alchemist are under the strain’s effects.

**Soul Diesel.** Once per turn, when a Specter or the Skeletal Alchemist deals damage to a humanoid with an attack, it deals an extra **1d4 damage of the same type as the attack**. The bonus damage is part of the attack’s damage and does not apply against creatures that are not humanoids.

At **Initiative Count 20**, one intact active jar emits a pulse of necrotic energy. One undead creature in the tomb gains **5 Temporary Hit Points**. The characters can prevent this by destroying the active jars during combat.

Roll 1d4 to determine which active jar pulses. If the number rolled was assigned to a jar that has been destroyed, nothing happens.

### Breaking an Active Jar

AC 12  
HP 10

When an active jar is destroyed, its trapped souls erupt outward and the ritual machinery discharges through the vessel. Every creature within 5 feet must make a **DC 12 Wisdom saving throw**, taking **2d6 Psychic damage** on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that fails the save is also **Frightened for 1 minute**. It can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Every undead creature within 5 feet takes **1d6 Radiant damage** as the jar’s necrotic energy collapses. The spirits then disappear. A character proficient in Arcana or Religion realizes that the souls have finally been allowed to pass into the afterlife.

Destroying an active jar weakens the ritual and prevents it from empowering the undead at Initiative Count 20. This gives the players an important tactical choice: fight the guardians normally, or smash the very artifacts they came to investigate.

# The Lost Adventurers

After the battle, the characters recognize several spirits within the remaining jars from descriptions given by people in [[Doobius House]].

These are the missing explorers.

The characters have three choices.

### Release Them

Destroying the jars allows the spirits to pass onward.

For several moments, translucent figures appear throughout the chamber.

Some simply disappear.

Others thank the characters.

One whispers:

_"He'll come back for them."_

Then the chamber falls silent.

This can garner some bonus xp for good RP.

### Take the Jars

The characters can instead remove the jars.

The dungeon contains six jars total: two inert jars in Area 3 and four active jars in Area 7. Skarguther values intact inert jars at **100 gp each** and intact active jars at **300 gp each**.

Possessing one, however, is both dangerous and morally questionable.

### Study Them

A character who spends 10 minutes examining an active jar and succeeds on a **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana or Nature)** check realizes that the vessels are distilling Soul Diesel: trapped souls mixed with humanoid blood and refined into a potent Arcannabis concentrate.

The strain’s spiritual energy can be transferred into another ritual or magical object. A creature that ingests it gains **1d4 bonus damage of the same type as its attack against humanoids** for 1 hour, with the bonus applying once per turn. Whatever Dopendis Gravebreath intended to do here required an enormous amount of Soul Diesel.

# 8. Secret Vault — Dopendis Gravebreath's Wealth

A hidden stone button can be discovered with a **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** or **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check.

Pressing it causes part of the wall to slide open.

Inside is Dopendis Gravebreath's emergency treasury.

The characters find:

- 180 gp
- 420 sp
- 4 bloodstones worth 50 gp each
- An ornate silver chalice worth 75 gp
- 2 Potions of Healing

A small locked box also contains one of Dopendis Gravebreath's personal seals.

The symbol depicts:

**An open hand holding a flaming horned skull.**

A character who succeeds on a **DC 13 Intelligence (Religion)** check recognizes it as a local cult’s blasphemous variation of the symbol of [[Orcus]]. The seal can identify Dopendis Gravebreath’s followers, other cult safehouses, or agents connected to [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]].

---

# 9. Secret Vault — Arcane Storage

Another secret button opens this chamber.

Inside are shelves containing magical supplies and unfinished experiments.

The characters discover three spell scrolls:

- **Scroll of Detect Magic**
- **Scroll of Protection from Evil and Good**
- **Scroll of Gentle Repose**

They also find a peculiar black crystal.

A successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check reveals that the crystal once contained spiritual energy similar to the Jars of Souls. It was not merely a storage device: the cult used crystals like this to feed stolen souls into a larger necromantic working.

Unlike the jars, however...

**This crystal is empty.**

Someone used it. A character who knows of [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]] recognizes the residue as similar to the energy released beneath [[Blacklung Quarry]].

# Ending the Adventure

When the characters leave the tomb, the whispers surrounding the clearing are gone if they released the trapped souls.

The forest feels alive again.

Birds return.

Wind moves through the trees.

Back in [[Doobius House]], the families of the missing adventurers finally learn what happened to their loved ones.

If the characters released their souls, the community considers them heroes.

But the discoveries within the tomb raise a much larger question. The Orcus cult did not build this laboratory for [[Dopendis Gravebreath]] alone. They were using it as a hidden refinery, turning harvested souls into **[[Soul Diesel]]** for a mysterious rite.

Dopendis Gravebreath spent decades gathering souls.

He constructed an entire laboratory.

He created magical vessels capable of stealing the dead from the afterlife.

And yet...

**Dopendis Gravebreath isn't here.**

His ritual was never completed.

Somewhere in the world, Dopendis Gravebreath may still be alive.

And eventually, he will discover that someone has broken into his tomb.

# Scaling the Adventure

### 3 Characters

Remove one Animated Armor from one of the Area 3 encounters.

The Ritual Chamber contains **2 Specters only**.

### 4 Characters

Run the adventure as written.

### 5 Characters

Run the adventure as written, but give the Ritual Chamber skeleton **2 additional Skeleton allies**.

# Adventure Rewards

**1200 XP each** for completing the adventure and resolving the fate of the trapped souls. This is a challenging Tier 1 adventure reward under [[Character Advancement]].

**150 gp each** from the [[N.E.R.D]] scholar for investigating the tomb and returning with evidence. This payment is divided among the characters who complete the contract.

Additional rewards:

- **100 gp** for each intact inert Jar of Souls recovered for [[Skarguther Dungleboof]].
- **300 gp** for each intact active Jar of Souls recovered for Skarguther.
- Up to **1,400 gp total** from Skarguther if all six jars are recovered intact; this is a party payment, not a reward per character.
- Approximately **450–700 gp** worth of treasure found throughout the dungeon, divided among the party.
- **2 Potions of Healing.**
- **3 low-level spell scrolls.**
- A story award for uncovering [[Dopendis Gravebreath]], the Orcus cult, and the Great Working.

Optional bonus XP:

- **+50 XP each** for rescuing or laying to rest the souls of the missing adventurers.
- **+100 XP each** for recovering reliable evidence of the Orcus cult’s soul experiments.

If the characters complete only part of the adventure, use the partial-XP guidelines in [[Character Advancement]].

## Future Hook: Dopendis Gravebreath Returns

Don't have Dopendis Gravebreath attack immediately.

Weeks or even months later, the characters discover the symbol of the **hand holding the flaming horned skull** somewhere else.

Perhaps on a corpse.

Perhaps in another abandoned laboratory.

Perhaps carried by someone asking questions about the adventurers who entered the tomb near [[Doobius House]].

Eventually they learn the truth:

**Dopendis Gravebreath knows their names, and surviving members of his wider Orcus network know that the characters have found the jars of Soul Diesel.**
