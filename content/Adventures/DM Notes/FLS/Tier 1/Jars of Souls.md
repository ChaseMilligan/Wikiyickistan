
_A D&D 5e (2024) adventure for 4–5 3rd-level characters_

A few miles beyond the village of **Haverton**, something ancient has emerged from beneath the earth.

For months, hunters and woodsmen have whispered about a stone doorway that appeared at the foot of a grassy hill beyond the forest. No storm uncovered it. No landslide exposed it. One morning, it simply existed.

A dozen adventurers have entered since.

Only one returned.

Half-starved and barely coherent, the survivor spoke of stone guardians, corpses crawling from their tombs, and terrible glass vessels filled with swirling darkness.

But one detail disturbed the people of Haverton more than any other.

He swore that after his companions died, **he could still hear them screaming from inside the tomb.**

The tomb is the hidden laboratory of **Opendis**, a necromancer who discovered a means of capturing mortal souls and storing them within enchanted vessels.

Opendis has disappeared.

His experiments have not.

---

# Adventure Hook

The characters hear about the tomb while staying in Haverton.

Several families are desperate to learn what happened to the missing explorers. A local scholar is equally interested in the strange structure and offers the characters **100 gp** if they can determine its purpose and return with evidence.

The sole survivor, **Edric Vale**, can also be questioned.

Edric remembers little clearly, but repeatedly warns:

> "Don't touch the jars. Gods above, don't touch the jars."

He remembers seeing his companions die—and then hearing their voices begging him for help from somewhere deeper inside the tomb.

---

# Reaching the Tomb

The tomb lies roughly **two days from Haverton** through dense forest.

The journey doesn't need to be a full wilderness adventure. Instead, use it to establish the increasingly unnatural atmosphere surrounding the tomb.

During the second day, animals become scarce.

Birdsong disappears.

That evening, characters occasionally hear whispers between the trees.

A character who succeeds on a **DC 12 Wisdom (Perception)** check hears fragments:

_"Come closer..."_

_"It hurts..."_

_"Please..."_

_"There's room for you too..."_

These aren't ghosts wandering the forest. They are psychic echoes emanating from the Jars of Souls.

---

# 1. The Entrance

A grassy clearing surrounds a large hill. Built into its base is an ancient stone doorway leading downward.

Weathered stairs descend into darkness.

Characters approaching within 30 feet hear faint whispers from below.

A **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check reveals that these aren't ordinary haunting manifestations. Something beneath the hill is magically attracting or containing spirits.

The stairs descend approximately 40 feet before reaching Area 2.

---

# 2. The Landing

A circular chamber marks the true entrance to the complex.

An enormous necromantic rune has been carved into the floor.

Four stone pedestals surround it, each holding a grotesque idol depicting a forgotten entity devouring humanoid spirits.

A successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana or Religion)** check identifies the circle's purpose:

**It channels spiritual energy toward the center of the tomb.**

The large double doors leading to Area 7 have no handles or visible locks.

Magic seals them.

Casting _Detect Magic_ reveals strong Necromancy and Abjuration magic.

The doors open only when someone speaks:

**"Syvethis."**

The characters can discover the command word in Area 6.

Attempting to force the doors open automatically fails.

---

# 3. Immortal Guardians

Two chambers contain Opendis's earliest successful Jars of Souls.

Each room contains:

- Two stone guardian statues
    
- One Jar of Souls
    
- Ancient necromantic inscriptions
    

When a creature approaches within 10 feet of the jar, the statues animate.

### Encounter

Use **2 Animated Armors** in each room.

However, the guardians aren't interested in pursuing intruders through the dungeon.

They exist solely to protect the jar.

If characters retreat from the chamber, the constructs return to their original positions after 1 minute.

### The Jar

A black ceramic vessel rests upon a stone pedestal.

Ghostly faces occasionally push outward from its surface before disappearing.

Each jar contains several tortured souls.

Touching it causes the creature to hear dozens of overlapping whispers.

The jar is worth approximately **300 gp** to the right buyer, although selling such an object should have obvious moral consequences.

### Breaking the Jar

AC 12  
HP 10  
Immune: Poison, Psychic

Destroying the jar releases the souls trapped inside.

A wave of spectral energy fills the chamber.

Every creature within 10 feet must make a **DC 12 Wisdom saving throw**.

On a failure, the creature is **Frightened until the end of its next turn**.

The spirits then disappear.

A character proficient in Arcana or Religion realizes the souls have finally been allowed to pass into the afterlife.

### Taking the Jar

The characters may instead carry the intact vessel.

Doing so could attract the attention of necromancers, undead creatures, religious authorities—or Opendis himself.

---

# 4. Forsaken Sarcophagi

Several sarcophagi line the corridors surrounding the central chamber.

They initially appear dormant.

They aren't.

The tomb's guardians awaken whenever someone interferes with Opendis's experiments.

The **first time the characters move, damage, or destroy a Jar of Souls**, two nearby sarcophagi burst open.

### Encounter

**2 Ghouls** emerge.

Afterward, the remaining sarcophagi occasionally rattle or produce scratching sounds, but don't immediately open.

When the characters eventually open the doors to Area 7, **2 additional Ghouls** emerge from the remaining sarcophagi.

This splits the original eight-monster encounter into manageable battles while maintaining the feeling that the entire tomb is waking around them.

---

# 5. East Libraries

The northern library has largely collapsed.

Rotten books cover the floor beneath fallen shelves.

Most are unreadable.

The southern library is protected by a locked wooden door.

**DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand using Thieves' Tools)** opens the lock.

Alternatively:

AC 15  
HP 15

Inside are Opendis's research notes.

Characters spending at least 10 minutes examining them learn:

### Opendis

Opendis was a powerful mage obsessed with the boundary between life and death.

He believed souls weren't merely spiritual entities.

They were **energy**.

Energy that could be collected.

Stored.

And eventually consumed.

He constructed this laboratory to develop vessels capable of trapping souls before they could reach the afterlife.

His writings repeatedly refer to something called:

**"The Great Working."**

Apparently, Opendis intended to gather hundreds—perhaps thousands—of souls to power a ritual.

Exactly what that ritual would accomplish isn't explained.

Opendis's current whereabouts are unknown.

---

# 6. West Libraries

The southern library has collapsed.

The northern chamber remains intact but locked.

The door requires a **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand using Thieves' Tools)** check or can simply be destroyed.

Inside are records describing the tomb's magical protections.

Characters learn that Opendis concealed the entire structure using an enormous illusion and transmutation ritual.

The enchantment was designed to last for decades.

The tomb's recent appearance means the spell has finally failed.

More importantly, they discover the command used to enter Opendis's ritual chamber:

## SYVETHIS

Speaking the word near the doors in Area 2 causes ancient mechanisms to grind beneath the floor.

The central doors slowly open.

---

# 7. Ritual Chamber

The doors open onto an enormous circular chamber.

Broken ritual equipment covers the floor.

Black candles have melted into strange shapes.

Silver wire forms incomplete magical circles.

And surrounding the central ritual platform are **four Jars of Souls**.

Unlike the previous vessels, these are active.

Ghostly figures visibly swirl within them.

Some pound desperately against the inside of the glass.

Among them are the souls of the adventurers who disappeared from Haverton.

As the characters enter, spectral guardians rise from the floor.

### Encounter

Use:

**2 Specters**

and

**1 Skeleton**

The skeleton wears the remains of ceremonial robes and acts as the ritual chamber's physical caretaker.

The Specters are bound spirits forced to defend the laboratory.

### Soul-Fueled Guardians

The jars power the undead.

At **Initiative Count 20**, one intact jar emits a pulse of necrotic energy.

One undead creature in the chamber gains **5 Temporary Hit Points**.

The characters can prevent this by destroying the jars during combat.

### Destroying a Jar

AC 12  
HP 10

When destroyed, its trapped souls erupt outward.

Every undead creature within 15 feet takes **1d6 Radiant damage**.

This gives the players an important tactical choice:

Fight the guardians normally...

or start smashing the very artifacts they came to investigate.

---

# The Lost Adventurers

After the battle, the characters recognize several spirits within the remaining jars from descriptions given by people in Haverton.

These are the missing explorers.

The characters have three choices.

### Release Them

Destroying the jars allows the spirits to pass onward.

For several moments, translucent figures appear throughout the chamber.

Some simply disappear.

Others thank the characters.

One whispers:

_"He'll come back for them."_

Then the chamber falls silent.

### Take the Jars

The characters can instead remove the jars.

Each could theoretically be worth **300 gp** to collectors of forbidden magic.

Possessing one, however, is both dangerous and morally questionable.

### Study Them

A character who spends 10 minutes examining a jar and succeeds on a **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana)** check realizes the vessels could potentially be modified.

They don't merely contain souls.

They can **transfer their spiritual energy into another ritual or magical object**.

Whatever Opendis intended to do here required an enormous amount of power.

---

# 8. Secret Vault — Opendis's Wealth

A hidden stone button can be discovered with a **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** or **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** check.

Pressing it causes part of the wall to slide open.

Inside is Opendis's emergency treasury.

The characters find:

- 180 gp
    
- 420 sp
    
- 4 bloodstones worth 50 gp each
    
- An ornate silver chalice worth 75 gp
    
- 2 Potions of Healing
    

A small locked box also contains one of Opendis's personal seals.

The symbol depicts:

**An open hand holding a screaming skull.**

This symbol can later identify Opendis's followers or other laboratories.

---

# 9. Secret Vault — Arcane Storage

Another secret button opens this chamber.

Inside are shelves containing magical supplies and unfinished experiments.

The characters discover three spell scrolls:

- **Scroll of Detect Magic**
    
- **Scroll of Protection from Evil and Good**
    
- **Scroll of Gentle Repose**
    

They also find a peculiar black crystal.

A successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana)** check reveals that the crystal once contained spiritual energy similar to the Jars of Souls.

Unlike the jars, however...

**This crystal is empty.**

Someone used it.

---

# Ending the Adventure

When the characters leave the tomb, the whispers surrounding the clearing are gone if they released the trapped souls.

The forest feels alive again.

Birds return.

Wind moves through the trees.

Back in Haverton, the families of the missing adventurers finally learn what happened to their loved ones.

If the characters released their souls, the community considers them heroes.

But the discoveries within the tomb raise a much larger question.

Opendis spent decades gathering souls.

He constructed an entire laboratory.

He created magical vessels capable of stealing the dead from the afterlife.

And yet...

**Opendis isn't here.**

His ritual was never completed.

His most important research is missing.

And one of his soul-storage crystals has already been emptied.

Somewhere in the world, Opendis may still be alive.

And eventually, he will discover that someone has broken into his tomb.

---

# Scaling the Adventure

### 3 Characters

Remove one Animated Armor from one of the Area 3 encounters.

The Ritual Chamber contains **2 Specters only**.

### 4 Characters

Run the adventure as written.

### 5 Characters

Run the adventure as written, but give the Ritual Chamber skeleton **2 additional Skeleton allies**.

---

# Adventure Rewards

If the characters explore most of the dungeon, defeat the guardians, discover Opendis's research, and resolve the fate of the trapped souls, they have accomplished a significant adventure appropriate for 3rd level.

Recommended rewards:

**100 gp** from the Haverton scholar.

Approximately **450–700 gp worth of treasure** throughout the dungeon.

**2 Potions of Healing.**

**3 low-level spell scrolls.**

Most importantly, they discover **Opendis and the Great Working**, providing a ready-made villain and plot thread for later adventures.

## Future Hook: Opendis Returns

Don't have Opendis attack immediately.

Weeks or even months later, the characters discover the symbol of the **hand holding the screaming skull** somewhere else.

Perhaps on a corpse.

Perhaps in another abandoned laboratory.

Perhaps carried by someone asking questions about the adventurers who entered the tomb near Haverton.

Eventually they learn the truth:

**Opendis knows their names.**
