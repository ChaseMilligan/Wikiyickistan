# The Twilight Hunter

*A 5th-edition monster-hunt adventure for four characters*

> **Recommended levels:** 3, 8, or 13  
> **Primary location:** [[Grim Forest]]  
> **Nearby settlement:** [[Doobius House]]  
> **Recommended damage types:** Ranged attacks and fire damage

## Introduction

A new danger has entered the cursed [[Grim Forest]], terrorizing the nearby residents of [[Doobius House]]. The **Bloodhound**, a mechanical and shapechanging creature, stalks the mist-covered woods in search of blood.

The characters trek through the forest, following a trail deliberately left by the Bloodhound. When they reach his lair, they discover that the hunter has been using the attacks to lure adventurers into a trap. The hunters have become the prey.

The party must navigate the Bloodhound’s obstacles, survive his wild pack, and face the shapechanging beast before he harvests their blood for his experiments.

## Background

When monsters stalk the night, there will always be those who hunt them. Most hunters have a reason: revenge, protection, or gold. **Roul Hasdrubal** hunted for the pleasure of the chase and the eventual kill.

Roul was a fearless hunter whose prowess was overshadowed only by his reputation for brutality. As age weakened him, he rebuilt his limbs with mechanical replacements and replaced failing organs with those taken from powerful beasts. He then harvested the life-prolonging blood of shapechangers, inadvertently changing his own physiology.

The process left Roul mentally unstable and physically malformed. Now known as the Bloodhound, he has become the kind of monster he once hunted. Those he once protected are now prey in his endless quest for fresh blood and improved concoctions.

## Motes of the Divine

This hunt can serve as the first of five adventures collectively known as **Motes of the Divine**.

A mysterious benefactor found Roul as he bitterly aged and offered to help him. Through experimentation and infusion with an energy called a **mote of the divine**, Roul transformed into the creature he is today. Disgusted by the result, the stranger called him a “failed experiment” and vanished.

Roul now seeks to finish the experiments and repair his body. His journal can reveal that the mysterious benefactor called himself **Creo** and may have experimented on others using the same power.

## Adventure Summary

- **Monster:** The Bloodhound, a former monster hunter who uses blood to morph and empower his mechanical body.
- **Motivation:** Become the ultimate predator by gaining the powers of the creatures whose blood he consumes.
- **Previous actions:** Local residents and hunters have been mauled and left as bait to lure adventurers into the forest.
- **Location:** The Bloodhound stalks [[Grim Forest]] and prepares traps in his lair.

## Adventure Hooks

### Curiosity: The Twilight Curse

Rumors from local taverns are becoming impossible to dismiss. Something strange is happening in [[Grim Forest]], already avoided for its dark reputation. Residents and travelers have gone missing or been found ravaged.

Perhaps the old necromancer has returned to reclaim the cursed wood. Perhaps a new pack of creatures has made the pale forest its home.

### Morality: The Missing Hunters

**[[Barnabas Huggins]]**, owner of the [[Hotbox Inn & Tavern]] in [[Doobius House]], asks for help. Two regular hunters disappeared while traveling through the forest, and a third hunter sent after them has also failed to return. Barnabas has heard enough stories from travelers to believe something far worse than ordinary wolves is hunting in the woods.

### Compensation: A White Whale

**[[Dr. Stein]]** believes the attacks may be evidence of an unusually advanced biomantic creature. He offers additional compensation for a living specimen, useful observations, or recoverable components, provided the party does not let his research interests put them in greater danger.

## Mariam Shelley

Mariam Shelley is a lawful good halfling commoner and the owner of the Spark & Flame, the best—and only—tavern in Coldmore.

She is normally bold, energetic, and relentlessly cheerful, but worry for her missing husband and daughter has left her withdrawn and tense.

**Personality:** Bold and energetic; currently worried about her family.  
**Appearance:** Rosy cheeks, thick blond curls, and strong forearms.  
**Desire:** Retrieve her family and restore joy to Coldmore.  
**Fear:** The dissolution of her community.

> “What’ll it be? Grandma’s Old Peculiar? Liverwort ale? How about nightshade for those bloody beasts out there.”

Mariam willingly shares the following information:

- The creatures attack travelers in the forest but have not been seen near Coldmore.
- Hunters have found villagers, travelers, and animals savagely mauled.
- Some locals believe a pack of dire wolves is responsible.
- Franklin and Claire have been missing for two days.
- An experienced hunter sent after them never returned.
- A necromancer once ruled the woods and cursed the region.

# Evenfall Forest

The [[Evenfall Forest]] lives up to its name: the sun never truly rises or sets over the gloaming woodland. Fog and mist blanket the forest floor, tracking is difficult, and getting lost is easy.

The Bloodhound has left a deliberate trail leading directly to the ambush in his lair. He is a master hunter and trapmaker who enjoys torturing prey and leaving macabre scenes as both trail and warning.

### Tracking the Bloodhound

The characters need **four Tracking checks**, with at least one success, to find the Bloodhound’s lair. Each of the first three checks brings the party to one of the clues in **Know Thy Enemy**.

Use forest random encounters from *Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting* to populate hostile encounters as needed.

### The Twilight Curse

The forest lies near a thinning in the veil between the Material Plane and the Plane of Shadow. Long ago, a necromancer tapped into that plane and cursed the region with perpetual twilight.

The curse continues to drain colour from the plants and water. The woods are quiet, the trees pale-barked, and the undergrowth nearly monochromatic. The Bloodhound’s presence further corrupts the region.

At the southeastern edge of the forest lies the secluded village of [[Coldmore]]. The region exports pale forest timber and prized Evenfall truffles.

## Unique Flora and Fauna

### Silver-Tailed Cheshire Fox

These foxes have ebony fur and long twin tails ending in razor-sharp silver tips. They hunt in packs and use stealth to ambush intruders.

Use jackal statistics with these changes:

- The fox has a Multiattack action and makes two Tail attacks.
- **Tail:** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 1 piercing damage. The target must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw, taking 4 (1d8) poison damage on a failure, or half damage on a success.

### Twilight Wasp

Twilight wasps have purple-black crystalline carapaces and glittering, star-filled wings. Their venomous stings leave the surrounding flesh black and painfully itchy for 3d8 hours.

Their carapaces can be crushed into a powder used by healers as a coagulant.

### Greyshade Strangler

These thorn-covered vines have grey leaves tipped with red. They hide in undergrowth and grab creatures with pulses, feeding on spilled blood.

A leaf can be harvested with a component DC of 15 and used as an ingredient for a potion of poison resistance.

# Know Thy Enemy

While following the trail, the party encounters a savaged dire wolf, Franklin and Claire sheltering together, and the burned corpse of the hunter Isla. The order of these clues is up to the DM.

The clues reveal that the Bloodhound:

- Is a Construct using natural and metal weaponry.
- Can travel through shadows.
- Has an aversion to fire.

## Clue 1: More Than a Beast

A mangled dire wolf hangs from vines tied to a tree. Its wounds include ragged teeth marks, precise slashes, and punctures made by metal blades.

A character proficient in Nature or who succeeds on a DC 13 Wisdom (Nature) check recognizes the vines as tanglevine, a plant used in snares.

A successful Wisdom (Survival) check identifies multiple types of fang marks, as if a single mouth had been constructed from various animal teeth. The marks and medicinal smell suggest a Construct rather than a natural beast.

## Clue 2: Shadow Hunter

Franklin Shelley is the sole survivor of a hunting party attacked by the Bloodhound. His daughter Claire found him, made a campfire, and has been treating his injuries.

Magical healing or a successful DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check using a healer’s kit allows them to return safely to Coldmore.

Franklin and Claire can explain that:

- The Beast attacked from the shadows in a blur of fur, fangs, and claws.
- It walked on two legs and wore a flowing cloak.
- It remained at the edge of their campfire and never became clearly visible in the light.
- It seemed to move from one location to another in an instant.

## Clue 3: Fear of Fire

Smoke leads to a charred clearing where the corpse of **Isla**, a Coldmore hunter, lies beside a broken bow.

Isla was ambushed by the Bloodhound. She swung her oil lantern, spilling oil around herself and creating a ring of fire. The flames kept the Bloodhound away, but Isla eventually died from smoke inhalation.

A DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check finds the overturned lantern and reveals what happened. A DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check confirms that Isla died from smoke inhalation rather than wounds.

# Crimson Hollow

The Bloodhound’s lair lies within the ruins of the necromancer’s former stronghold, deep in Evenfall Forest. The ruins stand on a nexus where the veil between the Material Plane and the Plane of Shadow is thinnest.

The Bloodhound uses the cursed waterways to create his shapechanging potions.

## Entering Crimson Hollow

The characters arrive at the southwestern edge of a large field split by a stream. A bloody trail crosses a stone bridge, passes crumbling walls and ancient columns, climbs a flight of stairs, and ends at a cave beside a scum-covered pond.

The Bloodhound shifts from shadow to shadow using Shadow Jump and taunts the characters from different directions. He has lured them into his lair and wants them to struggle.

### Hazard: Cursed Waters

A stream runs from a 5-foot-deep pool at the northern end of Crimson Hollow. When a creature enters the water for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on the hunt’s Constitution saving throw or take the hunt’s necrotic damage.

### Ruins

The 20-foot-high ruins limit sight and provide high ground for the Bloodhound. He uses Shadow Jump to reach them and escape melee combat.

### Helping Hand

Coldmore’s hunters do not join the battle directly, but one can assist a character with a potion of healing or free a character caught in a trap before retreating into the forest.

If the party rescued Franklin, Claire is the hunter who lends this assistance.

# The Battle Ahead

The battle has three waves. Each wave introduces new challenges and gives the party opportunities to use the clues discovered in the forest.

## Wave 1: The Trapper

The Bloodhound has hidden bear traps, punji traps, and tanglevines throughout the approach.

### Detecting Traps

A creature that is not blinded can spot traps within 10 feet by using an action and succeeding on an Intelligence (Investigation) check. A creature with a passive Wisdom (Perception) score at least 5 higher than the trap DC detects them automatically.

Each trap occupies a 5-foot square. A Small or larger creature triggers a trap when it enters the area for the first time on a turn.

### Trap Table

| d6 | Trap |
|---:|---|
| 1–2 | Bear trap |
| 3–4 | Punji trap |
| 5–6 | Tanglevine |

**Bear Trap.** The target makes a Dexterity save or takes piercing damage. On a failure, its speed becomes 0 unless it is immune to being grappled. A creature can use an action to free itself or another creature with a successful Strength (Athletics) check.

**Punji Trap.** The target makes a Dexterity save or takes piercing and poison damage, has its speed reduced to 0 until the end of the turn, and must succeed on a Constitution save or become poisoned for 1 hour.

**Tanglevine.** Thorny vines create an effect identical to *entangle* in a 20-foot-radius circle for 1 minute.

### Wave 1 Tactics

The Bloodhound uses Thorn Wall to funnel characters toward traps, taunts them, and teleports between shadows. He uses his Nailbow from cover and uses Darkness if cornered.

Wave 1 ends when one of the following occurs:

- Five traps are triggered.
- The characters reach the northern third of the field.
- The Bloodhound is reduced to half his hit points or fewer.
- The characters illuminate at least half the location with bright light.

## Wave 2: The Hunter

The Bloodhound drinks a shapechanging potion, assumes a hybrid werewolf-like form, and howls to summon his wild pack.

### Level 3 Enemies

- 1 Emaciated Bloodhound
- 3 swarms of ravens
- 2 wolves

### Level 8 Enemies

- 1 Bloodhound
- 4 dire wolves
- 2 swarms of ravens
- 2 wolves

### Level 13 Enemies

- 1 Sanguine Bloodhound
- 4 poison wolves
- 2 swarms of dire ravens

The pack flees only if the Bloodhound is slain. At higher levels, he focuses the pack on the target of his Sanguine Mark.

Wave 2 ends when the Bloodhound is reduced to 0 hit points and Unleash the Beast activates.

## Wave 3: The Beast

The Bloodhound crushes his remaining potions against his chest and transforms into a raven-like monstrosity with jagged wings of bone and steel. His canine companion, **Snarl**, emerges from the northern cave.

### Level 3 Enemies

- 1 Emaciated Bloodhound in mythic state
- 1 dire wolf

### Level 8 Enemies

- 1 Bloodhound in mythic state
- 1 poison wolf

### Level 13 Enemies

- 1 Sanguine Bloodhound in mythic state
- 1 blood wolf

The Bloodhound uses Pack Howl, Razor Gust, and Shadow Jump aggressively. Wave 3 ends when he is reduced to 0 hit points. Remaining pack members retreat.

### Optional Ending: Pack Attack

For a one-shot climax, on initiative count 0 after the Bloodhound dies, roll a d4. That many beasts from the Wave 2 encounter pour from the forest and attack. More beasts stop emerging after 1 minute, forcing the party to flee or find safety.

# Aftermath

The people of Coldmore are grateful. Mariam offers the party free room and board for a week. Franklin recovers with only a slight limp, and Claire begins following the characters to learn from them.

Old Man Salty is disappointed that the creature was not Snowfang, but pays part of his promised reward and offers other leads about the legendary beast.

The Evenfall Forest is cleansed of the Bloodhound’s influence, but its ancient twilight curse remains. The ruins may contain clues to a possible cure.

If using **Motes of the Divine**, Roul’s journal describes Creo, the mote of the divine, and the possibility that other creatures were subjected to the same experiments.

# Appendix A: Magic Items

## Bloodhound’s Caress

*Weapon (claw), uncommon; rare or very rare variants available*

This claw has 4 charges and regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn.

**Arterial Slash.** When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can expend 1 charge to force a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target bleeds for 1 minute, taking 1d6 slashing damage at the end of each turn and regaining only half as many hit points from healing. A creature without blood automatically succeeds. The effect ends when a creature uses an action to succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine) check.

Rare and very rare variants increase the charges, save DC, and damage.

## Bloodhound’s Immobiliser

*Weapon (firearm), uncommon; rare or very rare variants available*

This firearm has 4 charges and regains 1d4 charges daily at dawn.

**Dead Stop.** Once per turn, expend 1 charge when attacking to fire an enlarged nail. On a hit, the target takes an extra 1d6 piercing damage and a Large or smaller creature must succeed on a DC 13 Strength save or be pinned against an adjacent surface. A creature within 5 feet can use an action to remove the nail with a successful DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check.

Rare and very rare variants increase the charges, damage, and save DC.

## Bloodhound’s Widebrim

*Wondrous item, uncommon, requires attunement*

The hat has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

**Hunter’s Spirit.** As an action, expend 1 charge to summon an invisible-or-visible spectral bloodhound or falcon for 1 hour. The spirit cannot interact physically. The bloodhound grants advantage on smell-based Perception checks and can track a scent up to 1 mile away. The falcon grants advantage on sight-based Perception checks and allows clear vision up to 3 miles away where unobstructed.

Rare and very rare variants include the Sic ’Em property and improve the range, attack, damage, and save DC.

## Cranium Rat Cowl

*Wondrous item, rare, requires attunement*

**Illumination.** As a bonus action, the wearer creates or extinguishes bright light in a radius of up to 20 feet and dim light for an equal additional distance.

**Psionics.** The wearer can cast *command*, *comprehend languages*, and *detect thoughts* using a save DC of 15.

# Appendix B: Spells

## Blood Curse

*5th-level biomancy or divination*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, M (a drop of the target creature’s blood, consumed)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 8 hours  
**Classes:** Cleric, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

You create a trail leading to the creature from which the blood originated. The accuracy depends on how long ago the blood was exuded. A mishap ends the spell and triggers Malediction.

**Malediction.** While within 90 feet of and able to see the target, you can use an action to force a Constitution save. The target takes 8d8 necrotic damage and becomes poisoned for 1 minute on a failure, or takes half damage without becoming poisoned on a success.

## Detect Heartbeat

*2nd-level divination*

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Self, 30 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (small hearing horn)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You automatically succeed on Wisdom (Perception) checks to hear heartbeats within 30 feet. The sense is blocked by 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of lead, or 3 feet of wood or dirt. You know the direction of each heartbeat but not its distance.

While the spell lasts, you have disadvantage on saves against deafened and vulnerability to thunder damage.

## Exsanguinate

*3rd-level biomancy or conjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a vial of venom)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute  
**Classes:** Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock

The target must save against the spell. The first time it takes damage on a turn, it must save again or begin bleeding for 1 minute. A bleeding creature takes 4d6 necrotic damage at the start of each turn and can repeat the save at the end of each turn.

# Appendix C: Bloodhound Reference

Bloodhounds are creatures born of biomancy and artifice. Their bodies are sculpted from biological components and mechanical replacements. As they progress, their behaviour and thought patterns become increasingly like those of their chosen animal inspirations.

## Regional Effects

Within 6 miles of the lair, shadows become darker and a creature’s darkvision range is halved. Within 1 mile, animals become aggressive and difficult to handle. Humanoids feel watched and hunted.

These effects fade over 1d10 days after the Bloodhound dies.

## Lair Actions

On initiative count 20, losing ties, the Bloodhound takes one lair action. He cannot use the same effect two rounds in a row.

- **Darkness:** Create a 20-foot-radius sphere of magical darkness until the next initiative count 20.
- **Thorn Wall:** Create tangled, thorny vines as *wall of thorns* for 1 hour.
- **Pack Howl:** During Waves 2 and 3, summon one member of the wild pack.

| 3rd | 12 | 3 (1d6) | 2nd-level wolf |
| 8th | 14 | 7 (2d6) | 4th-level dire wolf |
| 13th | 16 | 14 (4d6) | 6th-level poison wolf |

## Emaciated Bloodhound

*Medium Construct, chaotic evil*

**AC** 14  
**HP** 52 (8d8 + 16)  
**Speed** 40 ft.; fly 40 ft. in mythic state  
**STR** 12 (+1), **DEX** 13 (+1), **CON** 14 (+2), **INT** 10 (+0), **WIS** 14 (+2), **CHA** 10 (+0)  
**Saves** Dex +3, Wis +4  
**Skills** Acrobatics +3, Perception +4, Stealth +5, Survival +4  
**Resistances** Nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing  
**Immunities** Poison; charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned  
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 14  
**Languages** Common, Sylvan, Undercommon  
**CR** 2 (450 XP)

**Aversion of Fire.** After taking fire damage, the Bloodhound has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of his next turn.

**Immutable Form.** The Bloodhound is immune to effects that alter his form.

**Keen Hearing and Smell.** Advantage on Perception checks relying on hearing or smell.

**Legendary Resistance (1/Day).** If the Bloodhound fails a save, he can succeed instead.

**Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attacks against a creature adjacent to one of the Bloodhound’s allies.

**Unleash the Beast.** When reduced to 0 hit points, the Bloodhound instead resets to 52 hit points, regains Legendary Resistance, gains a fly speed of 40 feet for 1 hour, and gains Mythic Actions. Defeating him after this trait activates awards an additional 700 XP.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The Bloodhound makes one Bite and one Claws attack. He can replace one attack with Nailbow.

**Bite.** +3 to hit, reach 5 ft.; 6 (1d10 + 1) piercing damage.

**Claws.** +3 to hit, reach 5 ft.; 6 (2d4 + 1) slashing damage.

**Nailbow.** +3 to hit, range 100/400 ft.; 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

### Legendary Actions

The Bloodhound has 3 legendary actions and regains them at the start of his turn.

- **Attack:** Make one Claws or Nailbow attack.
- **Shadow Jump:** In dim light or darkness, teleport up to 30 feet to another visible area of dim light or darkness and Hide.

### Mythic Actions

- **Shadow Pounce:** Use Shadow Jump, then make one Bite or Claws attack.
- **Razor Gust (Costs 2 Actions):** Creatures in a 30-foot cone make a DC 11 Dexterity save, taking 10 (3d6) slashing damage on a failure or half on a success. The Bloodhound can then fly half his speed.

## Bloodhound and Sanguine Bloodhound

Use the same abilities and actions as the Emaciated Bloodhound, adjusting the statistics for the hunt level.

## Wild Pack Reference

Use the appropriate wolf, dire wolf, raven swarm, poison wolf, swarm of dire ravens, and blood wolf stat blocks for the chosen hunt level. Remaining pack members flee when the Bloodhound dies.
