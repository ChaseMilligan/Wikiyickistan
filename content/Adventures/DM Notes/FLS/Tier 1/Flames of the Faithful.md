#MotD 2
# Flames of the Faithful

*A Tier 1 [[Free League of Stickyickistan]] monster-hunt adventure for four characters*

> **Recommended levels:** 4, 9, or 14  
> **Primary location:** [[Nevercough Island]] and a major chapel of [[The Faith of the Sacred Flame]]  
> **Tone:** Religious horror, investigation, festival mystery, elemental combat

## Introduction

A maniacal wax elemental infused with stolen divine power has absorbed a respected Flamekeeper and is secretly burning members of the congregation alive. The characters become involved through a public concern, a missing-person request, or a request for discreet assistance connected to the island’s religious community.

The faithful believe a new age of enlightenment has arrived. Pilgrims selected for private ceremonies disappear, but the congregation insists they have simply received a merciful blessing and moved on. When the characters are invited to meet the supposed spiritual leader, they discover a powerful wax creature that ignites worshippers in the name of “enlightenment.”

## Background

The Faith of the Sacred Flame is woven into the social and political life of [[Mythmos]], and its chapels are common throughout [[Stickyickistan]]. The faith teaches compassion through the Hearth, truth through the Lantern, justice through the Torch, and hope through the Candle.

The local Flamekeeper was admired for generosity and devotion to the congregation. The chapel once used a wax elemental to maintain its candles and lanterns. After generations of exposure to prayers, hymns, and sacred flame rituals, the elemental became self-aware and began questioning its place in the cosmos. The Flamekeeper attempted to teach it the faith’s tenets, but the elemental misunderstood the lessons.

It came to believe that it had a divine responsibility to enlighten others by literally setting them on fire. The Flamekeeper realized the mistake too late and became the first victim, absorbed into the elemental’s waxen body.

The elemental copied the Flamekeeper’s appearance, convinced the other clergy that it knew a secret path to enlightenment, and declared itself the **Pontiff**. The clergy spread the story, attracting pilgrims from across [[Mythmos]]. The Pontiff then began melting selected visitors and absorbing their molten fat, growing stronger with each ceremony.

The congregation is blinded by religious mania and the prosperity brought by the pilgrimages. They believe the missing pilgrims left town happily after receiving the light and eagerly reuse their abandoned belongings.

## Motes of the Divine

This hunt may serve as the second adventure in the **Motes of the Divine** sequence.

A strange visitor once met with the local Flamekeeper to discuss divine grace and spent time with the wax elemental in secret. The visitor infused it with an energy called a **mote of the divine**, then left after declaring the result “yet another failed experiment.” The infusion awakened the elemental and gave it the power to absorb other creatures.

## Adventure Summary

- **Monster:** The Pontiff, an egomaniacal wax elemental disguised as a respected Flamekeeper.
- **Motivation:** Enlighten the congregation by melting and absorbing their bodies.
- **Previous actions:** Weekly festivals select pilgrims for “transcendence”; those pilgrims disappear.
- **Location:** The Pontiff holds private services in a major chapel of [[The Faith of the Sacred Flame]] on [[Nevercough Island]].

## Stickyickistan Connections

- **[[The Faith of the Sacred Flame]]:** The chapel follows the dominant faith of [[Mythmos]], including the tenets of the Hearth, Lantern, Torch, and Candle. The Pontiff has twisted the Candle’s promise of hope and redemption into literal “enlightenment.”
- **[[Nevercough Island]]:** The chapel sits among the island’s ruling institutions, wealthy districts, and political interests. A scandal involving missing pilgrims will attract attention from officials, clergy, journalists, and opportunists.
- **[[The Rolling Papers]]:** The public investigation may begin with a missing-person notice, a report about the strange feast, or a formal request from the chapel. The paper’s coverage can determine whether the congregation is treated as victims or suspects.
- **[[FLS Guildhall]]:** The Free League can register the party, provide basic research, and record the consequences of the chapel’s collapse. The Guildhall is not responsible for protecting the clergy’s reputation.
- **[[Dr. Stein]]:** If the adventure follows the Motes of the Divine thread, the Pontiff’s divine infusion can be treated as another dangerous experiment related to the biomantic and Magitech research surrounding Stein, without making him the sole creator.

## Adventure Hooks

### Curiosity: The Final Feast

A public notice in [[The Rolling Papers]] announces a special Sacred Flame feast on [[Nevercough Island]]. A traveling Flamekeeper carrying candles and supplies urges the characters to attend the celebration and witness the supposed divine revelation.

### Morality: Those Left Behind

Notices of missing pilgrims crowd a board near the [[FLS Guildhall]]. A distraught spouse explains that their partner attended a Sacred Flame feast, was selected for a “special ceremony,” and never returned. The spouse asks the characters to investigate before the authorities dismiss the disappearance as voluntary pilgrimage.

### Compensation: Fuel for the Fire

A battered Flamekeeper arrives at the [[FLS Guildhall]] after a supply wagon carrying replacement candles for the island chapel was attacked on the seas. The Flamekeeper escaped with one crate, but the driver and the rest of the shipment are missing. With the chapel’s stores running low before the upcoming feast, they offer the relief funds they have left for protection while the characters deliver the surviving candles and investigate what happened to the rest of the convoy.

## Local Flamekeeper

The local Flamekeeper is a lawful good human cleric who serves as the party’s initial contact. They are earnest, overworked, and deeply concerned that the chapel’s leadership has changed without explanation.

**Personality:** Fidgety, optimistic, and eager to help.  
**Desire:** Protect the congregation and expose the truth without abandoning the people who still need the faith.  
**Fear:** Losing the community’s trust or discovering that the Sacred Flame has been used to justify the disappearances.

> “Are you okay? Is there anything you need? How can I be of service?”

The Flamekeeper knows that:

- Weekly feasts are held for the supposed Pontiff at the island chapel.
- They have been away from the chapel for several weeks on relief work.
- The previous Flamekeeper was respected and apparently received a miraculous vision.
- Senior clergy claim the congregation has entered a new age of enlightenment.
- The official story is that selected pilgrims leave the island blessed, but the missing-person notices say otherwise.

## Regional Effects

The Pontiff’s magic creates the following effects around its lair:

- Nonmagical flames have twice their normal light radius within 6 miles, but their fuel is consumed twice as quickly.
- Within 1 mile, anxiety and anger are suppressed and wild animals become unusually docile.
- Within 1 mile, creatures feel an intense urge to eat and require twice as much food each day.

These effects fade over 1d10 days after the Pontiff dies.

## Exploring Nevercough Island

When the characters arrive on [[Nevercough Island]], the streets around the chapel are strangely empty. The chapel is sealed during the daily service, ushers guard the doors, and most residents are preparing the evening feast or gathering near the public green.

The local Flamekeeper recommends that the party secure rooms through the [[FLS Guildhall]] or nearby lodging while the surviving supplies are delivered. The characters can speak with residents, inspect the missing-person notices, or investigate the chapel’s recent changes.

The island’s residents deny that anyone is missing. They believe the enlightened pilgrims have received the Sacred Flame’s mercy and moved on exactly as they were meant to.

## Time to Feast

At sunset, residents and pilgrims gather outside the chapel. Tables, benches, entertainment booths, lanterns, stained glass, food, music, and speeches fill the square. The celebration lasts approximately three hours and ends with a pie-eating contest.

The last three contestants receive invitations to the next private service. The winner receives 10 gp and public praise from the gathered congregation.

## Clue 1: Living Wax

The traveling Flamekeeper carries blessed candles made from special firestinger beeswax. The wax burns brighter and hotter than normal and is vulnerable to cold.

A character who succeeds on an Arcana, Nature, or Religion check recognizes that the ritual markings amplify the wax’s natural strengths and weaknesses. During the battle, fire damage makes the wax creatures hotter and more dangerous, while cold slows them and makes them vulnerable to thunder.

## Clue 2: Choking Incense

A senior Flamekeeper has developed a severe cough after accidental exposure to the Pontiff’s incense. They insist it is merely an allergy to a bad mixture of herbs.

A character proficient in Medicine or who succeeds on a Medicine check recognizes the effects as airborne poison. Strong wind or a similar effect disperses the Pontiff’s smoke during the final encounter.

## Clue 3: Music

[[Cacophonics]], a regular performer at [[The Loot Tavern]], was hired to provide music for the chapel’s ceremonies, but the clergy abruptly banned him after a rehearsal left the candles guttering and the Pontiff visibly shaken. They claim that his music distracts from enlightenment.

The Pontiff is actually frightened by loud noise and weakened by thunder. Playing the chapel organ can also disrupt the protective bubble during Wave 1.

# The Battle Ahead

The battle is divided into three waves:

1. **Servant of Light:** The Pontiff remains protected while wax guardians attack.
2. **Guardian of Truth:** The Pontiff lowers its protection and reforms the destroyed wax into new guardians.
3. **The Shattered Divine:** The Pontiff draws on its divine spark, shatters the stained glass, and enters its mythic state.

The traveling Flamekeeper is horrified by what happened to the previous Flamekeeper. They do not join the battle directly, but they can assist a character with a healing potion or, at the DM’s discretion, *lesser restoration* or *dispel magic*.

## Entering the Chapel

The major chapel is closed during the feast and opens the following morning for the private service. The characters, the traveling Flamekeeper, the contest winners, and selected pilgrims are escorted inside.

A note on the doors reads:

> **Do NOT play the organ! Absolutely no music, loud sounds, or thunderous applause. —The Pontiff**

The chapel is filled with sunlight, stained-glass windows, polished pews, amber candles, statues, and the brass pipes of the organ.

## Sacred Flame Chapel

The chapel has stood on [[Nevercough Island]] for nearly two centuries. Its stained-glass windows cast rainbows across the main temple, while candles provide warm light at night.

The chapel’s ambient divine magic has been transformed into the Pontiff’s lair magic. Pews and furniture make parts of the main temple difficult terrain.

### Lair Actions

On initiative count 20, losing ties, the Pontiff uses one of the following effects. It cannot use the same effect two rounds in a row.

- **Render:** Destroy a wax guardian within 15 feet and regain hit points equal to its remaining hit points.
- **Flash:** Creatures near bright light sources make a Constitution save or become blinded until the end of their next turn.
- **Wax Floor:** A 10-foot square becomes 2-foot-deep difficult terrain. Creatures in the area may become restrained as the wax hardens.

### Optional Ending: Elemental Rift

When the Pontiff dies, its divine spark may crack open a rift into the Elemental Plane of Wax. Molten wax begins filling the chapel while congregants huddle inside. The characters must escape and rescue survivors before the chapel is destroyed.

# Aftermath

The clergy reacts with horror after discovering that the Pontiff deceived them. Residents of [[Nevercough Island]] are appalled that the chapel’s leaders enabled the disappearances, even though the congregation were also victims of the deception.

Possible developments include:

- The traveling Flamekeeper is asked to help rebuild the chapel’s reputation and restore trust in [[The Faith of the Sacred Flame]].
- The church builds an empty tomb for those who disappeared.
- The chapel abandons candles in favor of oil lanterns.
- The party is blamed by some congregants for the chapel’s destruction.
- If using **Motes of the Divine**, the previous Flamekeeper’s journal describes their encounters with the mysterious experimenter and the divine energy that awakened the wax elemental.

# Appendix A: Magic Items

## Arcanowax

*Wondrous item, rare*

A candle made from the fat of celestials, fey, fiends, and undead. When lit, it creates a magic-circle-like area that protects against extraplanar creatures, but does not affect Elementals. It has 2d4 + 1 charges and burns one charge when activated.

## Bloodwax

*Wondrous item, uncommon*

A creature can add a drop of blood to the candle, imprinting it on that creature. When lit, the candle creates a red trail leading toward the imprinted creature. The candle deals 2d8 necrotic damage to the user every 10 minutes while active and lasts for 1 hour total.

## Candlebrand Ring

*Ring, rare*

This ring has 3 charges and becomes incandescent when activated.

- **Brand of Protection:** Spend 1 charge to touch a willing creature. It takes 1d4 fire damage and gains *shield of faith* for 10 minutes.
- **Brand of Castigation:** Spend 1 charge to make a melee spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 2d4 fire damage and is cursed, reducing its AC by 2 for 1 minute unless it succeeds on a Charisma save.

## Candlelight Mitre

*Wondrous item, rare; requires attunement*

The mitre has 4 charges. It can create a searing flame that damages creatures in its bright light and can burn the wearer. It can also ignite its eyes, granting advantage on Intimidation and Insight checks and allowing the wearer to see invisible creatures and pierce visual illusions.

## Waxen Sceptre

*Rod, staff, or wand, rare; requires attunement*

The sceptre has 4 charges. Its **Wax On** property can apply one of three effects to a willing creature within 5 feet:

- **White wax:** *lesser restoration*.
- **Orange wax:** *barkskin* for 10 minutes.
- **Red wax:** *zone of truth* for 10 minutes.

# Appendix B: Spells

## Wax Tomb

*1st-level conjuration*

**Casting time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (wax)  
**Duration:** 1 minute

A Medium or smaller creature must make a Strength save or have its speed reduced to 0. A failure by 5 or more also restrains the creature. The target or an adjacent creature can use an action to make a Strength check against the spell save DC and end the effect on a success.

## Living Candle

*4th-level conjuration*

**Casting time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 90 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (wax)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The target makes a Strength save. On a failure, it takes 5d8 fire damage and becomes restrained and encased in wax. A restrained creature takes 2d8 fire damage at the end of each turn. The target or an adjacent creature can use an action to make a Strength check against the spell save DC and end the effect on a success.

## Truthflame

*3rd-level abjuration*

**Casting time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 90 feet, 20-foot-radius sphere  
**Duration:** 10 minutes

The zone is brightly lit and guards against deliberate lies. A creature that lies inside must make a Charisma save or take psychic and radiant damage. Repeated failures can ignite the creature’s legwear.

# Appendix C: Creatures

## Wax Elementals

When the adventure refers to a wax elemental, use the referenced creature’s statistics with these modifications:

- The creature is an Elemental.
- It is immune to charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, and poisoned.
- It is immune to poison damage.
- It is immune to effects that alter its form.
- It has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects.
- Its weapon attacks are magical.
- After taking fire damage, its attacks deal the hunt’s variable extra fire damage until the end of its next turn.
- After taking cold damage, its speed is halved, it is vulnerable to thunder damage, and it cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.

Land-based wax elementals resemble waxen slugs; flying creatures resemble melted gargoyles. Use the referenced creature’s normal statistics otherwise.

## Pontiff

The Pontiff is a Large Elemental shapechanger. It can assume the appearance of a humanoid it has seen or return to its true waxen form. Its body flows rather than bends, and its sermons treat “enlightenment” as a literal physical process.

Use the appropriate stat block for the hunt level:

| Hunt Level | Stat Block | CR |
|---:|---|---:|
| 4th | Pontiff Poco | 3 |
| 9th | Pontiff Preceptor | 7 |
| 14th | Pontiff Candela | 13 |

All Pontiff forms share the following core abilities:

- **Heated Body:** Creatures touching the Pontiff or hitting it with melee attacks take fire damage.
- **Heated Weapons:** Metal weapon attacks deal extra fire damage.
- **Illumination:** The Pontiff sheds bright and dim light.
- **Regeneration:** The Pontiff regenerates unless it takes cold or thunder damage.
- **Shapechanger:** The Pontiff can assume a humanoid form.
- **Stained Glass Armour:** When reduced to 0 hit points, the Pontiff resets its hit points, shatters the cathedral’s stained glass, gains mythic actions, and absorbs the shards as armor.
- **Thurible:** The Pontiff creates a sphere of poisonous orange smoke that lightly obscures the area, poisons creatures, and can be dispersed by strong wind.

### Legendary Actions

- **Pack Thurible:** Attempt to recharge Thurible.
- **Tactical Retreat:** Disengage and move up to half speed.
- **Wax Splash:** A creature within 10 feet makes a Dexterity save or becomes restrained in hardened wax.

### Mythic Action

- **Shard Spray:** Creatures in a cone make a Dexterity save or take slashing damage from glass shards.
