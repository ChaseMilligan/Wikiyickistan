# The Last Batch

_A D&D 5e (2024) adventure for 4–5 4th-level characters_

The characters have already survived the supernatural disaster beneath [[Blacklung Quarry]] and the soul experiments in the tomb beyond [[Doobius House]]. Now, someone is trying to recover what those adventures left behind.

A sealed shipment has vanished from the road between [[Doobius House]] and [[Nevercough Island]] in the [[Grim Forest]]. Its manifest lists ordinary mining equipment, but the cargo is actually a set of empty soul vessels, alchemical glassware, and a handwritten account of the cult’s next objective. The shipment was meant to help rebuild the cult's operation after [[Sarbrindar]] was defeated in the quarry and Dopendis Gravebreath’s laboratory was exposed.

The characters must find the shipment, determine who is behind it, and stop the cult from beginning a new Soul Diesel distillation operation before the stolen equipment reaches its destination.

## Adventure Summary

1. The characters receive a public contract at the [[FLS Guildhall]], and any characters that were involved in the previous adventures have the option of taking a post from [[The Roach Clip]].
2. They investigate an abandoned wagon and follow the shipment’s trail.
3. They search a temporary cult waystation and recover the **Ash Ledger**.
4. They travel to a ruined pumping station beneath the forest.
5. They interrupt the cult’s attempt to restart the Soul Diesel process.
6. They decide what to do with the captured cultist, the surviving equipment, and the Ash Ledger.

## Adventure Rewards

**900 XP each** for completing the adventure: recovering the stolen shipment, stopping the cult from restarting the Soul Diesel process, and deciding what becomes of the Ash Ledger. This is a long Tier 1 adventure reward under [[Character Advancement]].

**175 gp each** from the [[Free League of Stickyickistan]] for recovering the shipment and returning with reliable evidence of the cult’s activity. This payment is divided among the characters who complete the contract.

Additional rewards:

- **100 gp total** from [[N.E.R.D]] if the party turns over the Ash Ledger, intact alchemical notes, or other useful research evidence.
- **50 gp total** for recovering salvageable alchemical glassware or mining equipment from the stolen shipment.
- Any mundane equipment recovered from defeated cultists or the waystation, subject to DM approval.
- A story award for uncovering the cult’s attempt to rebuild the Soul Diesel operation after [[Sarbrindar]] and [[Dopendis Gravebreath]] were defeated or exposed.

Optional bonus XP:

- **+50 XP each** for capturing a cultist alive for questioning.
- **+50 XP each** for preserving the Ash Ledger instead of destroying it.
- **+100 XP each** for preventing any new Soul Diesel distillation from being completed.

If the characters complete only part of the adventure, use the partial-XP guidelines in [[Character Advancement]].

# The Ashen Choir

_A D&D 5e (2024) adventure for 4–5 4th-level characters_

A wave of faint, overlapping voices begins drifting through the lower tunnels of [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]]. The sounds are not loud enough for most citizens to hear, but priests, gravediggers, Dusters, and anyone who has handled necromantic relics describe the same thing: a choir of the dead, singing from somewhere beneath the old mortuary drains.

The disturbance can follow any earlier Orcus adventure, but does not require them to happen in a fixed order. If [[Sarbrindar]] has already been defeated, the voices are drawn to residue left by the quarry disaster. If [[Dopendis Gravebreath]]’s laboratory has already been exposed, they are drawn to the jars and Soul Diesel evidence recovered from the tomb. If [[The Last Batch]] has already happened, the choir is responding to the Ash Ledger and the interrupted shipment. If none of those events have occurred yet, the disturbance is simply one of the cult’s hidden backup rites beginning to fail on its own.

The characters must descend into abandoned funerary tunnels beneath the [[Dungsweepers]]’ mortuary, find the source of the singing, and decide whether to silence, preserve, or study the dead voices before the choir becomes strong enough to wake corpses across the district.

## Adventure Summary

1. The characters receive a public contract from the [[FLS Guildhall]] after citizens report disturbing voices beneath [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]]. Characters with Duster ties or previous Orcus-cult experience may also receive a private warning through [[The Roach Clip]].
2. They interview mortuary workers, Dusters, grieving families, and frightened tunnel crews to learn where the voices are loudest.
3. They explore the old mortuary drains beneath the [[Dungsweepers]]’ offices, avoiding unstable tunnels, corpse-water hazards, and minor undead stirred by the choir.
4. They discover a hidden reliquary chamber where the Orcus cult stored funerary nameplates, ashes, teeth, and scraps of death records stolen from multiple sites.
5. They confront the **Ashen Cantor**, a failed cult medium whose body is dead but whose voice continues conducting the trapped echoes of the dead.
6. They decide what to do with the choir: release the voices, preserve them as evidence, let the Dusters guide them toward [[Heralds of Dust|True Death]], or allow [[N.E.R.D]] to study the phenomenon.

## Modular Series Notes

This adventure should work whether it is played before or after [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]], [[Jars of Souls]], or [[The Last Batch]]. Do not require the same characters to have played the earlier sessions.

Use whichever hook fits the table:

- **After The Tenebrous Stone-r:** the choir reacts to death-energy residue carried out of [[Blacklung Quarry]].
- **After Jars of Souls:** the choir reacts to black jars, Soul Diesel samples, or notes brought back from the tomb beyond [[Doobius House]].
- **After The Last Batch:** the choir reacts to the Ash Ledger, which contains partial names of the dead stolen for the cult’s next rite.
- **Before any of them:** the choir is a separate cult cache breaking down, foreshadowing the larger Orcus network without revealing its full purpose.

## Adventure Rewards

**900 XP each** for completing the adventure: locating the source of the choir, preventing a mass undead outbreak in the district, and resolving the fate of the trapped voices. This is a long Tier 1 adventure reward under [[Character Advancement]].

**175 gp each** from the [[Free League of Stickyickistan]] for investigating the disturbance and returning with reliable evidence. This payment is divided among the characters who complete the contract.

Additional rewards:

- **100 gp total** from the [[Dungsweepers]] if the party prevents damage to the mortuary tunnels or recovers stolen funerary records.
- **100 gp total** from [[N.E.R.D]] if the party preserves safe evidence of the choir phenomenon without leaving the dead trapped.
- Any mundane equipment, ritual tools, or salvage recovered from the cult reliquary, subject to DM approval.
- A story award for earning the trust of local Dusters, the [[Dungsweepers]], or families whose dead were disturbed by the cult.

Optional bonus XP:

- **+50 XP each** for identifying at least one stolen name and returning it to the correct family or temple.
- **+50 XP each** for preventing collateral damage in the mortuary or surrounding Undercity tunnels.
- **+100 XP each** for resolving the choir without destroying recoverable evidence or leaving any voices trapped.

If the characters complete only part of the adventure, use the partial-XP guidelines in [[Character Advancement]].
