# The Last Batch

_A D&D 5e (2024) adventure for 4–5 4th-level characters_

The characters have already survived the supernatural disaster beneath [[Blacklung Quarry]] and the soul experiments in the tomb beyond [[Doobius House]]. Now, someone is trying to recover what those adventures left behind.

A sealed shipment has vanished from the road between the forest and [[Nevercough Island]]. Its manifest lists ordinary mining equipment, but the cargo is actually a set of empty soul vessels, alchemical glassware, and a handwritten account of the cult’s next objective. The shipment was meant to help rebuild the Orcus operation after the quarry and Dopendis Gravebreath’s laboratory were exposed.

The characters must find the shipment, determine who is behind it, and stop the cult from beginning a new Soul Diesel distillation before the stolen equipment reaches its destination.

## Adventure Summary

1. The characters receive a contract at the [[FLS Guildhall]].
2. They investigate an abandoned wagon and follow the shipment’s trail.
3. They search a temporary cult waystation and recover the **Ash Ledger**.
4. They travel to a ruined pumping station beneath the forest.
5. They interrupt the cult’s attempt to restart the Soul Diesel process.
6. They decide what to do with the captured cultist, the surviving equipment, and the Ash Ledger.

## Adventure Hook

The characters begin at the [[FLS Guildhall]] on [[Nevercough Island]]. A notice on [[The Rolling Papers]] offers work for a party willing to travel to [[Doobius House]] and investigate a missing supply wagon.

> **MISSING WAGON: A Seeker of the Vein supply wagon disappeared on the forest road after leaving [[Doobius House]]. Find the wagon, recover any surviving cargo, and learn what happened to its crew. Payment: 500 gp each, plus bonuses for recovered evidence and rescued travelers.**


## Character Connections

Each character should have at least one reason to take the job:

- A former quarry worker wants to know who is rebuilding the operation.
- A survivor of the tomb wants to make sure the cult cannot repeat the experiments.
- A member of [[The Seekers of the Vein]] wants the stolen equipment recovered.
- A member of the [[Order of the Emerald Breath]] wants to prevent Soul Diesel from being produced again.
- A character connected to [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]] recognizes the route near [[Blacklung Quarry]].
- A character who knows [[Skarguther Dungleboof]] wants to recover evidence before it disappears into the Dusters’ unofficial archives.

## Part 1: The Missing Wagon

The wagon disappeared on an old road leading away from [[Doobius House]]. It should have reached the Guildhall two days ago.

### Read Aloud

> The road is empty except for a wagon lying on its side in a ditch. One wheel has shattered, the canvas cover has been sliced open, and crates are scattered across the mud. A dead horse lies in the weeds. There is no sign of the driver, but the forest around the road is unnaturally quiet.

The wagon belongs to [[The Seekers of the Vein]]. Its cargo included six empty glass vessels, silver wire, blood-preservation salts, Arcannabis processing tools, and three crates of ordinary mining supplies.

The wagon was attacked, not wrecked by accident. The attackers removed the unusual equipment and left most of the mundane cargo behind.

### Searching the Scene

A character who spends 10 minutes searching the wagon can discover the following clues. Each clue can be found with a successful **DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Wisdom (Survival)** check.

- Black wax stains on the broken cargo crates.
- A strip of cloth marked with a crude flaming, horned skull.
- Drag marks leading into the forest.
- A smear of blood mixed with dark plant resin.
- A boot print that matches the tread of a quarry worker’s boot.

A character who participated in [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]] recognizes the black wax and the Orcus symbol as signs of the cult encountered near [[Blacklung Quarry]]. A character who participated in [[Jars of Souls]] recognizes the silver wire and glass vessels as Soul Diesel equipment.

### Encounter: Roadside Retrieval Team

The attackers left behind a small group to destroy evidence and ambush anyone investigating the wagon.

Use:

- **4 Cultists**
- **2 Scouts**

The cultists attempt to burn the wagon and flee into the forest. The scouts use the trees for cover and retreat if half the group is defeated.

If captured, a cultist knows only that the cargo is being taken to **the old waterworks**. They have been promised food, shelter, and a place in the “new operation.”

## Part 2: The Forest Trail

The trail follows an old service path through dense forest. The party can reach the waystation in four hours if they follow the tracks successfully.

The trail contains three signs of the cult’s movement:

- A broken glass shard wrapped in black cloth.
- A tree marked with a smear of Soul Diesel.
- A dead courier with a sealed message sewn into their coat.

The message reads:

> **The quarry is silent. The jars are compromised. The forest laboratory is lost. The last batch must be completed at the waterworks before the Dusters find the ledger.**

This confirms that the cult knows about both earlier disasters. It also establishes that the Ash Ledger is valuable enough for the cult to kill to protect it.

### Optional Travel Complication

If the party fails two or more group checks while following the trail, they encounter the cult’s hunting animals.

Use **2 Dire Wolves**. They are not trying to kill the characters. They harass the party, attempt to separate one character, and flee when injured.

A successful **DC 13 Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check reveals that the wolves have been exposed to Soul Diesel. Their eyes glow faintly, and their attacks leave a brief dark smear on the target.

## Part 3: The Waystation

The cult uses an abandoned ranger station as a temporary waystation. The stolen equipment is stored here before being taken to the waterworks.

### Read Aloud

> The ranger station leans beneath the weight of years and bad weather. Its windows are boarded, but orange light flickers through the gaps. The smell of hot metal, wet earth, and strong Arcannabis hangs in the air. Somewhere inside, glass clinks against glass while someone hums badly along with a song that has no melody.

The waystation contains three areas.

### The Front Room

The front room contains broken furniture, empty food tins, and a table covered in maps. The maps show [[Doobius House]], the road toward [[Blacklung Quarry]], and several unmarked paths through [[The Outback]].

A successful **DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation)** check finds a route marked **WATERWORKS**. The route leads to an old pumping station built around a natural spring.

### The Storage Room

The storage room contains the stolen cargo. Two of the six glass vessels are cracked, but four remain intact. The silver wire has been bundled into portable circles.

A character with Arcana or Nature proficiency recognizes that the equipment is designed to process Soul Diesel, not store ordinary Arcannabis.

The equipment is fragile. If the party transports it carelessly, one glass vessel breaks. The characters can prevent this with a successful **DC 12 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check or by spending 10 minutes packing the crates properly.

### The Back Room

The cult keeps prisoners here. The characters find **Marnie Sporegood**, a Seeker of the Vein courier, and **Tallow Wick**, a local hunter. Both are alive but exhausted.

Marnie explains that the cult is led by a person called **the Batchmaster**. The Batchmaster is not Dopendis Gravebreath. They are an apprentice or successor who has been trying to continue his work.

Tallow heard the cult arguing about the Ash Ledger. It contains the names of people who supplied blood, Arcannabis, and magical equipment to the Orcus network.

### Encounter: The Waystation Guards

Use:

- **1 Cult Fanatic**
- **4 Cultists**
- **1 Animated Armor**

The cult fanatic tries to destroy the maps and the ledger before fighting to the death. The animated armor protects the Soul Diesel equipment.

If the characters rescue the prisoners, award the optional rescue XP listed in the rewards section.

### The Ash Ledger

The Ash Ledger is a black leather notebook bound with wire. Its pages are written in three inks: red for names, black for payments, and green for Arcannabis shipments.

The ledger contains:

- Payments to a supplier near [[Blacklung Quarry]].
- A note describing the forest laboratory as “Dopendis’s abandoned refinement site.”
- A reference to the failed quarry operation.
- A list of surviving cult contacts.
- A final page describing the waterworks ritual.

The ledger does not identify the Orcus cult’s ultimate leader. It does identify enough suppliers and messengers to prove that the quarry operation and the Soul Diesel laboratory belonged to the same broader network.

A character who studies the ledger for 10 minutes can identify a name connected to their past adventures. Choose one:

- A quarry worker who escaped the events of [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]].
- A cult courier who visited the tomb in [[Jars of Souls]].
- A supplier who sold equipment through an otherwise legitimate business.

## Part 4: The Old Waterworks

The old waterworks lies three hours farther into the forest. It was built to pump spring water toward an abandoned settlement, but the pipes now carry dark liquid instead.

### Read Aloud

> The waterworks rises from the trees like a rusted iron skeleton. Pipes climb its walls and disappear into the earth. Dark liquid drips from a broken valve, hissing when it touches the stones. Beneath the building, something beats in a slow rhythm, like a heart trying to remember how to work.

The waterworks has three important areas.

### The Intake Chamber

The intake chamber contains a deep pool of clear spring water. A thin film of Soul Diesel floats on its surface.

A creature that touches the liquid hears a dozen voices say, “Please do not put that in the water.”

A character can shut the intake valve with a successful **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** or **Intelligence (Investigation)** check. Doing so prevents the ritual from contaminating the spring.

### The Pump Room

The pump room contains the stolen silver-wire circles and four empty vessels. The cult has already begun transferring Soul Diesel into the machinery.

A character who examines the machinery can disable one part of it with a successful **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana)** check. Three successful checks disable the ritual before the final encounter. A failed check causes the machinery to spray Soul Diesel in a 10-foot area. Creatures in the area must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** or be Poisoned until the end of their next turn.

### The Distillation Vault

The Batchmaster is completing the final distillation here. The chamber is filled with brass pipes, silver wire, black candles, and a large glass vessel containing a swirling black-green liquid.

### Encounter: The Batchmaster

Use:

- **The Batchmaster**, use the **Cult Fanatic** stat block with the following changes:
  - The Batchmaster has 45 hit points.
  - The Batchmaster can use a bonus action to command the machinery.
  - Once per round, the Batchmaster can cause one 10-foot square within the chamber to spray Soul Diesel. Creatures in that area make a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** or take **2d6 Poison damage** and become Poisoned until the end of their next turn.
- **2 Specters**
- **2 Animated Armors**

The Batchmaster wants to complete the batch, not simply kill the characters. At initiative count 20, if the machinery is still active, the Batchmaster can choose one effect:

- One specter regains 5 hit points.
- One animated armor gains 5 temporary hit points.
- A creature within 10 feet of the central vessel must succeed on a **DC 12 Wisdom saving throw** or hear the voices of every soul in the batch and become Frightened until the end of its next turn.

If the characters disabled three parts of the machinery, the Batchmaster cannot use this initiative effect.

### The Final Vessel

The final vessel contains a concentrated batch of Soul Diesel, but it is not stable. The characters have three options:

- **Destroy it.** The vessel breaks, releasing the trapped spiritual energy. Each creature within 15 feet must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **3d6 Psychic damage** on a failure, or half as much on a success. The spring remains clean.
- **Purify it.** A character can spend 10 minutes using holy water, a suitable cleansing spell, or a successful **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or Nature)** check. The Soul Diesel becomes inert and the trapped souls are released safely. The party earns the clean resolution bonus.
- **Take it.** The vessel can be transported, but it is unstable. Each hour, the carrier must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** or suffer a brief psychic episode. Skarguther will accept the vessel only if the characters can prove that the souls inside remain alive and trapped.

## Ending the Adventure

If the characters destroy or purify the final vessel, the waterworks stops shaking. The pipes run clear, and the voices fade from the spring.

If the characters purify the vessel, the freed souls appear as pale silhouettes in the steam. One of them points toward a map of the wider region and whispers:

> “There are more places like this.”

If the characters take the vessel, the cult’s surviving contacts begin hunting them. Skarguther pays for the vessel only after examining it at the Dungsweepers’ mortuary. He does not pay for a destroyed or safely purified batch, but he may offer a favor for proof that the souls were released.

The Ash Ledger can be given to [[The Seekers of the Vein]], the [[Order of the Emerald Breath]], [[Skarguther Dungleboof]], or the Free League. Each recipient reacts differently:

- The Seekers want to protect their reputation and investigate their suppliers.
- The Order wants to destroy every contaminated tool and cleanse the waterworks.
- Skarguther wants to preserve the evidence and identify the trapped souls.
- The Free League wants a public report and names of surviving cult agents.

## Adventure Rewards

**900 XP each** for completing the adventure. This is a long Tier 1 adventure for 4th-level characters under [[Character Advancement]].

Additional rewards:

- **500 gp total** from the public Guildhall contract, divided among the party.
- **200 gp total** from Skarguther for the Ash Ledger and proof of the cult’s network, divided among the party.
- **+50 XP each** for rescuing Marnie Sporegood and Tallow Wick.
- **+100 XP each** for purifying the final vessel and releasing the trapped souls safely.
- **+100 XP each** for preserving the spring and preventing contamination of the waterworks.
- A story award for identifying surviving members of the Orcus network.
- Access to future work from [[The Seekers of the Vein]], the [[Order of the Emerald Breath]], or [[The Roach Clip]].

If the characters complete only part of the adventure, use the partial-XP guidelines in [[Character Advancement]].

## Scaling the Adventure

### 3 Characters

Remove two Cultists from the roadside encounter. Remove one Specter from the final encounter.

### 4 Characters

Run the adventure as written.

### 5 Characters

Add two Cultists to the waystation encounter and give the Batchmaster 10 additional hit points.

## Future Hook: The Network Survives

The Ash Ledger proves that the quarry and forest laboratory were not isolated events. They were two operations within a wider Orcus network.

The ledger contains a list of three unconfirmed locations. One has been crossed out. One is marked **ACTIVE**. One is marked only with a drawing of a flaming horned skull.

The name beside the active location is missing.

Dopendis Gravebreath remains at large, and someone is still paying for Soul Diesel research.
