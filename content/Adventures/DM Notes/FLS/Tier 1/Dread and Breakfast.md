# Dread and Breakfast

*A Tier 1 Free League of Stickyickistan monster-hunt adventure for four to five 3rd-level characters*

## Introduction

**Dread and Breakfast** is a monster-hunt adventure set in the [[Grim Forest]], a dark and overgrown forest within [[The Outback]] of [[Stickyickistan]]. The Grim Forest surrounds the **Neck**, a heavily traveled trade route connecting [[North Booftown]] with inland settlements such as [[Doobius House]]. Travelers, caravans, and adventurers have begun disappearing along the route.

The characters must investigate the disappearances, identify the monster responsible, and survive an encounter with a tavern mimic that has made an entire roadside inn part of its body.

## Adventure Background

The [[Grim Forest]] is one of the oldest and most dangerous regions of [[The Outback]]. It was once a rich source of straight timber, but generations of colonial logging and magical industry left much of the forest warped, overgrown, and saturated with arcane residue. Ancient battlefields, abandoned war machines, hostile plants, and undead now lie beneath its canopy. The forest’s history is preserved mainly through the oral traditions of the indigenous communities around [[Doobius House]].

The Neck is a heavily traveled route through the Grim Forest connecting [[North Booftown]] with inland settlements such as [[Doobius House]]. Its roads pass through areas where the forest’s old battlefields and magical contamination are especially concentrated. The [[Nevercough Tribunal]] and the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] have historically protected the route, but patrols have become less reliable as threats from the forest multiply.

[[Dr. Stein]] established a concealed biomantic laboratory somewhere near the Neck, using the remote forest as a place to conduct experiments beyond the scrutiny of Stickyickistan’s authorities. His work introduced additional chimeric creatures and unstable magical residue into an already damaged ecosystem. Some of Stein’s wards still redirect or suppress his escaped creations, but they were never designed to contain a creature created from the laboratory itself.

While staying in Stein’s hidden laboratory, his half-brother [[Jonjo Starr]] attempted to create a phylactery using a perpetually refilling amphora. He mistook a mimic for the vessel. The ritual fused Jonjo, the mimic, and part of Stein’s biomantic laboratory into a single creature: the **Grim Forest Terror**, a tavern mimic that disguises itself as a welcoming roadside inn.

Jonjo’s creature feeds on travelers along the Neck. Its wards protect its exterior from direct attacks, forcing anyone who wants to destroy it to enter the tavern-shaped body. The resulting disappearances have revived old fears that the Grim Forest is becoming impassable and that another forgotten weapon or experiment may be waking beneath the trees.

Dr. Stein has lost both his laboratory and his brother. Under pressure from trade leaders and the [[Nevercough Tribunal]], he seeks help from [[Heliana]] and the Free League to end the disappearances, recover Jonjo if possible, and prevent the Grim Forest Terror from spreading beyond the Neck.

## Summary

- **Monster:** The Grim Forest Terror, a tavern mimic that disguises itself as a welcoming inn.
- **Motivation:** Consume travelers and absorb enough life force to sustain Jonjo’s failed attempt at immortality.
- **Previous Actions:** Dozens of travelers have disappeared, along with Dr. Stein’s tower and half-brother.
- **Location:** The Neck, a major trade route through the [[Grim Forest]].
- **Central Challenge:** The Grim Forest Terror cannot be harmed from outside. The characters must enter the tavern and fight it from within.

## Adventure Hook

The characters find the following notice in [[The Rolling Papers]] or on the Free League’s legal monster-hunting board.

> **TRAVELERS MISSING ALONG THE NECK: LICENSED HUNTERS REQUESTED**
>
> Caravans and travelers have disappeared along the trade route through the [[Grim Forest]]. The [[Nevercough Tribunal]] seeks capable adventurers to investigate the disappearances, protect future caravans, and remove the creature responsible.
>
> Dr. Stein offers additional compensation for the recovery of his missing tower or the safe return of his half-brother, [[Jonjo Starr]].
>
> Report to the northern road station at the edge of the Grim Forest. Ask for the biomancy researcher with the metal lungs.

The characters are met at the road station by Dr. Stein, who provides a painting of Jonjo and a magical biomatter transponder. The device allows Stein to teleport to the party if they locate his tower or his brother.

## Dr. Stein

Dr. Stein is an infamous biomancy wizard whose body is now more arcane life-support system than flesh. He is brilliant, distracted, and only partially aware of how many of his experiments have escaped into the surrounding wilderness.

He believes the party can either destroy the Grim Forest Terror or recover Jonjo. He provides a reward for either outcome, but pays more if Jonjo is returned alive or intact enough to be studied.

## The Grim Forest

The Grim Forest is a dark, root-filled woodland inside [[The Outback]]. Twisted conifers crowd the Neck, their branches interlocking above the canopy. Biomantic influence has caused the trees to alter their shapes during their lifetimes rather than across generations.

The Neck is the shortest route between [[North Booftown]] and inland settlements such as [[Doobius House]]. It is busy enough that travelers regularly risk the forest despite its reputation.

### Tracking

The characters must track the Grim Forest Terror through the Neck. Use four Tracking checks and require no successes to locate the creature, since the party is following a well-traveled road and the monster’s activity is obvious. A failed check does not create a hostile encounter, but another traveler may report a recent disappearance or warn the party about a strange inn.

If the characters leave the Neck and enter the deeper forest, use two Tracking checks and require two successes. Tracking checks in the deep forest have advantage because the creature’s unusual tracks are obvious, but each check creates a hostile forest encounter.

After the characters complete the required checks, they find a clearing containing evidence of the Grim Forest Terror. Each check represents approximately half a day of travel.

### Unique Flora and Fauna

The Grim Forest contains many of Dr. Stein’s escaped experiments. Use official creature stat blocks whenever possible and describe them as biomantically altered:

- **Owlbear:** A failed attempt to reproduce the original owlbear, with an unusually aggressive temperament.
- **Giant Scorpion:** A frog-like or amphibious mutation with unusually potent venom.
- **Awakened Shrubs or Awakened Trees:** Trees and plants altered by biomantic runoff.
- **Mimics:** Escaped experiments and descendants of the Grim Forest Terror.

## Know Thy Enemy

During the investigation, the characters can find one or more clearings containing evidence of the Grim Forest Terror:

- A pile of bleached bones and untarnished metal.
- Hundreds of mismatched footprints facing outward from a rectangular area.
- A frightened survivor named Guthric.
- A patch of burned earth where an alchemical fire was deflected by the creature’s exterior wards.

### Clue 1: Acid

The Grim Forest Terror consumes travelers and dissolves their flesh. The pile of bones and metal is waste it cannot digest.

A successful DC 12 Intelligence (Nature) check identifies acid damage on the bones and metal. A successful DC 14 Wisdom (Medicine) check reveals that the pile contains no bones from the ankle downward, suggesting the victims’ feet were removed and used by the creature as locomotion.

### Clue 2: The Tavern Mimic

The Grim Forest Terror disguises itself as a warm, welcoming roadside tavern. When it moves, hundreds of mismatched feet sprout from its underside and trample the forest.

A successful DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check identifies that the hundreds of footprints are humanoid and all face away from the rectangular outline of the creature.

### Clue 3: Warded Exterior

Dr. Stein’s wards prevent the Grim Forest Terror from being harmed from outside. Spells, weapons, and flames are deflected or redirected. The characters must enter the tavern to damage its inhabitants and central nervous system.

### Guthric

Guthric is an alignment-good human commoner and former caravaneer’s apprentice. He hides near one of the clearings and approaches the party if he hears them discussing the monster.

Guthric explains that his caravan stopped outside the tavern because the guards could not afford an inn. The guards entered after hearing screams but never returned. Guthric threw alchemist’s fire at the building, but the flames were deflected and burned the ground instead.

## The Battle Ahead

The tavern mimic is both monster and lair. The characters can only harm it by entering the building and fighting from within.

The party faces three consecutive waves:

1. Furniture and smaller mimics.
2. A shrinking effect that places the characters on a gigantic dinner table.
3. Jonjo Starr’s tavern-mimic form and its central nervous system.

Track damage toward the wave thresholds rather than reducing the tavern mimic’s hit points normally.

### Wave Damage Thresholds

| Hunt Level | Wave 1 | Wave 2 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3rd | 90 | 180 |
| 8th | 230 | 460 |
| 13th | 350 | 700 |

Areas of effect and ongoing damage count only once per round toward a threshold, using the highest simultaneous instance of damage.

## A Tavern’s Domain

The [[Grim Forest]] Terror establishes itself at a busy crossroads or isolated section of the Neck. The surrounding six miles show signs of its presence:

- Hundreds of mismatched footprints cross the road.
- Piles of bleached bones and untarnished metal appear beneath the trees.
- Travelers tell stories about an innkeeper whose patrons all speak and move alike.
- Other taverns along the Neck have begun refusing travelers after dark.

### Lair Actions

While the [[Grim Forest]] Terror lives, it can use the following lair actions on initiative count 20. It cannot use the same action two rounds in a row.

| Hunt Level | VDC | Vdam |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3rd | 12 | 3 (1d6) |
| 8th | 14 | 7 (2d6) |
| 13th | 16 | 14 (4d6) |

- **Drool:** Acid falls on a 30-foot square. Creatures beneath it make a Dexterity saving throw, taking Vdam acid damage on a failure or half damage on a success.
- **Peristalsis:** The tavern contracts. Each non-mimic creature inside makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, it is pushed 15 feet, falls prone, and takes Vdam bludgeoning damage. On a success, it moves 5 feet without falling or taking damage.
- **Sink:** A 10-foot square of floor becomes sticky and soft. Creatures in the area make a Strength saving throw or become restrained. A restrained creature sinks 2 feet at the end of its turn and takes Vdam acid damage.

## Entering the Tavern

When the characters reach the tavern, read or paraphrase:

> Laughter spills from a warmly lit wooden building in a forest clearing. Silhouettes of drinkers move behind sackcloth curtains, and hundreds of different footprints lead to the walls before stopping abruptly.

Inside, read or paraphrase:

> A dozen patrons laugh around a smokeless fire while a landlady raises filled tankards over the bar. The air is cold and stale despite the hearth.
>
> “New customers!” she says with an unblinking smile. “First one’s on the house.”

The tankards contain acid. A creature that drinks takes Vdam acid damage.

The door seals after the characters enter. Characters notice the tavern is unnatural if they investigate the repeating gestures, fixed smiles, cold fire, or eyes appearing briefly in the walls.

## Wave 1: Furniture

If the characters act aggressively or try to leave, the smaller mimics attack.

- **3rd level:** 1 Mimic and 2 Swarms of Mimics.
- **8th level:** 1 Mimic, 1 Mirror Mimic, and 2 Swarms of Mimics.
- **13th level:** 2 Mirror Mimics and 4 Mimics.

Use official stat blocks for Mimics and other published mimic creatures available to the DM. The mimics use the tavern’s furniture, walls, and patrons as cover.

## Wave 2: Shrink

When the Wave 1 damage threshold is reached, the tavern shrinks the characters and their equipment to roughly 2 inches tall and places them on a huge table. The table’s cutlery, coins, cups, and food become the battlefield.

Read or paraphrase:

> The tavern lurches. The table rises around you, stretching into a towering landscape. Cups become wells, forks become iron bars, and a smear of purple sauce lies two feet deep across the tabletop.
>
> Then the cutlery moves.

- **3rd level:** 4 Mimics with half their normal hit points.
- **8th level:** 5 high-threat Mimics and 2 standard Mimics.
- **13th level:** 3 Mirror Mimics and 3 high-threat Mimics.

When the Wave 2 threshold is reached, the characters return to normal size.

## Wave 3: Jonjo Starr

Jonjo’s face extrudes from the tavern wall as the [[Grim Forest]] Terror reveals its central nervous system.

Read or paraphrase:

> The table vanishes and the world snaps back to its proper scale. A humanoid face pushes through the tavern wall, stretched across pale lilac strands.
>
> The building shudders around you.
>
> “You’re proving to be quite the nuisance.”

The characters may fight Jonjo’s tavern-mimic form or use Dr. Stein’s biomatter transponder. If a character spends an action operating the device for three consecutive rounds, Dr. Stein appears and traps Jonjo in a magical locket, ending the encounter.

### Wave 3 Enemies

- **3rd level:** 1 Emaciated Tavern Mimic Brain and 1 Mimic.
- **8th level:** 1 Tavern Mimic Brain and 1 Mirror Mimic.
- **13th level:** 1 Nourished Tavern Mimic Brain and 2 Mirror Mimics.

Use the appropriate official stat blocks from the adventure’s source material. The central nervous system is part of the tavern mimic and transfers damage to the main creature.

## Optional Ending: Digestif

When Jonjo is defeated or captured, the tavern begins to collapse. Acid fills the interior, the floor becomes difficult terrain, and the structure folds inward.

At initiative count 0, the tavern fills with 1 foot of acid. Each creature still inside takes Vdam acid damage for each foot of acid. At the end of the third round, the tavern collapses.

# Aftermath

Dr. Stein pays the agreed compensation and, if Jonjo is saved, offers to teach one biomancy spell to a wizard in the party.

The [[Nevercough Tribunal]] reopens the Neck once the Grim Forest Terror is gone. Merchants and caravans begin using the route again, though travelers continue to avoid roadside taverns for several months.

The Grim Forest remains dangerous. Dr. Stein’s escaped experiments, mimics, and altered plants provide future hunting contracts.

## Further Adventure Hooks

- Dr. Stein’s wards fail, allowing chimeric creatures to reach North Booftown.
- A new roadside inn appears on the Neck, but nobody remembers building it.
- Mimic eggs disguised as gold coins spread through Stickyickistan.
- The [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] hires the party to escort a caravan through the reopened route.
- A strange route beneath the forest leads toward [[Doobius House]] or the deeper Outback.

## Harvesting

The [[Grim Forest]] Terror provides the normal components for a monstrosity, plus unique materials that can be harvested from its mimic body and Jonjo’s central nervous system:

| DC | Components |
|---:|---|
| 10 | Pouch of teeth |
| 15 | Tongue and stomach |
| 20 | Brain and skin |

The remains also contain a [[Mimickey]], which can be preserved and socketed onto a held or worn item if the DM uses that optional crafting rule.
