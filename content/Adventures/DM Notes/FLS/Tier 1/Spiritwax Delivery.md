Now that the stolen bricks have been repossessed, Rob can be tasked with trying to deliver them himself. A buyer has been reached through a network of informants on the Silt Road.

# Briefing

- Deliver 6 bricks to a [[Drow]] buyer in [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]]
- Don’t let the [[Nevercough Tribunal]] patrols find them.
- Watch out for the opps ([[Shadow Wizard Money Gang]] & [[BASED NEGATIVE SQUAD]]).
- Don’t let the bricks break (if a brick is destroyed, alcohol spills and can ignite).

# Scene 1: Checkpoint At the Bone Crypt

The city guard knows that entrances to Osslurraggath are often visited by folks involved in nefarious activities. The nearest entrance to the undercity is mostly blockaded by a group of city guards. There is more than one way in and out of the undercity, but the old bone crypt is the only commonly known entrance. 

The old bone crypt is an old ruined mausoleum that collapsed into what is now the undercity is used as an entrance to the necropolis. Cold stone walls tower above a massive hole in the floor that descends into the tunnels that make up the upper levels of [[The Silt Road]], and lead into the undercity.

Bone Crypt entrance [Encounter Map](https://www.czepeku.com/fantasy/maps/rosehydra-crypt/no-giant-roses-day)

Players must find a way to sneak around the guards (VDC Stealth), bribe them (VDC Persuasion for 2 gp), convince them they aren’t trying to enter the undercity (VDC Deception), defeat them, or find another entrance which will result in an extra tracking check.

## Encounter: City Guard Checkpoint

> The tunnel narrows ahead, and flickering torchlight spills across a makeshift barricade of crates and spears jammed into the stone. A half-dozen Tribunal guards lounge around a firepit, their armor mismatched but marked with the silver scales of authority. Beyond them, the stairwell spirals down into the depths of Osslurraggath. The guards stand alert as you approach, eyes narrowing.

#### Option 1: Stealth (DC 13)

**Success:** 
> Hugging the shadows of the tunnel walls, you slip past the checkpoint. The guards mutter among themselves, laughing at some crude joke, never noticing your silent passage. The faint echo of your footsteps fades as you disappear into the undercity.

**Failure:**
> A boot scrapes too loudly across the stone. “Oi! Who’s creeping about?” one of the guards barks, spears shifting toward you. The firelight glints on their helmets as suspicion sharpens their gaze.

#### Option 2: Bribe (Persuasion DC 12, and 2 gp)

**Guard Dialogue (friendly but greedy):** 
> “Well, well… what’s this? Late travelers, eh? You know this passage ain’t exactly… open. But hey, rules can be bent. For the right price.” He rubs his fingers together with a sly grin.

**If the bribe is offered:**
> “Aye, that’ll do. You lot never crossed our path tonight. Move along, before I change my mind.”

**If refused/too low:**
> “That’s an insult. Maybe we search your packs instead, hm?” He steps closer, spear tip tapping against the stone. Roll Initiative.

#### Option 3: Deception (DC 14)

**Guard Dialogue (suspicious):**
> “State your business. Folks headed down there usually ain’t the honest sort.”

**If they succeed:**
> The guard captain narrows his eyes, then shrugs. “Hmph. Alright then. Go on before I change my mind.”

**If they fail:**
> “Funny… your story stinks like spoiled ale. Boys, stop ’em!”
#### Option 4: Combat

**Guard Challenge:** 
>The guard captain slams his spear into the cobbles, steel ringing. “Enough games. You lot aren’t passing without blood.” The guards ready their weapons, forming a wall across the passage. Behind them, the stairwell to Osslurraggath yawns dark and unwelcoming.

#### Option 5: Alternative Route

**If they search instead of confronting guards:**
>Skirting back through side alleys of brick and dripping pipework, you find a half-collapsed aqueduct that smells of rust and fungus. It winds into the depths — a longer, filthier path, but one that avoids Tribunal eyes.

# Scene 2: Navigating Osslurraggath

This will require 2 tracking checks with 1 success. On a success trigger a narrative encounter, and a Hostile encounter on a fail.

#### Narrative encounter

***The Warrior’s ghost.*** 
A powerfully-built spectral figure (LG ghost) appears directly before the party, where it hovers, stationary. It uses emotions and images to telepathically convey that it can’t speak or move, and that it has unfinished business it needs help to complete (GM’s discretion, for example: killing a monster, passing on a message, or returning to sunlight). It asks that one of the party hosts it, conveying it will help them in return.  

***Host.***
While a party member hosts the ghost, it gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls it makes. In addition, the host counts as a CR 4 undead for the purposes of abilities that turn undead and spells like chill touch and protection from evil and good. If the adventurer acts contrary to the ghost’s alignment, or the ghost feels like the host has no intention of helping it, it abandons the host.

The warrior wants to return to sunlight, but can’t without a host taking him there.

#### Hostile Encounter

The party gets ambushed by a group of BNS members consisting of 3 Money Spell Shadow Soldiers (Shadow Acolyte) and a Based Money Shadow Slayer (Etherean Trickster).

# Scene 3: The Port of Osslurraggath

When they reach the ports of Osslurraggath, the client is waiting for them on a Drow battleship.

Before payment is received, the client wants to test some of the product, but as he begins melting the brick, a group of Rum Gremlins come rushing through the gates and attack the party in an attempt to get to the alcohol.

The players must defend the client and their ship while they test the product which will take 5-6 rounds of combat. The Rum Gremlins will fight to the death for the alcohol, and the client will join in the fight once they have tested the product.

When the Rum Gremlins are defeated the client will pay the party, and the adventure can fade to black with the party in the ports of Osslurraggath, or they can make 3 more tracking checks requiring 1 success to return to Xon's bastion.
