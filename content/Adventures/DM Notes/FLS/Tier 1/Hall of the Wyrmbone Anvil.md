# Adventure Primer

The shrill winds of the highlands whistle through narrow crags and ridges, carrying with them whispers of ancient craft and more recent horrors. Here lies the [[Green-Quartz Mine]], a once-thriving source of prospecting wealth. Alas, it is now silent beneath layers of dust and fear. Normally a marvel of bustling commerce of precious stones and coal, the mine also serves as the only known entrance to a greater complex known as the [[content/Adventures/DM Notes/FLS/Tier 1/Hall of the Wyrmbone Anvil]]. Within those buried halls, it is noted, lies a magical, dwarvish anvil carved from the bones of the last ember-drake of [[Mount Zooka]]. It is said that smiths’ hammers can be blessed upon its surface to become magical.

Dwarf forgers imprisoned an extraplanar abomination in the dungeon eons ago to siphon its power. The binding runes have degraded over time. The monstrous thing, now free, creeps forth. It is a harbinger of madness and death. The mine was sealed 10 days ago. The Mining Guild speaks of a terrible curse. Many see opportunities in this conundrum: A famous blacksmith artisan seeks to have her hammer blessed at the Wyrmbone Anvil. A squire in the guild’s employ is tasked with seeking valiant souls willing to brave the dangerous mine and the ancient dungeon beyond it. And a miner who gazed at the amorphous monster feels a strange calling to learn more about its origin.
