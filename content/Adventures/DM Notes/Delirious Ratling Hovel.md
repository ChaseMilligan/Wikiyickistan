# Hook

A low ranking member of the [[Amethyst Academy]]

# Top Floor

1. Ratling Warrior Patrol
	1. 2 Ratling warriors patrol this hallway
2. Webs and Larvae & Random Armaments
    1. 1 Larva hides in the webs in this room guarding a magic item
3. Wall Trap
    1. Stepping on the tile on the cross section of the hallway triggers a dart trap in the alcove. The trap shoots 3 total darts. The first character in order must succeed a DC 10 DEX save, or get hit by 1d3 darts for 1d2 piercing damage each. If the first character succeeds in dodging the darts, the character immediately behind them in a straight line must make the same save. If any character fails the save, and doesn’t get hit by all 3 darts, the character immediately behind them in a straight line must make a save for the remaining darts.
4. Speaking Skull & Random Relic
    1. This room contains a magic item, and a sentient speaking skull of an adventurer who perished in the dungeon.
	2. The skull has gone a bit crazy
5. Green Slime floor
    1. The floor in this room is covered in green slime hazard from the DMG
6. Juvenile Mimic & Random Implement
    1. This room contains a few chests.
    2. Chest on the left contains a Random magic item
    3. One of the chests contains some random bones
    4. The right most chest is a juvenile Mimic
7. Stairs to Bottom Floor
# Bottom Floor

1. Dark Hallway
	1. This hallways is dark and connects the two other rooks on the bottom floor
2. Ruined Room
    1. Here is where the Burrow Warden is relaxing while his Ratling warriors attempt to mine the Delirium from the other room
3. Delirium Deposit
    1. Here there are two Ratling Warriors attempting to mine pieces of Delirium
	2. The Ratlings hold 3 chips of delirium each
    3. A Delirium Geode can be procured from this room given enough time. May result in more encounters
# Alternate Entrance / Exit

1. DC 15 Investigation / Survival (Depending on how they decide to look)
2. There is another Speaking Skull here that won’t try to hurt them unless attacked first. It will tell them about its friend (the other speaking skull) that has a magic item
