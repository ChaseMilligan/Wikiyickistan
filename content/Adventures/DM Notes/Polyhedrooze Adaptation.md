# Background

Years ago, hidden atop a jagged peak beyond Stickyickistan's city limits, a terrible wyrm named [[Terplorr the Blazenborn]] ruled over a volcanic mountain lair, his throne nestled deep within the caldera. A horde of slavish kobolds served his every whim, toiling beneath the smoke-choked sky. Adventurers eventually brought an end to Terplorr’s tyranny, but in the heat of the final moments of the battle, the mountain collapsed in on itself. The caldera and much of the upper peak were seemingly lost to ruin.

But Terplorr’s hoard was no ordinary treasure pile, it pulsed with latent draconic magic. That ancient arcana preserved the central sanctum, keeping the heart of the dungeon suspended in a warped pocket of reality amid the wreckage. Years passed, and the world forgot Terplorr’s lair.

That is, until [[Dr. Stein|Dr. Francis Norman Stein]], a disgraced biomancer obsessed with forbidden research, found it.

Now surrounded by the toxic sprawl of the [[Seekers' Scrapyard]], the ruined caldera offered perfect cover for Stein’s grotesque experiments. Driven by whispers in the dark and his fascination with bodily transformation, Stein repurposed the ancient dragon’s sanctum as a laboratory. Here, he refined a dangerous mutagen, [[Mut-x]], designed to force evolution through arcano-biological trauma. But there was more than science at play…

During a failed planar fusion experiment involving ooze samples retrieved from extraplanar filth dimensions, Dr. Stein unintentionally opened a conduit to the Slime Pits of the [[Abyss]]. There, something answered: the Faceless Lord [[Juiblex]].

One night, Stein’s chamber was rocked by the arrival of a young black dragon, drawn to the corrupted lair by the promise of dominion over the filth-ridden scrapyard. This wyrmling, ambitious and cruel, saw the ruins as his destined territory. But Dr. Stein had other plans.

Using a mix of magitech sedatives, eldritch glyphs, and a ritual fed by Mut-x, Stein subdued the dragon and bound it to the Abyssal conduit. What emerged from the chrysalis of suffering was no longer just a dragon, it was [[Goob’lath]] the Black, a tar-caked abomination drenched in Juiblex’s favor. Where once scales shimmered with obsidian gloss, now thick black ooze bubbles from between fused plates. His thoughts burn with psychic echoes of Terplorr’s greed, distorted by Mut-x and Juiblex’s hunger.

Goob’lath believes Juiblex will one day consume all, and that only those who serve will be spared. Thus, he wages war against order, containment, and form itself. In the scrapyard, Goob’lath has built an empire of rot, infecting broken magitech, mutating the ground, and raising Tar Elementals from between the Planes of Earth and Water. He is worshipped as a god by a cult of black-winged kobolds, who smear themselves in his tar to imitate his new form. These fanatics assist in creating the polyhedroozes and tar elementals by dragging fresh scrap and prey into the dungeon’s depths.
