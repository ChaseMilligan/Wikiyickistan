## Christmas Special: League of Labyrinthia

You’ve been chosen to appear on the number one prime-time game show, League of Labyrinthia, hosted by Zahur Al-Nasir, a powerful Djinn.

He plans to teleport your party to a snow globe demiplane in Ysgard, where an endless blizzard howls around a fake Santa village. This place is both a physical prison and a mental trap for the cruel fey known as Krampus. Long ago, Krampus was the dark shadow that followed Santa across the Material Plane on the winter solstice, punishing misbehaving children until Santa cast him into this prison millennia ago. The demi plane’s magic suppresses Krampus’s mind so completely that he truly believes he is Santa Claus, and never even thinks to escape. Zahur wants to exploit the fear Krampus inspires by staging a holiday spectacle: adventurers fighting a “Santa” who is actually a monster, and doesn’t know it. Once you enter, there is only one way out. Not even death frees you. To escape Krampus’s foul wrath, you must collapse the demiplane in on itself. You will spill forth into the Astral Plane while Krampus is sealed somewhere between the threads of the Weave forever.

**Goals:** 
1. Survive. 
2. Find a way to collapse the demi plane and escape. 
3. Put on as good of a show as you can!

**Warning:** In Ysgard, death may not be permanent. You might wake up in the blizzard and have to fight on. Same goes for all other creatures who perish in combat in Ysgard.
