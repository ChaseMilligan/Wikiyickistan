# The 4 Prisoners of A.S.S.C.U.N.T

_An Adventure for four 4th-level infiltrators and four 8th-level prisoners_

[[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]], the first recognized Bloodhound squad of the [[Ashen Shadow Society Creature Utilisation & Neutralisation Taskforce]], has been arrested and imprisoned in one of [[Mythmos]]’s most secure detention sites. The [[Ashen Shadow Society]] wants the squad back before its members, contacts, field notes, and recovered specimens become someone else’s property. Rather than risk a Raven or Shadowsong squad on a politically radioactive extraction, the Society assigns the job to an expendable support crew hungry enough to prove they belong, or maybe just stupid enough to play along.

In this adventure, the players control both sides of the operation: four 4th-level support-squad infiltrators working their way into the prison from outside, and four 8th-level members of [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] fighting, scheming, and surviving from within. The infiltrators must create openings, smuggle tools, sabotage defenses, and locate the prisoners. The prisoners must exploit those openings, mislead guards, survive lockdowns, and break themselves out when the moment comes.

## Adventure Background

[[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] is the first recognized Bloodhound squad of the [[Ashen Shadow Society Creature Utilisation & Neutralisation Taskforce]], the Society’s specialized monster-capture, specimen-harvesting, and creature-neutralization branch. Its members have survived missions involving criminal syndicates, unstable arcane artifacts, hostile cults, and dangerous creatures from [[The Outback]]. That success made them valuable to the [[Ashen Shadow Society]], but it also made them visible.

During a job outside Stickyickistan, Unit BH-Umbra Δ9 was captured and transferred to **Giantfrost Penitentiary**, a remote detention fortress in the northernmost reaches of Mythmos. Built into a frozen mountain stronghold surrounded by killing cold, high cliffs, and miles of white wilderness, Giantfrost uses altitude, isolation, and weather as part of its security. It is where distant authorities send prisoners too dangerous, politically inconvenient, or magically troublesome to keep anywhere warmer.

The Ashen Shadow Society wants the squad recovered before its members, field notes, contacts, and harvested specimens become someone else’s property. The Society is not acting out of loyalty. Unit BH-Umbra Δ9 knows too much about the Ashen Citadel, ASSCUNT field protocols, Society clients, illicit component markets, and contracts the Society would prefer not to explain in court.

Rather than risk a Raven or Shadowsong squad on a politically radioactive extraction, the Society assigns the job to an up-and-coming support crew. Officially, the crew is being given a chance to prove it belongs among the real squads. Unofficially, it is expendable. If the heist succeeds, the Society gets its Bloodhounds back. If it fails, the Council of Shadows can deny the operation, disavow the crew, and let Giantfrost Penitentiary keep the bodies.

## Adventure Hooks

The [[Ashen Shadow Society]] needs [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] extracted from [[Giantfrost Penitentiary]] before Mythmos authorities sell, dissect, extradite, or magically interrogate the taskforce. The Society cannot openly send its best operatives without admitting ownership of the prisoners, so the job is passed down to a support crew with just enough talent to be useful and just little enough status to be deniable.

Use one or more of these hooks to bring the 4th-level infiltrators into the operation:

**Support Squad Assignment.** The characters are members of an Ashen Shadow support squad. [[Zara Blackthorn]], [[Kezrin Malvek]], or another handler gives them the mission as a chance to prove they deserve recognition beyond errand work, corpse hauling, and disposable backup duty. Success could earn them real squad status. Failure means the Society never knew them.

**Bloodhound Bounty.** The Society frames the job as a recovery contract: bring back its missing Bloodhounds, their field notes, and any confiscated specimens taken from them at arrest. The infiltrators are promised payment, promotion, and first claim on minor salvage that the Society does not classify as restricted property.

**Inside Contact.** One of the prisoners gets a message out of Giantfrost Penitentiary through a smuggled tooth, a shed skin sample, a trained parasite, a coded prayer, or some other ASSCUNT field trick. The message reaches the Society with a short warning: the prison is preparing to move Unit BH-Umbra Δ9 somewhere worse.

**Expendable Test.** The Council of Shadows or the Enigmatic Quartet quietly uses the mission as a trial run for a new crew. The infiltrators are not told everything. They receive maps, aliases, and partial security notes, but the Society withholds enough information to see whether they can improvise under pressure.

**Personal Stake.** One or more infiltrators owe Unit BH-Umbra Δ9 a debt, trained under an ASSCUNT hunter, lost a friend during the arrest, or wants access to the taskforce’s creature-harvesting knowledge. The Society’s motives are profit-driven, but the characters may have more personal reasons to risk the prison.
