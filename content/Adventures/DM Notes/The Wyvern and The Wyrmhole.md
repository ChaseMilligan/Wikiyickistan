Zara will approach Dr. C Rothvell, and ask him to carry out a special task for the enrichment of the society. There are a few steps that they need him to take, starting with crafting a Mind Blade. The society will then have him put a squad together to embark on a monster hunt (this is before the forming of CUNT, so monster hunts are still seen as up for grabs by any bounty hunting squad). Once the squad has been formed, you will hunt down a Wyvern. The society needs Dr. Rothvell specifically for this hunt because he is the only member in the society who can craft a Mind Blade which will be used in a ritual if you are able to slay the Wyvern. When the Wyvern is defeated is when the real fun begins. A ritual must be performed with the Mind Blade and the corpse of the Wyvern binding the two together. This ritual will spawn an aberration known as a Bicephal Wyrmhole which is the monster we really want to harvest. Once the Wyrmhole is conjured, you must also defeat it, and harvest a pouch of it's claws plus whatever else you can get, but the claws are of utmost importance. The society is looking to acquire a Graviturgic Warp-hilt which can be socketed on to any martial weapon to give it special aberrant properties.

# Notes
- The society wants you to enchant a sword into a Mind Blade.
- They then want you to take the sword with you to hunt a Wyvern.
- Kill any Wyvern by any means, and do not harvest it's components.
- You MUST magically preserve the Wyvern corpse to give you enough time to complete a ritual with it. If you do not, you will have to locate and kill another Wyvern.
- Cast a rite with the corpse to transform it into an enigmatic entity known as a Bicephal Wyrmhole
- DO NOT DIE and slay the Wyrmhole to harvest its claws.
- Consider anything else you can harvest a bonus.
- The Mind Blade will be destroyed in the process.
- There is no time limit on this assignment
## The Ritual
- Enchant a sword into a Mind Blade
- The society will supply a mundane sword and a robust Essence to be used for this purpose
- You can choose any sword (Longsword, Shortsword, Rapier, Scimitar, etc.).

- Casting takes 10 minutes
- Must be done while the magical essence is still present in the corpse
- The Mind Blade is plunged into the heart of the Wyvern's corpse
- For the 10 minutes the spellcaster must remain in contact with the corpse.
- Then the spellcaster makes a DC 15 Arcana check.
- On a failure, you must spend another 10 minutes casting to try again.
- On a success the Mind Blade is bound to the wyvern corpse which will then transform into a Bicephal Wyrmhole over the next minute.
- You have that one minute to prepare before the Wyrmhole is fully formed, and immediately starts attacking anything and everything.

# Sealed Addendum

**SEALED ADDENDUM — FOR DR. CESSIX ROTHVELL ONLY**  
**Operation: GRAVITY SQUEEZE**  
**Subject:** Rite of Mind–Corpse Convergence (Creation of a Bicephal Wyrmhole)

---
### Required Assets

The Ashen Shadow Society will provide:
- **One mundane sword**
    - You may specify any common pattern: _longsword, shortsword, rapier, scimitar,_ etc.
- **One phial of Robust Essence**

You must procure:
- **A freshly slain wyvern corpse**
    - Intact enough that the **heart and thoracic cavity are accessible and structurally sound**.
    - The corpse must still retain **its innate magical essence**.

---
### Rite Procedure
1. **Impale the Vessel**
    - Plunge the **Mind Blade into the wyvern’s heart**.
2. **Maintain Contact**
    - For **10 minutes**, you perform the rite.
    - You must remain in **continuous physical contact** with the corpse or the blade for the full casting.
3. **Arcane Test**
    - At the end of the 10 minutes, make a **DC 15 Arcana check**.
    - **On a failure:**
        - The binding fizzles.
        - You may attempt the rite again with **another 10 minutes** of casting, as long as the corpse’s magical essence remains.
    - **On a success:**
        - The Mind Blade’s essence binds to the corpse and is **destroyed**.
        - The wyvern begins transforming into a **Bicephal Wyrmhole**.

---
### Emergence Window
- After a successful rite, the transformation completes over **1 minute**.
- During that minute, the creature is not fully active—you have that time to **prepare** (reposition, buff, set traps, etc.).
- When the minute ends, the **Bicephal Wyrmhole fully forms** and **immediately attacks anything and everything** nearby.
