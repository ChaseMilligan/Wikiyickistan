# Meditation Readout

> **As you breathe, spores drift into your lungs—half golden motes that glow like starlight, half rancid threads of black mold. Your vision blurs, and suddenly you are elsewhere.**
> 
> You stand in a vast mycelial forest. To your left, towering mushrooms of **pale yellow and rancid brown** pulse and rot, their caps oozing foul vapors. A skeletal figure draped in lichen emerges from the decay. Her voice is both a whisper and a command:  
> _“Kneel, and I will gift you strength beyond mortality. Rot is the truth of all flesh. Rule with me, as my chosen bloom of ruin.”_
> 
> To your right, luminous **golden-capped mushrooms** rise like cathedral domes. Their caps hum with psionic light, their spores raining down like glittering stars. A radiant form of woven hyphae and light extends a hand to you. Her voice is calm and resonant:  
> _“Rise, and I will teach you to see beyond fear. My gift is not dominion, but awakening. Become the teacher of light and harmony.”_
> 
> **Both voices echo inside your mind, pulling you in two directions. The forest floor cracks beneath your feet, half-rotting, half-glowing. The spores inside you writhe—choosing will shape your soul.**

### Mechanics of the Choice

1. **Initial Save.**  
    The character must make a **DC 18 Wisdom saving throw** as the two presences wrestle inside them.
    - On a success, they remain lucid and can choose freely.
    - On a failure, they are overwhelmed by one voice (DM’s choice, or roll a d2), but may attempt a second save at disadvantage if they resist.
2. **Choice Point.**  
    Ask the player: _Which hand do you reach for?_ (or do they resist both). Their choice determines the gift they carry out of the vision.
		a. Cubensis (Boon of Awakening)
		b. Zuggtmoy (Boon of Corruption)
		c. Neither
3. **Refuse Both.**  
    If the player resists both, the vision shatters. They awaken unharmed, but the spores remain dormant inside them. Either side could still whisper later—keeping the conflict alive.

# Cubensis, the Golden Teacher (Boons of Awakening)

### 1. **Psionic Bloom**

- **Benefit:** You gain **telepathy (30 ft.)** and advantage on Insight checks. Once per long rest, you may cast **detect thoughts** without components.
- **Drawback:** Your mind becomes vulnerable to overwhelming visions. Whenever you take psychic damage, make a DC 15 Wis save or be stunned until the end of your next turn.

---
### 2. **Radiant Mycelium**

- **Benefit:** Once per long rest, as a bonus action, release a **halo of golden spores**. For 1 minute, allies within 10 ft. gain 1d6 temporary hit points at the start of their turn.
- **Drawback:** Your body subtly glows with golden motes, making Stealth checks in dim light or darkness at disadvantage.

---
### 3. **Teacher’s Insight**

- **Benefit:** Once per long rest, when you fail an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check, you may reroll it with advantage.
- **Drawback:** Your mind is “opened.” Whenever you sleep, you roll a d6: on a 1, you experience overwhelming visions and gain no benefit from a long rest unless you succeed on a DC 15 Con save.

---
### 4. **Spores of Harmony**

- **Benefit:** As an action, release spores in a 10-ft radius. All creatures of your choice must succeed on a DC 16 Wis save or be affected as though under **calm emotions**. Usable once per long rest.
- **Drawback:** You feel others’ emotions too strongly. You have disadvantage on Charisma saving throws.

# Zuggtmoy, the Rot Queen (Boons of Corruption)

### 1. **Rot-Touched Flesh**

- **Benefit:** You gain resistance to poison damage and immunity to the poisoned condition. Once per long rest, you may cast **blight** without components.
- **Drawback:** Your skin shows fungal lesions. Creatures have advantage on Medicine checks to heal you, or identify you as diseased or corrupted.

---
### 2. **Spore Servant’s Call**

- **Benefit:** Once per long rest, as an action, you can raise a recently slain Medium or Small beast/humanoid as a **spore thrall** (zombie stats, lasts 1 hour).
- **Drawback:** You smell faintly of rot, giving you disadvantage on Persuasion checks against humanoids in civilized areas.

---
### 3. **Infesting Strike**

- **Benefit:** At the end of each long rest, roll 1d6. Once per turn when you hit with a melee attack, you may deal that amount of extra poison damage.
- **Drawback:** At the end of each long rest, make a DC 15 Con save or lose max HP equal to your daily bonus damage 1d6 roll (restored at the start of the next long rest).

---
### 4. **Rotting Charisma**

- **Benefit:** Once per long rest, as an action, release spores in a 15-ft radius. All humanoids must succeed on a DC 16 Wis save or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.
- **Drawback:** Allies have disadvantage on their saving throw against the benefit of this boon.
