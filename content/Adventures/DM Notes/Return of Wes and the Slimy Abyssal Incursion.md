## **Rundown for Next Session** 

On the airship ride home, when the airship was on autopilot, the captain shared a story from Stickyickistan's past. He recanted the tale of [[Terplorr the Blazenborn]]. A Red Dragon once feared by the pioneers, and revered by the natives, used to reside in the caldera of a mountain that has since been destroyed. The mountain was only known by it's indigenous name, [[Wachii  Ka'paakitayk]], and it used to stand where the [[Seekers' Scrapyard]] is today. As part of the same mountain range as [[Mount Zooka]], Wachii Ka'paakitayk had the second highest peak. The mountain was completely leveled as a result of a battle in the dragon's lair that ultimately ended in it's defeat.

As the airship soars above the ocean you see the city skyline break the horizon. As you enter the city's airspace I will have you all make a perception check. 
- DC 20 to see a flying figure above the scrapyard. 
No perception check is needed with your birds eye view to see that somethings in certain areas of the city look off. You can see from where you are, the puddle of tar in the scrapyard seems to have grown significantly, and has reached the gates no longer being contained within the scrapyard. You also see a faint green glow from the canals in the city streets, and the same faint glow along the coast line by a shipyard / dock.

When you arrive back at the citadel, Kezrin and Zara will explain the Subsidiary Guild deal. The society is looking to leverage a legal concept of Subsidiary Guild creation often referred to as just sub-guilds. The offer essentially relinquishes executive control of the Creature Utilization and Neutralization Taskforce over to you, the party, but the Ashen Shadow Society maintains partial control of finances, contract records, and employee status of the sub-guild as well as their rights to a portion of all revenue generated by the sub-guild. The main reason for this is it legally separates A.S.S from C.U.N.T which allows the society to save some money on resources, and the taskforce becomes its own legal entity affording it the liberty to make contacts, and sign them on their own. Mechanically in game this won't really change anything except your main base will be your bastion instead of the musty low-rank guild squad quarters.

Zara and Kezrin will provide you with a list or a lineup of low level guild recruits for you to choose your hirelings from (flavor), or you can try to find one from the streets, but all of your employee records must be reported to A.S.S per your sub-guild agreement.
