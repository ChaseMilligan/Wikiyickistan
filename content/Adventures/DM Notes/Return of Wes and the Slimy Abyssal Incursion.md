Wesley Scantlin >> Woozely Castlinn

- Wes, and Goob'lath is going to waste the city and turn its residents into slimes that obey the faceless demon lord of Ooze, Juiblex, unless he is stopped.

[[Wes Scantlin]] has returned to [[Stickyickistan]], but he has changed. After being slain, his body was found by [[Goob’lath]]’s cultists which they preserved and brought to their master. Goob’lath saw the vile corpse of the equally disgusting man as a fitting vessel for his own master, [[Juiblex]], to possess whenever he desires to manifest in the material plane. While not possessed, Wes still acts of his own will, but his motives have been twisted by Juiblex. Wes is now enslaved by the insatiable urge to spread Juiblex’s influence by turning creatures into oozes or other shapeless lifeforms that answer to Juiblex.

Wesley arrived in the city under the cover of darkness. He snuck into the sewers and massacred all the [[Dungsweepers]]. He has since been staying in the main water treatment facility, which he disabled magically, for the downtown region. Here he has been conducting rituals to pollute the city’s water with potion of Ooze, so when people drink it they may become diseased. Those that succumb to the disease, which is most, are transformed into oozes that obey Juiblex upon death.

#### Finishing up on the Isles of Y'ummm
Ask if there is anything else they wish to do on the isles of Y'ummm. The effects of the Gummydusa curse fade over 1d4 + 1 days. They can return to the port town of Fondant Falls where a guild airship is waiting to pick them up.

#### Terplorr intro
On the airship ride home, when the airship was on autopilot, the captain shared a story from Stickyickistan's past. He recanted the tale of [[Terplorr the Blazenborn]]. A Red Dragon once feared by the pioneers, and revered by the natives, used to reside in the caldera of a mountain that has since been destroyed. The mountain was only known by it's indigenous name, [[Wachii  Ka'paakitayk]], and it used to stand where the [[Seekers' Scrapyard]] is today. As part of the same mountain range as [[Mount Zooka]], Wachii Ka'paakitayk had the second highest peak. The mountain was completely leveled as a result of a battle in the dragon's lair that ultimately ended in it's defeat. I am going to make this story into a campaign of its own, so the story told to you will be determined by the outcomes of that game we will play at some point in the future.

#### Bastion & Hirelings
Also aboard the airship, Kezrin and Zara will congratulate you guys on another successful venture for the society, and then explain the Subsidiary Guild deal. The society is looking to leverage a legal concept known as Subsidiary Guild creation often referred to as just sub-guilds. The offer essentially relinquishes executive control of the Creature Utilization and Neutralization Taskforce over to you, the party, but the Ashen Shadow Society maintains partial control of finances, contract records, and employee status of the sub-guild as well as their rights to a portion of all revenue generated by the sub-guild. The main reason for this is it legally separates A.S.S from C.U.N.T which allows the society to save some money on resources, and the taskforce becomes its own legal entity affording it the liberty to make contracts, and sign them on their own. Mechanically in game this won't really change anything except your main base will be your bastion instead of the musty low-rank guild squad quarters.

Zara and Kezrin will provide you with a list or a lineup of low level guild recruits for you to choose your hirelings from (flavor), or you can try to find one from the streets, but all of your employee records must be reported to A.S.S per your sub-guild agreement. Alternatively to making a hireling PC you can choose a monster stat block of CR 1/8 or lower. My goal for these characters, is to allow you guys to explore other characters while keeping it related to your main character. I plan on quest hooks for your hirelings coming from you for things that might seem menial for your main character. For example, let's say Fungi is making a potion, but he doesn't have a pouch of Rafflesia pollen which he needs. Instead of going to the Loot Tavern, or some other Monster Component merchant, you could send your hireling on a monster hunt to collect the Rafflesia pollen for you. I also think that leveling up your hirelings should afford you/them some bonuses when they do their job in your bastion. Another case in which I see the hirelings getting used is if there is a mission with multiple locations. Obviously you guys can't be everywhere at once, so you could send your hirelings to the another location in the mission. I will make sure to scale encounters appropriately, and let you know out of character if sending your hirelings to a particular location is a good idea. There may be certain encounters I won't scale like bosses/mini bosses. If you send your hirelings to take on a boss/mini boss, I won't stop you, but I will tell you that you may be sending them to perish.

In this act, the party is returning from their adventure on the [[Islands of Y'ummm]]. They are returning by airship, and when they return things in the city are different. 
#### Intro
As the airship soars above the ocean you see the city skyline break the horizon. As you enter the city's airspace I will have you all make a perception check. 
- DC 20 to see a flying figure above the scrapyard. 
No perception check is needed with your birds eye view to see that some things in certain areas of the city look off. You can see from where you are, the puddle of tar in the scrapyard seems to have grown significantly, and has reached the gates no longer being contained within the scrapyard. You also see a faint green glow from the canals in the city streets, and the same faint glow along the coast line by a shipyard / dock. Fade to black

Fade back in to the citadel with Kezrin bringing up the recent happenings in the city where oozes and tar elementals have been spotted in various places around the city since they have been gone. The next time the party sees Wes, he will be in the dungeon of [[The Faith of the Sacred Flame]]'s grand cathedral after he finishes corrupting the Flamekeepers. This can only happen once the party has dealt with all the other ooze infestations around the city.
