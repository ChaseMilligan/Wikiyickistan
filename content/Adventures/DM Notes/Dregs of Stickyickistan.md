# Dregs of Stickyickistan

## The Delerium Trade

[[Delerium]] reached [[Stickyickistan]] through salvage crews, smugglers, researchers, and [[Magitech]] manufacturers importing material from the ruins of [[Drakkenheim]]. At first, the crystals were treated as an expensive but dangerous magical resource. That changed when alchemists discovered that raw Delerium could be processed into an intensely euphoric inhalant, commonly called **Dreg** or **Delerium Dust**.

Delerium Dust is not a normal narcotic. It is a powdered form of unstable crystal mixed with alchemical solvents and magical reagents. When inhaled, it floods the user with energy, confidence, vivid sensory distortions, and a temporary feeling of magical potential. The high is powerful but short-lived, encouraging repeated use. Users often describe the first experience as feeling like their body has become stronger, brighter, and more important than it really is.

The drug is cheap to produce from low-quality Delerium, easy to conceal, and highly profitable. It first spread through the [[Industrial Zone]], where exhausted workers and desperate laborers used it to stay awake during long shifts. From there, it moved through clubs, gambling dens, criminal networks, and wealthy circles looking for a more dangerous thrill.

## Why Delerium Creates Dregs

Raw Delerium is chaotic and contaminating. Processing it into a drug does not make it safe. It only changes how the contamination enters the body. Each dose exposes the user to a small amount of eldritch energy, and repeated use allows that energy to accumulate.

The early effects are easy to mistake for ordinary drug abuse: sleeplessness, agitation, emotional volatility, paranoia, and a growing need for larger doses. Over time, the user develops physical changes. Their eyes may reflect octarine light, their skin may become cold or translucent, and strange growths may appear beneath the flesh. Some users develop compulsive movements, distorted voices, or an inability to recognize their own reflection.

Eventually, the contamination reaches a point where the body and mind can no longer contain it. The user undergoes a violent transformation into a [[Delerium Dregs]]. The resulting creature retains fragments of its former identity, but its memories, emotions, and instincts have been warped by the contamination. The transformation is unpredictable. Some Dregs become swollen and powerful, while others develop extra limbs, exposed bone, luminous organs, or fluid bodies suited to the environment where they changed.

Delerium Dregs are not created only by drug use. Direct contact with raw Delerium, exposure to Delerium Dust during its manufacture, and magical accidents can also cause enough contamination to trigger a transformation. The drug trade is simply the most common reason Dregs keep appearing in populated areas.

## Why Dregs Keep Appearing

Dregs continue to appear in Stickyickistan because the supply chain has never been completely stopped. Delerium Dust is produced in hidden workshops using contaminated crystals smuggled out of [[Drakkenheim]], along with unstable material recovered from mines and industrial waste sites around [[Mount Zooka]]. Every time one workshop is shut down, another supplier takes its place.

Several factors keep the problem alive:

- **Profit:** Delerium Dust sells for far more than the raw material costs to produce it.
- **Addiction:** Users return to the drug despite the physical and psychological damage, often committing crimes or selling possessions to obtain more.
- **Magitech demand:** Manufacturers and researchers buy Delerium for legitimate purposes, creating legal routes that smugglers can exploit.
- **Contaminated waste:** Failed batches, crystal shavings, and used alchemical equipment are dumped into sewers, scrapyards, and industrial refuse sites.
- **Criminal protection:** Dealers pay gangs, corrupt officials, and private security forces to keep production sites hidden.
- **Incomplete knowledge:** Many people understand that Delerium is dangerous, but few understand that repeated exposure can cause a delayed transformation.

The result is a cycle. Delerium is mined or salvaged, processed into a drug, sold to users, and discarded into the city. Some users die, some recover, and others become Dregs. Those Dregs then gather near contaminated sites, spreading panic and drawing attention away from the people who profit from the trade.

## The Dreg State

A Delerium Dreg is a transformed person, not a creature that was born monstrous. Most Dregs are driven by madness, fear, territorial instinct, and violent confusion. They do not attack because they need to eat. Their bodies are sustained by eldritch contamination, although they still need to breathe and may imitate feeding behaviors from broken memories.

Dregs often retain small pieces of their previous lives. One may repeat a work song, recite a family name, ask for a missing child, follow an old patrol route, or beg someone to hide the crystals. These moments of recognition are real but brief. A Dreg may speak coherently for a few seconds before becoming frightened or aggressive again.

Dregs also tend to gather. A lone Dreg may follow the sounds, magical resonance, or movements of another. Groups gradually become larger and more dangerous, especially near drug dens, abandoned workshops, scrapyards, and contaminated sewers. They are not organized in the usual sense, but a particularly lucid or advanced mutant may be able to direct them for a short time.

## The Delerium Dust Economy

The street trade has several grades of product:

- **Chips:** Weak, impure material sold to new users. The high is brief, but repeated use still causes contamination.
- **Shards:** The common street product, strong enough to produce the characteristic euphoria and magical sensations.
- **Crystal Dust:** Highly concentrated material sold to experienced users, wealthy thrill-seekers, and criminal enforcers. A single dose can cause a severe reaction.
- **Black Dreg:** Failed or deliberately intensified batches containing visible crystal fragments. It is cheap, extremely addictive, and responsible for many sudden transformations.

Dealers often claim that their product is purified, stabilized, or safe for magical bloodlines. These claims are unreliable. The only genuinely stable form of Delerium is material that has been properly converted into [[Arcanite]] or another finished magical item. Anything sold as an inhaled raw crystal product remains dangerous.

## Public Understanding

The public knows that Delerium Dust is destructive, but many people do not understand how quickly it can ruin a life. Wealthy users frame it as an exotic magical vice, while industrial employers may quietly tolerate its use because workers remain awake for longer shifts. Families often hide a relative's addiction out of shame or fear that authorities will take them away.

The appearance of a Dreg is usually treated as a monster attack rather than the final stage of addiction and contamination. This allows dealers and manufacturers to distance themselves from the consequences. A dead or transformed user is described as unstable, cursed, or criminal, while the people who sold them the drug disappear behind layers of intermediaries.

## Common Explanations for New Outbreaks

When Dregs appear in a new part of Stickyickistan, one or more of the following explanations may be true:

- A new dealer has opened a distribution point nearby.
- A contaminated batch has entered circulation.
- A hidden workshop is venting Delerium Dust into the surrounding area.
- Industrial waste has been dumped in a sewer, scrapyard, or abandoned building.
- A group of users has gathered around a particularly potent crystal deposit.
- A smuggling route from Drakkenheim has been disrupted, forcing dealers to use a more dangerous supply.
- Someone is deliberately creating Dregs to use as disposable muscle, test subjects, or a source of contaminated biological material.

## Tone and Themes

Delerium Dust should be presented as alluring, destructive, and exploitative. The drug gives users a brief feeling of power while gradually damaging their bodies, minds, relationships, and communities. The people who become Dregs are victims of contamination, addiction, criminal exploitation, and magical negligence, even when they have harmed others.

The horror of the Dregs is not only that ordinary people can become monsters. It is that the transformation is profitable for someone, and that the city has reasons to look away.
