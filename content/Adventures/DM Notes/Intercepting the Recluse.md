Sightings of slime and black winged kobolds around the city are becoming all too common. [[The Seekers of the Vein]] are now defending themselves from a class action law suit. They have cut ties with the [[Ashen Shadow Society]] as it has become clear that the problem is too large to keep the public unaware. The task of dealing with the faceless hoards has fallen upon anyone willing to take it up. Adventurers from all across [[Stickyickistan]] and beyond have now been venturing into the scrapyard in hopes of being the ones to find the source of the blight. Many denizens of the city have been increasingly reporting sightings of what looks like a giant flying serpentine puddle of murky liquid in the skies above the city. Another alarming occurrence since the arrest of [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] is the arrival of a mysterious individual in [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]] who calls himself "The Blurry One" . The Blurry One claims to be the harbinger of the slimy incursion who is returning to the city from the Abyss to claim vengeance on his killers. Locals claim to be able to faintly hear what they often describe as "nostalgic melodies" from the sewers at night.

The trial of [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] is complete. Their sentences vary in length, but the judge made sure to throw the book at all of them. They have been labelled as dangers to society for various reasons, and therefore will be shipped over 1000 miles away to the infamous [[Giantfrost Penitentiary]] near the north pole of [[Mythmos]]. [[Ashen Shadow Society Creature Utilisation & Neutralisation Taskforce|ASSCUNT]] needs to make sure this does not happen. They have come up with a plan to intercept the airship meant to ship the prisoners out of the city, which is known as The Recluse. A Raven rank squad has been assigned to this extraction mission where they will be flown on a society owned airship to board the recluse with the prisoners on board, and release them, but they will need some help. [[Ashen Shadow Society|ASS]] has requested that the [[Heist Squad]] assist the Raven squad by infiltrating the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition|THC]] guards assigned to the flight on The Recluse. Once the Raven squad has boarded The Recluse, and apprehended the captain, the Heist Squad should activate to release the prisoners using what info they have gained through being undercover. If all goes well, no one will die, and [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] will be brought back to the [[Ashen Citadel]] where they will be transitioned into the [[Ashen Shadow Society's Protected Outlaw Operatives Program]], aka A.S.S.P.O.O.P.

# Adventure Intro

The characters are summoned to the [[Ashen Citadel]] by [[Zara Blackthorn]], a senior member of the [[Ashen Shadow Society]]. She tells them that the Society's own [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] has been arrested, tried, and sentenced to transport at [[Giantfrost Penitentiary]]. Their convictions are legitimate, and the prisoners are dangerous, but ASSCUNT believes they are too valuable to lose. The Society intends to give them a chance to serve under its protection rather than spend the rest of their lives in a distant prison.

The transport ship, [[The Recluse]], is scheduled to leave [[Stickyickistan]] with the prisoners and a full [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition|THC]] security detail. Zara's plan is to place the Heist Squad among the guards before departure. Once aboard, they must maintain their covers, study the ship's security procedures, and prepare for the arrival of a Raven rank squad. The Raven squad will board The Recluse during the journey, take control of the captain, and create the opening the Heist Squad needs to release the prisoners.

Zara makes the terms clear: this is an extraction, not a massacre. The characters should avoid killing the THC guards, crew, or any civilians, and must not expose the Ashen Shadow Society unless there is no alternative. If the operation succeeds, the prisoners will be brought back to the [[Ashen Citadel]] and enrolled in the [[Ashen Shadow Society's Protected Outlaw Operatives Program]], known as A.S.S.P.O.O.P. If it fails, [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] will be taken north, and the Society's involvement may become public. Or worse...

> [!read-aloud]
> The summons bears the mark of the Ashen Shadow Society, but the message contains no details. At the Ashen Citadel, Zara Blackthorn gives you the truth: Unit BH-Umbra Δ9 has been tried, convicted, and sentenced to a life behind the walls of Giantfrost Penitentiary. Their punishment is deserved. Their future, however, is still negotiable.
>
> An airship called The Recluse will soon leave Stickyickistan with the prisoners aboard. You will board it first, disguised as members of the THC guard detail. Later, another Society squad will seize the ship. When that happens, you must free the prisoners and get them home. Keep the crew alive, protect your covers, and remember that the guards are doing their jobs. This is a rescue mission, not a war.
# Infiltrating the THC

## Joining the THC Detail

Because [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] is an ASSCUNT squad, [[Kezrin Malvek]], the taskforce's director, has authorized the operation and asked [[Zara Blackthorn]] to coordinate the infiltration. Zara meets the characters in [[Stickyickistan]] and provides uniforms, badges, and forged transfer orders for their chosen cover identities. The characters are replacing members of the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition|THC]] detail assigned to escort [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] aboard [[The Recluse]]. Zara directs them to report to the THC staging area before the airship's scheduled departure.

The Recluse is preparing to carry the prisoners north to [[Giantfrost Penitentiary]]. The flight will take several days, giving the Heist Squad time to establish their covers, learn the ship's security procedures, and identify the best opportunity to help the Raven squad seize the vessel. Zara warns them that their real allies will not reveal themselves until the operation is underway.

Eight guards are assigned to The Recluse, including any characters taking their places. If the characters express concern over the fate of staff they are replacing, Zara assures them that the people were not killed and will be released safely once the extraction is complete.

The players can make up whatever names they like for their cover identities. Those are the names of the THC guards or ship staff they have replaced. Zara warns them that senior officers may know the original staff by sight, so the characters should avoid drawing attention to themselves until they have learned enough about their assumed identities.

## Narrative Encounters

### 1. The Missing Guard

One of the THC guards the characters are replacing appears at the staging area unexpectedly. They were supposed to be safely detained by Zara, but escaped their temporary holding location and arrived looking for their assigned post.

The guard recognizes that the characters are not who they claim to be, but does not immediately raise the alarm. They are frightened and want to know what happened to their fellow guards.

The characters could:

- Convince the guard they are part of a confidential reassignment
- Threaten or restrain them
- Recruit them into the operation
- Stage a fake accident or medical emergency
- Admit the truth and ask for their help

If handled well, the guard can provide useful information about the THC detail, including personalities, routines, and the location of the security office.

---

### 2. The Unexpected Inspection

Before [[The Recluse]] departs, a senior THC officer orders a surprise inspection of all guards, uniforms, weapons, and transfer documents.

The inspection is not specifically intended to expose the characters. It is a routine measure following rumours that someone may attempt to interfere with the prisoner transfer. However, the officer asks questions that the characters’ forged identities do not fully answer.

Each character should be asked one personal question about their assumed identity, such as:

- Where did you serve before joining this detail?
- Who was your last commanding officer?
- Why were you transferred here?
- Which prisoner are you assigned to watch?
- What is the emergency signal for the holding compartment?

The players can support one another, create a distraction, or redirect the inspection toward a genuine administrative error. Failure should increase suspicion, but the inspection does not need to expose the entire group immediately.

---

### 3. The Prisoners’ Public Transfer

[[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] is brought through a public area of [[Stickyickistan]] before being loaded onto The Recluse. Crowds gather to see the infamous prisoners, and several people shout accusations or throw objects at them.

The characters are expected to maintain order while secretly protecting their future allies. One prisoner may deliberately provoke the crowd, while another quietly tries to communicate with the characters.

The encounter gives the players a chance to decide how they want their covers to look:

- Strict and loyal to the THC
- Cruel enough to discourage interference
- Calm and professional
- Distracted and easy to overlook
- Secretly sympathetic to the prisoners

A character can subtly pass a message, adjust a restraint, prevent someone from harming a prisoner, or use the crowd as cover for a small act of sabotage.

---

### 4. The Unscheduled Mooring

A pressure gauge begins to fail as The Recluse passes over the industrial districts of [[Stickyickistan]]. The captain orders the airship to make an emergency mooring at a nearby platform so the crew can inspect the damage. The delay is inconvenient, but it also gives the characters a chance to move through parts of the ship that would normally be under strict guard.

The characters can use the disruption to:

- Inspect the security office while its staff are distracted
- Learn which bulkheads can be sealed during an emergency
- Plant equipment or messages near the prisoner compartment
- Create a believable reason to alter the guard rotation
- Determine whether the damage is accidental or sabotage

The problem is genuine, but someone has taken advantage of it. A loose component may have been deliberately placed in the engine housing, and a careful investigation could reveal that an outsider has already accessed the ship. If the characters interfere too openly, the captain orders every guard to remain at their post until the source of the damage is found.

---

### 5. The Guard Captain’s Security Drill

The THC guard captain announces an unplanned security drill to test the detail before The Recluse leaves. The drill requires the guards to assemble, secure the prisoner compartment, check the restraints, and respond to a simulated breach. The captain expects everyone to know their assigned duties and notices anyone who hesitates.

The characters must participate without revealing that they do not know the original guards’ procedures. They can support one another, copy the actions of nearby guards, deliberately perform their duties too well, or turn the drill into an opportunity to study the ship’s security systems.

During the exercise, the characters can learn:

- Which guards carry keys to the prisoner compartment
- How long it takes to seal the holding area
- Which alarm signals summon the entire detail
- Where the guard captain stands during an emergency
- Which crew passages remain unguarded during a security response

The drill becomes more dangerous when the captain introduces a surprise complication: one restraint reports a false malfunction, or a sealed door fails to open on schedule. The characters must resolve the problem while maintaining their cover. A convincing response earns the captain’s confidence, while a poor one causes the detail to assign an additional guard to the prisoners during the flight.

# Adventure Conversion

## The Raven Squad Is Cast Off

Several hours into the flight, the Raven squad arrives aboard its Society-owned airship and boards The Recluse by grappling lines. The THC guards have little time to react before the Raven operatives reach the flight deck and confront the captain.

The captain responds by throwing The Recluse into a steep dive. Before the Raven squad can secure him, he cuts the harness lashing him to the helm. The dive sends the Raven squad and the captain tumbling overboard, leaving the Heist Squad alone aboard the airship with [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] and the THC detail.

> [!read-aloud]
> The grappling lines snap taut as the Raven operatives cross onto the deck. Then the captain wrenches the helm hard to one side. The Recluse plunges into a steep dive, and crates, weapons, and bodies slide across the deck. With a sharp metallic crack, the captain cuts the harness holding him to the helm. The Raven squad and the captain tumble into the clouds below, leaving you aboard a ship with no one at the controls of the vessel which is rapidly plummeting towards the open ocean.

Without a pilot, there’s no chance of The Recluse righting itself, let alone continuing its voyage. The Heist Squad must finish the extraction alone and find a way to stop the airship from crashing.

### Regaining Control of the Helm

A creature occupying the Helm crew station of an airship typically requires the appropriate training or magical attunement to access the station’s actions. [[The Recluse]], however, is unique. The airship is powered not only by a captive air elemental, but also by the spirit of a long-dead former captain who was absorbed into the ship’s elemental core. The spirit’s presence allows a creature occupying the Helm to bypass its usual requirements and use the station’s actions.

A character that takes the Study action and succeeds on a DC 9 Intelligence (Arcana) check recalls that airship Helms are normally restricted to qualified pilots. The character also recognizes that The Recluse’s unusual construction may allow someone without the expected qualifications to control it, if they can persuade the spirit within the elemental core to grant them access.

The ship is currently tilted steeply enough that the airship’s vertical surfaces are horizontal to the ground. The characters’ options now depend on where they were when the captain entered the dive. Give the players a few minutes to describe their characters and what they were doing prior to the Raven squad’s attack.

**On the Deck.** While the captain shouted for the characters to take shelter within The Recluse’s interior, those who ignored the order were hastily issued harnesses and now find themselves lashed to a safety rail that runs along the airship’s gunwale.

**Inside the Airship.** Those who heeded the captain’s orders are in a room of their choosing within the airship’s interior.

#### Reaching the Helm

With the captain gone, the ship’s Helm is unoccupied. A character who wishes to right the airship must first reach and interface with the Helm.

**Inside the Airship.** Due to the ship’s tilted orientation, creatures inside the ship must climb to access other areas. A creature without a Climb Speed that attempts to climb inside the ship must succeed on a DC 11 Strength (Athletics) check or fall.

**On the Deck.** Creatures wearing harnesses on the ship’s deck can climb the deck’s vertical surface without falling. A creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls while wearing a harness.

As a Utilize action, a creature can make a DC 11 Strength (Athletics) check to remove the harness safely without falling. On a successful check, the harness is removed without incident. When a creature without a harness is pushed or gains the Prone condition, it must succeed on a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw or fall, potentially off the ship and to its death.

#### THC Response

The THC guards aboard The Recluse do not know that the Heist Squad is working with the Raven squad. Once the ship begins its dive and the Raven operatives vanish overboard, the remaining guards attempt to restore order. Four THC guards engage the characters if they see them moving toward the Helm or the prisoner compartment. The guards wear harnesses and use the ship’s rails and fittings to move safely around the tilted deck.

The guards are not trying to destroy the ship, but they will fight anyone they believe helped the Raven squad seize it. Characters who maintain their cover can redirect the guards, claim that they are responding to the emergency, or convince them that the captain ordered them to reach the Helm.

#### The Spirit in the Machine

A character attempting to operate the Helm notices an unusual telepathic presence reaching out to them when they touch it. The presence is the spirit absorbed into The Recluse’s elemental core. It is initially Indifferent to the character.

A character that takes the Influence action and succeeds on a DC 17 Charisma (Deception, Intimidation, or Persuasion) check changes the presence’s attitude to Friendly and convinces it to allow access to the Helm’s actions. Characters proficient with Navigator’s Tools make this check with Advantage. The presence’s attitude changes to Hostile if the check fails by 5 or more.
