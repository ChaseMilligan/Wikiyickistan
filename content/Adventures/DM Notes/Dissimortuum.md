Through your research of the history of the Stickyickistanian civil war, the [[Emerald Blight]], and the [[Ruins of Spliffholm]], you learned of the existence of a horrid creature of biomantic origins. During the [[Emerald Blight]], [[Valmorra the Hollowed]] used a forbidden bone-grafting technique to create creatures she called Dissimortuum. These twisted undead humanoids have gray flesh and black claws that drip blood, and usually a third arm hangs from the side of the creature’s body, its hand clutching a large sack stained with blood. These creatures were once used as weapons of terror during the civil war, but now they wander the [[Ruins of Spliffholm]] collecting body parts from their victims.

Your curiosity in these creatures lead you to the [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]] where the Dungsweepers of the city toil. Today these folk deal with the everyday refuse of the city above, but during the civil war they were the ones who had to clean up the bloody necrotic messes left behind after the battles. In doing so, they encountered the Dissimortuum on occasion. Some older members of the Dungsweepers shared tales of them that have been passed down the line to today's Dungsweepers. Their oral history states that these creatures all posses some sort of cursed mask of bone. Not much can be said for sure about this relic other than it is nigh indestructible, and any Dungsweeper stupid enough to don it has gone mad. Most researchers best guess is that it may have something to do with how the Dissimortuum reproduces, or resurrects. Either way, information on this subject alone is quite sought after in the circles of shadowy intellectuals such as yourself aside from what else may be procured from the creature's corpse.
