# Acheron Pursuit Overview

1. Characters are informed of the bounty hunters plans to obtain a Crystal Ball of Scrying
2. Characters are given their own NEC for Quabriel, so they know where the bounty hunters are going should the decide to pursue them. This is part of the bounty hunter's plans.
3. If the players opt to pursue the bounty hunters, they can go to the Loot Tavern, to try to learn more about where they might be going, and anything else the bounty hunters may have said.
4. Then order a Rusty Mule, and walk back through the Tavern door into the streets of the Gate-city Rigus, where they are told the bounty hunters went to travel through the gate to Acheron where Quabriel resides.
5. Walking around the city streets asking anyone where the gate to Acheron is, they will know.
6. Eventually they will reach the seal of the gate which is guarded by 8 knights of Rigus' military.
7. If the characters approach them, a guard will glance down at a note held in his right palm, and ask them to state their business.
8. If the players choose to observe the gate for a while they should be able to very easily pick up on the fact that only military members have been approaching the gate, and they are all getting turned away. Asking around the city about the gate to Acheron with a successful DC 12 Persuasion can reveal that the seal is usually open, and has been since the semi-recent military victory over a massive incursion of goblinoid hoards from the other side of the gate.
9. The players don't know, but the bounty hunters have made a deal with the guards to aid them in catching the PCs, so they have closed the seal to make sure the PCs are the only ones who can walk into the trap the bounty hunters have set up
10. Regardless of what the PCs say their business is, the guards have been instructed not to trust the PCs which have been described on the note given to them by [[Solmenda]], so the guards will say they need to go get their superior before they can escort the PCs through the seal to the gate.
11. At any time the players can make a VDC insight check to try and gain the sense that something is afoot
12. When the characters reach the end of the seal, the guards will say this is where you go from on your own, and we will attempt to close the cold iron portcullis to close the seal again. Players can try to stop this. Ask them to make a perception when the enter the first chamber of the seal.
13. There will be two Glyph's of warding in the chamber placed so at least one of them will be triggered. A DC 15 Perception reveals Solmenda's exploding Glyph, and a DC 19 will reveal Hazeus's Glyph which is enspelled with Weave the Elder Sign
14. The bounty hunters are hiding in a 4th level Private Sanctum, and therefore cannot be perceived by any non-magical means, or divination spells. It can be dispelled.
15. Make sure to expend the spell slots needed to set up the trap.
16. If the players notice or trigger the trap, and the seal is closed behind them, the party will have to fight the bounty hunters to the death. The bounty hunters will first and foremost be trying to bind the party in Dimensional & Magebind Shackles. The Cold Iron Portcullis makes it impassable by magical means. 
17. The portcullis can be hit with an AC of 30 and 50 HP. It blocks spells cast at it, or any means of magical travel through it. A creature that attempts to pass through the portcullis by magical means must make a VDC Con save or take VDC force damage.
18. If the party defeats the bounty hunters they can harvest them if they want and loot all their magic items.
19. The guards will simply watch the battle happen since they have already been paid off by the bounty hunters, so they no longer care if the bounty hunters succeed. They aren't too concerned with the bounty hunter's business plus even if the bounty hunters fail they have the party trapped in their seal to the gate of Acheron. If the party succeeds, the guard will point out the very compromised position the party is in, and suggest they do as the guard says.
20. The guard will make a deal with the party. Either the party actually follows through with killing Quabriel since the bounty hunters can't anymore, or the guard will gather 100 troops to swarm you from the city side of the seal, and they will open the gate to Acheron allowing more hoards of goblinoid warriors through the other side, and the party will be in the middle. 
21. If the party agrees they will be told to approach the gate, and be ready for it to open, then jump in. They urge you to be fast, so they can close the gate once again.
22. The party of course can find another way to leave Acheron, like hiring an Oarsman on the Styx to take them to a tavern in the nine hells where they can use a [[Port Ale]] to return to the Loot Tavern.
