After spending your long week of enchanting at the citadel, you return to your old lab to check the mail and see you have received correspondence from [[Skarguther Dungsweeper]]. The letter starts off by explaining how glad he and his associates are about the procurement of a Dissimortuum mask. Skarguther will briefly mention his association with a group known as the [[Heralds of Dust]] that are based out of the city of Sigil. The [[Dungsweepers]] and the Dusters work closely together in [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]]. Every now and then, Skarguther travels to Sigil for "business meetings" with the Factol of the Dusters. The two of them are always interested in commissioning monster hunters to procure, or craft relics of undeath. However that is not why he has reached out to you this time. Skarguther explains that the Dungsweepers have had to shut down one of their trash compactors because of a mismanagement of hazardous arcane material that made it into the machine, and spawned some strange elemental creatures. This creature has obviously created an unsafe work environment for my employees, and our exorcists attempted to rid us of the monster, but it was ineffective. Based on what I read in the reports this creature seems to be bound to the hazardous waste in the trash compactor because it has not tried to leave. It appears generally shapeless, but takes on a serpentine form when it emerges from the surface. There are also now a few other creatures in the toxic liquid that may be some kind of offspring, so it must be dealt with ASAP. He hopes to meet with you once more in the Undercity where he can help you gain access to the affected trash compactor.
