> Esteemed colleague,
> 
> My associates and I are most pleased with the successful procurement of the Dissimortuum mask. Word of your work has reached even my contacts among the Heralds of Dust in Sigil. The Dungsweepers and the Dusters cooperate very closely here in the Undercity Osslurraggath to keep Stickyickistan clean, and make sure death is respected. Hopefully my association with the Dusters doesn't make you shy away from further business with us, but you seemed like a very open minded individual.
> 
> However, my present concern is less academic. One of our trash compactors has been shut down after hazardous arcane waste was mishandled. From the contaminated slurry, a strange, largely shapeless elemental has manifested. It rises in a serpentine form from the surface but appears bound to the toxic mass and has not attempted to leave the basin. I thought someone of your expertise may already be privy to, or be able to discern what creature this may be, and capable of exterminating it in the most efficient manner.
> 
> Our exorcists’ attempts to dispel or banish it have failed. Worse, smaller creatures now move within the same foul medium, likely offshoots or offspring. The situation is dangerous for my workers and untenable for our operations.

> 
> Skarguther Dungleboof

# Narrative/Combat Encounter: Molvayas and the Spawn of Kyuss

One of the most used entrances to the Undercity is an abandoned cathedral with a graveyard. While making your way through the graveyard on your way to the cathedral you hear a voice shout out in common

"Blast that god forsaken cult who brought this blight upon the innocent! They will never know Tue Death."

You can spot a humanoid not too far off in the distance, clearly an undead, that appears to be engaged in combat with a Zombie. He is adorned in an exquisite robe, and baubles of gold, and silver. You can see on the back of his lightly billowing robe is the same recognizable symbol that sealed the Letter you received.

Perception DC 14 sees a few more shambling zomboid figures. Perception DC 17 from a distance in the fog over 30 feet allows the player to discern these are not regular zombies, but something much scarier.

If Molvayas spots the player he will shout out for help. Molvayas will not attack the character unless attacked first.

If the player helps Molvayas in ridding the graveyard of the Kyuss spawn, he will bestow a charm of incorporeality upon them given they are willing.
