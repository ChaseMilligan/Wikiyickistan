# Greyhawk

In trying to work the Keep on the Borderlands into the Greyhawk campaign world, I think a good location would be Rookroost in the Bandit Kingdoms of the Flanaess. To make it work, I will say that the bordering Duchy of Tenh got fed up around 600 CY with the constant threat of the nearby Bandit Kingdom Capital hosting all sorts of bad characters, and serving as a strategic position for evil Iuzian forces. The Duchy of Tenh sent their troops to clash with the bandits of Rookroost who's guerilla tactics were no match for the rigidly structured attacks of the trained militia, and cavalry from Tenh. The war lasted a decade and saw the impressive bandit capital of Rookroost that once boasted a population of 17,000 completely razed to the ground. All except for the fortified keep on the hill at the center of the city was obliterated. After the war ended, A wealthy Baron and the adventurer's guild from Tenh took to mending the fortifications of the keep, and now use it as a bastion of peace amid an otherwise brutal wilderness of the Bandit Kingdoms. The keep’s stalwart defenders protect their community from the forces of chaos that would revel in its fall. The Castellan of the Keep makes sure things stay as orderly as possible, and often hires adventurer's from the guild house to make foray's into the wilderness in exchange for gold and or glory.

# Forgotten Realms

In the the Forgotten Realms campaign, the Keep of the Borderlands can easily be placed in the Backlands, and not much else needs to be said or changed. The Backlands are a region in the Western Heartlands of Faerun known for its sparse population, wild nature, and many monsters. It was called the Backlands mainly by those that dwelt on the Sword Coast. The central grasslands were known as Pelleor's Prairie.

There are no established borders, but the Backlands are generally described as the lands east of the Serpent's Tail Stream, the Forest of Wyrms, and the Trielta Hills, all the way to the edge of Anauroch. Its southern border was the River Reaching and the northern edge was usually defined as the southern-most Greypeak Mountains. This region included the Greycloak Hills even though some maps placed them at approximately the same latitude as the Greypeaks.

This region has no government to speak of. The Keep is the only semblance of law and order for miles around.

# Stickyickistan

The Keep could be the current day town of [[Doobius House|Doobius House]]. This area is kind of all that remains of the frontier on the island. It is too far from the city, and the coast to attract many residents, but it does attract burgeoning adventurers from the city who are looking to get their foot in the door of a guild. Most people who live here are native [[Halflings]], or [[Twothirdling]], but the guard is comprised mostly of [[Humans]] that are sent from the city in shifts by the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]].
