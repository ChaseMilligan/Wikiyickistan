# Hunt: Ashes in the Wind

**Level:** 6  
**Complexity:** 1  
**Duration:** 1 Session  
**Target:** Fire Elemental (Monster Manual)  
**Suggested Biome:** Volcanic or Wasteland (can be reskinned for deserts, scorched plains, or battlefield ruins)

---

## Introduction

Rumors speak of a burning creature that never dies, laying waste to nearby roads and ruins. Survivors describe it as a creature of flame and fury that incinerates all in its path, then vanishes into smoke.

---

## Background

A **Fire Elemental** has broken free from an unstable planar rift. It has taken refuge in a ruined shrine, feeding on ambient fire magic. Wildfires and ash storms plague nearby regions. The creature seems to be drawn to places of magical significance, igniting anything it nears.

### Quest Hooks

- **Curiosity:** Arcane scholars believe this elemental may offer insights into interplanar instability.
- **Morality:** Nearby settlements are threatened by rolling firestorms. Someone must stop the elemental before it reaches populated areas.
- **Compensation:** A local consortium offers **1,000 gp** and a **Potion of Fire Resistance** for the elemental’s destruction.

### Rewards

- **XP:** 5,000 (split among the party)
- **Gold:** 1,000 gp (upon completion)
- **Magic Item:** *Potion of Fire Resistance* (SRD)

### Key NPCs

- **Displaced Elder:** A calm but resolute voice for the refugees, seeking protection and relocation.
- **Warden of the Wastes:** A grim ranger tasked with monitoring regional threats, familiar with signs of elemental activity.
- **Arcanist Archivist:** A curious scholar offering lore and minor magical support in exchange for elemental samples.

---

## Location

The battle takes place in a scorched ruin, cracked by heat and trembling with fire magic. Ash falls like snow. Chunks of obsidian litter the field.

---

## Tracking the Elemental

The players must traverse a blasted, ash-choked wasteland to find the elemental’s lair. The tracking journey is divided into two zones:

### Zone 1: Scorched Approach  
**Type:** Rocky highlands  
**Checks Required:** 2  
**Successes Required:** 1  
**Check Duration:** 6 hours each

#### Suggested Checks
- **Wisdom (Survival)** – Look for signs of burned terrain. *(DC 13)*
- **Intelligence (Nature)** – Assess the creature’s path. *(DC 13)*
- **Wisdom (Perception)** – Spot smoke trails. *(DC 13)*
- **Dexterity (Acrobatics)** – Cross hazardous terrain. *(DC 13)*

**Failure = Hostile Encounter:** 2d4 Fire Mephits

---

### Zone 2: The Ashen Lair  
**Type:** Cracked obsidian shrine  
**Checks Required:** 3  
**Successes Required:** 2  
**Check Duration:** 2 hours each

#### Suggested Checks
- **Charisma (Performance)** – Mimic a fire call. *(DC 16)*  
- **Wisdom (Survival)** – Follow heatwaves or tracks. *(DC 13)*  
- **Intelligence (Arcana)** – Detect fire magic concentrations. *(DC 13)*  

**Critical Success = Shortcut to lair**  
**Critical Failure = Surprise attack by fire snake**

## Know Thy Enemy

### Clue 1: "It Burns Without Fuel"

- **Arcana or Nature (DC 13):** Fire Elementals are immune to fire, vulnerable to cold.
- **Prep:** Use cold-based spells or force damage.

### Clue 2: "It Moves Like Wind"

- **Perception (DC 13):** The creature moves quickly and unpredictably.
- **Prep:** Plan for mobility; slow spells are effective.

### Clue 3: "It Leaves No Corpse"

- **Religion or Arcana (DC 13):** It may return to the Elemental Plane if destroyed.
- **Prep:** Capture remains to prevent rebirth or return.

## The Battle Ahead

**Boss:** Fire Elemental (Monster Manual, CR 5, 1,800 XP)  
**Allies:** 2x Fire Mephits (CR 1/2 each, 100 XP each)  
**Total XP:** 2,000 XP  
**Tactical Adjustments:** Boost threat via lair effects and terrain.

**Lair Features:**

- **Flaming Floor:** DC 13 DEX or take 1d4 fire damage on movement.
- **Ash Clouds:** Lightly obscure vision; ranged attacks at disadvantage.

---

## Aftermath

### Curiosity:

Scholars study the elemental remains. The party may be hired to find the rift it came from.

### Morality:

Civilians return to scorched homes; a firebreak is built with party help.

### Compensation:

1,000 gp and a Potion of Fire Resistance awarded by the local consortium.
