### Public Notice

> **MINING EMERGENCY AT GREENQUARTZ™ MINE: LICENSED ADVENTURERS REQUESTED**

[[The Seekers of the Vein]] seeks licensed adventurers for an urgent expedition into the closed [[GreenQuartz™ Mine]] in [[The Outback]]. Eldritch creatures have emerged from the Hall of the Wyrmbone Anvil, attacking miners and prospectors. Several workers remain unaccounted for, and mining operations have been suspended until the danger is dealt with.

Applicants should be prepared for dangerous tunnels, hostile creatures, and ancient magic. The expedition should secure the mine and rescue any survivors. [[Pearl Firehand]], the FLS Guildhall blacksmith, asks the party to carry her smithing hammer into the Hall and place it upon the Wyrmbone Anvil, so it may be blessed and used to forge magical equipment.

The Seekers of the Vein will provide payment for a successful expedition.

## Rewards

- **Up to 1,200 XP each** for completing the adventure fully.
- **200 gp each** from [[The Seekers of the Vein]] if their intermediary survives.
- The FLS Guildhall blacksmith, Pearl, becomes capable of forging magic items according to the [[Sources/Crafting|Crafting]] rules if the party blesses her hammer upon the Wyrmbone Anvil. Currently she can only do manufacturing of mundane items, and items of exotic materials.
- Possible additional treasure recovered from the dungeon.
- Possible bonus XP for helping anyone trapped inside the mine or returning useful information about the threat.
