# Adventure Summary

**Curse of Slaad** is a dangerous investigation beginning at the [[FLS Guildhall]] on [[Nevercough Island]] and leading into [[The Outback]] near [[Zooka Mire]]. A [[N.E.R.D]] research outpost has gone silent while monitoring strange magical disturbances from beyond the Material Plane. The party must investigate the disappearance, uncover what happened at the outpost, and deal with whatever trouble is waiting there.

## Public Notice

The planar-studies faculty of [[N.E.R.D]] is seeking capable adventurers to investigate a failed research outpost near [[Zooka Mire]]. The outpost was established to monitor disturbances in the Weave around the [[Arcanite]]-rich regions near [[Mount Zooka]], but its researchers have stopped responding to messages and magical communication.

The FLS is authorized to investigate the site, locate any survivors, identify the cause of the disturbance, and prevent further danger to [[Stickyickistan]]. Applicants should be prepared for wilderness travel, hostile creatures, and unstable magic.

Interested adventurers should register at the [[FLS Guildhall]] on [[Nevercough Island]].

# What Players Should Know

- **Content / vibe:** Arcane horror, body horror, dangerous magic, academic negligence, and dark comedy.
- **Combat / roleplay / exploration mix:** Investigation, wilderness travel, dungeon exploration, and high-risk combat.
- **Character hooks:** Good for monster hunters, researchers, Free League contractors, bounty hunters, explorers, or adventurers protecting Stickyickistan.
- **Special notes:** Expect unstable magic, strange environments, and limited opportunities to rest. Characters with Arcana, Medicine, Survival, or monster-hunting skills will be especially useful.
