# Adventure Summary

**The Twilight Hunter** is a monster-hunt adventure beginning in [[Doobius House]] and leading into the dangerous [[Grim Forest]] of [[The Outback]]. Travelers and hunters have gone missing near the forest, and the residents of Doobius House need capable adventurers to investigate the attacks before the danger spreads to the village and the roads beyond it.

# The Rolling Papers Advertisement

**Submitted by:** [[Free League of Stickyickistan]]  
**Tier:** Tier 1  
**Recommended Level:** 3  
**Expected Length:** One-shot, approximately 3–4 hours  
**Tone:** Forest horror, monster hunt, dark mystery, dangerous wilderness  
**Location:** [[Doobius House]] / [[Grim Forest]]

## Headline

> **HUNTERS AND TRAVELERS MISSING NEAR THE GRIM FOREST: LICENSED ADVENTURERS REQUESTED**

## Public Notice

Travelers and local hunters have gone missing near the edge of the [[Grim Forest]]. The [[Free League of Stickyickistan]] is seeking capable adventurers to investigate the disappearances, locate survivors, and protect the residents and travelers of [[Doobius House]].

Applicants should report to the [[Hotbox Inn & Tavern]] in Doobius House and ask for [[Barnabas Huggins]], who can provide local information and directions into the forest.

# The Roach Clip Tip

A coded tip begins circulating independently after the public hunt notice appears in the Rolling Papers. An anonymous buyer offers discreet payment for possibly illegal biological and mechanical components recovered during the investigation, creating a competing recovery contract alongside the official hunt. The tip includes separate instructions for a dead drop and payment through an underground network.

# What Players Should Know

- **Content / vibe:** Forest horror, missing travelers, body horror, dangerous wildlife, biomantic weirdness, and dark comedy.
- **Combat / roleplay / exploration mix:** Investigation and tracking followed by dangerous wilderness exploration and a climactic multi-stage combat encounter.
- **Character hooks:** Good for monster hunters, Free League contractors, bounty hunters, researchers, wilderness explorers, Doobius House natives, or adventurers seeking unusual monster components.
- **Special notes:** Characters should be prepared for difficult tracking, limited visibility, traps, hostile creatures, and little downtime once the hunt begins. Ranged attacks and fire damage may be especially useful.

# Rewards

- **950 XP each** for defeating the primary threat and completing the hunt
- **125 gp each** for completing the official Free League investigation
- **250 gp each** for rescuing the missing hunters
- A separate, privately negotiated payment from the anonymous [[The Roach Clip]] buyer for qualifying biological and mechanical components recovered during the investigation
- Additional payment or research compensation may be available through a patron for useful observations or recoverable components
- Opportunity to harvest unusual monster components for crafting bespoke magic items
- Possible bonus XP at the DMs discretion.
