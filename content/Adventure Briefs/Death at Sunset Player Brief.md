### Public Notice

> **VILLAGERS MISSING FROM REDWOOD GROVE: HEROES REQUESTED BEFORE THE FOREST SICKENS FURTHER**

The council of [Redwood Watch](Redwood%20Watch.md) seeks capable adventurers to investigate a spreading blight in nearby [Redwood Grove](Redwood%20Grove.md). Once a symbol of peace between six reconciled Eladrin families, the grove has begun showing signs of corruption, and villagers who entered to investigate have not returned.

Applicants should be prepared for hazardous forest travel, frightened locals, old draconic legends, and a community desperate for answers. Council members [Gwenhumara Goldmoss](Gwenhumara%20Goldmoss.md) and [Selenar Woodwise](Selenar%20Woodwise.md) will meet responding adventurers in Redwood Watch to explain the situation and provide directions into the grove.

Redwood Watch asks that the missing villagers be found if possible, the source of the forest’s sickness identified, and any immediate threat to the village ended.

## History

Characters can learn the following by spending 4 hours studying in the library, or by paying the librarian to do it for you (1 gp per hour):

- Millenia ago, six quarreling Eladrin families reconciled and each planted a redwood sapling in a circular grove to symbolize their peace.
- The grove later became known for an ancient green dragon called **[[Death-at-Sunset]]**, named for the twilight attacks that made the surrounding forest infamous.
- About a century ago, the Eladrin warrior **[[Ethvarn]]** and her allies slew Death-at-Sunset. Ethvarn died in the battle, and the surviving Eladrin withdrew from the grove.
- [[Redwood Watch]] began as a small outpost of wardens left behind to watch the forest. Over time, it grew into the village that stands there today.
- Some villagers believe the current blight is tied to old dragon-haunted history. Others insist the dragon is long dead and that something else must be responsible.

## Rewards

- **Up to 900 XP each** for completing the adventure.
- **150 gp each** from the council of [Redwood Watch](Redwood%20Watch.md) for investigating the grove and returning with answers.
- Additional gratitude, lodging, and local support from Redwood Watch if the missing villagers are recovered or avenged.
- Possible treasure or useful materials recovered from the grove.
- Possible bonus XP for rescuing villagers, protecting Redwood Watch, preserving the grove, or uncovering the truth behind the corruption.
