# Adventure Summary

**Dread and Breakfast** is a monster-hunt adventure set in the [[Grim Forest]], a dangerous woodland within [[The Outback]] of [[Stickyickistan]]. Travelers and caravans have begun disappearing along the Neck, a trade route connecting [[North Booftown]] with inland settlements such as [[Doobius House]]. The characters are hired to investigate the disappearances and uncover the truth behind a welcoming roadside tavern that is far more alive than it appears.

The adventure culminates in a three-wave encounter inside a tavern mimic. Characters should be prepared for a strange, dangerous fight involving smaller mimics, shrinking magic, acid, and a creature that cannot be harmed from outside.

## Public Notice

Caravans and travelers have disappeared along the trade route through the [[Grim Forest]]. The [[Nevercough Tribunal]] seeks capable adventurers to investigate the disappearances, protect future caravans, and remove the creature responsible.

[[Dr. Stein]] offers additional compensation for the recovery of his missing laboratory or the safe return of his half-brother, [[Jonjo Starr]].

Applicants should report to the northern road station at the edge of the Grim Forest. Ask for the biomancy researcher with the metal lungs.

# What Players Should Know

- **Content / vibe:** Forest horror, missing travelers, acid, body horror, biomantic experiments, mimics, and dark comedy.
- **Combat / roleplay / exploration mix:** Investigation and tracking followed by a dangerous three-wave combat encounter.
- **Character hooks:** Good for monster hunters, caravan guards, Free League contractors, researchers, bounty hunters, or adventurers seeking payment and unusual monster components.
- **Special notes:** The party will investigate the Grim Forest, track the creature along the Neck, and enter a tavern mimic to fight it from within. Expect little downtime between combat waves.

# Character Creation

- Create a **3rd-level** character.
- Use the character creation rules in [[Character Building]].
- Any official species, class, or background is welcome. Stickyickistan is home to species from across the multiverse.
- Use standard array or roll ability scores before the session. If you do not roll beforehand, use standard array.
- Use fixed hit points when gaining levels.
- Choose a non-evil alignment.
- Your character should have a reason to accept monster-hunting or caravan-protection work and cooperate with the rest of the party.
- Consult the DM before bringing your character to the table. The DM has final approval over character options and concepts.

## Starting Equipment

Your 3rd-level character begins with:

- Normal starting equipment and gold.
- An additional 250 gp.
- One uncommon magic item.
- One common magic item.
- One uncommon [[Arcannabis]] zip.

Potions count as your uncommon magic item. Do not use deprecated homebrew magic items labeled “Arcannabis” on D&D Beyond. Use the current Arcannabis options approved by the DM.

## Stickyickistanian Backgrounds

Choose one of the following connections or create your own:

- A member or contractor of the [[Ashen Shadow Society]], a bounty-hunting guild that collects bounties, relics, and monster components. Characters associated with the Society are generally lawful neutral and cannot be evil-aligned.
- An employee or associate of [[The Seekers of the Vein]], a mining and jeweler’s guild with interests in rare magical materials.
- A member of the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]], a lawful mercenary organization operating throughout Stickyickistan.
- A caravan guard, merchant, or traveler who regularly uses the Neck.
- A researcher or former student connected to Dr. Stein or [[N.E.R.D]].
- A resident of [[North Booftown]] or [[Doobius House]] who wants to protect the trade route.
- A monster hunter responding to the public notice in [[The Rolling Papers]].

## Setting Context

- [[Stickyickistan]] is a magical island city-state dominated by the active volcano [[Mount Zooka]].
- [[The Outback]] is a vast wilderness beyond the city containing ruins, monsters, magical anomalies, and isolated settlements.
- [[Grim Forest]] is a dangerous forest within The Outback, shaped by colonial logging, old battlefields, magical contamination, and abandoned war machines.
- The Neck is a major trade route connecting [[North Booftown]] with inland settlements such as [[Doobius House]].
- The [[Nevercough Tribunal]] wants the route kept open, while Dr. Stein wants his missing laboratory and brother recovered.
- Mimics, biomantic experiments, and escaped creatures are known dangers in the region.

- Investigation
- Medicine
- Nature
- Perception
- Survival

Acid resistance, healing, control effects, and abilities that help with confined spaces may be especially useful.

## What Characters Know

Before play begins, characters know the following:

- Travelers and caravans have disappeared along the Neck.
- A strange roadside tavern has been seen near recent disappearances.
- Survivors report identical patrons, cold fires, and an innkeeper with an unnatural smile.
- Dr. Stein’s laboratory and half-brother, [[Jonjo Starr]], are also missing.
- The public contract offers payment for restoring safety to the route and additional compensation for finding Jonjo.
- The creature may not be vulnerable to attacks made from outside the tavern.

## Character Hooks

Your character might:

- Have answered the public notice in [[The Rolling Papers]].
- Be responsible for protecting a caravan traveling along the Neck.
- Know someone who disappeared in the Grim Forest.
- Have professional or personal ties to Dr. Stein, [[N.E.R.D]], the [[Ashen Shadow Society]], or the [[Nevercough Tribunal]].
- Want to study mimics, biomancy, or unusual monster components.
- Be seeking payment, fame, or future work from the Tribunal or Free League.

# Rewards

- **250 gp per character** for restoring safety to the Neck.
- **125 gp per character** for finding Dr. Stein’s brother.
- XP from the adventure’s encounters.
- Additional coins and monster components from the tavern mimic.
- Possible future work from Dr. Stein, the [[Nevercough Tribunal]], or the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]].
- The opportunity to harvest unusual mimic components for crafting.
