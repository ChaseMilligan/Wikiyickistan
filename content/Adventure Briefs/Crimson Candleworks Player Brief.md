### Public Notice

> **TRIBUNAL SEEKS LICENSED ADVENTURERS FOR SUSPECTED ILLEGAL ARCANNABIS OPERATION**

The [Tribunal Highblade Coalition](Tribunal%20Highblade%20Coalition) seeks licensed adventurers for an urgent investigation in the [Old Market District](Old%20Market%20District). [Wick & Wax Candleworks](Wick%20%26%20Wax%20Candleworks), a seemingly ordinary candle shop, is suspected of aiding the distribution of a prohibited [Arcannabis](Arcannabis) strain within [Stickyickistan](Stickyickistan).

Applicants should be prepared for a lawful search, possible resistance, suspicious merchandise, and conditions unsuitable for casual shoppers. The Coalition authorizes the recovery of evidence, seizure of illegal product, and apprehension or elimination of hostile parties as necessary.

Responding adventurers should report to the [Highblade Civic Bureau](Highblade%20Tower) and ask for [Ziggy Tootokes](Ziggy%20Tootokes), who will provide the warrant details, any known risks, and directions to the target site.

## Rewards

- **Up to 1,200 XP each** for completing the adventure.
- **185 gp each** from the Coalition bounty and recovered currency.
- Possible additional valuables recovered as evidence or treasure, including gemstones or marked criminal property.
- Any mundane equipment recovered from defeated enemies, subject to DM approval.
- Possible future work and investigative support from [Ziggy Tootokes](Ziggy%20Tootokes) and the [Tribunal Highblade Coalition](Tribunal%20Highblade%20Coalition).
- Possible bonus XP for recovering evidence, securing illegal product, protecting civilians, or apprehending those responsible.
