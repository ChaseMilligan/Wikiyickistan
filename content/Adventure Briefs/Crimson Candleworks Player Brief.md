# Adventure Summary

**Crimson Candleworks** is an urban investigation in [[Stickyickistan]] involving an apparently ordinary candle shop in the [[Old Market District]]. The [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] believes the business is connected to an illegal [[Arcannabis]] operation and needs licensed adventurers to investigate the property, recover evidence, and bring those responsible to justice.

## Headline

> **TRIBUNAL SEEKS LICENSED ADVENTURERS TO INVESTIGATE SUSPECTED ILLEGAL ARCANNABIS OPERATION**

## Public Notice

The [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] is seeking capable adventurers to investigate [[Wick & Wax Candleworks]] in the [[Old Market District]]. The business is suspected of supporting the distribution of a prohibited [[Arcannabis]] strain throughout [[Stickyickistan]].

Applicants should expect resistance and are authorized to investigate the premises, recover evidence, secure illegal product, and eliminate or apprehend those responsible. Report to [[Highblade Tower]] and ask for [[Ziggy Tootokes]].

# What Players Should Know

- **Content / vibe:** Urban crime, illicit Arcannabis, undead, body horror, murder, and dark comedy.
- **Combat / roleplay / exploration mix:** Investigation, infiltration, evidence gathering, and dangerous close-quarters combat.
- **Character hooks:** Good for monster hunters, investigators, city guards, Free League contractors, bounty hunters, Arcannabis enthusiasts, or adventurers seeking legitimate work and unusual monster components.
- **Special notes:** Characters should be prepared to investigate a business that may not be what it appears to be. Stealth, social skills, investigation, and combat abilities may all be useful.

- A silver signet ring bearing a criminal organization’s symbol
- Any mundane equipment recovered from defeated enemies
- Possible future work and investigative support from [[Ziggy Tootokes]] and the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]]
