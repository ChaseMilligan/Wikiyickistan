### Public Notice

> **THC SEEKS LICENSED ADVENTURERS AFTER KNIGHTS VANISH NEAR UNCOVERED ARCANNABIS GROWTH**

The [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] seeks licensed adventurers for an urgent recovery and containment contract beyond the city roads of [[Stickyickistan]]. Recent ground collapse in [[The Outback]] has exposed the buried Shrine of the Oathsworn Knights, an old Highblade military shrine now threaded with a sealed growth of wild [[Arcannabis]].

Applicants should be prepared for unstable ruins, oathbound dead, arcanite interference, and unusual Arcannabis pollen. **Sir Emil Corvian**, **Sir Petros Valescu**, and **Squire Joseph Wrenhollow** were sent to secure the site and examine the growth, but no report has been received. Once a party is formed, report to the THC contract desk at [[Highblade Tower]] on [[Nevercough Island]], or to the guildhall officer holding this notice, for travel papers and route details.

The Coalition offers payment to capable adventurers who can determine the fate of the missing patrol, document the unusual Arcannabis growth, recover survivors if possible, and ensure that whatever still keeps watch beneath the ruins does not wander back toward Stickyickistan.

## Rewards

- **Up to 600 XP each** for completing the adventure.
- **225 gp each** from the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]] for investigating the shrine, documenting the Arcannabis growth, and resolving the disturbance.
- Gratitude, formal commendation, or future contract preference from the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]].
- Possible future favor with House Highblade contacts for discreet handling of military relics and rare Arcannabis specimens.
- The opportunity to claim approved salvage, monster components, and non-restricted relics after Coalition review.
- Possible bonus XP for rescuing missing personnel, preserving important records, protecting viable Arcannabis samples, or resolving the matter without needless damage to the shrine.
