_[Free League of the Backlands](https://www.dndbeyond.com/campaigns/join/71003411012186196)_ is set in the classic high-fantasy world of Faerûn. This counts as a season in the Forgotten Realms campaign setting, and characters from other Forgotten Realms adventures can be played, assuming they abide by the **[House League Ruleset](https://chasemilligan.github.io/Wikiyickistan/House-League/House-League-Ruleset)** restrictions. This season will definitely take your characters from level 1 to 3, and is meant to help get new / more people into the game by keeping sessions short, and non-committal. It is possible to run higher tier adventures in this setting, but it will focus on tier 1 adventures. There are many popular pre-written tier 2 modules that can be used to follow up adventures from this season.

### **The Backlands**
lie deep within the **Western Heartlands** of Faerun, a rugged frontier between civilization and the uncharted wilds. Known for its **sparse population, rolling grasslands, and monstrous denizens**, the region is spoken of with a mixture of awe and dread by those who dwell along the **Sword Coast**. Merchants and nobles in Waterdeep call it _the edge of the world_, though many a brave soul has ventured there seeking fortune, freedom, or simply a place beyond the reach of kings.

The Backlands stretch **east of the Serpent’s Tail Stream, the Forest of Wyrms, and the Trielta Hills**, reaching as far as the **barren edge of Anauroch**. To the south runs the **River Reaching**, while the **southernmost Greypeak Mountains** mark the region’s upper limit. Within these boundaries lie the **Greycloak Hills** and the **central grasslands known as Pelleor’s Prairie**. Wide, windblown plains where herds of aurochs roam and ancient ruins sink into the soil of forgotten empires.

There are **no kingdoms here**, no banners to rally behind. Long since ancient battlegrounds, monster-haunted trails, and fallen strongholds struggle against the hostile wilderness. Except of course for, **the Keep of the Backlands**. A Stone fort on the north side of the River Reaching, it is the sole beacon of law and order for miles around. Its walls guard a tenuous peace between humanity and the encroaching chaos of the wild. To the folk who dwell within, it is home and haven; to adventurers, it is **the final outpost before the frontier**.

*1 hex = 40 miles. Approx. 2 days travel.*

The town of **Hill's Edge** on the **Dusk Road** south of the River Reaching is a tenday's travel due south of the Keep, and is generally the last stop in civilization for adventurer's travelling to the Keep from the Sword Coast. The trail to the Keep is a roughly beaten in dirt trail surrounded by thick brambly foliage.

# House League

This campaign will follow a structure similar to **Adventurer’s League**, short, episodic sessions allowing players to rotate characters and DMs freely, without disrupting story continuity. We’ll use a standardized **[House League Ruleset](https://chasemilligan.github.io/Wikiyickistan/House-League/House-League-Ruleset)**, loosely based on RAW with a few amendments for balance, pacing, and cross-table consistency.


> [!NOTE] DM’s Note  
> Other DMs are encouraged to run adventures in the Forgotten Realms! That way people can play their characters and progress in your game too. Planescape, and Spelljammer settings are compatible with Forgotten Realms.

# Character Creation

Join the [campaign](https://www.dndbeyond.com/campaigns/join/71003411012186196) on D&D Beyond. 

Characters begin at level 1. Use **point buy** or the **standard array**. Follow the **PHB + 1** rule (Player’s Handbook plus one other official source). Optional backgrounds and species from other Forgotten Realms books such as; _Sword Coast Adventurer’s Guide_, _Mordenkainen Presents_, etc. — are allowed as long as they fit thematically.

#### Species

Any species from these sources. Must be within the PHB+1 restriction.
- Player's Handbook / Basic Rules (2014 or 2024)
- Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse
- Spelljammer: Adventures in Space
- The Tortle Package
- One Grung Above
- Mordenkainen’s Tome of Foes
- Elemental Evil Player's Companion
- Volo's Guide to Monsters

A warning for players that the Keep of the Backlands is not a large population center, so expensive adventuring equipment will likely not be available. For example, if you make a character with Spelljammer options like a laser pistol, you will not be able to purchase the energy cells needed as ammo for the laser pistol in the Keep. You could get it shipped to the Keep for five times the listed price in 6d6 days.

#### Backgrounds

Choose any background from the Core Rules within PHB+1.

# The Realms

The world of the Forgotten Realms spans continents and countless cultures, but the League’s adventures will focus mainly on the area around the Keep of the Backlands. Other locales may appear as one-shots or side arcs, but the League’s heart remains in the wild frontier around the Keep. Some players might play through the same adventure more than once as different characters. Make sure to let your DM know if you have already done an adventure as a different character, so they can maybe switch some stuff up, or take other anti-meta gaming measures. Everyone is expected to be respectful of the game, and not use out of character information. Since all of this is Core content, experienced players have likely seen many of these stat blocks before. Again, DMs have the free will to make adjustments based on APL, and player knowledge, but are not expected to customize an entire adventure around characters.

# Experienced Players

Some adventures might allow for a a single adventurer, and their sidekick (Tasha's Cauldron). Since some of the adventures in this season are meant for inexperienced players, I may run adventures like this for more experienced players that want an extra challenge.
