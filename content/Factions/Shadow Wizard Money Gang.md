The **Shadow Wizard Money Gang** (_SWMG_) are an infamous crew of spell-slinging degenerates who thrive in the streets of [[Stickyickistan]] and [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]]. They are equal parts **occult fraternity, stoner cult, digital thieves’ guild, and black-market cartel**.

### **What They’re About**

The Shadows’ philosophy is simple:

> _“We love drugs. We love guns. We love nukes. We love drinking lean, and Runescape. We love robbing people on Runescape. But most of all—we love casting spells.”_

In their eyes, everything is currency—whether it’s coin, contraband, pixels, or power. They can often be seen abusing healing magic even when at full health just for the rush.

### **Operations**

- **Drug Alchemy** – they brew and sell psychoactive potions, shrooms, fentanyl, and [[Alcohol]] out of trap houses across [[Stickyickistan]] and [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]].
- **Arcane Firearms & Boom Magic** – shadow-forged pistols, enchanted crossbows, and unstable “nukes” (jerry-rigged magical detonators).
- **Runescape Rackets** – yes, even in Stickyickistan they play and scam in [[Runescape]]. They love killing and robbing players for all their loot, then claiming to be "smoking on they pack" in chat.
- **Street Robbery** – invisibility cloaks, charm person muggings, and shadow-step getaways.
- **Spell Hustling** – everything from fake divinations to lethal fireballs for hire.

### **Culture & Aesthetic**

- Constantly drinking an alcoholic concoction of their own creation they call "[[Lean]]".
- Flashy robes patched with graffiti sigils and gold chains.
- They chant absurd slogans in the streets like _“We love casting spells!”_ while tossing off minor cantrips for fun.
- Guild houses vary, but can look like trap dens crossed with arcane laboratories, with half-collapsed PCs hooked into planar networks.
