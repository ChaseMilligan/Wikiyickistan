## Overview

Formed by [[House Stonemug]], **The Seekers of the Vein** is a mining and jeweler’s guild that has grown into an economic empire. The Seekers control much of the extraction, processing, and distribution of [[Arcanite]], adamantine, rare gemstones, and other valuable minerals in [[Stickyickistan]]. Their influence reaches from the mines beneath [[Mount Zooka]] to the forges and refineries of [[Bongstad]], and from local workshops to international trade routes.

The Seekers are officially a guild of prospectors, miners, jewelers, surveyors, engineers, and merchants. In practice, they function as a vertically integrated resource monopoly. They identify deposits, secure mining rights, extract the ore, process the material, craft finished goods, and negotiate the prices at which those goods enter the market.

## Power and Influence

Every major shipment of Arcanite, adamantine, or rare gemstones moving through Bongstad is expected to pass through Seeker-controlled channels. This gives the guild considerable influence over supply, pricing, industrial contracts, and the development of [[Magitech]]. Their wealth allows them to fund expeditions, hire mercenaries, influence officials, and absorb smaller mining concerns before they become serious competitors.

The Seekers are not the most politically powerful faction in Stickyickistan, but they are difficult to oppose openly. The [[Nevercough Tribunal]] relies on their mines and refineries, while industrial guilds rely on their raw materials. Even rivals who resent the Seekers’ control often need access to their supply networks.

## Operations

### Mining and Prospecting

The Seekers maintain mines and survey stations throughout Stickyickistan, especially around [[Mount Zooka]] and the volcanic regions near [[Bongstad]]. Their prospectors use geological expertise, divination, Magitech instruments, and hired adventurers to locate new veins before competitors can discover them.

Some expeditions are routine mining operations. Others enter unstable lava tunnels, abandoned ruins, monster nests, or regions where Arcanite has warped the environment. The guild is willing to accept significant risk when a deposit promises enough profit.

### Processing and Manufacturing

Raw materials are transported to Seeker-controlled facilities where they are sorted, refined, enchanted, and prepared for sale. Their metalworkers supply weaponsmiths, armorers, artificers, and industrial manufacturers. Their jewelers produce enchanted accessories and luxury items for nobles, merchants, and wealthy adventurers.

The Seekers’ partnership with [[The Forgewright Guild]] ensures that much of Stickyickistan’s [[Magitech]] industry receives materials sourced from Stonemug-controlled mines. This relationship gives both factions enormous influence over the city-state’s technological development.

### Salvage and Waste

The Seekers’ industrial operations produce enormous amounts of waste. Broken extraction machinery, spent Arcanite casings, contaminated runoff, slag, and failed Magitech are transported to [[Seekers' Scrapyard]], an 800-acre salvage site in the [[Industrial Zone]].

The guild claims that valuable material is recovered and that hazardous waste is carefully contained. In reality, the scrapyard is difficult to monitor, and scavengers, cultists, rival factions, and dangerous creatures frequently move through its waste fields. The [[Zooka Mire Collective]] and [[The Iron Sovereign]] also reclaim discarded materials for their own purposes.

## Craftsmanship and Commerce

The Seekers have built their reputation on exceptional craftsmanship and aggressive business tactics. Their jewelers create some of the finest enchanted accessories in the city-state, while their metalworkers produce components sought after by the best artificers.

Their merchants are equally important to the guild’s success. They manipulate supply, delay shipments, play buyers against one another, and use exclusive contracts to create artificial scarcity. A customer may believe they are negotiating for a rare gemstone when the price was decided months earlier in a sealed meeting between guild representatives.

## Security and Enforcement

The Seekers employ guards, mercenaries, survey teams, legal advocates, and specialized adventurers to protect their interests. Security duties include defending mines, escorting shipments, suppressing theft, investigating sabotage, and removing creatures from valuable sites.

When possible, the guild avoids direct responsibility for violence. Contractors and hired adventurers are given narrow objectives and limited information, allowing the Seekers to deny involvement if an operation becomes politically inconvenient. Competitors who threaten the guild’s control may find their equipment sabotaged, their contracts withdrawn, or their access to essential materials quietly blocked.

## Relationship with Other Factions

- **[[House Stonemug]]:** The noble family behind the Seekers. The guild provides House Stonemug with wealth, leverage, and a powerful industrial base.
- **[[The Forgewright Guild]]:** Strategic partners in the processing and manufacture of Magitech. Their cooperation keeps Stickyickistan’s industrial sector supplied.
- **[[Bongstad]]:** A major center of Seeker mining, refining, and metalworking activity. The relationship is profitable but sometimes strained by Dwarven resistance to outside control.
- **[[Nevercough Tribunal]]:** A political authority that depends on Seeker materials while attempting to regulate the guild’s influence.
- **[[Zooka Mire Collective]]:** Occasional reclaimers of industrial waste and potential rivals in the control of discarded resources.
- **[[The Iron Sovereign]]:** A dangerous faction interested in the same abandoned Magitech and mechanical components found in the guild’s waste streams.
- **[[Seekers' Scrapyard]]:** The guild’s principal dumping ground, salvage operation, and recurring source of security problems.

## Reputation

To wealthy merchants and industrialists, the Seekers are reliable partners who transform dangerous resources into prosperity. To independent miners, they are a monopolistic employer that buys discoveries cheaply and controls access to the best claims. To workers, they are a source of stable wages and dangerous labor. To their political enemies, they are an economic empire hiding behind the language of guild administration.

The Seekers consider themselves explorers and entrepreneurs rather than tyrants. They believe that whoever discovers, extracts, refines, and protects a resource has earned the right to control it. Their critics argue that the guild’s greatest talent is not finding veins beneath the earth, but finding ways to make everyone else dependent on them.

## Adventure Hooks

- The Seekers hire the party to escort a survey team into an unstable Arcanite mine.
- A rival mining company claims the Seekers sabotaged its equipment and asks the party for protection.
- A shipment from [[Bongstad]] disappears before reaching the guild’s refinery.
- Something in [[Seekers' Scrapyard]] has begun consuming discarded Magitech.
- The guild offers a lucrative contract to recover a gemstone from a monster-infested ruin, but conceals the true danger.
- Workers at a Seeker mine begin hearing voices from behind a newly opened Arcanite vein.
