# ASSCUNT

**Abbreviation:** ASSCUNT  
**Parent Organization:** [[Ashen Shadow Society]]  
**Director:** [[Kezrin Malvek]]  
**Headquarters:** [[Ashen Citadel]]

The **Ashen Shadow Society Creature Utilisation & Neutralisation Taskforce**, commonly shortened to **ASSCUNT**, is a specialized subsidiary of the [[Ashen Shadow Society]] dedicated to the capture, study, harvesting, and neutralization of dangerous creatures.

Unlike ordinary bounty-hunting squads, ASSCUNT teams are expected to recover more than a target’s head or corpse. Monster organs, venom, magical glands, bones, hides, chitin, blood, mutagenic fluids, and other useful components are catalogued and delivered to the Society’s researchers. The taskforce exists to turn the dangers of [[The Outback]] and the wider multiverse into equipment, weapons, medicines, arcane components, and profitable research.

## Purpose and Mandate

ASSCUNT operates under a dual mandate:

1. **Utilisation:** Recover valuable biological and magical components from monsters and other dangerous creatures.
2. **Neutralisation:** Eliminate or contain creatures that threaten [[Stickyickistan]], the [[Ashen Shadow Society]], or the Society’s clients.

The taskforce also investigates unusual specimens, tracks invasive creatures, escorts researchers into hostile environments, and secures sites containing dangerous remains or unstable biomantic materials. A mission may begin as a routine bounty and become an excavation, containment operation, salvage expedition, or field experiment.

## Operations

ASSCUNT squads are often assigned targets that ordinary hunters consider too strange, dangerous, or difficult to process. They may be sent to recover a single rare organ, clear a creature from a settlement, investigate a monster outbreak, or retrieve biological material before a rival faction finds it.

Recovered components are transported to the [[Ashen Citadel]], where they are sorted, preserved, and distributed. Some materials are sold to approved clients. Others are sent to the Citadel’s forges, laboratories, alchemical facilities, or biomantic researchers. Particularly hazardous specimens are held in containment until they can be destroyed or studied safely.

The taskforce maintains working relationships with the Society’s pilots, handlers, researchers, armorers, and information specialists. It also has reasons to monitor industrial sites such as [[Seekers' Scrapyard]], where discarded [[Magitech]], alchemical waste, and monster remains can attract opportunistic creatures.

## Field Protocols

ASSCUNT hunters are expected to follow several basic protocols:

- Record the creature’s behavior, habitat, and known weaknesses before harvesting it.
- Preserve components according to their volatility, magical charge, and risk of contamination.
- Mark any corpse or specimen exposed to curses, parasites, spores, or transformative effects.
- Avoid transporting unstable materials through populated areas whenever possible.
- Report rival harvesting operations, cult activity, and unauthorized biomantic experimentation.
- Treat recovered components as Society property unless a contract states otherwise.

These protocols are frequently ignored when a squad is under pressure, when a specimen is unusually valuable, or when a hunter believes the Society is concealing information.

## Rank and Squad Structure

ASSCUNT operates through small field squads drawn from the wider Society. Most squads begin as Novice units and can earn promotion through successful contracts, recovered components, and the neutralization of high-risk threats. The first recognized rank above Novice is Bloodhound.

The taskforce’s first known Bloodhound squad is [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]]. Its members have gained a reputation for surviving missions that should have killed less experienced hunters, including operations involving criminal syndicates, dangerous arcane artifacts, hostile cults, and creatures from the Outback.

## Reputation

Within the Ashen Shadow Society, ASSCUNT is respected for producing results and feared for what its members bring back. Other guilds see the taskforce as a source of rare materials, dangerous competitors, or both. The public knows little about its mandate, though rumors circulate about hunters returning from the Outback with jars of living tissue, speaking bones, cursed organs, and specimens that continue moving after death.

The taskforce’s official position is that every creature is either a threat, a resource, or a problem requiring further study. Its hunters are still deciding which category best describes the Society itself.
