## Overview

The **Zooka Mire Collective** is a breakaway sect of the [[Golgari Swarm]] that established itself in [[Zooka Mire]], a swampy region of [[The Outback]] just beyond the limits of [[Stickyickistan]]. The Collective also maintains a presence in [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]], where it frequently clashes with the [[Heralds of Dust]] over territory, resources, and competing philosophies of decay.

The Collective does not consider itself a conventional guild or political faction. It behaves more like a single organism whose members function as specialized tissues. Individual members retain their own identities, but their labor, knowledge, and resources are ultimately directed toward the survival and expansion of the whole.

## Philosophy

The Collective embraces the tension between life and death. It encourages growth in Stickyickistan’s decaying communities while spreading decay through places that have grown stagnant or overdeveloped. To its members, decomposition is not destruction. It is transformation, nourishment, and preparation for the next cycle.

The Collective holds [[Zuggtmoy]] and [[Cubensis]] in high regard, though their followers interpret fungal life in very different ways. Most members would willingly become one of Zuggtmoy’s mindless thralls if given the opportunity. Within [[The Rotwake]], however, devotion is divided more evenly between Zuggtmoy and Cubensis.

## Role in Stickyickistan

The Collective performs services that the city-state needs but prefers not to acknowledge. Its members dispose of carcasses, organic refuse, and [[Magitech]] waste produced by civilization. They also cultivate food, fungi, medicines, vermin, and other resources for forgotten communities and the urban poor.

The Collective maintains a tense but necessary relationship with the [[Dungsweepers]]. The Dungsweepers are the city’s official street and stable cleaners, responsible for keeping public spaces clear of waste and refuse. The Collective handles what the guild cannot safely, legally, or practically manage, including industrial runoff, contaminated carcasses, magical waste, and refuse from the deepest parts of [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]]. Dungsweepers often deliver sealed loads to Collective agents rather than risk transporting them through official disposal channels.

The relationship is built on mutual dependence rather than trust. The Dungsweepers need the Collective’s knowledge of decomposition, fungi, vermin, and hazardous materials, while the Collective relies on the guild’s access to the city’s streets, stables, drains, and waste routes. Some Dungsweepers exchange information or supplies with the Collective, while others consider the arrangement a dangerous compromise that allows a growing faction to spread beneath the city.

The guild’s poverty and lack of workers make the Collective’s assistance difficult to refuse. The Collective sometimes provides food, medicines, pest control, and protection to Dungsweepers who cooperate with it. In return, the Dungsweepers may conceal Collective activity, redirect waste shipments, or warn them when [[The Nevercough Tribunal]] begins an inspection. The guild’s CFO, [[Skarguther Dungleboof]], is therefore an important intermediary, though it remains unclear whether Skarguther is a willing ally, a pragmatic negotiator, or simply trying to keep the city’s refuse from becoming everyone else’s problem.

This makes the Dungsweepers a potential bridge between surface society and the Collective. If the relationship collapses, Stickyickistan could face overflowing streets, contaminated waterways, uncontrolled vermin, and open conflict in the undercity. If it deepens, the Dungsweepers may become the Collective’s most influential human-facing network.

This makes the Collective both useful and dangerous. Its scavengers and decomposers operate at the fringes of the food chain, but they are not content to remain there. The Collective seeks to become a dominant ecological and political force, capable of deciding what grows, what decays, and who receives the resulting nourishment.

## Organization

The Collective is organized according to biological function rather than conventional rank. Its internal structure resembles a living organism:

- **Nucleus:** The leadership and decision-making center that directs the Collective’s growth.
- **Nutrient Systems:** Scavengers, cultivators, alchemists, and processors who convert waste into food, medicine, materials, and magical resources.
- **Defenses:** Warriors, undead, insects, and plant creatures that protect the Collective from outside threats.
- **Sensory Network:** Spies, messengers, insects, fungi, and sympathetic communities that report activity across the Collective’s territory.
- **Reproductive Growth:** Colonists, converts, fungal cultures, undead plants, and other members who establish new outposts and expand the Collective’s influence.

## Leadership

The current Guild Master is [[Fester Von Zaaza]], an undead necromancer who revived himself after death. Fester’s authority rests less on tradition than on his ability to preserve the Collective, defeat rivals, and keep its many communities connected.

Fester is advised by [[The Rotwake]], a council of shamans, arcanists, and rogues. The Rotwake serves as both advisory parliament and intelligence network. Its members rarely convene publicly or all at once, making it difficult for outsiders to determine who speaks for the Collective at any given moment.

Fester’s insect minions extend the Rotwake’s reach. They act as scouts, sensory organs, and messengers, carrying instructions between distant enclaves and reporting disturbances at the Collective’s edges. Fester may privately rank members of the Rotwake, but those rankings are known only to him.

## Succession and Internal Politics

The Collective experiences more changes in leadership than many established guilds. Its members believe in continuous cycles and accept assassination as a legitimate means of political conquest. A leader need not be alive to rule, and death is not always considered a defeat.

This philosophy creates a shifting political environment. A successful challenger may replace a leader, merge with their remains, bind their spirit, or preserve their predecessor as a useful adviser. Alliances between Rotwake members are often temporary, and apparent loyalty may represent patience rather than obedience.

## Territories

### Zooka Mire

[[Zooka Mire]] is the Collective’s primary territory. Its swamps provide shelter, food, burial grounds, laboratories, and breeding sites for the Collective’s plant-undead hybrids, insects, fungi, and native Halfling communities. The Collective’s influence spreads through waterways, root systems, fungal networks, and scavenger trails.

### The Undercity

In [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]], the Collective competes with the [[Heralds of Dust]]. The two factions share an interest in death, refuse, and forgotten communities, but disagree over what those things should become. The Collective views decay as a living cycle, while the Heralds treat it as evidence that life is merely a temporary mistake.

## Roles

- **[[Zaazarin]]:** Native [[Halflings]] of [[Zooka Mire]] who serve as guides, cultivators, hunters, traders, and community leaders.
- **[[Mortispora]]:** The ranks of the dead, including undead laborers, guardians, shamans, and preserved ancestors.
- **[[Gutterhive]]:** Shepherds of the cycle who tend insects, fungi, vermin, waste systems, and the Collective’s living networks.

## Notable Members

- [[Fester Von Zaaza]], Guild Master and undead necromancer
- [[Sibrek Hollowspore]], notable Collective member and creator of specialized Warforged

## Adventure Hooks

- The Collective offers to solve a city district’s waste problem, but its solution would spread fungal growth through nearby neighborhoods.
- A Rotwake member hires the party to assassinate a rival and preserve the normal cycle of leadership.
- A shipment of dangerous Magitech waste disappears between the Industrial Zone and Zooka Mire.
- The Heralds of Dust accuse the Collective of stealing corpses from Osslurraggath.
- A village in The Outback asks the party to protect it from a new Gutterhive colony.
- Fester Von Zaaza dies again, and three competing members of the Rotwake claim to be his successor.
