Formally known as the 4th Provisional Expeditionary Force to Reclaim [[Drakkenheim]], the Hooded Lanterns are an irregular military drawn up from veterans of the Colonial War and remnants of the old [[Spliffholm]] City Watch. Led by the dour Lord Commander Elias Drexel, they wage a guerrilla war against the monsters, bandits, and scavengers that have taken root in the ruins. Each has pledged their swords and lives to rebuild Spliffholm and restore the ruins. Some noble houses have set aside their feuds to support the Hooded Lantern’s efforts. These families believe once the city is restored, evidence will emerge revealing the true heir to the throne of Spliffholm... or enable a cunning house to seize control of the city.
