# Blossom Union

The **Blossom Union** is a fellowship of druid-monks who maintain [[Springarden]], a sacred garden deep within [[Springwood]]. They care for the garden’s ancient trees, flowering paths, holy springs, and fey crossings while protecting pilgrims who come seeking healing, peace, and communion with nature.

The Union combines druidic stewardship with disciplined martial training. Its members cultivate plants, tend wounded creatures, guide travelers, study fey activity, and defend Springarden from threats. They believe nature is strongest when growth, decay, instinct, and wisdom remain in balance.

The Union welcomes visitors but expects them to respect the garden. Polluting the pools, harming its creatures, damaging sacred plants, or stealing shrine offerings is considered a serious offense. Those who violate the garden may be barred from Springarden or required to perform a period of service under the Union’s supervision.

## Springarden’s Keepers

The Union maintains the garden through several duties:

- Caring for the sacred pools and their giant flying koi.
- Maintaining paths, bridges, shrines, and flowering groves.
- Treating injured pilgrims, caretakers, and forest creatures.
- Monitoring the boundary between the Material Plane and the Plane of Fey.
- Protecting the garden during Feyfest.
- Investigating creatures or magic that disturb Springarden’s natural calm.

## Notable Members

- **[[Threeflower]]:** A timid gnome druid who guides visitors and serves as a field representative for the Union.
- **[[Pyrax]]:** A [[Lizardfolk]] fire druid who studies controlled burns and forest renewal.
- **[[Archdruid Xyxl]]:** The Union’s senior spiritual authority and guardian of Springarden’s oldest traditions.
