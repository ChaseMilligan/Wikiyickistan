The [Queen’s Men](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/modr/faction-agents#QueensMenFaction) are a loose affiliation between rogue offshoots of a hundred gangs of brigands, outlaws, and scoundrels such as the [[Mandem on Booftown]] and [[Cough City Crew]], that all owe fealty to the enigmatic [Queen of Thieves](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5525084-queen-of-thieves). These reprobates prey upon the pilgrims, prospectors, and explorers drawn to the city, extort and rob adventurers, smuggle delerium to disreputable clients in distant lands, and plunder the fantastic treasures and incredible wealth left behind in the city. The Queen of Thieves dreams of building an influential and powerful criminal empire forged in the anarchy and lawlessness of the ruins, conspiring against the other factions at every turn.
