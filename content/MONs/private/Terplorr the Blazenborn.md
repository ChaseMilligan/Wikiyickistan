Terplorr is a Historical Dragon that was slain by famous adventurers. He made his lair in the caldera of a mountain that once stood where [[The Seekers’ Scrapyard]] is today. That mountain was only known by its indigenous name, [[Wachii Ka'paakitayk]].  Terplorr was very laidback, and respectful for a red dragon according to the natives, but the humans who fought against him claim otherwise. Obviously both sides have their biases, so it is most likely the truth lies somewhere between. In truth he was quite laidback for a red dragon, but to say he was respectful might be a stretch. He did indeed respect the land as the natives did, and an innate connection to the unique fauna and flora of [[Stickyickistan]], but he was a dragon after all. It has been theorized that since many herbivores of the island consumed [[Arcannabis]] for its incredible nutrient density, the calming and introspective properties of the Arcannabis may have permeated throughout the entire food chain of the island due to its isolation. This could be why Terplorr had such a demeanor, but it did not stop him from razing hundreds of native settlements over the centuries. Nowadays he is looked upon as a martyr who died leading a resistance by the indigenous population, and a monstrous rebel who murdered innocents by the general population.
