### Cubensis, the Golden Teacher

The Celestial Queen of [[Myconids]], Lady of Vision and Harmony, **Cubensis** is a radiant being whose only desire is to awaken the minds of the living with golden spores, guiding them toward higher understanding, unity, and transcendence. Rather than corrupt and consume, her spores kindle revelation, expanding perception, healing wounded spirits, and illuminating hidden truths.

Though her form is otherworldly, Cubensis can shape her luminous, mycelial body into a bipedal figure of golden grace. Ancient murals and songs describe her as a towering mushroom crowned with a radiant spotted cap, her flowing mantle woven from filaments of glowing hyphae. Unlike the grotesque parodies of mortality wrought by [[Zuggtmoy]], Cubensis appears as a living symbol of enlightenment, a beacon in dark places where hope has withered.

Cubensis’s principal lair is the **Sanctum of Golden Vision**, a radiant mycelial palace that shimmers like a dream located in [[Arborea]]. It consists of two dozen immense golden-capped mushrooms that glow softly with inner light. Their gills radiate a psionic hum that resonates like a choir of distant voices. Bridges of crystalline hyphae connect the towering stalks, and inside their hollow chambers grow radiant gardens of luminous moss and spores that shimmer with prismatic color.

The air is rich with golden motes that drift like starlight, not choking or corrupting, but opening the mind to visions of harmony and deeper truths. Visitors describe the lair as both wondrous and overwhelming - its beauty is so profound that it can be disorienting.

Devotees of Cubensis gather not in cults of subjugation, but in circles of communion. Her followers are suffused with golden spores that grant fleeting visions of clarity and connection. Many describe the experience as becoming part of something vast and eternal, where self and other blur into harmony. To mortals who embrace her guidance, she is not a conqueror, but a teach, though the revelations she imparts can be overwhelming, reshaping lives and destinies forever.

Where Zuggtmoy spreads rot and enslavement, Cubensis spreads radiant consciousness and the healing mycelium of the mind.
