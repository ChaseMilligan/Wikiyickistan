title: History of Stickyickistan
---

Stickyickistan is an island on the planet of [[Mythmos]], shrouded in magic and home to one of the largest economies in the realms. Its wealth is built primarily on the cultivation and export of [[Arcannabis]], along with trade in [[Arcanite]] and other resources. The island’s size and distance from other landmasses are difficult to measure. Even the most sophisticated magic and technology fails beyond a certain distance from its shores.

The waters around Stickyickistan form a region much like a magical Bermuda Triangle. Pilots and sailors report violent weather, distorted horizons, impossible currents, and terrifying creatures from above and below. Vast deposits of [[Arcanite]] beneath [[Mount Zooka]] have warped time, space, and the Weave around the island, producing these phenomena and making accurate maps difficult to create. Stickyickistan can be seen from [[Lumytholarispace]] and appears on maps of Mythmos, but it always looks smaller than it seems when approached.

# Modern Stickyickistan

Stickyickistan is a city-state built around the active [[Mount Zooka]], whose obsidian slopes and glowing Arcanite veins dominate the island’s skyline. Approximately 27 million people live across a dense network of terraced districts connected by bridges, roads, canals, lifts, and industrial transit lines. The Capitol stands on [[Nevercough Island]], a small landmass sheltered within the [[Percolat Inlet]]. The climate is generally tropical, with hot, humid weather across the urban core. Farther inland, desertification has created increasingly arid regions, a legacy of [[Human Colonialism]] and industrialization.

The city rises in layers around the volcano. Wealthy residences climb into the jungle, where enchanted gardens and fortified estates overlook the ocean. Beneath them, poorer settlements cling to the mountainside in a haze of ash, smoke, brimstone, and sulfur. Above these districts, the [[Industrial Zone]] follows the crater’s rim and extends into the mountain through mines, foundries, refineries, and [[Magitech]] workshops.

[[Mount Zooka]] is an active [[Arcanite]] Cone, a rare volcano formed above rich deposits of raw Arcanite. Its slopes are black and glassy, marked by glowing veins of molten rock and slow-moving lava flows. Violent eruptions scatter volcanic stone, magma, and chunks of raw Arcanite across the island. Miners, magitech engineers, alchemists, and bounty hunters maintain dangerous outposts along the crater’s rim, searching for ore, magical artifacts, and volatile energy. The Dwarves of [[Bongstad]] have carved a fortified settlement into the mountain’s base, using its heat to power their forges.

Arcanite fuels much of Stickyickistan’s wealth and technology. Once extracted and purified, it can be worked into weapons, armor, artifacts, and industrial systems. The [[Nevercough Tribunal]] and [[The Seekers of the Vein]] operate mines and outposts around the crater, while [[Tokenheim Extractions Inc.]] and other industrial concerns process the ore and produce much of the city-state’s Magitech. Waste from the [[Industrial Zone]] feeds salvage operations at [[Seekers' Scrapyard]] and is collected by groups such as [[The Seekers of the Vein]], [[Zooka Mire Collective]], and [[The Iron Sovereign]].

The island’s soil is rich in nutrients and infused with Arcanite. Flora and fauna grow with unusual fertility and magical potency, especially in [[North Booftown]], the agricultural heartland. [[Astallon Acres]] lies along the wetlands bordering the [[Zooka Mire]], where [[Ezra Astallon]] pays farm workers unusually well and resolves local problems with money. [[Arcannabis]] remains the city-state’s primary export, while Arcanite, magebred animals, and other natural resources provide additional wealth.

Since the arrival of Human colonists, [[Alcohol]] has been outlawed because the government considers it a threat to the Arcannabis market. [[Zooka Malt]], a beverage made from Arcannabis buds, has become the principal alternative to traditional ale. The city’s merchants sell countless infused varieties, from cheap street brews to carefully aged drinks served in the private lounges of [[Bobo Bush Estates]].

[[Nevercough Island]] is the seat of political power and old money. At its heart stands the [[Capitol Building]], where Chief Magistrate [[Dabnar Nevercough]] rules through courts that dispense justice and corruption in equal measure. The island also contains the [[Grand Arcanum Library]], the [[Nevercough Museum]], elite magical academies such as [[N.E.R.D]], high-end boutiques, apothecaries, and specialty Magitech shops. The former settlement of [[Nevercough City]] remains the name commonly used for the island and its historic urban core.

The [[Ashen Citadel]] rises over Nevercough Island as the headquarters of the [[Ashen Shadow Society]], Stickyickistan’s most infamous bounty-hunting guild. Its dark stone walls, Magitech barriers, arcane wards, training facilities, forges, laboratories, archives, and holding cells make it both fortress and command center. Few outsiders enter without a contract, and fewer leave without becoming part of someone else’s case file.

South of Nevercough Island and north of the volcano lies [[Lower South Booftown]], commonly called [[Booftown]]. This middle-class district is known for family-owned shops, public parks, and relatively stable neighborhoods. A slow-moving river of thick, dark lava separates it from [[Upper South Booftown]], known as [[The Ashtray]].

The Ashtray is a smoke-blackened district of crooked tenements, ash-slick alleys, rooftop walkways, hidden grow rooms, and half-legal additions built over generations. Its native [[Halfling|halfling]] population is large, but it also shelters outcasts, fugitives, smugglers, mercenaries, failed apprentices, and anyone too useful, desperate, or dangerous to fit elsewhere. The [[Mandem on Booftown]] and [[Cough City Crew]] fight over Arcannabis, underground fighting, protection rackets, gambling, bootlegged Alcohol, and black-market Magitech. Neither gang fully controls the district. The Sootsteps connect rooftops and exterior stairways, the Cinder Courts host dice games and informal trials, and [[The Black Stalls]] sell stolen goods, poisons, counterfeit papers, and illegal Magitech parts. To outsiders, the district is dangerous. To locals, it is home.

To the east of the volcano lies [[Bobo Bush Estates]], where merchant lords, arcane industrialists, politicians, and retired crime bosses live in lavish villas protected by enchanted gardens and high-security Magitech. Deals worth more than a kingdom’s treasury are made over fine Arcannabis and aged Zooka Malt. Espionage, blackmail, and silent assassinations decide more than elections ever do.

North Booftown is the center of food production and Arcannabis cultivation. Its volcanic soil produces crops that grow larger, faster, and stronger than those found elsewhere. The region also belongs to [[House Northbourne]], the least powerful noble house in the [[Nevercough Tribunal]]. Publicly, the Northbournes dominate animal breeding, training, and handling. They own [[The North Booftown Downs]] and founded the [[Handler’s Guild]], whose expertise includes prized racehorses, exotic creatures, and [[Magebred Animals]].

In criminal circles, the Northbournes are called [[The Norfies]]. The family does not use the name, since it can imply criminal affiliations and attract the attention of the [[Nevercough Tribunal]] or the [[Tribunal Highblade Coalition]]. Behind their respectable public image, the Norfies control much of the city-state’s illicit Alcohol trade, manipulate racing odds and outcomes, operate gambling syndicates, and host underground fighting rings. Their agents and intermediaries preserve the noble family’s plausible deniability.

On the western side of the city, [[Bongstad]] is a Dwarven settlement carved into Mount Zooka. Its maze of tunnels, bridges, terraces, and magitech pipes carries molten slag and processed Arcanite between refineries and forges. Bongstad combines old Dwarven traditions with Arcannabis-fueled invention. Miners and blacksmiths share the settlement with inventors, alchemists, and experimental herbalists. Visitors are advised to bring a strong constitution, a high tolerance for Zooka Malt, and no outstanding gambling debts.

Beneath the city lies [[The Undercity Osslurraggath]], an underground metropolis ignored by the urban elite and denied official recognition by the Nevercough Tribunal. It is home to [[The Mortuary]], the [[Dungsweepers]], exiles, criminals, and people the surface city would rather forget. Its provisional government has little legal authority, but it governs the people who live below. Osslurraggath began as bone vaults and necropolis tunnels before collapsing into deeper Underdark channels, creating a refuge for the poor, the diseased, the dead, and the unwanted. The [[Heralds of Dust]] established a philosophy of decay there.

Beyond the city lies [[The Outback]], a largely unexplored wilderness where civilization gives way to ancient ruins, arcane forests, hidden rivers, swamps, bogs, mountains, beaches, and magical anomalies. [[Springwood]] is a massive naturalized park and sacred woodland that remains in an impossible state of spring. Its settlements include [[Kerning’s Crossing]], known for apothecaries and its reliance on the [[Solarium Botanica]]. 

Farther out, [[Doobius House]] remains a humble frontier village inhabited mainly by indigenous [[Twothirdlings]] and a few full Halflings. Its former importance came from its role as the Gate to the Outback and from the military workshops established there during Elyrian expansion. The [[Grim Forest]] borders the village, its history preserved mainly through indigenous oral traditions. The forest contains hostile plants and undead, while an ancient awakened radiant tree guards its edge.

The Outback also contains [[Zooka Mire]], a swamp just beyond the city limits where the [[Zooka Mire Collective]] operates, and the distant [[Molten Maw]], an underground volcanic city inhabited mainly by [[Duergar]] and [[Svirfneblin]]. Molten Maw’s Veins are semi-intelligent rivers of magical lava, while [[The Silt Road]] moves contraband, monster parts, forbidden magic, fugitives, and secrets beneath surface authorities. The Outback’s raw magic, combined with Mount Zooka’s influence, has made Stickyickistan a hotspot in the multiverse. For bounty hunters, smugglers, prospectors, and treasure seekers, its wilderness promises lost knowledge and untapped power. It also promises that something dangerous is always waiting beyond the city’s last road.

# Past Stickyickistan

Stickyickistan did not always have its current tropical climate. In earlier ages, the island maintained a more balanced pattern of hot and cold regions, as well as arid and humid zones. The northern part once held an ice cap, while the south resembled a Mediterranean climate more than a Caribbean one. Industrialization caused severe climate change. Rising temperatures melted the ice cap and turned some old-growth forests into desert. Recent conservation efforts have slowed the damage, but much of the original landscape has been lost.

## [[Human Colonialism]]

Long ago, the island’s humanoid inhabitants were small communities of [[Halflings]], [[Eladrin]], [[Goblins]], and [[Lizardfolk]]. Many followed a polytheistic faith devoted to [[The Three Sisters]], lived in close relationship with the land, and respected the creatures that shared it. The island flourished, its soil was fertile, and its forests teemed with life.

That balance was disrupted when ambitious Human settlers arrived. Seeking control of the island’s resources, they treated the Halflings as obstacles and launched a violent invasion. The settlers established [[Nevercough City]] on what they called [[Nevercough Island]], forming the nation that would eventually become Stickyickistan.

The Halflings resisted, but they were outnumbered and outmatched. Their leaders eventually negotiated for survival. The resulting pact granted them a protected enclave in what is now [[Upper South Booftown]] and acknowledged their right to participate in the new nation’s prosperity.

The settlers later discovered [[Arcannabis]], an extraordinary plant whose magical properties became the foundation of the island’s economy. Properly cultivated, Arcannabis could heighten the senses, expand magical abilities, and reveal visions of unseen realms. [[Arcannabis Cultivation]] became a specialized tradition passed from generation to generation, and merchants soon traveled from distant lands to purchase the crop.

Economic prosperity did not erase the divisions created by colonization. Human settlers and Halfling communities continued to live with distrust, cultural conflict, and unequal access to power. Over time, their traditions and technologies blended into a distinctive culture of music, art, food, and festivals. This cultural exchange created a shared identity, but racial tensions never disappeared entirely.

Stickyickistan’s modern identity is therefore built on both coexistence and unresolved history. Its architecture, markets, and festivals reflect the fusion of Halfling traditions with Human innovation, while the island’s political institutions continue to bear the marks of conquest and resistance.

## The Doobius House Resistance

Stickyickistan was once a nation with several population centers rather than a single city-state. The main [[Human]] settlement on [[Nevercough Island]] was [[Nevercough City]], named for its patriarchal noble family. Across the [[Percolat Inlet]], [[Bongstad]] was carved into the base of [[Mount Zooka]]. The native [[Halfling]] reserve occupied land between a great lava river and the mountain’s slopes, where the terrain was barely habitable.

Farther inland, [[Doobius House]] was founded by [[Duke Doobius Highblade]] as the “Gate to the Outback.” The frontier settlement attracted adventurers, prospectors, artificers, and others willing to live beyond the protection of the coastal communities. At its height, Doobius House rivaled [[Nevercough City]] and became famous for producing arcane ballistae and nature-infused war machines.

Its frontier role gave Doobius House an unusual population. Elyrian settlers, native Halflings, craftspeople, hunters, and exiles lived beside one another, though not as equals. The Highblades claimed the land and profited from its workshops, while the Twothirdlings and other native communities supplied much of the local knowledge, labor, and military experience that kept the settlement alive.

When [[Duke Doobius Highblade]] died, his Human heirs inherited the family’s formal authority and attempted to preserve the old order. The [[Twothirdlings]], who heavily outnumbered the Elyrian population around Doobius House, rejected that arrangement. Led by [[Jimmyjock Labingi]], the duke’s disinherited Twothirdling son, they seized the estate and surrounding settlement. They declared that Doobius House belonged to the people who had made it livable and defended it.

Jimmyjock formed a provisional government and began negotiations with the Elyrian nobility of [[Nevercough City]]. The negotiations quickly failed. The nobles refused to recognize the Twothirdlings’ claim, while Jimmyjock refused to surrender the land or accept an arrangement that would leave his mother’s people subject to the family that had rejected him.

## The [[Arcane Civil War]]

The dispute over Doobius House became one of the first major armed conflicts between Elyrian settlers and the native resistance. House Highblade forces tried to retake the estate and secure the frontier road, while volunteers from Doobius House fortified the settlement and turned the surrounding wilderness into a weapon.

The resulting skirmishes spread into what is now called the [[Grim Forest]]. Doobius House’s militia was badly outnumbered, but its fighters knew the terrain intimately. They attacked at night, cut supply lines, sabotaged war machines, and disappeared into the forest before larger Elyrian forces could respond. Old arcane ballistae, nature-infused war machines, trained beasts, and improvised traps allowed the resistance to ambush troops who expected a quick victory.

The fighting around Doobius House became entangled with the wider power struggle among the [[Four Founding Houses]]. The houses disagreed over how quickly the frontier should be conquered, who would control its resources, and whether the Twothirdlings should be expelled, absorbed, or granted limited autonomy. Rival nobles supported, supplied, or undermined the campaign according to their own interests. House Highblade demanded that its claim be restored by force, while other factions used the conflict to weaken Highblade influence and seize the roads, workshops, and military technology produced at Doobius House.

As the dispute widened, the conflict spread beyond the frontier. Industrial sabotage disrupted foundries and supply depots. Magical duels between noble arcanists devastated sections of the growing settlements. Factions summoned fiends, created unstable war golems, and unleashed necromantic plagues in attempts to gain the upper hand. The original dispute over Doobius House became only one front in a larger struggle for control of the island’s government, resources, and future.

The war was fought through espionage and sabotage as much as open battle. Couriers disappeared along frontier roads, military contracts changed hands through bribery, and entire workshops were destroyed rather than allowed to fall into an enemy’s hands. The [[Grim Forest]] became a maze of abandoned siege engines, burned supply routes, hidden militia camps, and battlefields reclaimed by rapidly growing vegetation.

The Twothirdling resistance did not cause the entire [[Arcane Civil War]], but it exposed the weakness of Elyrian rule. Doobius House became a symbol for both sides. To the resistance, it proved that settlers could be defeated. To the nobles, it proved that frontier autonomy threatened the authority of every ruling house. The conflict widened into a prolonged civil war that reshaped the island’s political order.

As the cost of the campaign mounted, the nobles of Nevercough City grew weary of trying to dominate a settlement that refused to surrender. Doobius House could not be conquered cheaply, and continued warfare threatened trade routes and the stability of the coastal settlements. The wider civil war also forced the houses to redirect troops and resources away from the frontier.

Jimmyjock used his knowledge of Elyrian law and military practice, combined with the indigenous values of his mother’s people, to negotiate a settlement. The final agreement recognized the Twothirdlings’ right to govern Doobius House while allowing Elyrian families and institutions to remain under negotiated protections. The settlement did not erase the violence or the unequal power that produced it, but it prevented House Highblade from reclaiming the town by force.

Doobius House remained an autonomous and heavily armed frontier community for generations. Its workshops continued producing arcane ballistae and nature-infused war machines, officially to defend against the dangers of [[The Outback]], but also as a reminder that the town’s independence had been won through resistance. Jimmyjock’s songs preserved the memory of the conflict, while Elyrian histories recast him as a rebel and thief of noble property.

After the [[Arcane Civil War]], the surviving settlements eventually united into the city-state of Stickyickistan. Doobius House lost much of its former independence as roads, bureaucracy, and urban expansion absorbed the frontier town into the wider state. Even so, the resistance remains central to its identity. It was the place where the Twothirdlings first proved that Elyrian settlements could be challenged, and where Jimmyjock Labingi turned a disputed inheritance into a homeland.
