A master entertainer and a bit of a sadistic showman, [[Zahur al-Nasira]] invites (sometimes by force) contestants from across the multiverse to compete in high-stakes challenges unlike any other. Participants ranging from bumbling fools to hardened adventurers are sent into his infinitely evolving labyrinthine dungeon of stairs constructed entirely within Zahur’s personal extra-dimensional domain. Adventurers that end up here are sorted into parties at random and each party is assigned a mysterious Patron who leads them on their staircase to a door. The party does not choose the staircase, rather it chooses them. The correct staircases to get to their destination simply fly into place as the Patron walks forward ahead of them to the door. When the staircases reach a threshold, their Patron will begin the ritual to open the door which involves a verbal component where the Patron begins to tell the story, and a somatic component of a weaving or tugging motion on the literal strands of the Weave itself.

The rules are simple: survive, overcome, and profit—or perish in spectacular fashion. Each room in the labyrinth holds the promise of gold and glory or an untimely, hilarious demise. The harder the dungeon, the greater the payout—and the higher the ratings. Zahur ensures that every show is filled with unexpected twists, sending waves of chaotic magic to spice up the competition and keep the audience on the edge of their seats.

Zahur brings viewers from all overt the multiverse to come watch the spectacles, but a dimension door called the Labyrinthian Link can be found in [[Stickyickistan]] on [[Nevercough Island]] downtown. Citizens can buy tickets for the events and pass through the portal to watch the show.
