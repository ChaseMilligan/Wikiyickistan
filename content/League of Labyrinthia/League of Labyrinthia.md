"Welcome, mortals and immortals alike, to the greatest spectacle across space, time, and dimensions!"

![[lol.jpg]]

A master entertainer and a bit of a sadistic showman, [[Zahur al-Nasira]] invites (sometimes by force) contestants from across the multiverse to compete in high-stakes challenges unlike any other. Participants ranging from bumbling fools to hardened adventurers are sent into his infinitely evolving labyrinthine dungeon of stairs constructed entirely within Zahur’s personal extra-dimensional domain. Adventurers that end up here are sorted into parties at random and each party is assigned a mysterious Patron who leads them on their fated staircase to a door. The party does not choose the staircase, rather it chooses them. The correct staircases to reach their destination simply fly into place as the Patron walks forward ahead of them to the door. When the staircases reach a threshold, their Patron will begin the ritual to open the door which involves a verbal component where the Patron begins speaking in an esoteric language only known to them and the gods, and a somatic component of a weaving or tugging motion on the literal strands of the Weave itself.

![[ChatGPT Image Oct 9, 2025, 06_09_16 PM.png]]

The rules are simple: survive, overcome, and profit, or perish in spectacular fashion. Each staircase in the labyrinth holds the promise of gold and glory or an untimely demise. The harder the quest, the greater the payout, and the higher the ratings. Zahur brings viewers from all over the multiverse to come watch the spectacles, but a dimension door called the Labyrinthian Link can be found in [[Stickyickistan]] on [[Nevercough Island]] downtown. Citizens can buy tickets for the events and pass through the portal to watch the show. The show can also be watched on magitech screens where they are available for purchase.

# [[A League of Our Own]]

This is basically my creative description of how a DM takes the players of the game on a journey, but specifically for an Adventurer's League setting. Each session is standalone and meant to be kept to an hour or two (maybe 3 let's be real I wanna play DnD). Sessions are like episodes of a show, and don't always feature all the same characters. These episodes may be one offs like a movie, or part of a series. These series of adventures share settings, plot points, or NPCs with official modules and are referred to as "seasons." They feature storylines adjacent to the main plot found in published modules and are optimized for Adventurers League play. Characters will not always be the same level, DMs must follow RAW as close as possible, and characters must start at level 1. Refer to [[A League of Our Own]] for more information.
