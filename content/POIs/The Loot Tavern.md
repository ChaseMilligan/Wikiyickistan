**The Loot Tavern**, also known as **Lut’s Tavern**, is an extraplanar adventuring hub, monster-hunting lodge, crafting hall, and bar of questionable financial accountability. Perched atop a magically warded island drifting through the Astral Plane, it serves as a sanctuary for adventurers, astral travelers, vagabonds, monster hunters, and anyone willing to pay for a drink before doing something dangerous.

Its reputation is simple: monster carvin’, item craftin’, familiar findin’, drink drinkin’, and, for the daring, a seemingly endless supply of monsters in need of slayin’.

The tavern is closely associated with [[Heliana]], Director of her own Monster Hunting Guild and author of [[Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting]]. In the Free League of Stickyickistan ([[Free League of Stickyickistan]]), the Loot Tavern can be used as a recurring quest hub, planar rest stop, crafting location, and source of monster-hunting contracts.

> “Fancy yourself a hunter? Ready to do the research, find the clues, and prepare for battle against fearsome foes? No? Well, have a firewhiskey and then let me ask you again…”
>
> — [[Heliana]], Director of the Monster Hunting Guild

## No Fixed Address

The Loot Tavern has no permanent location. It drifts through the Astral Plane, appearing to those who know how to reach it and vanishing from the concerns of tax collectors, road authorities, and most sensible cartographers.

Getting there usually requires two things:

1. A tavern.
2. An indigo-hued drink called **[[Port Ale]]**.

A creature that drinks port ale and steps through the door of a tavern within one minute is transported to the exterior of the Loot Tavern. Bottles of port ale may be sent by [[Heliana]], sold by questionable dealers, discovered in old recipes, or handed to adventurers by someone who refuses to explain where they got it.

Leaving the Loot Tavern is almost as strange. The tavern’s portal can send travelers across the planes, but only if they first drink the correct **teleportation flight**: a specific combination of beverages mixed to point the portal toward the desired destination. Without the right drink, a traveler may end up somewhere wildly inconvenient.

## The Tavern’s Tenants

The Loot Tavern is home to a rotating crowd of hunters, crafters, drinkers, fugitives, bards, familiars, and people who claim they are “just passing through” but have been there for six months.

Its most important tenants include:

- [[Lutecia]], the tavern’s namesake, bartender, mixologist, and social center.
- [[Heliana]], monster hunter, author, and quest giver.
- [[Humperdink]], biomancer, familiar-maker, researcher, and dramatic dresser.
- [[L'Arsene Upin]], artificer, gadgeteer, thief, and crafter of dangerous solutions.
- [[Peeper]], Heliana’s anxious scribe and the tavern’s tireless bookkeeper.
- **[[Granny Weathertax]]**, the bouncer, unofficial authority, and reason most bar fights end quickly.
- **[[Cacophonics]]**, resident bard, question-asker, and professional exaggerator.
- **The Fearsome Five**, a much-mocked adventuring party also known by less generous patrons as the Fearful Five.

Together, these figures make the Loot Tavern feel less like a normal inn and more like a magical workplace sitcom built around monster parts, bad decisions, and extremely strong drinks.

## Services

The Loot Tavern is useful to adventurers because nearly every room is attached to some form of service.

### Lut’s Bar

[[Lutecia]] serves drinks from across the planes, including cocktails used to direct the tavern’s teleportation portal. Her bar is the center of the tavern’s social life, rumor economy, and interplanar travel infrastructure.

### Heliana’s Cave

[[Heliana]] buys and sells monster components, teaches monster-harvesting techniques, cooks magical meals, assists with harvesting preserved monster corpses, and sells copies of [[Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting]].

### Humperdink’s Laboratory

[[Humperdink]] provides biomantic expertise, strange experiments, familiars, and spell scrolls. His laboratory is colorful, chaotic, and usually only mostly safe.

### L’Arsène’s Caravan

[[L'Arsene Upin]] crafts items, buys and sells magic items, tinkers with arcanomechanical devices, and turns rare monster parts into things that may or may not explode.

### Peeper’s Library

[[Peeper]] maintains the tavern’s library and helps adventurers research monsters before a hunt. Those who take the time to study may uncover clues, weaknesses, habits, or warnings that help them survive.

## Using the Loot Tavern in Stickyickistan

In the Free League of Stickyickistan, the Loot Tavern can serve as:

- A quest hub for monster hunts.
- A place to research dangerous creatures.
- A planar tavern that connects distant regions of [[Mythmos]].
- A crafting and harvesting center.
- A source of recurring NPCs.
- A place to buy strange services, magical drinks, spell scrolls, and monster parts.
- A neutral meeting ground for adventurers from different parts of the multiverse.

The Loot Tavern is especially useful when a DM wants to run an adventure inspired by [[Heliana's Guide to Monster Hunting]] or other [[FLS & The Loot Tavern|Loot Tavern]] material.

## Stickyickistanian Notes

The Loot Tavern’s relationship to Stickyickistan is practical, strange, and probably underreported. Adventurers from [[Mythmos]] may reach it through port ale, planar mishaps, monster-hunting contracts, or invitations from [[Heliana]] herself.

Some claim [[The Rolling Papers]] has tried to review the tavern multiple times, but the assigned journalists keep returning three weeks late with no notes, new tattoos, and extremely strong opinions about [[firewhiskey]].

Others insist [[The Roach Clip]] maintains a permanent dead drop somewhere under Lut’s Bar. The tavern denies this, mostly because admitting it would require paperwork.

## Loot Tavern IRL

**[Loot Tavern]((https://loottavern.com/)** is a third-party tabletop RPG publisher best known for creating flavorful, high-production-value 5e-compatible content. Their work often focuses on imaginative magic items, monsters, subclasses, adventures, and setting material with a strong sense of personality and presentation.

In-world or at the table, Loot Tavern content is useful as a source of unusual treasure, eccentric NPC inspiration, and ready-to-drop-in mechanics. Their products tend to lean into bold fantasy concepts, striking art, and memorable item design, making them a good fit for campaigns that enjoy high-magic rewards, weird curiosities, and loot that feels more distinctive than standard treasure table results.
