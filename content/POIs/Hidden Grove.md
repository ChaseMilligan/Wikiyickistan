
The **Hidden Grove** is the secluded home, cultivation site, and spiritual sanctuary of [[Thalion Mossgrove]]. It lies outside the main paths of [[Doobius House]], somewhere between the village and the deeper wilderness of [[The Outback]]. Thalion’s grove is difficult to find without permission, and the surrounding plants quietly discourage careless visitors.

Villagers sometimes call the place the “witch’s hut,” but the name reflects suspicion of Thalion’s faith and Arcannabis practices rather than any actual evidence of witchcraft. To those Thalion trusts, the Hidden Grove is a peaceful refuge where plants, memory, ancestry, and the changing seasons are treated as parts of the same living cycle.

## Overview

**Type:** Private grove, Arcannabis garden, herbalist’s home, ritual site, and component workshop  
**Caretaker:** [[Thalion Mossgrove]]  
**Location:** Near [[Doobius House]], [[The Outback]], [[Stickyickistan]]  
**Faith:** [[The Old Faiths]]  
**Known for:** Rare Arcannabis strains, monster-component evaluation, spiritual ceremonies, and botanical knowledge

## Finding the Grove

The Hidden Grove is not marked on public maps. Thalion gives directions only to people he trusts, and [[Barnabas Huggins]] may direct respectful adventurers there when they need specialized Arcannabis knowledge or help with unusual monster components.

A person searching without guidance may walk past the entrance repeatedly, follow a path that loops back to where they began, or become distracted by pleasant smells and unusual plants. These effects are not intended to harm visitors; they are the grove’s way of asking whether someone is approaching with patience or entitlement.

A character who treats the surrounding land respectfully and succeeds on a DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Nature) check can notice that the plants are guiding rather than obstructing them. A character who has been invited by Thalion finds the entrance without difficulty.

## Exterior

The grove is surrounded by mature trees, thick undergrowth, hanging moss, and flowering vines. The plants grow in a way that creates privacy without forming an obvious wall. Small paths appear between the roots for invited guests and vanish again after they pass.

At night, pale flowers open along the approach and provide soft natural light. During the day, the grove is shaded and cool even when the surrounding wilderness is hot. The air smells of damp soil, herbs, smoke, and Arcannabis resin.

The grove has no formal gate. Instead, two old trees lean toward each other over the entrance, their branches woven together like an arch. Small wooden charms hang from the branches, each carved with a leaf, seed, animal, or seasonal symbol.

## Thalion’s Hut

Thalion’s home is a small hut built from wood, woven branches, clay, and living roots. Its walls are sturdy but irregular, and its roof is covered in moss, broad leaves, and flowering plants. Translucent leaves over the windows glow faintly at night.

Inside are:

- A central hearth and low cooking table.
- Shelves of seeds, dried leaves, jars, and plant samples.
- A sleeping nook separated by hanging woven curtains.
- A worktable for examining Arcannabis and monster components.
- A small shrine devoted to [[The Old Faiths]].
- Stone bowls used for offerings, water, ash, and soil.
- Thalion’s cultivation journals and handwritten strain notes.

The hut is comfortable, calm, and more spacious inside than its exterior suggests. Thalion’s Eladrin magic has allowed the roots beneath the floor to grow into chambers for storage and preservation.

## Cultivation Gardens

The grove contains several raised beds and planting areas. Each section is cultivated for a different purpose:

- **Flavor beds:** Arcannabis plants grown for aroma, taste, and social use.
- **Medicine beds:** Plants used for healing, sleep, pain relief, and recovery.
- **Ceremony beds:** Strains reserved for rites connected to ancestry, memory, dreams, and the seasons.
- **Transformation beds:** Plants cultivated with monster components or unusual magical soil.
- **Quiet beds:** Low-scent plants grown for privacy and meditation.
- **Experimental beds:** Dangerous or unfinished strains that Thalion monitors carefully.

Thalion can cultivate or craft Arcannabis strains when he has the required seeds, time, tools, and components. He does not allow visitors to harvest from the gardens without permission.

## Drying House

A small open-sided structure contains racks, hanging bundles, clay trays, woven baskets, and sealed jars. Thalion dries herbs and Arcannabis here, carefully labeling each batch with its strain, harvest date, growing conditions, and intended use.

The drying house is also where Thalion preserves monster components that may be used in cultivation. Blood, glands, bone fragments, skins, venom, spores, and other materials are separated according to their magical and biological properties.

A careless visitor may ruin weeks of work by exposing the wrong component to sunlight, moisture, heat, or another ingredient. Thalion is forgiving of honest mistakes but not of people who ignore instructions.

## Stone Basins

Several shallow stone basins collect rainwater, springwater, ash, sap, and nutrient-rich runoff. Thalion uses them to prepare soil treatments, wash components, and observe changes in plants.

The basins are also used for simple divination. By studying the movement of floating leaves, ash, or oil on the water’s surface, Thalion may notice disturbances in the surrounding land. He can sometimes detect changes near the [[Grim Forest]] before travelers or officials report them.

## Ritual Circles

Small ritual circles are arranged throughout the grove. Each is made from stones, roots, seeds, and seasonal offerings. They are used for meditation, prayer, Arcannabis ceremonies, ancestor remembrance, and blessings for new growth.

The circles are not inherently magical to every visitor, but they become more responsive when Thalion performs a ritual. Flowers may open out of season, leaves may turn toward a speaker, or the air may briefly carry the scent of a different season.

Visitors are expected to ask before entering a ritual circle. Stepping into one carelessly is considered deeply disrespectful.

## Grove Defenses

The Hidden Grove does not contain lethal traps. Thalion prefers defenses that confuse, embarrass, redirect, or temporarily disable intruders.

Possible effects include:

- Paths that loop back toward the entrance.
- Vines that tie shoelaces, belts, or weapon straps together.
- Sleep-inducing pollen.
- Thorny plants that block passage without attacking.
- Flowers that loudly announce the presence of intruders.
- Illusions of extra paths or false doors.
- Sudden growth that separates intruders from one another.
- A strong but harmless smell that makes tracking difficult.

If someone threatens Thalion or attempts to steal a dangerous strain, the grove becomes more assertive. Plants may restrain the intruders, cover their equipment, or force them back toward the village.

## Services and Trade

Thalion does not operate like an ordinary merchant. He accepts coin reluctantly and prefers barter, favors, useful information, or respectfully harvested components.

He may provide:

- Arcannabis seeds or finished products.
- Strain identification and cultivation advice.
- A ceremonial or medicinal blend.
- Evaluation of monster components.
- Help replacing a dangerous ingredient with a safer one.
- A ritual blessing or spiritual consultation.
- Information about plants, creatures, and ecological changes.
- A hidden route through the surrounding wilderness.
- A safe place to rest while recovering from an expedition.

Thalion refuses components harvested carelessly, taken from protected creatures, or gathered in ways that needlessly damage the land.

## Important Relationships

- **[[Barnabas Huggins]]:** Barnabas sells some of Thalion’s novice-level Arcannabis at the [[Hotbox Inn & Tavern]] and directs respectful customers to the grove.
- **[[Doobius House]]:** The grove quietly supports villagers with medicines, advice, and plant knowledge, even though some locals fear Thalion’s beliefs.
- **[[The Old Faiths]]:** Thalion’s spiritual practices shape the grove’s rituals and cultivation methods.
- **The local church:** Church officials consider Thalion a heretic and witch because of his faith and Arcannabis ceremonies.
- **[[Grim Forest]]:** Thalion monitors changes in the forest and may recognize ecological or magical disturbances before others do.

## Secrets

- Thalion keeps private records of every monster component accepted at the grove, including who harvested it and where.
- Some of the strongest strains are reserved for ceremonies, healing, and emergencies rather than sale.
- Thalion suspects that industrial Arcannabis cultivation could permanently damage the land if left unchecked.
- The grove contains hidden seed stores that would be extremely valuable to growers, churches, criminal organizations, and industrial factions.
- The roots beneath the hut extend farther than the visible grove and may connect to older natural pathways beneath [[The Outback]].
