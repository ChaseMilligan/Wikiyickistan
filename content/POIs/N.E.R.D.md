# N.E.R.D

**N.E.R.D**, short for **Nerds Enjoy Researching Data**, is an arcane educational institution in [[Stickyickistan]] dedicated to magical research, mathematical theory, glyphwork, history, planar studies, and experimental problem-solving.

The institution is known for producing brilliant scholars, eccentric mages, obsessive researchers, and the occasional overconfident disaster. Its halls are filled with lecture chambers, enchanted chalkboards, sealed archives, unstable student projects, and libraries containing books that probably should not be available to first-year students.

## Role in Stickyickistan

N.E.R.D serves as one of [[Stickyickistan|Stickyickistan’s]] major centers of arcane education and magical research. Its professors and students are often consulted by city officials, adventuring parties, merchants, and private patrons when a problem requires brains, spells, obscure citations, or dangerous fieldwork.

The institution maintains an uneasy relationship with practical power. It claims to pursue knowledge for its own sake, but its research is valuable to nobles, guilds, inventors, monster hunters, and political factions across the region.

## Connections

- [[Stickyickistan]] — The institution’s home city and primary source of students, funding, and problems.
- [[The Loot Tavern]] — A potential partner for monster research, field studies, and practical applications of dangerous knowledge.
- [[Heliana]] — Her work may be cited in monster biology, harvesting, and field research courses.
- [[Arcannabis]] — Studied by alchemists, botanists, and experimental herbalists within the institution.
- [[Magitech]] — A major field of research, invention, and frequent explosions.
- [[Solarium Botanica]] — Possible partner or rival in botanical, alchemical, and Arcannabis-related research.
- [[Underdark]] — Source of field expeditions, dangerous samples, lost ruins, and things the faculty insists are “manageable risks.”
- [[The Silt Road]] — Officially condemned, unofficially useful for acquiring restricted texts, rare reagents, and questionable artifacts.
- [[Abyss]] — A subject of planar study, religious caution, and several banned dissertations.

## Reputation

N.E.R.D is respected, mocked, feared, and heavily footnoted. Graduates are known for being clever, curious, and alarmingly willing to test theories in real-world conditions.

## Quest Hooks

- **The Unpeer-Reviewed Portal:** A student thesis accidentally opens a portal to somewhere in the [[Abyss]], and the faculty needs outsiders to close it before accreditation inspectors arrive.

- **Missing Field Researchers:** A N.E.R.D expedition into the [[Underdark]] vanished while collecting samples for a [[Magitech]] thesis. Their last sending mentioned “singing crystals” and “something grading us.”

- **The Forbidden Citation:** A sealed archive book has started adding new footnotes in fresh blood. The professors want the party to find the original author before the bibliography becomes a summoning circle.

- **Botanical Rivalry:** A rare plant sample from the [[Solarium Botanica]] has been stolen, mutated, or mislabeled, and now several Arcannabis researchers are accusing one another of academic sabotage.

- **The Silt Road Syllabus:** Restricted reagents purchased through [[The Silt Road]] have gone missing from a locked laboratory. N.E.R.D wants them recovered quietly before anyone asks why the school had them.

- **Monster Biology Practical:** A captured monster meant for a [[Heliana]]-style dissection lab escapes into the campus stacks. The faculty insists it is “mostly sedated.”

- **Magitech Midterm Meltdown:** A student-built [[Magitech]] engine begins warping gravity in one wing of the institution. The party must stabilize it while students argue over whether this still counts for extra credit.

- **The Plagiarist’s Curse:** Several students copied from an ancient planar text, and now each has been cursed with a different extraplanar side effect. The party must trace the curse back to its source text.

- **Tenure Track Assassination:** A professor researching politically sensitive magic is targeted by rival scholars, hired killers, or a faction that does not want their findings published.

- **Knowledge for Its Own Sake:** N.E.R.D hires the party to retrieve an artifact from [[The Loot Tavern]], the [[Underdark]], or a black-market auction, but neglects to mention that the artifact is still awake.
