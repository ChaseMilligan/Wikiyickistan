[[Redwood Grove]] is an ancient circular stand of towering redwoods planted centuries ago by six elven families to mark the end of a bitter feud. Each family planted one sapling as a living vow of peace, and over time the six trees grew into mighty pillars whose roots intertwined beneath the forest floor. The grove became a sacred meeting place, a site of remembrance, reconciliation, and seasonal rites for the elves who would later found [[Redwood Watch]].

The grove’s peace was broken during the old dragon troubles, when a green dragon remembered in local stories as Death-at-Sunset made the redwoods a place of fear. Though the dragon was slain a century ago by Ethvarn and her allies, many villagers believe the grove never fully forgot what happened beneath its boughs. Today, the redwoods still stand, vast and beautiful, but the shadows beneath them feel too deep at dusk, and old stories stir whenever sickness spreads through the leaves.
