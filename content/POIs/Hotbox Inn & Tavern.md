
The **Hotbox Inn & Tavern** is the primary public house of [[Doobius House]], an aging inland village in [[The Outback]] of [[Stickyickistan]]. It serves as a place to eat, sleep, drink, buy novice-level [[Arcannabis]], exchange rumors, and find temporary work before heading into the surrounding wilderness.

The inn is owned and operated by [[Barnabas Huggins]], a stout, rosy-cheeked man whose booming laugh can be heard from the village square. Barnabas is a natural storyteller, practical innkeeper, and careful observer of local life. He knows which travelers arrived, which hunters failed to return, and which rumors are worth investigating.

## Overview

**Type:** Inn, tavern, rumor exchange, and informal job board  
**Owner:** [[Barnabas Huggins]]  
**Location:** [[Doobius House]], [[The Outback]], [[Stickyickistan]]  
**Primary clientele:** Villagers, hunters, travelers, caravan workers, adventurers, and passing merchants  
**Known for:** Food, drink, novice-level Arcannabis, local gossip, and frontier hospitality

## Description

The Hotbox is a broad, weathered building with a sagging porch, patched shutters, and a hand-painted sign showing a smoking wooden box surrounded by curling flames. The common room is warm even in poor weather, thanks to a broad hearth and a kitchen that is almost always in use.

Inside, sturdy tables fill most of the room. A long counter runs along one wall, backed by shelves of bottles, clay mugs, dried herbs, and sealed Arcannabis containers. Hunting gear, old trail maps, broken weapons, and faded notices decorate the walls. A few tables are permanently claimed by locals, while the seats nearest the door are reserved for travelers who may need to leave quickly.

The inn is comfortable rather than refined. The floors creak, the furniture has been repaired many times, and every room smells faintly of smoke, ale, cooked food, leather, and Arcannabis. The building’s age is part of its charm; it has survived enough bad winters, road disputes, monster scares, and drunken arguments to feel like one of the village’s more reliable landmarks.

## Barnabas Huggins

Barnabas is warm, welcoming, and larger-than-life, but his jolly manner hides a sharp eye. He can usually tell when a customer is lying, hiding an injury, or preparing to ask for something illegal.

### Voice and Mannerisms

- Speaks loudly and laughs even louder.
- Tells long stories that blend truth with exaggeration.
- Wipes his apron with a rag that never seems to become clean.
- Winks when sharing a rumor, secret, or questionable deal.
- Calls regular customers “family” and suspicious strangers “future paperwork.”

### Attitude

- Warm and hospitable toward travelers.
- Protective of the inn’s peace and the village’s reputation.
- Pragmatic about food, drink, smoke, gossip, and coin.
- Willing to hear out adventurers, but dislikes reckless people who endanger locals.
- Keeps track of what happens on the roads through [[The Outback]].

Barnabas sells food, drink, and novice-level Arcannabis, but he is not a grower and does not craft strains himself. For specialized Arcannabis cultivation or unusual monster components, he may direct customers toward [[Thalion Mossgrove]].

## Rumors and Information

Barnabas is an important source of local information. He can provide rumors about:

- Conditions along the roads through [[The Outback]].
- Travelers or hunters who have gone missing.
- Activity near the edge of the [[Grim Forest]].
- Strange plants, undead, or dangerous wildlife.
- Events in [[Doobius House]] and nearby settlements.
- Adventurers looking for temporary work.
- Merchants carrying food, supplies, Arcannabis, or unusual goods.

Barnabas does not guarantee that every rumor is true. He usually knows who told him the story and whether that person was sober when they told it.

## Adventuring Connections

The Hotbox can serve as a local starting point for adventures involving [[Doobius House]], [[The Outback]], and the [[Grim Forest]]. Barnabas may:

- Introduce adventurers to hunters, guides, and caravan workers.
- Provide directions toward dangerous wilderness areas.
- Offer shelter to people returning from an expedition.
- Pass along public jobs that arrive through [[The Rolling Papers]].
- Mention discreet opportunities heard through travelers or [[The Roach Clip]], without officially endorsing them.
- Hold payment, messages, or supplies for trusted local contacts.
- Ask adventurers to investigate threats before they reach the village.

Barnabas is not a formal Free League officer, but Free League adventurers are welcome at the Hotbox. He understands that dangerous work keeps Doobius House connected to the wider world, even if he would prefer fewer adventurers arriving injured and more of them paying their tabs.

## Conduct and Reputation

Barnabas tolerates loud drinking, card games, arguments, and the occasional broken chair. He does not tolerate violence against staff, theft from villagers, cruelty toward animals, or anyone deliberately bringing a monster into the common room.

Adventurers who protect Doobius House, pay their bills, and share useful warnings become trusted guests. Those who cause trouble may find their names added to Barnabas’s private ledger, along with a note explaining exactly why they are no longer welcome.
