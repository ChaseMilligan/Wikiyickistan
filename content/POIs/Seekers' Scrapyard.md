# Seekers' Scrapyard

The [[Seekers' Scrapyard]] is an 800-acre waste field on the edge of Stickyickistan’s [[Industrial Zone]]. It is owned and operated by [[The Seekers of the Vein]], the mining and jeweler’s guild controlled by [[House Stonemug]]. Every day, the guild sends obsolete equipment, broken extraction machinery, failed [[Magitech]], spent Arcanite casings, slag, damaged tools, and contaminated industrial refuse here rather than paying to transport it farther from the city.

From a distance, the scrapyard looks like a wasteland of rusted gantries, collapsed cranes, slag hills, cracked ore carts, and heaps of blackened metal. Up close, it is an active salvage site. Guild crews sort valuable Arcanite fragments, adamantine scraps, rare gemstones, and reusable components from the waste. Independent scavengers, hired prospectors, and members of [[The Seekers of the Vein]] compete to find anything that can be repaired, refined, or sold. The scrapyard’s proximity to [[Bongstad]] and the city’s Magitech industry makes even its refuse valuable.

The largest sections of the yard are divided into unofficial zones: the slag fields, where molten waste has cooled into jagged black ridges; the machine graveyard, where broken extraction rigs and abandoned Magitech constructs are stored; the chemical ponds, where alchemical runoff collects in brightly colored pools; and the deep heaps, where older waste has been buried beneath newer deposits. Maps of these zones are unreliable because machinery is constantly moved, scavengers dig new routes, and sections of the ground collapse without warning.

The Seekers maintain a guarded salvage depot and a network of temporary watch posts throughout the yard. Their official policy is that all discarded material remains guild property, and scavengers caught stealing valuable components can face fines, imprisonment, or quiet retaliation. In practice, the guild lacks enough personnel to monitor the entire 800 acres. Bribery, forged salvage permits, and territorial agreements with local gangs are common.

The scrapyard’s waste is also collected by groups that use it for their own purposes. [[The Seekers of the Vein]] recover raw materials for mining and jewelry production, while the [[Zooka Mire Collective]] diverts chemical runoff and organic waste toward experiments in the nearby mire. [[The Iron Sovereign]] searches the heaps for discarded Magitech, damaged constructs, and components useful for its warforged revolutionaries. The guild officially condemns these activities, but rarely investigates too closely when the waste disappears without creating a public problem.

## The Cult of Goob’lath

The scrapyard has become a center of activity for the [[Cult of Goob'lath]], a growing sect devoted to [[Goob'lath]], the black slime dragon. Most of the cult’s followers are black-winged kobolds, but oozes and a small number of Humans also venerate the dragon. Cultists hide among scavenger crews, coat themselves in the tar spreading through the deeper sections of the yard, and imitate Goob’lath’s scales as a sign of devotion.

The tar is more than decoration. It allows the cult’s Tar Elementals and other ooze servants to move through parts of the scrapyard with surprising speed. Pools of the substance have appeared near drainage channels, collapsed storage pits, and abandoned processing stations. Some workers believe the tar is industrial waste. Others suspect it is being deliberately spread by the cult as part of a larger ritual.

Cult activity has increased around a sealed section of the yard where the ground is warm, the waste shifts without explanation, and black slime appears even after the area has been cleaned. The Seekers have placed warning signs and guard posts around the site, but they have not admitted publicly that several workers and scavengers have disappeared there.

## The Ruined Alchemical Dungeon

Beneath one of the older waste mounds lies the remains of a ruined alchemical dungeon. The complex was exposed when a collapsed processing platform opened a shaft into the earth. Its original builders are unknown, but the surviving chambers contain cracked glasswork, corroded brass pipes, sealed laboratories, and fragments of alchemical equipment unlike anything currently used in the city.

The dungeon was occupied by black kobolds who attacked the [[Frockfit Builder Clan]], a goblin construction crew hired by the Seekers to build a secure vault inside the ruins. The kobolds had turned parts of the dungeon into a defensible warren and used the surrounding waste heaps as cover for raids. Their attacks stopped after [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]], a Bloodhound squad of the [[Ashen Shadow Society]], entered the scrapyard and cleared the site.

During the operation, Unit BH-Umbra Δ9 also defeated a [[Dodecahedrooze]] found within the ruined complex. The creature’s presence suggests that the dungeon’s alchemical remains are still active, contaminated, or capable of producing new life. Several chambers remain sealed, and the goblin builders have been instructed not to open them without written authorization from the Seekers.

The [[Frockfit Builder Clan]] is constructing a fortified vault in the surviving central chambers. Officially, the vault will store rare Arcanite, recovered artifacts, and hazardous industrial materials. Unofficially, the Seekers may also intend to use it to conceal evidence of illegal dumping, experimental waste, cult activity, and any discoveries they do not want the [[Nevercough Tribunal]] to inspect.

## Dangers

Visitors to the scrapyard face more than unstable piles of metal. Common hazards include:

- Collapsing waste heaps and unstable scaffolds.
- Toxic fumes and alchemical runoff.
- Buried Magitech that activates without warning.
- Tar Elementals and other oozes moving through drainage channels.
- Territorial scavengers and armed salvage crews.
- Cultists of Goob’lath disguised as laborers.
- Unstable constructs assembled from discarded parts.
- Hidden shafts leading into the ruined alchemical dungeon.
- Sealed chambers containing forgotten experiments.

## Adventure Hooks

- The Seekers hire the party to recover a valuable Arcanite core from a collapsed machine graveyard.
- A scavenger disappears after finding a sealed entrance beneath the deep heaps.
- The Cult of Goob’lath begins spreading tar toward the Industrial Zone.
- The Frockfit Builder Clan requests protection while opening a new section of the alchemical dungeon.
- A damaged Magitech construct from [[Tokenheim Extractions Inc.]] awakens and begins assembling other machines.
- Unit BH-Umbra Δ9 asks the party to investigate a sealed chamber they were unable to enter.
- The Nevercough Tribunal suspects the Seekers are hiding hazardous waste and sends inspectors into the yard.
- A Dodecahedrooze survives beneath the scrapyard and begins feeding on discarded magical equipment.
