An 800 acre scrapyard where [[The Seekers of the Vein]] dump their waste and old equipment. Activity of the [[Cult of Goob'lath]] has been reported in this area. [[Unit BH-Umbra Δ9]] has discovered a ruined Alchemical dungeon where they defeated a [[Dodecahedrooze]] and some black kobolds that attacked the [[Frockfit Builder Clan]] of goblins. The goblins were hired by the Seekers to construct a vault in the remains of the dungeon.
