# Springarden

Springarden is a sacred garden and healing sanctuary deep within [[Springwood]], where warm springs, clear pools, flowering islands, and ancient trees exist in perpetual bloom. Maintained by the [[Blossom Union]], the garden welcomes pilgrims, travelers, and those seeking rest beneath the gentle influence of the Feywild.

The boundary between the Material Plane and the Plane of Fey is unusually thin here. Small fey creatures cross freely between the worlds, and the garden’s seasons never progress beyond spring. During the annual Feyfest, a celestial alignment weakens the barrier further, filling the garden with music, glowing lights, strange visitors, and unpredictable magic.

Springarden’s pools are considered holy. Their waters soothe the mind, ease minor ailments, and calm hostile emotions. Pilgrims leave offerings at the Shining Shrine, while the Blossom Union tends the gardens, guides visitors, and protects the site from creatures that would exploit its magic.

Although Springarden is usually peaceful, its beauty can conceal danger. Fey crossings, enchanted wildlife, sudden changes in mood, and planar disturbances are common enough that visitors are expected to respect the garden’s rules. Those who damage the shrine, pollute the springs, or steal sacred offerings may be barred from the grounds or required to serve the Blossom Union as penance.

## Notable Features

- **The Shining Shrine:** The garden’s holiest site and destination for pilgrims.
- **The Sacred Pools:** Warm, clear springs surrounded by flowering trees and giant flying koi.
- **The Blossom Walks:** Mossy paths, bridges, and stepping stones connecting the garden’s islands.
- **The Healer’s Hut:** A small sanctuary where the Blossom Union treats injured visitors and caretakers.
- **The Fey Gate:** A quiet clearing where the boundary between worlds is especially thin.
