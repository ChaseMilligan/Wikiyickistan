# Blacklung Quarry

**Blacklung Quarry** is one of [[Stickyickistan]]’s oldest and most productive sources of [[Arcanite]]. Located on the lower slopes of [[Mount Zooka]], the quarry consists of open cuts, abandoned shafts, and natural caverns following rich veins of ore. Black volcanic dust hangs in the air and coats the surrounding stone, giving the mine its name.

The quarry is owned by the dwarf [[Roggan Stonebrah]]. Its workers extract raw Arcanite for [[The Seekers of the Vein]], which distributes the material to Stickyickistan’s [[Magitech]] industry and forges, especially those in nearby [[Bongstad]].

The surface works are crowded with weathered mining equipment, ore carts, collapsed lifts, and black-stained stone. A deep elevator shaft descends into the oldest workings, where the walls glitter with Arcanite and the air grows hot, dusty, and difficult to breathe. The lower tunnels are unstable and have been closed several times throughout the quarry’s history.
