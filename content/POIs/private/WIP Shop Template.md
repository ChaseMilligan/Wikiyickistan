# Notes

Additional DM notes.

## Pricing Notes

- Use normal item prices when convenient.
- Adjust monster-part value by rarity, danger, freshness, legality, and shop specialization.
- Shops should not buy every part at full value.
- The more specialized the shop, the better prices it offers for its preferred materials.
- Rare recipes should often be rewards, not simple purchases.

## Tone Notes

This shop should make monster hunting feel like an economy, not just a loot table. Every jar, hide, fang, organ, recipe, and contract should imply a creature, a story, or a problem waiting to happen.
