
**Greywake Docks** are the merchant docks on the east side of [[Nevercough Island]], where grey brick roads run down to the waters of the [[Percolat Inlet]] and the wealth of [[Stickyickistan]] arrives by rope, sail, spell, steam, and suspicious paperwork.

The docks are close enough to the island’s wealthy districts to be useful, but far enough from the polished civic center that the ruling elite can pretend not to smell the fish, tar, bilgewater, cheap incense, wet rope, and monster cargo. The [[FLS Guildhall]] sits on a grey brick road not far from the docks, making it easy for adventurers to find work from ship captains, merchants, smugglers, monster brokers, and travelers fresh off the water.

## Character

Greywake is loud at every hour. Sailors shout over gulls, dockhands curse at [[Magitech|magitech]] cranes, customs clerks argue with captains, and merchants try to look respectable while moving goods that absolutely require three different permits they probably don't have. Fog off the inlet often mixes with volcanic haze from [[Mount Zooka]], giving the harbor its name: a grey wake that clings to hulls, alleys, warehouses, and anyone who waits too long near the water.

All sorts of sea vessels dock here:

- Merchant cogs and coastal haulers
- Fishing boats and crabbers
- Luxury barges serving Nevercough elites
- Arcannabis cargo boats
- Monster-hunting skiffs
- Magitech paddlecraft
- Foreign trade ships
- Privateer vessels with freshly repainted names
- Smuggling boats pretending to be very bad fishermen

## Notable Features

- **The Grey Brick Road:** A worn road of smoke-grey bricks leading from the docks toward the [[FLS Guildhall]] and deeper into [[Nevercough Island]]. Generations of carts, boots, hooves, wheels, and leaking barrels have stained it almost black in places.
- **Customs Sheds:** Official inspection houses where clerks weigh cargo, check permits, collect fees, and pretend bribes are processing errors.
- **The Salt Ledger Warehouses:** Stacked warehouses full of spices, tools, monster parts, textiles, reagents, and goods no one wants listed clearly.
- **The Tide Cranes:** Magitech dock cranes used to unload heavy cargo, including caged beasts, alchemical tanks, and sealed relic crates.
- **The Pilgrim Steps:** A cleaner passenger landing used by travelers, minor nobles, pilgrims, and anyone trying to avoid stepping in fish guts.
- **The Low Moorings:** Cheaper berths used by smugglers, desperate captains, independent hunters, and boats with too many locks on their cargo holds.

## Power on the Docks

Greywake Docks are legal in theory and negotiated in practice. The Nevercough authorities control the customs houses, but dock unions, merchant combines, noble factors, private guards, and underworld fixers all claim pieces of the harbor.

The [[Free League of Stickyickistan]] benefits from this mess. Public contracts from [[The Rolling Papers]] often begin with a captain needing guards, a merchant needing recovery work, or a dockmaster needing someone expendable to inspect a strange crate. Less official jobs circulate through [[The Roach Clip]], usually involving cargo that was lost, stolen, mislabeled, cursed, or never supposed to exist.

## Adventure Hooks

- A ship arrives with no crew, no damage, and something heavy moving below deck.
- A merchant hires the party to recover cargo dropped into the [[Percolat Inlet]] before customs notices what it is.
- A privateer captain claims a rival vessel is smuggling cursed relics through the Low Moorings.

## Notes

Greywake Docks are a useful starting point for sea travel, smuggling jobs, merchant disputes, monster cargo accidents, and Free League contracts that begin with someone saying, “It was supposed to stay in the crate.”
