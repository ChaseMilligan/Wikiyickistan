Gwenhumara Goldmoss is one of the elder councilors of [[Redwood Watch]], a gentle elf healer remembered for tending the wounded after the old dragon troubles in [[Redwood Grove]]. She carries herself with quiet dignity, speaking softly even when fear hangs over the village. Many locals come to her for poultices, blessings, practical advice, and comfort when old griefs return.

In recent months, Gwenhumara has grown troubled by nightmares and waking anxieties about the grove. She does not claim to know exactly what is wrong, but she believes the sickness spreading through the redwoods is more than ordinary blight. Though too afraid to enter the grove herself, she gladly aids injured adventurers, shares what she knows of Redwood Watch’s history, and pleads for the missing villagers to be found or given peace.
