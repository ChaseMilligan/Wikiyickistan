# Blacklung Quarry

**Blacklung Quarry** is one of [[Stickyickistan]]’s oldest and most productive sources of [[Arcanite]]. The quarry is a sprawling network of open cuts, abandoned shafts, and natural caverns on the frontier of [[The Outback]]. Its name comes from the black volcanic dust that coats the lungs of careless miners—and from the dark stone surrounding its deepest Arcanite veins.

The quarry is owned by the dwarf [[Roggan Stonebrah]], who has operated it for years with a reputation for fair wages, practical safety rules, and stubborn refusal to abandon his workers. Most of the quarry’s ore is sold through [[The Seekers of the Vein]] and eventually finds its way into Stickyickistan’s [[Magitech]] industry.

## Current Situation

The quarry ceased operations after repeated supernatural incidents. The miners first reported hearing pickaxes striking stone in shafts that had been sealed for decades. Then workers began disappearing in small groups. Every rescue party vanished as well, though the sound of mining continued each night from somewhere below.

Roggan hired **Brother Highwind** of the [[Order of the Emerald Breath]] to investigate. Brother Highwind never returned. Since his disappearance, foul-smelling [[Arcannabis]] smoke has drifted up the elevator shaft. Those who breathe it experience waking nightmares, visions of dead companions, and the sensation that something is whispering from inside their own skulls.

The disturbances are connected to the Orcus cult that once supplied [[Dopendis Gravebreath]] with Arcanite and stolen souls. After the tomb near [[Doobius House]] was abandoned, the cult moved its unfinished necromantic working beneath Blacklung Quarry. The mine’s Arcanite veins amplify the ritual, while the smoke carries psychic residue from the souls consumed below.

## Locations

### The Quarry Face

The abandoned surface works are scattered with rusting mining equipment, collapsed lifts, and piles of black stone. The air tastes metallic, and thin curls of smoke rise from cracks in the ground even when no fire burns below.

A character proficient in Nature, Investigation, or Survival can determine that some of the recent tracks belong to miners, while others are too long-strided and deep to have been made by humanoids.

### The Elevator Shaft

The main lift descends into the oldest section of the quarry. Its cables have been cut, but the platform sometimes rises by itself during the night. The shaft walls glitter with raw Arcanite and are covered in fresh pick marks that form no recognizable mining pattern.

Anyone who looks down the shaft for more than a few seconds hears a familiar voice calling their name from the darkness.

### The Sealed Workings

The deepest tunnels were closed after a cave-in generations ago. The cult reopened them from below and marked the passage with a symbol resembling **an open hand holding a screaming skull**—Dopendis Gravebreath’s seal and a blasphemous variation of the symbol of [[Orcus]].

The walls here contain fragments of glass, black wax, and silver wire. These materials match the soul-storage experiments found in Dopendis Gravebreath’s hidden laboratory.

### The Ritual Chamber

Below the sealed workings lies a natural cavern transformed into a necromantic engine. Arcanite crystals jut from the walls like black teeth, channeling energy toward a stone platform at the center. The cult has installed empty soul-storage crystals around the platform, waiting to be filled.

A thick cloud of Arcannabis smoke hangs over the chamber. It is not ordinary smoke: it contains psychic impressions from the souls used in the ritual. Creatures exposed to it may see the missing miners, hear Brother Highwind’s prayers, or mistake an ally for a corpse.

The chamber is the likely destination of the empty black crystal recovered from Dopendis Gravebreath’s laboratory. The cult’s work here is incomplete, but the quarry is already functioning as a conduit for necrotic and Arcanite energy.

## Rumors and Clues

- The miners did not all die in the tunnels. Some were taken alive for the cult’s experiments.
- The nightly pickaxe sounds are produced by undead laborers enlarging the ritual chamber.
- Brother Highwind discovered that the corruption is being fed by stolen souls, but he was captured before he could destroy the Arcanite conduit.
- Dopendis Gravebreath may have visited the quarry personally, but no one knows whether he is still beneath it.
- Destroying the central Arcanite conduit will disrupt the ritual, but may also cause the oldest tunnels to collapse.

## Adventure Connection

Blacklung Quarry is the next stage of the Orcus cult’s plan after the events of [[Jars of Souls]]. The characters may arrive here because they followed the evidence from Dopendis Gravebreath’s empty crystal, accepted Roggan Stonebrah’s bounty through [[The Rolling Papers]], or were asked by the [[Order of the Emerald Breath]] to find Brother Highwind.

The quarry can serve as the primary location for [[The Tenebrous Stone-r]]. If the characters have already explored the tomb, they recognize the cult’s seal, silver-wire circles, and soul-storage technology. If they have not, the quarry provides the first evidence that the supernatural disturbances are part of a larger Orcus conspiracy.

## Aftermath

If the characters disrupt the ritual, the smoke clears from the elevator shaft and the pickaxe sounds finally stop. The quarry remains dangerous, however: unstable tunnels, contaminated Arcanite, and surviving cult agents make reopening the mine impossible without a thorough cleansing.

Roggan Stonebrah is grateful if the miners are rescued, but he cannot afford to pay the full bounty unless the characters also secure the main workings. The Seekers of the Vein want the Arcanite recovered. The Order of the Emerald Breath wants the corrupted crystals destroyed. These demands put the characters between Stickyickistan’s hunger for magical resources and its need to prevent the cult from using them again.
